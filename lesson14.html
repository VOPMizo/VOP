ZIRLAI SAWMPALINA

DAM REINA THURUK

Châng Pawimawh Bîk: “Chutichuan, in ei pawhin, in in pawhin, in tih apiangah pawh, Pathian ropuina tûr hlîrin ti rawh u.” I Korinth 10:31

Mi tam tak chuan tisa leh thlarau lam inkungkaihna hi a chik taka zuk ngaihtuahna an nei ngai lo. Dân narânin tisa leh rilru lam hnathawh khawihchhetu chuan thlarau lama ṭhanna pawh a dal ṭhin a ni.

Pathian Lehkhabu hian mihringte tâna Pathian duhzâwng a puan chhuah avâng leh, Pathian chuan mihringte ṭhatna tur a ngaih pawimawh avângin, Bible-ah hian Pathian duhzâwng ang chu kan thlen phâk theihna tura kan nunna kaihruai tur thil tam tak kan hmu thei ang.

THU AWIHNA LEH HRISELNA

1. Hrisêlna thu ngaihtuahin thu awihte hnênah Pathian thutiam engnge ni?
“Lalpa i Pathian âw hi ngun taka i ngaihthlâk a, a mithmuha ṭha a tihzawngte i tih a, a thupekte i ngaihsan a, a thuruat zawng zawngte i pawm chuan Aigupta mite chunga hri ka lentir angte kha i chungah reng ka lentir lovang; Kei Lalpa a tidamtu che ka nih hi.” Exodus 15:26.

Pathianin A mite hlimna leh hriselna a ngaih pawimawh pui a nih si chuan an nun dân leh khawsak dânte chu fimkhur takin an enfiahin an ngaihtuah ve ngei tur a ni.

NUNNA DAN DIK

2. Nun thianghlima nung tûrin Paula’n eng dân nge a zam?
“Chutichuan, in EI pawhin, in IN pawhin, IN TIH APIANGAH PAWH, PATHIAN ROPUINA TUR HLIRIN TI RAWH U.” I Korinth 10:31.

MAHNI TA IN NI LO

3. Pathianin min siam avânga A ta kan nih bâkah, A ta kan nihna kawng dang eng nge ni?
“A nih leh in taksa chu, in chhûnga awm Pathian hnên aṭanga in hmuh THLARAU THIANGHLIM IN a ni tih in hre lo em ni le? Mahni ta pawh in ni lova, mana lei in ni tawh asin; chuvangin, in taksaah chuan Pathian chawimawi rawh u.” 1 Korin 6:19, 20.

Min tlan nân Isua Kristan man a pe ta a ni. Mihringte tân thihna tuar lo selang chuan lei mihringte hi Setana khua leh tui an ni kumkhua ang. Nimahsela, KRISTA THISENA LEI kan nih tawh avangin A duh dan anga nung leh khawsa turin kan nunnaah hian rawn inrawlh theihna a nei a. Kan taksate hi Thlarau Thianghlim chênna hmun thianghlim a ni tur a ni, tin, Pathian chu thuneitu diktak a nih avangin A chênna chu engtia enkawl leh cheibawl tur nge ni ang tih pawh min ruat sak theihna a nei a ni.

PUAN LAWKNA PAWIMAWH TAK

4. Kan taksate tikhawlo lo tura thu pawimawh tak puan lawkte chu engtenge ni?
“PATHIAN IN IN NIH leh Pathian Thlarau Thianghlim nangmahniah a awm reng tih in hre lo em ni? Tupawhin Pathian in chu a TIHKHAWLOH chuan chu mi chu Pathianin a TIKHAWLO vang; Pathian in chu a thianghlim si a, chu in chu nangmahni in ni e.” 1 Korin 3:16,17.
“TISA leh THLARAU BAWLHHLAWHNA zawng zawngah I INTIFAI ang u, Pathian ṭih dêk chunga thianghlimna tifamkimin.” 2 Korinth 7:1.

EI TUR ATANA PATHIAN RUAT

5. A tirin mihring ei atan Pathianin engnge a ruat?
“Tin, Pathian vêkin, ‘Ngai teh u, lei chung zawng zawnga THLAI CHI AWM TINRENG leh thing tin reng, THING CHI NEI RAH AWMNATE CHU, ka pe che u a ni; IN CHAW ATAN A NI ANG,’ a ti a.” Genesis 1:29.

Pathianin mihring a siam zawh veleh a chaw tur pawh engnge ni ang tih a hrilh nghâl a. THEI RAH leh THLAI CHITE chu a chaw atân a ruat sak a, chu mi hnuah ‘THLAI CHU I EI TAWH ANG’ tiin a pek belh leh a ni. (Genesis 3:18).

Mihring Siamtu chuan mihring tana ṭha ber tur a hria ang tih chu phat rual a ni lo va. Ei leh bâr tur thilah pawh an tâna ṭha ber tûrte a hrilhin a kawhhmuh vek a. Chu mi kawnga Pathian zirtirna chu hriselna atâna thil ṭha tak an nihzia, tun laia ei leh bâr kawnga thiamna bîk neite hian chiang takin an fiah tawh a ni.

SA HI CHAW ṬHA BER A NI LO

6. Sa ei kawngah Pathianin eng angin nge phal chin leh phal loh chin a neih?
“Heng sate hi a ni, in ei theih tur chu: bawngpa te, beram te, kêl te, sangai te, sakhi pa te, sathâr te, ram kêl te, ram lawi te, ram berâm te hi. Tin, sa tinrêng, tin kâk chhuak, ṭhialrêt ṭhial chi tawh phawt zînga mi chu in ei thei ang.” Deuteronomy 14:4-6.

Bible-a bung hnih, Deuteronomy 14 leh Leveticus 11 - te hi ei atâna ṭha leh ṭha lo thliar hranna an ni deuh bik a. Sa ṭhenkhat chu “thianghlim” tih an ni a, ṭhenkhat erawh chu “tenawm” tih an ni thung a. Sa thianghlimte hi Pathianin ei a phal tak nain, a tîra A ruat ang chiah chu ani lo.

Natna leh tuarnate hi tam tak chu ei leh bâr kawnga kal sualna avânga lo awm an ni. Dr. Olser, damdawi lama mi thiam hmingthang tak chuan “Natna inkai theih leh chetsual avanga tuarnate tih loh, natna dangte hi chu za zela sawmkua chu ei tur ṭha leh dik ei loh vânga lo awm an ni,” tiin a sawi.

Mihring hi a thil châkzawng – hrawk leh kaa tui apiang te leh pi pu dânten an thunun tur a ni lova, Pathianin A lehkhabua dân a zamte hian an thunun zawk tur a ni. Ei leh bâr kawnga Pathian ropuina tur hlira kan awm theih dân ber chu Pathian Thu zirtirna anga ei leh in a ni.

BIBLE-IN ZU IN A DEM

7. Vânram pawnlama awm turte chu tutenge ni?
“Mi fel lote chuan Pathian ram an luah lo vang, tih in hre lo em ni le? Bumin awm suh u inngaih hmangte emaw . . . zu ruihhmangte emawin . . . . Pathian ram an luah lovang.” I Korinth 6:9,10.
“Hlîng bawn anga awmin an uain in chuan chiah huh hnêp angin awm mah sela, buhpâwl ro ang maia tihboral vek an ni ang.” Nahuma 1:10.

Tumahin taksa leh thlarau tâna chhiat hlauthâwn awm hauh lovin zu an in reng thei lo. Zu in chîng mi chu miten pawisaa an inkhêl ṭhîn ang hian a nunna chu inkhelh nân a hmang a ni. Solomona chuan hetiangin zu chanchin min hrilh:

“Uain hi nuihzabûra mi siamtu a ni a, zupui hi insual bûng bûngna a ni; chu mi buma awmte chu an fing lo a ni.” Thufingte 20:1.

Pathian rorêlna chu an ṭhenawm khawvêngte tâna zu satu emaw, petu emaw chungah pawh a thleng dâwn a ni:

“A ṭhenawm hnena zu in tur petu chu a chung a pik e. Nang an saruak i hmuh theih nâna in tur chawhpawlh a tiruitu chu!” Habakuka 3:15.

Dr. Geo Thomason, MD, FRCS, damdawi lama mi thiam hmingthang tak chuan tihian a sawi: “Zuin a khawih ber lai chu in thunun theihna lai te hi a ni a, chûng chu a hmet zeng a, tichuan, miin chet dan mawi leh dik a neihte chu a lak bo sak ṭhin a ni. Ṭhenrual zinga fel taka a khawsak dante a tihchhiatsak a, chhia leh ṭha a thliar hran theihna te a tihbo sak ṭhin.

“A inthunun theihna ber, a thluak bâwr khat lai chu zu chuan a tihmut ṭhin avângin rante ang maiin nun dân mawi leh chêtze fel hriatna nei lovin a khawsaktir ta ṭhin a ni.

“Chu chauh pawh ni lovin, a hriatna thazâm, a hah leh chauh hriat theihna pawh a khawih chhiatsak bawk ṭhin. Chuvang chuan a ni, zu in miin ngui deuh bêr bûr emaw, chau deuh emawa a inhriata, zu a han ina harh ta huaia inhriatna a neih ṭhin ni. Chau a ni tih a inhriat theihna chu a zuk khawih chhiat ṭhin avângin chau a nih rêng a inhre lova, hahdam nia inhriatna a nei zâwk ṭhin. Chu chu mi mihring bumtu mai a ni tih kan hre thei. Zu rui harh fim lehte pawh hian an zu inin a nghawng nasatzia thu mawlh an sawi ṭhin a ni. An harh fim rual chuan chau leh ngui an ni tih an inhriat theihna kha a lo harh ve nghal a, zuin tur ang maia a tihrawlte a fan chiam avangin a hmet zawi vek ṭhin a ni.

“Zuin thluak bawra inthunun theihna tak mai a khawih buaizia miin a hriat thiam tawh hnu chuan zuin dan bawhchhiatna leh, hurna te a rawn kaihhnawih ṭhinzia hi hriat thiam harsa a ti ngai lo vang. Mi tam takin zu an in hma, zuin an chhia leh ṭha hriatna a tihdel hma chuan ram dân bawhchhetu emaw, khaw êng zah lova hmeichhia pawngsual chawt chawt emaw, tual thattu emaw an ni thei ang tih rêng rêng an ring ngai lo. Nimahsela, zuin an inthunun theihna a han khawih zen meuh chuan an rin phak ngai loh thilte chu an ti si ṭhin a ni,” tiin min hrilh.

He thil khawlo tak, zu hi sorkarin engtinnge a tihdawn tih thu chu kan sawi lo vang a, amaherawhchu, mi mal ṭheuhin enginnge kan tih ang tih chu kan ngaihtuah ngun hle a ṭûl ang.

Salvation Army pâwl hruai ṭhin thu, Evangeline Booth chuan zu chanchin hetiang hian a sawi: “Zu hian thil dang zawng aiin thisen a dâwt kâm nasa a; in leh lo a theh thang ral nasa a; mi lei a chawitir nasa a; naupang a suat nasa a; nupa kawpchawi a kai ṭhen zing êm êm a; mi fel a tikhawlo nasa a; mitdelna a thlen zingin kebaina te a thlen zing êm êm a; ngaihtuahna a pawt hniam a; hmeichhia a timualpho hnemin mipa a khawih chhe zingin thlân laih tur a titam êm êm a. Thihna rawn thlentir thei, thihna tur dang zawng zawng aiin hêng kan han tarlante hi a rawn thlentir nasa zawk a ni.”

Mi ropui tak pakhat pawhin zu hnathawh ṭhat lohzia hetiangin a sawi: “Zu hi suahsualna hlâwm a niber mai a, khawlohna pa, tenawmna nu, anchhia a zawnga anchhe khawlo ber, Diabola ṭhian bul ber; Pathian hmêlma huatthlala ber a ni,” tiin.

THLAI TUR PAI BIBLE-IN A HNAWL

8. Nun dan leh khawsak dan lawilo tak zui tlatte hnena Pathian thuchah engnge ni?
“Chutichuan in zinga mipa emaw, hmeichhia emaw, chhûngkua emaw, hnam emaw . . . . AN THINLUNGIN TUNAH HIAN LALPA KAN PATHIAN CHU HAWISANIN, in zingah thil kha (margin-ah tûr pai thlai) leh sai chawr chhuahna bulte in lo awm dah ang e; tin, chuti a nih zawngin he chhechham thu hi a hriatin, ‘Thil rova thil hnâwng tihhlum tum tih angin’ (tuihal hnêna ruihna belhchhahin) . . . tin, a thinlungin mal a insawm chawp dah rual ang e; LALPA CHUAN A NGAIDAM DAWN HAUH LOVA, Lalpa thinurna leh a thikna chuan chu mi chu a ur ang a, he lehkhabua anchhe chuang zawng zawng hi a chungah a awm ang a, Lalpan van hnuai ata a hming chu a thai bo bawk ang.” Deut. 29:18-20.

Nunna tichhe thei thil vuan tlattute chunga rorêlna lo thleng tur chu Pathianin a puang lawk diam tawh a ni. Heng chang thute hian TUR PAI THLAITE LEH RUIHNA te hi an hnâwl (dem) a ni.

Mihringin tûr pai thlai chi tam tak an hman ṭhinte hi ngaihtuah teh. An zinga a lar deuh ber leh chhiatna thlen nasa ber chu vaihlo (a tûr pai chu nicotine) hi a ni. America (USA) mipui ringawt pawhin kum khat chhûngin sikret tlûklehdingâwn zahnih an zu ral a ni. Inzawm thliaiin dah ta ila, lei hi a kiar lai berah pawh vawi zahnih tehmeuh a vet chhuak zo dâwn tihna a nih chu! He mi bâkah hian kum tin churut tlâwn tlûk-leh-dingâwn riat lai an zu ral bawk.

Fimkhur taka an lo en chhin ṭhin tawhna aṭangin mei zu mite chu mei zu ngai lote aiin an rilru a muang zawkin an ṭuan a fûm zawk a, fuat an nei tlem zawk a, an hna a phuai zawk fo bawk tih hriat a ni.

Vaihlo emaw, sahdah emaw hi ruih theih thil a ni a, ruih theih leh tûr pai a nih avâng tak chuan miten an hmang ṭhin a ni. VAIHLO HI ENGTIKAWNG PAWHIN HMAN NI SELA, TAKSA TANA CHHIATNA A NI VEK. Lungphu rang lutuk te, Bright’s disease (kal nâ) an tihte leh pum pui pânte hi, tam tak chu vaihlo puak chhuah an ni.

Tin, vaihlo hian nunna a titawi bawk. Tun hnaia Johns Hopkins University-a Dr. Raymond Opearl-an mei zu heh tak te, zûk satliah deuha zu te leh zu ngai lote sâng tam tak zinga Insurance Company ten an tih dân ṭhin ang zuia enchhinna a neihah chuan, kum 30 leh kum 50 inkar mi mei heh takte chu mei zu ngai lote aiin a lêtin an thi tam zawk tih a hmu chhuak.

The Mutual Life Insurance Company pawhin chu company-a mi sâng tam takte zingah chuan mei zu ngai lote dam chhung chu mei zu heh takte aiin hmun thuma ṭhena hmun khatin a sei zawk tih an hmu chhuak bawk.

Pathian chawimawina tûra nun tumtu chuan vaihlo hi an sawi mawi thei ngai lo vang. Thil bawlhhlawh, pawisa tihek leh nungchang tichhe thei thil a ni si a.

VAIHLO CHANCHIN MAK TAKTE

Dr. Chas. L. Barber sawi angin, “Nu mei zu mi fa hrin chu dam lo sa a ni. Amahah chuan tûr a awm avângin a pian aṭanga hapta hnih lekah thi maithei a ni. Tin, chutianga thi tate chu an thih hnua an han zaina aṭangin an thin te, lung te leh kawchhûng thil dangte a lo khawih chhe sa tawh reng a ni tih hriat a ni. Mei zu deuh rengte fa, za zêla sawmruk chu kum hnih an tlin hmain an thi zêl.” – Daniel H. Kress, MD, The Cigarette as a Physician Sees It, phêk 33, Pacific Press Publishing Association, Mountain View, California.

Dr. Stevens pawhin, “Vaihlo hian thluak hnathawh a khawih chhia a, hriat thiamna a tingawng a, hriatrengna a tichau bawk. Tin, rilru leh finna tichhetu a ni,” tiin a sawi. Geo. Thomason, MD, FRCS, Science Speaks to Young Men on Liquor, Tobacco, Narcotics, and Marijuana, phêk 75, Pacific Press Publishing Association, Mountain View, California.

Damdawi in pakhat, Saylor’s Sanitarium an tiha an dâktawr Saylor chuan, “Ngaih dân thar satliah ringawt lawm mi ka ni lova, nimahsela, sikret (vaihlo sial) zûk chungchângah hian inzirtîrna engemaw ṭan thuai a nih loh chuan rilru hniam lamah kan la intlânsiak thuai dâwn tih erawh hi chu ka sawi ngam a ni,” tiin a sawi – Ibid., phêk 76.

Tin, “Vaihlo hi lung tâna tûr chak tak” a nih thu Dr. Whittaker-an min hrilh bawk. – Ibid., p. 81.

“Vaihlo zûkna (sikret a ni deuh bîk a) hi lung lam natna pakhat, cardiovarscular disturbance, sipaiten an neih ṭhin paw chhuaktu pakhat a ni.” – Ibid., p. 85.

Sir Berkeley Moynihan an tih, pumpui leh ril pân enkawlna lama khawvêl mi thiam hmingthang tak chuan chutiang natna nei a enkawl mêkte tâna thil hlauhawm ber deuhthawa a ngaih chu mei zûk hi a ni. – Ibid., p. 88.

Pu Gandhi-an engtinnge a sawi ilo thlîr ve teh ang, “Mei zûkna hian finna a khawih buai a, thil chîn ṭhalo tak a ni. Dâktorte i biak râwn chuan, (dâktawr ṭha an nih phawt chuan) mei zûkna hian pânchhia a hring ve mêk a, emaw, awmtîr theitu emaw tal chu a ni tih an hrilh ang che.

“Mei zûk hian thaw a tirimchhia a. Thil chin khawlo tak a ni. Rêlah te pawh chuang sela mei zu mi chuan an bul hnaiah hmeichhiate leh midang meizu ngai lote an awm ang tih ngaihtuah lovin an pak khu luai luai a, mite chuan rimchhia an ti ang tia inthlahrunna rêng an nei ngai lo. . . Mei zu mi chuan pawisakna nei lovin khawiah pawh chil an pik puat puat mai a ni.

“Heng thilte bawiah hian kan awm ngai tûr a ni lo va, churut leh sikret, ram danga an siam chhuah emaw, mahni zial chawp emaw lakah te hian kan inthiar fihlim hlauh tûr a ni.”

INSUMNA DIK

9. Eng dânin nge Pathian fate chu kaihruai ang?
“Intihsiaknaah te chuan a bei ṭalh apiang chu engkimah an insum ṭhin a ni. Anni zawngin lallukhum chhe thei hmu tûrin kan bei a, keini erawh chuan lallukhum chhe thei lo hmu turin.” I Korinth 9:25.

Intihsiaknaa tel ṭhin, infiam mi tam takte chuan ei leh bâr kawngah taksa tâna ṭha lo zawng zawng lakah an invawng thianghlim a, lâwmaman an hmuh theih nân chûng thil ṭhalovah te chuan an insûm ṭhîn a ni. Chatuan nunna lallukhum chang tûra vân lam pana bei fan fantute tân chuan engkima insûm a ṭul zual tak meuh a ni. INSUMNA DIK TAK CHU THIL ṬHALO LAKA INSUMNA LEH HRISELNA ATANA ṬHATE FEL TAKA HMAN THIAMNA A NI.

Zû te, uain te, rakzû te, kâni te leh hriselna tichhe thei thilte rêng rêng lak aṭang hian insum tlat tur a ni.

Kâni (opium) ngaih hi India mite chunga ânchhiaah hian a chhe ber pakhat a tling a, he rama ruih theih thil zînga a lâr ber leh lo awm hmasa ber a ni. Kristian Hun lo inṭan tirh vel lai khan Arab sumdawng miten India ramah hian an rawn la lût a, Ganges lui dunghmar lama mi chêngte zîngah a lo darh ṭan ta a ni. Kum 1500 AD te kha chuan sumdâwnna nghet tak a lo ni tawh a. Galen-a, Grik daktawr hmingthang berte zînga pakhat pawhin kum zabi hnihna velah khân, “Kâni hi ruih theih thilah chuan a chak ber a, hriatna a tichawlawl a, mutna mi a neihtir ṭhin,” tiin a lo sawi tawh. Sorkar hian khap tlat mah sela, a chîng dawklak tawhte tân chuan hmuh theih dan kawng engemaw hi a awm thei zel mai zawng a nih hi.

Nau awmtu ṭhenkhat chuan an nau awm lai muttir nân, a hlauhawmzia hre hawt lovin an hmang ṭhin a ni.

India ramah hian chuan khawsakte a harsat êm avangin nute pawh hian an pasalte ang bawka lei an leh ve tâwrh tâwrh a ṭûl fo ṭhin a. An zinga ṭhenkhatte chuan a ṭhat lohzia hre lovin an hnathawh hlana naute chu buaipui an ṭûl loh nân, tiin kâni an pe ṭhin a ni. Kâni hian mihring taksain natna a dan theihna a tihchauh sak a, naupang zinga thihna titamtu pawh hi a ni ve ngeiin a rinawm. Kâni hi thil hlauhawm tak, Bible pawhina a khap a ni tih hi i hre ṭheuh ang u.

“PATHIAN IN IN NIH leh Pathian Thlarau nangmahniah a awm reng tih in hre lo vem ni? Tupawhin Pathian in chu a tihkhawloh chuan chu mi chu Pathianin a tikhawlo vang.” 2 Korinth 3:16,17.

Pathian Lehkhabua Pathian duhzâwng târlante nêna inrema nun duhtute chuan Kâni hi an hmang duh ngai lovang.

Isua Krista hi kan entawn tur a ni a, chuvangin kan nundan kaihhruai nan, “Khawvêl mite khawsak dan hi Isua Kristan engtinnge a ngaih ang? Engnge a in ang a, engnge a ei ang?” tiin zawt ṭhin ila, min ṭanpuitu ṭha tak a ni phawt ang.

Ruih theih thilte hi a tâwk a fang tal chu in turin mite hi thu leh A nun dânin A rawn fuih ang em? Chutiangin tumah min rawn fuih hauh lovang tih chu kan hre ṭheuh a ni; zu ruite rêng rêng hi zu ruih hmang nih duh vânga zu ruih hmang lo ni ta an ni love. Tlêm tlêmin an han in ṭan zeuh zeuh phawt a, chu mi hnuah zu heh tak an lo ni chauh a ni. “DEK SUH: TEM SUH: KENG SUH,” tih hi Krista fuihna thu an ni zâwk ngei ang.

Zu hi hmanphalna A rawn siam duh hauh lo vang a, zu inkhapna hi mihring zalênna khawih buaituah a ngai hek lovang.

Isua Krista chu a lo kal leh thuai dâwn ta tih hi i ring ve em? Amah lo tâwk tura inbuatsaihna lo neih diam i duh lâwm ni? Pathian fa dik takte chuan he thu hi an ngaipawimawh ṭheuh ang:

“Amah a lo inlâr chuan, mah angin kan awm dawn tih zawng kan hria, amah chu a awm pangngai ang takin kan hmu dâwn si a. TUPAWH AMA CHUNGA CHU MI BEISEINA CHU NEI APIANGIN AMAH A THIANGHLIM ANGIN A INTITHIANGHLIM ṬHIN.” 1 Johana 3:2, 3.
