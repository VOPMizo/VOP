ZIRLAI SAWMHNIH LEH PAKHATNA

THU SAWM PEK KHAWIH BUAINA

Châng Pawimawh Bîk: “Chungnungbera kalhin thu a sawi ang a, Chungnungbera mi thianghlimte a tichau ang, hunte leh dân chu tihdanglam a tum ang.” Daniela 7:25.
Note: He zirlai i zir ṭan hma hian zirlai 20-na kha chhiar nawn leh phawt ang che.

Zirlai 20-naah khân inlârnaa Daniela'n “ki tê tak tê” a hmuh kha engnge a nih a, engnge a entîr tih kan zir tawh a. Thil tam tak avângin chu ki tê tak tê chuan Rom Kohhran an entîrzia chiang takin kan hmu nghe nghe. He zirlaiah pawh hian chu mi tifiah lehzual tûr chanchin dang kan zir belh dâwn a ni.

KI TÊ TAK TÊ LO ṬHAN ZÊLNA

1. Khawvêl chung zawng zawnga thuneihna nei, “ki tê tak tê” chu thâwklehkhata lo ṭhang thut em ni?
“Ka kal hnuin CHINGHNE KAWLHTE IN ZINGAH AN LO LÛT ANG A, pawlho chu an zuah lo vang tih ka hria e.” Tirhkohte 20:29.

A ni, Pathian laka helna thlarau hi Krista zirtîrte zîngah ngei pawh khân a lo awm tawh a, Paulan hêng thu a sawi lai ngei pawh hian a awm a ni.:

“In zinga mi ngei pawh hi, zirtîrhote in hnêna pakaitir tûrin thu hran sawiin lo la awm ang.” Tirhkohte 20:30.

Thessalonika mite hnêna Tirhkoh Paula lehkhathawna kan hmuh anga Pathian mite inralring tûra ngenna nasa hi Pathian Lehkhabu châng dangah kan hmu lovang.

“Tuman engmahin tihder che u suh se; tluksanna chu a lo thlen hmasak zeta, DAN BAWHCHHEPA, boral fapa chu a lo lan loh chuan chu mi ni chu a thleng dâwn si lo va.” 2 Thessalonika 2:3.

Sualna awm zêl hi “dân bawhchhepa” leh “boral fapa” angin Paula hian a sawi chauh a ni lova, a hnathawhte pawh chiang takin a târlang nghâl a ni:

Ani chuan Pathiana an vuah apiang leh, sakhua zawng zawng dovin, an chunga awm tumin a inchawimawi ang: Chutichuan Pathian biak Ina ṭhuin Pathian ang hialin a intâr chhuak ang.” 2 Thess. 7:4

Thutak aṭanga kal sualna nasa tak hi chu a ṭiak tîr mêk a ni tih chiang takin Paula hian a hria a ni.

THU ROPUI TAK SAWI

2. Tu dodâlin nge ki tê tak tê (Papacy) chuan thu a sawi?
“Chungnungbera kalhin thu a sawi ang a.” Daniels 7:25.

Pope thuneihnain Pathian chauhin a neih, thuneihna te, nihna te leh thiltihtheihna te chu ama neih ve rêng anga kenkawh a tumna hian he hrilh lawkna mak tak hi a tithleng famkim ta a ni. Chu mi tifiah turin tam takte zînga tlêm tê chauh kan han târlang e:

“Pathian Lehkhabua Krista hming zawng zawng kohhran lu ber a nihna kâwktute chu Pope nihna anga chhiar an ni.” Bellarmine, “On the Authority of Councils,” Book 2, chap. 17.
“Pope chu lei leh vân leh purgatory (Infernorum) lal ber angin lallukhum chhâwng thuma khumtîr a ni.” – “Prompta Bibliotheca,” Ferraris, vol. 6, p. 26, Article “Papa.” (the Pope)
“Pope thu tihtlûkna leh Pahtian thu tihtlûkna chu pakhat a ni ..... chuvângin, tumahin Pope hnên aṭangin Pathian hnênah an zualko (appeal) ngai lo.” - Writings of Augustinus de Ancons, “On an Appeal from the Decision of the Pope.”
“Nang hi leia Pathian dang i ni e.” – From Oration of Christopher Marcellus. “History of the Councils,” vol. 19, column 109.

MI THIANGHLIMTE A TICHAU ANG

3. “Ki tê tak tê” thiltihtheihnaa thil danglam tak dang chu engnge ni?
“Chungnungbera mi thianghlimte a tichau ang,” Daniela 7:25

He hrilh lâwkna thu hrilhfiahin Albert Barnes chuan hetiang hian a sawi:

“He hrilh lâwkna hian Pope thuneihna thu a sawi a ni tih hi ringhlel an awm thei dâwn em ni le? Spain rama rinna hrang nei hremna (Inquisition) te, Waldenses-ho tihduhdahna te, Duke of Alva nunrâwn chhuahna te, Smithfields meipui te, Goa-a nghaisakna te hi; - Pope thiltihtheihna chu a ni ang tih loh theih a ni love.” – “Notes on the Old Testament,” commenting on Daniel 7.
“Kum 1208 khân Pope Innocent 3-nain Waldesnes leh Albigenses dona tûr thu a puang a, mi maktaduai tam takin an boral phah ta nghe nghe a. Kum 1540-80 chhûnga Jesuits din tirh aṭangin Protestant 900,000 lai tihboral an ni a, kum 30 chhûngin rinna hrang nei hremnaah mi 150,000 an boral bawk.” Ibid.

PATHIAN DÂN THLÂK TUMNA

4. Engtiangin nge ki tê tak têin Pathian dân tihdanglam a tum?
“Hunte leh dân chu tihdanglam a tum ang a.” Daniela 7:25.

Chiang taka he hrilhlâwknain a târlan chu Rom Kohhranin thu sâwm pêk dân tihdanglam a tum thu hi a ni. Chu dânah chuan ni sarih ni hi thianghlim a ni a, serh tûra tih a ni bawk.

Pope thuneihna erawh chuan ni sarih ni hmasa ber (Sunday) hi Chawlhni (Saturday) aia serh zâwk tûr leh, Pathian biakna nia hman zâwk tûr a ni e, tiin, thu a pe thung a ni. Chutianga thupêk tihdanglam a lo nih dân thu chu he zirlaiah hian kan zir ang.

DAN TIHDANGLAM CHU

5. Chawlhni (Sabbath) thu Pathian dânin a sawi dân chu engtinnge ni?
“Chawlhni hi thianghlim taka serh tûrin hre reng rawh. Ni ruk chhûngin i thawk tûr a ni a, i hna zawng zawng chu i thawk tûr a ni: A ni sarih ni erawh chu Lalpa i Pathian tân chawlhni a ni e: ... Chuvângin Lalpan Chawlhni chu MAL A SAWM A, a TITHIANGHLIM ta a ni.” Exodus 20:8-11.

Pathianin Amah pâwlna ni atân leh biak ni bîk atân a ruat, Ni Sarih Ni hi, kalendar aṭang pawh hian a lang chiang êm êm a ni.

6. Kristian tam zâwkten Pathianni (Sunday) hi chawlhna niah leh Pathian biak ni atâna an lo hman tâkna chhan engnge ni?

He thil mak tak hi hrilhfiahna pakhat chauh a awm-sakhaw thiltihtheihna, ki tê tak têin a entîrin Pathian dân a tihdanglam vâng a ni.

“Hunte leh dânte, chu tihdanglam a tum ang a.” Daniela 7:25.

POPE-IN A TIHDANGLAM ANGIN

7. Ki tê tak tê emaw, Pope emawin eng angin nge Pathian dân tihdanglam tuma a beih?
  1. A thu inchhâng tam berah thupêk pahnihna hi a hmaih hlauh, kohhran kalhmâng nên a inkalh avângin.
  2. Thupêk palina aṭangin serh tûra thupêk a nihna chhan tak leh, thupêk theihna lantîrtu tak chu a la kiang hlauh bawk.
  3. Tin, thupêk sâwmna a khak buai leh a, a zavaia sâwm a tlin theih nân thupêk sâwmna chu pahnihah a ṭhen sawm hlauh a ni.

“Hunte leh dân chu tihdanglam a tum ang” tih hrilh lâwkna hi Katholik Kohhran hian a tithleng famkim takzet a ni.

REMTIHNA RINAWM

8. Thu sâwm pêka Chawlhni tihdanglam tuma beihnaah. Pope thuneihna chu a mawhphurtu a ni tih hi chu thuneihna chuan a nemngheh pui rêng em?

Chutiang a nihzia a pawmpui bâkah a chhuang êm êm bawk a ni.

Cardinal Gibbons hun laia Katholik Kohhran chanchinbu, September 23, 1893-a chhuaka kan hmuh angin: “Katholic Kohhran chuan ...... Pathian rawngbâwltu a nihna hmangin Inrinni aṭanga Pathianniah nî a thlâk ta a.”

Katholikte ngeiin Bible aṭanga fiahna a awm lohzia an sawi:

Cardinal Gibbons chuan, “Pathian Lehkhabu hi Genesis aṭanga Thu Puan thlengin chhiar chhuak parh mah la, Pathianni (Sunday) tihthianghlim thu rêng rêng, tlar khat tê pawh i hmu lo vang. Pathian Lehkhabu chuan Inrinni (Saturday) serh tûr thu a sawi zâwk a ni,” tiin, a sawi – “Faith of our Fathers,” p. 89.
KATHOLIC PRESS : Sydney, Australia – August 25, 1900: “Pathianni hi Katholik din a ni a, serh tûr a nih chungchângah pawh Katholic rin dân bulpui aṭang chauhin ṭanfung siam theih a ni... Pathian Lehkhabu pum puiah hian, a bul aṭanga a tâwp thlengin, hapta kâr tina miten Pathian biakna an neihna ni tûr hi ni sarih ni aṭanga ni sarih ni hmasa bera suan tâkna thu rêng rêng a chuang hauh lo.” AMERICAN SENTINEL (N.Y), June 1,1893, page 173 (K.C. Father T. Enright, C.SS.R., of Redemptorist College): “Baibul chuan “Chawlhni vawng thianghlim tûrin hre reng rawh,” a ti a, Katholik Kohhran thung erawh chuan, ‘Chutiang ni lo ve, Pathian hnên aṭanga thiltihtheihna ka neih hmangin Chawlhni chu ka tibo tawh a, ni sarih ni hmasa ber serh tûrin thu ka pe zâwk a che,’ a ti a ni.”

ROMAN KATHOLIC THU INCHHÂNG HAI LANNA

9. Roman Katholic Kohhran thu inchhângin Kohhranina Pathian dân thianghlim a tihdanglam thu a zirtîr em?
“Zawhna – Eng ni hi nge Chawlhni chu?”
“Chhânna – Inrinni (Saturday) hi Chawlhni a ni.”
“Zawhna – Eng vângin nge Pathianni hi Inrinni aia kan serh zâwk si?”
“Chhânna – Katholic Kohhranin Laudikei Inkhâwmpuiah (A.D 336) Inrinni thianghlimna chu Pathiannia a suan tâk avângin Pathianni hi Inrinni aiah kan serh a ni.” – “The Convert's Catechism of Catholic Doctrine,” Rev. Peter Gierman, C.SS.R., p. 50, 2nd edition 1910.

Suala khat leh sual thei mihringin Pathian dân danglam thei lo tihdanglam tuma a beihna hi ngaihtuah teh. Tirhkoh Paulan hun lo awm zêl tûr thlîra, he thiltihtheihna lo ṭhang lian zêl tûr a hmuh a, “dân bawhchhepa, boral fapa, .... Pathiana an vuah apiang leh, sakhua zawng zawng dovin, an chunga awm tumin a inchawimawi ang; Chutichuan Pathian biak ina ṭhuin Pathian ang hialin a intâr chhuak ang,” a ti pawh hi a mak lo ve. 2 Thes. 2:3,4.

PROTESTANT-HOIN AN NGEMNGHET

10. He ni thlâk thuah hian Protestan-ten engnge sawi tûr an neih?

Pathianniah hian Pathian thuneihna a lang lo va, a lan lohzia chu Kohhrana mi thiam sângten vawi tam tak an sawi lang tawh ṭhîn:

Canon Robert Eyton:
“Thuthlung Tharah hian Pathiannia hna thawh loh tûr thu sawina kam khat emaw, chuti hawizâwnga sawi thawina emaw rêng rêng a awm lo.” – “The Ten Commandments,” p. 62 (Kegan Paul, 1894)
Sir William Dimville:
“Kristian hun inṭan hnua kum za engemaw zât lai a ral hnuah chauh Kristian Kohhranin Pathianni hi chawlhni angin a hmang ṭan chauh a ni.” – “Examination of Six Texts,” p. 291 (Chapman and Hall. 1894)
Watchman (Baptist):
“Pathian Lehkha Thuah hian ni hmasa ber hi Chawlhni anga a sawina rêng rêng a lang lova. Chawlhni anga serh chu tihmâkmawh a ni hek lo, chutianga sawina a awm na hek lo.”
Presbyterian mi pakhat ziak:
“Chawlhni hi dân – thu sâwm pêka pakhat a ni a. Chu ngawt pawh chuan he thupêk hi kumkhaw tlaitluana ding tûr a nihzia a tilang chiang hle.... Ṭhatna Dân pumpui hi ṭhiat tawh anga sawina fel tak a awm si loh chuan Chawlhni hi a ding reng tûr a ni... Krista zirtîrna pawhin Chawlhni hi chatuana ding tûr a nihzia a kâwk vek a ni.” – “Theology Condensed,” by T. C Blake, D.D., pp. 474, 475.
Khawvêl chanchin ziaktu sawi:
“Pathianni kût (festival of Sunday) hi kût dangte ang bawka mihring din chhuah chawp mai a ni a, Pathian thupêk ni anga chutianga hmana chu a postolte rin dân nên chuan nasa takin a inpersan a, Pathiannia Chawlhni va suanna pawh hi apostolte tum dân aṭanga nasa taka pênna a ni.” – History of the Christian Religion and Church,” by Neander, p. 136.
Encyclopedia:
“Pathianni tih hming hi Kristian lote sak a ni a, ni sarih ni hmasa ber atân an hming sak a ni, chu mi ni chu ni (sun) biak nâna an hman ṭhin a nih avângin; ... Ni sarih ni erawh hi chu Pathian ngeiin A malsâwm leh tihthianghlim a ni a...... a tâna serh thianghlim tûrin A thil siamte hi a ngiat a ni. He thupêk hi mi zawng zawngin kumkhuaa tihmâkmawha an neih tûr a ni.... Siamtuin ‘nisarih ni chu mal a sâwm a’ – ni dang zawng aia chungnung zâwk a nihzia a puang a, A duhsakna awmna bîk ni a ni.... Chutichuan mihringte hi an awm chhûng chuan leh khawvêl hi a awm chhûng chuan Chawlhni pawh hi a ding reng ang. A innghahna a awm chhûng chuan hnâwl mai theih a ni lo... An sawi fo ṭhin anga Judate Chawlhni, thupêk palinaa siam ngheh tâk mai a ni lo ve. Serh thianghlim tûra thupêkah hian Judate chungchang lanna rêng rêng a awm lo, thupêk pathumna emaw, parukna emawa a lang lo ang bawk hian.” – “Eadie's Biblical Eyclopedia,” 1872, p. 551.
Dictionary:
“Chawlhni hi Pathian din a nih avângin Lalpa tâna serh thianghlim tûr a ni. Mihringte hian sakhaw lam rawngbâwlna atân ni ruat a pui an ngah a, nimahsela, chûngte chuan, mihring din chawp an nih avangin, tumah a phuar lo. Chawlhni erawh hi chu chutiang a ni awzâwng lo mai. Thupêk palina hi chuan ‘Chawlhni hi thianghlim taka serh tûrin hre reng rawh,’ tiin, uar takin a sawi a ni..... Tihbo ngawt chu thiltih âwm lo tak a ni ang.” – “A Theological Dictionart,” by Rev. Charles Buck, 1830 Edition, p. 537.

PATHIAN DÂN DANGLAM THEI LO

11. Pathianin A dân a danglam theih lohzia chu engtinnge A sawi?
“A thununna thu zawng zawngte chu a rinawm a ni. Chûng chu kumkhaw tlaitluan atân tihnghehin a awm a.” Sâm 111:7,8.
“Hmasângin, i thuhriattirte aṭangin, Chûng chu kumkhua atân i tinghet tih ka lo hre tawh.” Sâm 119:152.
12. Krista Isuan A dân chu danglam thei a ni tiin, A zirtîr rêng em?
“Lei leh vân hi a boral hma loh chuan Dân thua mi chhun han tê tak tê emaw, thai han tê tak tê emaw, pakhat mah a boral tawp lo vang, a zaa a thlen kim hma loh zawng.” Matthaia 5:18.

Lalpa Isua Krista hian a va sawi chiang ṭha êm! Hawrap mal “i” chunga chhun han tê tak tê emaw, “t” han thai phei hlek emaw pawh hi, a zaa a thlen kim hma loh zawng, pakhat mah a boral lo vang, tih thu a sawi a ni. “Chhun han tê tak tê emaw, thai han tê tak tê emaw, pakhat mah a boral tawp lo vang.”

PATHIAN REMTIH LOHNA

13. Pathianin A dân khawih buai a remtih lohzia Baibul-in engtinnge min hrilh? Tihdanglam chawp mai mai zawmna chu chibai bûkna thlâwn mai a nihzia thu a sawi, hetiangin: “An zirtîrnaah mihring thupek an zirtîr avângin an mi biak ṭhin hi a thlâwn mai a ni.” Matthaia 15:9.

Chutichuan, Pathian Thu kan zirna aṭanga Pathian Chawlhni serh aia Pathianni serhna hi Baibul zirtîrna kalhzâwng thil a ni tih kan hriat tawh a, kan la serh tho si chuan kan biak chu a “thlâwn” mai a ni ang.

Pi pu dân zawma, thil dik lo tih kan chhunzawm phawt chuan Pathian dân hi engmah lovah kan siam tihna a ni tih thu pawh chiang taka hrilh kan ni.

“Tichuan, thurochhiah vângin Pathian thu chu in bawh bo ta a nih hi.” Matthaia 15:6.

Nisarih ni hmasa ber, Pathianni, ni sarih ni Chawlhni aia serhna hi mihring siam chawp thil zuina a ni a, chutiang chu Pathianin a pawt phawng vek ang tih hi kan hre tûr a ni.

“Kung zawng zawng ka Pa vâna mi phun loh chu pawh chhuahin a awm ang.” Matthaia 15:13.

NI HNUHNUNGAH THU AWI TURA MITE NGENNA

14. Ni hnuhnunga ram tina siam ṭhatna thuchah puandarh tûr chu engnge ni?
“Hêng thu zawng zawng khaikhâwmna hi ngaithla teh ang: Pathian ṭih la, a thupêkte vawng rawh; hei hi mihring zawng zawng tihtûr chu a ni mai.” Thuhriltu 12:13.
“Nunna Thing chungah chuan thu an neih theihna tûr leh, kawngkaah chuan khuaa an luh theihna tûra A thupêkte zâwm chu an eng a thâwl e.” Thupuan 22:14 (Sapṭawnga mi angin).
“Tin leia awmte hnênah leh, hnam tinrêng te, chi tinrêng te, ṭawng tinrêng te, mi tinrêng te hnêna tlângaupui tûr chatuan Chanchin Ṭha nei vantirhkoh dang vanlaizâwla thlâwk ka hmu a; tin, ani chuan aw ring takin, ‘A rorêl hun chu a lo thlen tak avângin Pathian chu hlau ula, chawimawi rawh u; vân te, lei te, tuifinriat te, tuihna te siamtu chu chibai bûk rawh u,” a ti a..... Hetah hian mi thianghlim, Pathian thupêkte leh Isua rinna thu zâwmtute chhelna lan theihna a awm.” Thupuan 14:6, 7, 12.

Ni hnuhnunga harhna awm tûra hrilh lâwkna chu Thu Sâwm Pêk zawm tûra kohna nêna inzawm tlat a ni. Tin, chu kohna chu rinna avânga felna thuchah nên inzawm a ni bawk. Thupêk zawmna leh Isua pawmna chu a kal kawp zêl tûr a ni. Dîk tak phei chuan pakhat an ni; Isua kan zui phawt chuan A thupêkte kan zawm lo thei si lo va.

Amaherawhchu, mi tam tak, hre hauh lova Pathianni hi Chawlhni anga serhtute an awm. Chûng mite chu anmahni huna êng an hmuh ang zêla ngaihtuah an ni ang. Keini ni hnuhnunga chêngte chuan Thupêk palina chungchânga kal sualna nasa tak hi mi dangte hnêna kawhhmuh tûrin mawhphurhna kan nei a ni.

PATHIAN THU I ZAWM ANG EM?

Chawlhni thu hi ni thu satliah mai emaw, Pathianni emaw, Chawlhni emaw chawlhna thu ringawt a ni lo va, Pathian thu nge i zawm ang a, mihring thu, tih a ni zâwk e. Pathian chuan ni sarih ni hi serh rawh, a ti a. (Exodus 20:10) Katholik thuneihna erawh chuan ni sarih ni hmasa ber serh rawh, a ti ve thung a ni. Chu ni chu Isuan mihringte tâna a tihthianghlim sa tawh aia hman tûr a lo din ve a ni. Tu thu nge i awi zâwk dâwn? Krista nge, mihring? Kan zain Petera anga mihring thu aia Pathian thu awi zâwk i thlang ṭheuh ang u. (Tirh. 5:29).
