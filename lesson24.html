ZIRLAI SAWMHNIH LEH PALINA

THLAAH HMEICHHIA

Châng Thlan: “Chutichuan pawl khatin an awm ang a, a vêngtu pakhat nên,” Johana 10:16.

KRISTA Isua berâm huang chu A KOHHRAN Hi a ni. Chu chu “Mitthi khaw kulh kawng-khârte pawhina an ngam loh”(Matthaia 16:18) thu chiang tak a sawi a ni. A Kohhran innghah chhan Lungpui chu Amah Krista ngei a ni bawk. “Lungphûm ren tawh sa lo chu lungphûm dang an rem thei tawh si lova, chu lungphûm chu Isua Krista chu a ni. “Krista Isua ngei chu a kila lung pawimawh ber niin,” (1Korin 3:11; Efesi 2:20). Krista chu a Lu, Kohhran lu ber a ni. (Efesi 1:22, 23; 5:23; Kolosa 1:18; 1 Petera 5:4.)

Apostle-te hun lai chuan Kohhran pakhat chauh a awm a, chu kohhranah chuan “chhandam mêk apiangte chu Lalpain nî tin an hnênah a teltîr zêl ṭhin,” (Tirhkohte 2:47). “Tirhkohte zirtîrin, inpâwlin, chhang phelin, ṭawngṭaiin ṭhahnemngai takin an awm reng a,” châng 42.

Tûn lai hunah hian Kristian ni lote zînga mi rinawm tam takin ṭhahnemngai takin Kohhran dik an zawng hlawm a, nimahsela kohhran pakhat mai hmu lovin kohhran tam tak, zirtîrna hrang hrang neite an hmu thliah thluah a, kohhran hrang hranga mîte chuan an pawl zirtîrna chu chatuana nunna thlentu a ni e, tiin an pawl zawm tûr chuan an lo sâwm ṭheuh a. Hetianga inṭhen darhnate hi an la awm tûrzia tirhkoh Paula chuan a lo sawi lâwk diam tawh rêng a ni. “Zirtîrna dik an ngaih peîh loh hun a la thleng dawn si, an bengte a zat avângin anmahni duhzâwng angin zirtîrtute an hnênah an khâwm zâwk ang a, an bengte chu thutak lamah an chhu-ngawng ang a, thawnthu lam panin an peng tawh ang,” (2 Timthea 4:3, 4). Isua Krista ngei pawhin, “An zirtîrnaah mihring thupêk an zirtîr avângin an mi biak ṭhin hi a thlâwn mai a ni,” (Matthaia 15:9) tiin min hrilh.

Hêng châng thute hian eng vânga tûn laia khawvêla sakhaw hrang tam tak lo awm nge an nih min târlan sak a. Thutak aṭanga kal pênna, Baibul thianghlim zirtîrna aia mihring thupêk pawm zâwkna avânga lo chhuak a ni. “I thu chu thutak a ni,” (Johana 17:17). Kohhran dik chu thutakah a chêng reng ṭhin. Chuvângin, Kohhran dik zawmnaa hian thutak zawnna pawh a tel ngei tûr a ni. Thutaka chên reng duhtute chuan Isua ṭawngkam “Keimah hi kawng leh thutak leh nunna ka ni, keimaha kal lo chu tumah Pa hnênah an thleng ngai lo,” (Johana 14:6) tih hi an hria ang. Krista leh A zirtîrna kan zui phawt chuan A Kohhranah kan lût tihna a ni.

KOHHRAN DIK INPUMKHAT THEIHNA DÂNTE

Eng thilin nge kohhran inpumkhatna awmtîr thei?

Kohhran inpumkhatna famkim hi kohhrana mitinin Pathian Lehkha Thianghlim zirtîrna rem lo leh kalh zâwng apiangte an paih vek hma loh chuan thil awm thei a ni ngai lo vang. Kohhran dik chu Baibul thu taka innghat tlat a nih avângin a inthlah dul mai thei lo va, mi dangte tih lung awi nân a rinna ṭhenkhat a paih thei hek lo. Thutak aṭanga kal sualte chu hruai kîr leh a tum zawk ṭhîn. Chûng kal sualna nêite chuan he Krista kohhran dîk hi an rawn zawm dawn chuan Pathian thu rem thlapa thurin ṭhenkhat an neihte chu an thu rem thlapa thurin ṭhenkhat an neihte chu an kalsan tûr tihna erawh chu a ni lo ve, a dikte chu chhawmin a dik lote chauh chu an kalsan tûr a ni.

Khawvêl hmaa Pathian Lehkhabu Thianghlim zirtîrna târlan hi Kohhran dik tih tur a ni a, chu chu khawvêl pawhin beisei theihna a nei. Chuvângin khawvêl hnêna a tih tûr a hlen theihna dâltu sakhua rêng rêngte chu Kohhran dik chuan a zawm thei lo a ni.

HMEICHHIA— KOHHRAN ENTIRTU

Thupuanah hian Kohhran entîrtu atân Johanan engnge a hman?
“Thilmak ropui tak vânah a lo lang ta a; HMEICHHIA NIA INTHUAM, a ke hnuai thla awm, arsi sâwm leh pahniha siam lallukhumkhum a lo awm a,” (Thupuan 12:1).
“Zion fanu (Pathian fate) chu HMEICHHE duhawm leh angah ka ngai tawh si a,” (Jeremia 6:2).

Hêng aṭang hian HMEICHHE THIANGHLIM chu PATHIAN KOHHRAN DIK entîrtu a ni tih a lang a ni.

2 Korin 11:2 kan chhiar chuan Paulan Pathian Kohhran entîrtu atâna hmeichhe thianghlim a hman thu kan hmu ang. Thupuan 14:4-ah pawh tlante chu nula thianghlim ang an nihzia kan hmu bawk. Chûng tlante chanchin chu hetiangin Johanan a sawi zêl a: “An kaah dâwt a chhuah rêng miin an hre ngai lo; sawisêl bo an ni si a,” tiin (châng 5).

KOHHRAN DIK HMUN FIANRIALAH

Hmeichhia emaw, Kohhran dik emaw chungah engnge lo thleng tur?
“Hmeichhia chu thlalêra Pathian buatsaih hmun a neihnaah chuan, a tlân bo va, chutah chuan ni sângkhat leh ni zahnih leh ni sawmruk an châwm theihna tûrin,” (Thupuan 12:6, châng 14 en bawk la).
“Ni khat hi kum khat atân ka ruat sak tawh si che a,” (Ezekiela 4:6).

Chutichuan Kohhran dik chu thlalêrah KUM 1260 CHHÛNG A AWM BO TÛR A NI. He hun chhûng hi, a hmaa kan zir tawh angin Pope thuneihna invâwr sân lai, A.D. 538 aṭanga A.D.1798 thleng a ni tih kan hria.

Krista Kohhran dik chu mitthi khaw kulh kawngkhârte pawhin an ngam loh tûr a nihna dik tak anga a lo lan leh kan beisei thei ang.

A HUN TAKAH KOHHRAN DIK A LO LANG

Englaiin nge Pathian Kohhran chu thlalêr aṭanga a lo lan leh?

Kum 1798 zawh veleh khawvêl ram hrang ram takah sakhaw lama harhna ropui tak a lo awm chiam a, chu harhna ropui tak chuan kum 1844 khân Apostle hun laia Kohhran zirtîrna thianghlim ang chiah nei, chatuana Chanchin Ṭha tlângaupuitu Kohhran a lo chhuak ta a. Tichuan, Pope thuneihna hnuaia tihduhdahna hun chhûng, kum 1260 chhûnga Pathian Kohhran bibo rih chu a lo lang leh ta a ni.

A RORÊL HUN THUCHAH

Isua Krista lo kal leh hma lawkin khawvêl pumpui huapin eng thuchah nge a puang ang?
“Leia awmte hnênah leh, hnam tinrêng te, chi tinrêng te, ṭawng tinrêng te, mi tinrêng te hnêna tlângaupui tûra chatuana Chanchin Ṭha nei vântirhkoh dang vânlaizâwla thlâwk ka hmu a; ani chuan, aw ring takin, ‘A rorêl hun chu a lo thlen tâk avângin Pathian chu hlau ula, chawimawi rawh u; vân te, lei te, tuifinriat te, tuihna te Siamtu chu chîbai bûk rawh u,’ a ti a” (Thupuan 14:6, 7).

Chanchin Ṭha puan darhna hna hi mihringte hnêna pêk a nih a vângin he laia vântirhkoh hian Kohhran dik, Pathian rorêlna hun lo thlen thupuang darhtu a entîr tih kan hre thei. He rorêlna thuchah hi chatuana Chanchin Ṭhaa mi a ni.

THUPÊK VAWNG KOHHRAN

He thuchah puan chhuahna avângin eng ang pawl nge lo chhuak ta?
“Hetah hian mi thianghlim, Pathian THUPEKTE LEH ISUA RINA THU ZAWMTUTE chhêlna lan theihna a awm,” (Thupuan 14:12).

Isua rina thu zâwmtute an ni. Isua ringtu emaw, Isuaa rinna neitute emaw tih mai ni lo vin, Isua rinna thu zâwmtute an ni. Hei hian Isua chu an nunna Lalpa atân an pawm tak zet a, an nunnaah Amah chu an chêntîr a, A rinna chu anmahniah tihlantîr an phal a, A thurin leh zirtîrnate chu a zain an pawm vek tihna a ni.

Isua rinna thu an zâwm chauh a ni lo va, Pathian thupêkte zâwmtu a ni bawk. Hei hian ‘khawngaihna avângin Pathian thupêk zawm a tûl tawh lo ve’ tih zirtîrna hi a dik lohzia a tilang chiang hle. Chuvângin, pawl khat, Pathian thupêk zawng zawngte zâwmtu leh zâwm tûra hriltute an awm ang tih min hriattîr bawk. “Tupawhin Dân zâwm kim mah sela, kawng khata a tihsual si chuan, a zaah chuan thiam loh changing a awm tawh a ni,” (Jakoba 2:10).

DÂN CHHINCHHIAHNA DIN THAR LEHNA

Kohhran dik hnênah eng hna bîk nge pêk a nih?
“Hriattîrna thu zial hi phuar la, dân thu hi ka zirtîrte zîngah nemin chhinchhiah rawh,” (Isaia 8:16).

Chhinchhiahna (seal) hi thuneihna entîrtu a ni a, lehkha ziaka zuk chhut a nih chuan chu lehkha chuan pawimawhna leh hmantlâkna a lo nei nghâl ṭhîn. Pathian dân, sâwm pêk pawh hian chhinchhiahna a nei a, chu chu Chawlhni thupêkah hian kan hmu. Chhinchhiahnaah chuan nihna (title) te, thuneihna (authority) te, thuneihna huam chhung (Jurisdiction) te a chuang ṭhîn. Chawlhni thupêk hian dân petu nihna, Pathian, thuneihna leh thiltihtheihna neitu, Siamtu te, A thuneihna huam chhûng lei leh vân leh tuifinriatte a târlang vek. Chuvângin, thu sâwm pêka a palina hian Pathian dân chhinchhiahna chu a nei a ni. Thupêk palina thu hi lo ennawn leh mah teh:

“Chawlhni hi thianghlim taka serh tûr a ni tih hre reng rawh. Ni rûk chhûngin i thawk tûr a ni a, i hna zawng zawng chu i thawk tûr a ni: a ni sarih ni erawh chu Lalpa i Pathian tân chawlhni a ni e ... ni rûk chhûngin Lalpan vân leh lei te, tuifinriat leh a chhûnga awm zawng zawngte chu a siam a, a ni sarihni chuan a lo châwl ta si a: chuvângin Lalpan chawlhni chu mal a sâwm a, a tithianghlim ta a ni,” (Exodus 20:8-11).

He thupêk hi Pathianni (Sunday) dânin a rawn luahlan khan chhinchhiahna laimu chu hnâwlin a lo awm ta a, chuvângin, thupêk palina dk tak chu dinthar a nih leh theihna hi Kohhran dik chuan a ngaituah êm êm ang tih kan ring thei. Chutianga chhinchhiahna dik rawn din thar leh tûr Kohhran dik chuan Chawlni dik hi a serh ngei bawk tûr a ni.

CHAWLHNI DIK SIAM ṬHATNA

Chawlhni dik siam ṭhatna hi an thuchah pakhat a ni tih enginnge lantîr?
“Van te, lei te, tuifinriat te, tuihna te Siamtu chu chibai bûk rawh u,” (Thupuan 14:7).

Chawlhni hi Siamtu thiltihtheihna hriatrengna a nih avângin Siamtu chibaibûk tûra kohna hi Chawlhni serh tûra kohna a ni tih chiang takin a lang a. Chawlhni serhna hi Siamtu hnêna rinawmna fiahna a ni.

Zâwlnei Isaia pawhin ni hnuhnunga Chawlhni din tharna awm tûr hi a lo sawi lâwk tawh rêng a ni:

“Tichuan, i laka lo la chhuak tûrte chuan hmun hlui chhe tawhte chu an rem leh ang a: chuan tam tak lungphûmahte chuan i rem chho leh ang; tichuan nang chu, ‘A chhe lai siamṭhatu,’ ‘Mi awmna tûra kawngte siamṭhatu,’ an ti ang che. Chawlhni bawhchhe lo tura, ka ni thianghlima i duhzâwng ti lo tûra i kephah chu i hawikîrtîra, chawlhni chu ni lawmawm leh Lalpa ni thianghlim chu zawhawm i tiha , mahni kawng zawh lova, mahni nawmsak bawl lova, mahni thu sawi lova i chawinawi chuan, Lalpa chungah chuan i lâwm ang,” (Isaia 58:12-14).

Hei hi ni hnuhnunga Kohhran dikin a siam ṭhatna hna a thawh tûr thu sawina a ni. Pope thuneihna Chawlhni dik a rawn tihdanglam khan dân aṭang chuan chhinchhiahna chu lâk chhuahin a awm a, thu sâwm pêk dânah chuan “a chhe lai” a lo awm ta a ni.

Siam ṭhatu, Kohhran dik chu, “a chhe lai siamṭhatu,” thupêk palina CHAWLHNI DIK HI A HMUN DIK TAKA RAWN HÛNGKAI LEHTU A NI.

DIK LOHNA HAILANNA

Kohhran dik chu “mi awmna tûra kawngte siamṭhatu” a nih chuan dik lohna hi eng angin nge a ngaih ang?
“Vântirhkoh dang, a pahnihna chuan, ‘Babulon khaw ropui, a mi ngaih chin vânga thinurna uain hnam zawng zawng intîrtu chu, a tlâwm ta,’ tiin a rawn zui a,” (Thupuan 14:8).

“Vântirhkoh” tih hi thuchah puang darhtu tihna a ni; hmasâng aṭang rêngin Pathianinmi dangte hnêna A thuchah puang darh tûrin mihringte hi A hmang ṭhin a. He laiah pwh hian A thuchah puangtu hi mihring bawk a ni. A nih leh Babulon hi eng aia ding nge ni thung? Babulon awm ṭan tirhna leh awm dân kan chhui chuan eng aia ding nge a nih ka hre thei ang.

Tui lêt hnua khawpui chanchin Baibul-a kan hmuh hmasak ber chu Babel emaw, Babulon emaw a ni (Genesis 10:10, margin); hmasâng Kaldai mite ṭawngin a awmzia hi “Pathian panna kawng,” “vân kawngkhâr” tihna a ni. Sinâr phai zâwlah khan Nova thlahte khan Babel Insâng, vân tawng phâk tûr an sa tlut tlut a, nimahsela, an thil tum khawlo tak chu Pathian huatzâwng tak a nih avângin an “vân kawngkhâr” chu an âtzia lantîrtu a lo ni ta zâwk a, mihringin “Pathian panna kawng” a siam chu chhiatna mai a lo chang zo ta a, awmze duhawm tak nei Babulon pawh chu ‘ṭawng buaina leh sakhaw buaina’ entîrtu atân hman a lo ni ta ni (Genesis 11:9; Jeremia 50:2, 38).

Chutichuan kan châng thu ngaihtuah lai pawh a lo chiang ta uar uar a. Tunlaia sakhaw temple, Pathian panna kawng leh vân kawngkhâr chu, “a tlâwm ta, a tlâwm ta.” Ziaktu ropui tak pakhatin a sawi angin, “hetianga buaina mangan thlâk tak a lo awmna chhan pakhat hi chu mi fing intihote sakhuaa chhiatna a awm vâng a ni.” Tun lai khawvêla sakhaw tam tak hian chuan an sakhaw nihna tak, mihringte suih khâwmna Pathian nêna pumkhata siamtu hi an hloh ta hlawm a ni.

Kohhran dikin eng dik lohna dang nge a rawn hailan?
“Vântirhkoh dang, a pathumna chuan aw ring takin, ‘Tupawhin sakawlh leh a lem chibai bûkin an chalah emaw, an kutah emaw chhinchhiahna an neih chuan, chu mite ngei chuan Pathian thinurna uain, a thinurna nova engmah pawlh loha siam chu, an in ang a, vântirhkoh thianghlimte mithmuhah leh Berâm No mithmuhah chuan mei leh kâta nghaisakin an awm bawk ang. Chûng mîte nghaisakna meikhu chu chatuanin a chho vang a, sakawlh leh a lem chibai bûktûte, a hming chhinchhiahna nei apiangte pawh chuan, a chhûn a zânin chawlh rêng an nei lo vang,” tiin, anni chu a rawn zui a,” (Thupuan 14:9-11).

Khawvêl hnêna Pathian ngenna hnuhnung hian kal sualna leh rin lohna avânga thil lo thlengte a rawn târlang nghâl a, hêng châng thute pawh hian Baibul zirtîrna aṭanga pên sualna ata hawi kîr leh tûrin min rawn hrilh a ni.

Hun Thim lai khan Rom Bishop chuan Vân Pathian hmuna inchawisângin ni be mite ni serh chu Thlarau lam thuneihna “chhinchhiahna” atân a lo ruat ta a. History ziaktuin a sawi angin, “Ni Pathian lim chawimawi nana an ni serh chu Pathianni an vuah a, chu chu ni nî (Sun's day) tihna a ni.”––Verstegan's Antiquities, p. 68.

Pathian Lehkhabu erawh hi chuan min Siamtu hnêna kan rinawmna fiahtu atân ni sarih ni Chawlhni hi chhinchhiahna atân a ruat thung a, chu nî chu kan vawn tûra Pathian thupêk (Ex. 31:13) a ni.

PATHIAN THILTIHTHEIHNA CHHINCHHIAHNA

Pathianin a thiltihtheihna chhinchhiahna atân leh A hnêna kan rinawmna chhinchhiahna atâna Chawlhni min pêk hrûlah hian elreltu sakhaw pawl khat chuan hmasânga ring lo miten ni biak nana an lo hman ṭhin ni chu a rawn chawi lâr ve a. Cardinal Gibbons hnêna lehkha thawn, October 28, 1895-a mi chhângin Chancellor H.F. Thomas chuan hetiang hian a ziak: “Katholic Kohhran chuan thlâk thlengna (Sabbath aṭanga Sunday) hi a awmtîr ngei a ni ti tehlul mai e ... chu thlâk thlengna thiltih chu sakhaw thilte chu kohhran thuneihna chhinchhiahna (mark) chu a ni.”

He thil pawimawhna tak hi kan zuk hre zo em aw? Rom Kohhran hian chanchin ṭha thu tam tak an tidanglam chauh a ni lo va, Chawlhni serhna hmuna Pathianni (Sunday) serhna a rawn hung tate hi sakhaw thila kohhran thiltihtheihna entîrtu a ni tiin a chhuang êm êm bawk a ni. He laiah hian thu sawi fiahna pawimawh tak mai kan nei. A tîra din a nih ang taka ni sarih ni Chawlhni serhtute chu Pathian dik chibai bûktute an ni a, ring lo mite leh pope thuneihnain a ruat Pathianni chawimawitute erawh chuan ka sualna chhinchhiah chu an pu a ni. Amaherawhchu, thu dik hre lova Chawlhni dik emaw tia Pathianni lo serhtute chu kal sualna chhinchhiahna nei angah kan ngai tûr a ni lo ve. Engpawh nise, he khawvêl hian fiahna a la kal tlang dawn––Pathianni serh tûrin mi tin hnênah thupêk a la chhuak dawn a. Chutih hunah chuan ni sarih ni, Chawlhni hi mihringte serh tûr a nihzia chiang takin khawvêl hriata puan a ni bawk ang. Chutianga chiang taka hêng thil pahnihnte hi târlan an nih huna mîten Chawlhni dik hi an hnar a, Pathianni hi an pawm zâwk hunah chuah a ni kal sual chhinchhiahna chu an dawn dawn ni.

KOHNA HNUHNUNG BER

11. Pathian mite chu sakhaw hrang hrang leh kohhran hrang hranga awm darh an la ni rih avângin khawvêl pum hnêna kohna lo thleng chu eng nge ni?
“Vân aṭangin aw dangin heti hian a sawi ka hria: ‘Ka mite u, a sualnaahte in tel lohna tûr leh, a hremnate inhmuh ve lohna tûrin, a chhûng ata chhuak rawh u,” (Thupuan 18:4).
“AN ZING ATA LO CHHUAK ULA, A HRANGIN AWM RAWH U, thil bawlhlawh rêng rêng pawh dek suh u ...Chutichuan ka lâwm ang che u,” (2 Korin 6:17).

Hêng Pathian thuchah hnuhnung pathumte hian sakhaw dik lo leh kal sualna rêng rêng chu hnâwl tûrin lei mihringte an rawn hriattîr a, thu tak, a ni lo zâwnga lo ṭhuai kawih tawhte chu siam ṭha leh tûrin an rawn hrilh bawk a ni. Dik lohna leh bumnate nuai bova, chanchin ṭha duhawm tak chu khawvêl hnêna thiltihtheihna nên puang darh leh tûr kan ni.

Vân aṭanga ngenna chu: Kristianna dik takah lo kîr leh rawh u, mi thianghlimte hnêna rinna pêkah chuan lo kîr leh rawh u, Baibul-ah lo kîr leh ula, Siamtu Pathian hnênah leh Sinai Tlânga A chatuan dânah leh Chawlnîah hian lo kîr leh ula. A thisen azâra tlanna chu pawm rawh u. Chatuana chanchin ṭha puan darhnaah leh huai taka sual donaah hian lo kîr leh rawh u. Pathian ram atâna chêng tlâk tûra mîte thinlung siam ṭhatnaah hian lo kîr leh rawh u, tih hi a ni.

He ngenna hian Krista tân rah a chhuah mêk a; mi tam takte thinlung chu siam ṭhatin a awm mêk zêl a ni. He hna hi hlen a nih hunah he khawvêl hi Pathian Fapa lo kal lehna atân a inpeih tawh ang.

ṬHAHNEMNGAI TAKIN I ZIR ANG U

Pathian duhzâwngte kan hriat thiam theih nân kan Baibul-te hi ṭhahnemngai takin i zir ṭheuh teh ang khai, Krista tâna thu hretu rinawm tak niin, “I Pathian tâwk tûrin inpeih rawh le” tih thuchah hi i puang ṭheuh teh ang u. Kan tih tûra rinawm taka kan awm phawt chuan rei lo teah tlante, “an kaah dâwt a chhuah rêng miin an hre ngai lo; sawisêl bo an ni si a,” “Aw Lalpa Pathian, Engkimtithei, nang, hnam tin Lalber, i kawngte chu a felin a dik a ni, tih hi an sa a,” tia an chanchin an ziakte rual hian lawm luhin kan awm ve ngei ang. (Thupuan 14:5; 15:3).

I Hnêna Kan Thuchah

He leiah hian Pathianin kohhran nung, nasa taka thawktu a nei a, chu kohhran chu Amah ngeiin a din a ni. Kohhran, church tih hi Grik ṭawng, Ecclesia aṭanga lâk a ni a, a awmzia chu “ko chhuak” tihna a ni.

Kohhran dik chu taksa nung nên tehkhin a ni a, chu taksa nung lu chu Krista a ni.

“Nimahsela, Pathianin pêng tinrêngte hi taksaah hian a duhna apiangah a awmtîr ṭheuh tawh a ni . . . . Amaherawhchu pêng tam tak an ni zâwk a, nimahsela taksa pumkhat chauh a ni si a. Mit hian kut hnênah, “Engah mah ka duh lo va che,’ a ti thei lo; lu pawh hian kephahte hnênah, ‘Engah mah ka duh lo va che u,’ a ti thei bawk hek lo . . . Taksaah hian inṭhenna rêng rêng awm lo va . . . Krista taksa chu in ni a, a pêngte in ni ṭheuh bawk a,” (1 Korin 12:18, 20, 21, 25, 27)

Taksa pêng tinrêngte hi an thawh tûr thawk reng thei tûra taksa nêna inzawmna nung an nei tûr a ni ang bawk hian, Kristiante pawh hian Pathian tâna Thlarau lama nunna an neih theih nân Krista nên an inzawm tlat tûr a ni.

“Pasalte u, in nupuite hmangaih rawh u, Kristan kohhranho a hmangaih a, an aia mahni a inpe ang khan . . .taksa pêngte kan ni si a . . . Chu thurûk chu a ropui a ni, nimahsela, kei zawngin Krista leh kohhranho thu ka sawi a ni,” (Efesi 5:25, 30, 32).

Krista leh a hnung zuitute inkâra inzawmna nghet tak awm hi nupui pasal inkâra hmangaihnaa inzawmna nen tehkhin a ni ṭhin. Movin a pasal hnêna rinawmna thu nghet taka a tiam tlat ang hian, Kristiante pawhin Krista hnenah rinawm taka awm tûrin thu an tiam ṭhîn a, an kârah chuan engmah a tla zep tûr a ni lo.

Tlangvâl pakhat hian ‘kohhrana tel ve kher a ṭûl lo ve, kohhrana tel kher lo pawhin Kristian ṭha tak a nih theih tho a lâwm,’ tiin, a pa a hnial tlat a. A pa, Pathian ṭih tak leh fing tak chuan thuk aṭangin chaichehin meiling hi a han chep chhuak a, a hrangin a han dah a. Rei lo teah chuan chu meiling chu a lo thi ṭan a, a linna leh ênna chu a lo chuai tial tial a, meihawl mai a lo chang ta a. Tlangvâl chuan a pa hnênah chuan, “Ka hre thiam ta e, ka pa, min hrilh fiah pawh a tûl tawh lo ve,” tiin, a hrilh ta a ni.

Kohhran member-in Krista taksa aṭanga a inlâk hran hian a lumna leh ênna chu a hloh thuai ṭhin a ni. Kohhran hi khawvêl ram tina chanchin ṭha pung darh tûra thupêk dawngtu a ni a, inṭhen darha, mahni nia tla hrang zeng zung chungin Pathian thu ruatte chu hlen theih rual a ni lo vang.

He zirlai pawimawh tak i zirnaah hian Pathianin malsâwm che rawh se.
