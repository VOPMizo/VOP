ZIRLAI SAWM LEH PAKHATNA

CHHINCHHIAHNA MAK TAKTE

Châng Thlan: “Chûng zawng zawng chu in hmuh hun chuan, amah chu a lo hnai tawh a, kawngka bulah ngei a awm tih hria ang che u.” Matthaia 24:33.

“KA LO KIR LEH ANG” ...NIMAHSELA, CHU MI NI LEH HUN THU CHU... TUMAN AN HRE LO”..... “IN RIN LOH HUNAH” .... “CHUVÂNGIN, INRING RENGIN AWM RAWH U.” Hêng ngenna leh inrin lâwkna tûra thuchahte hi Pathian thinlung aṭanga lo chhuak an ni. Zirlai sâwmpanganaah khân he hun ropui tak lo thleng tûr chanchin hi kan zir tawh a, chu hun chu lo la thleng ngei tûr a nihzia Pathian Lehkhabuin uar taka a sawi thu kan hmu tawh bawk. Chu hun lo thleng thuai tûr chu ngaihven leh hrethiam tûrin Pathianin min duh ṭheuh a ni.

Indo laia hmêlma thlawhnaten rûn an tum hmun an va thlen chuan, thlawhna kâptu sipai pâwlten chûng hmêlma thlawhnate chu chiang taka an hmuh theih nân, êng chak tak hmangin an lo chhun êng ruih ṭhîn. Chutiang deuh chuan Isua lo kal hma lawk hun hi Pathianin a rawn chhun êng phût a, chu hun rawn chhun êngtu chu Pathian thâwk khûm hrilh lâwknate hi an ni. “Tin, hrilhlâwkna thu nghet lehzual chu kan nei a ni, khua a lo vâr a... chu chu khâwnvâr hmun thima êng an ngaihsak anga in ngaihsak chuan in ti ṭha ang.” 2 Petera 1:19. “Keimah ang rêng rêng an awm lo; keiin a tâwp tûr chu a tîr aṭang rêngin ka sawi thei a,” tiin, Pathianin a Puang bawk. Isaia 46:9, 10. Pathian Lehkhabu hi Pathian thâwk khum a nihzia tilang fiahtu ber pakhat chu hrilhlâwkna theng famkimte hi an ni a, Isua lo kal leh hma lawka hril lâwkna thleng famkim mêkte phei hi chu a ropuiin a mak lehzual bîk a ni.

Chhinchhiahna Ropui Takte

Vân lama chhinchhiahna awm tûr hrilhlâwknate leh a thlen famkimna chanchin kan thlûr bîk deuh ang a. Hêng chhinchhiahnate hi a makin a ropui tak zet hlawm a ni. Khawvêla thil lo thlengte hi chu mihringin thunun theihna an nei thei a, nimahsela, ni leh thla leh ariste nên khian chuan an inkâr hlat êm avângin han thunun phâk theihna rêng an nei lo. Hun bi dik tak vawnga Pathianin a hertîr vêl avângin ni emaw, thla emaw âwkin a lem hun tûrte chu dik tak takin an sawi lâwk thei a; amaherawhchu, vâna thil DANGLAM bîk thlengte chu an hrilhlâwk thei lo va, an awmtîr thei hek lo.

He zirlai van lam chanchin târlante hi Krista lo kal lehna a hnaih tawhzia kan hriat theih nâna Pathianin a rawn hmante an ni a, min tibuaitu tûr an ni lo ve.

VÂNAH CHHINCHHIAHNA MAK TAKTE

1. Isuan A lo kallehna a hnaih tawhzia hriatna tûrin eng chhinchhiahna chanchin nge a sawi?
“Nimahsela, chûng ni hrehawm hnu lawkah chuan NI TIHTHIMIN A AWM ANG A, THLA A ÊNG TAWH LOVANG A, VAN AṬANGIN ARSITE AN LO TLA ANG... chûng zawng zawng in hmuh hunah chuan, amah chu a lo hnai tawh a, kawngka bul ngeiah a awm tih hria ang che u.” Matthaia 24:29-33.

He laia Kristan “ni hrehawm” a tih hi A.D 538 leh A.D 1798 inkâr chhûnga pope-in tihduhdahna a thlentîr hun kha a ni.

2. Vâna chhinchhiahnate awm hmain thil engnge lo thleng hmasa tûr?
“Tin, chhinchhiahna parukna chu a han phelh lai ka en reng a, tin, LIR NASA TAKIN A LO NGHING TA A; tin, NI chu sahmul saiip puan angin a lo DUM a, THLA pawh chu a pumin THISEN angin a lo awm ta a; tin, vâna ARISITE chu, theipui kung thlipuiin a nuai a, a helte a ṭil ang maiin, leiah a TLA ta a.” Thupuan 6:12, 13

LISBON LIRNGHING RAPTHLAK

November 1, 1755 khân Lisbon khuaah nasa tak maiin lîr a nghing chiam a. Chu chu tihduhdahna reh lam tawh khân a ni. A chanchin chu hetiangin kan chhiar a. “November 1, 1755-a lîrnghîng rapthlak chuan a tlêm berah mêl kili 4,000,000 a huam a, a nghîng hriat tham tawh meuh lohna ramah te pawh tui lamah chuan a la lang cheu va; Europe leh Africa leh America khawmual puite pangkhing aia zau zâwk a huam tel a, a nghîn nasat zualna erawh chu Europe chhim lam pangah a ni.” – Sears in Wonders of the World, pp. 50, 58. Hrilhlâwkna chuan “LÎR NASA TAKIN A LO NGHING ANG,” tiin min hrilh a, khawvel chanchin ziakna chuan Lisbon lîrnghîng chu ‘NASA TAKA NI’ tih min hrilh bawk. Chu lîrnghîng chu ni tihthima a awm hma lawka lo thleng tûr a ni.

NI TIHTHIM

3. Vâna chhinchhiahnate zîngah a engnge hmasa ber?
“Chûng ni hrehawm hnu lawkah chuan ni tihthimin a awm ang.” Matthaia 24:29.

Ni tihthim thu hi Markan chiang zâwk deuhin min hrilh a: “Nimahsela, chûng ni, (Pope thuneihna hun chhûng) hrehawmna hnuah chuan, ni tihthimin a awm ang a,” tiin, Marka 13:24.

He châng aṭang hian ni tihthima a awm hun tûr chu kum 1776 leh 1798 inkârah, “Chûng hrehawmna” leh pope thuneihna a tlâk hniam dâwn lai inkârah a ni ang tih kan hre thei. Khawvêl chanchin lamin engtinnge a sawi i lo en thung teh ang. Webster’s Dictionary of 1833-ah chuan hetiangin chhinchhiah a ni:

“May 19, 1780-a Ni Thim tih ṭhin hian CHU MI NIA KHUA A THIM KHUP AVÂNGA a hming hi pu hlen ta a ni ...... Zîng dâr 10 aṭangin a thîm ṭan a, a tûk chhûn thlengin a thim khup mai a. HE THIL DANGLAM TAK LO AWM CHHAN DIK TAK HI HRIAT A NI LO.” Tin, Lehkhabu dang pakhat Memoirs of the American Academy of Arts and Science, an tih Boston khuaa A.D 1785-a chhutah chuan, May 19, 1780-a THIMNA MAK TAK CHANCHIN, tih thupuia hmangin hetiang hian ziak a ni. “A thim hun chhung chu he hmunah hian a tlêm berah dâr kâr sâwmpali a ni... Hnai teah pawh thil hriat hran theih loh khawpin a thim a; ZAN ANG MAI A NI ZO TA VEK A.” (Phek 234-246 en la) Arsi chanchin ngaihtuah mi, Herschel chuan, “North America-a KHAW THIM TA MUP mai hi leilung thilmak ropui, ngaihnawm ti taka a chanchin miten an la chhiar ṭhin tûr, KHAWVÊL FINNAIN A HRILH FIAH THEIH SI LOH a ni,” tiin, a sawi. Dr. Adam-an a sawi dân ve thung chuan CHHUN PACHANG HI ZAN LAIA LO INLET THUT ang mai a ni ber e.... A lo thim thutna chhan hrilh fiah thiam lo, sâng tam takte chu nasa takin an thlabârin an chî-ai a; LEI HI A NGUI RUAI MAI A NI. Arsi chanchin hre mi, Dr. Samuel Stearns-a chuan a ngaihdân zâwttute a chhânnaah hetiangin a sawi: “AWK LEM AVANGA THIMNA CHU LO THLENG A NI LO TIH CHU NI VELA HERTE DINHMUN AṬANGIN A HRIAT THEIH A: HE THIMNA LO THLEN LAI HIAN THLA AWMNA CHU NI AṬANGA DEGREE ZA LEH SAWMNGA AIA TAM MAH A NI SI A. CHUVÂNGIN HE THIMNA RAWN AWMTIRTU CHU VAN KUALKHUNGA KAL TLANGA, VANTE KHI PUAN IN CHUNG ANGA KAI PHARAHTU A NI NGEI ANG,” tiin.....

Hetiang hian Pathian Lehkhabu châng tam takin lo thleng tûra an sawi ang ngeiin a lo thleng famkim ta a, Isuan a hun a sawiah chiah a lo thleng ta a ni. He thil hi Isua lo kal lehna hnaih tawhzia min hriattîrtu pawimawh tak pakhat a ni. He hrilhlâwkna dikzia hi en teh, phat rual loha dik a ni a, Setana emaw, mihringten emaw han tih ve theih rual a ni lo ve. Chuvângin, kan ngaihven hle a ṭûl a ni.

THLAAH THILMAK

4. Vâna chhinchhiahna dang awm chu engnge ni?
“THLA a ÊNG tawh lo vang a.” Matthaia 24:29. “THLA .... chu apumin THISEN angin a lo awm ta a.” Thupuan 6:12.

He hi vâna chhinchhiahna pahnihna a ni a, a chanchin pawh sawi tam a ngai âwm lo ve. Ni tihthima a awm ṭum bawka he thil hi lo thleng a ni. Ni a thim chuan thlain ênna rêng a nei thei si lo va.

Dr. Adams chuan thla thim thu chu hetiangin a ziak:

“Chu mi tlaia lêng chhuak zawng zawng deuh thaw chuan mahni in kawng an hloh hlawm a. A hma zâna khawvêl a chhun ên un avângin a lo thim ta thut mai chu, chhûn lama thimna a awm ta ang bawk chuan, thil danglam tak a tling a ni,” tiin. New Hampshire biala mi pakhat, Tenny-a pawhin, “Engkimtitheian êng a siam tirh aṭang khân he mi zâna thimna hi a la nasa ber âwm e. A thim vâng lai tak phei chuan thil siam zawng zawnga ênna nei zawng zawngte hi bo ta duak sela, chu ai pawha thim leh chuang tûr awmin ka ring lo hial,” tiin thim nasatzia a hril fâk bawk.

ARSI TLA

5. Vâna chhinchhiahna pathumna engnge ni?
“VÂN aṭangin ARSITE an lo tla ang.” Matthaia 24:29.
“Vâna ARSITE chu.... leiah an TLA ta a.” Thupuan 6:13.

He chhinchhiahna hi ni leh thla tihthim hnua lo awm tûr a ni. Khawvêl chanchin ziakna kan en chuan chutiang ngeiin a lo awm tih kan hmu thei ang. Yale University-a Arsi lam chanchin ziaktu, Denison Olmstead chuan,

“November 13, 1833 tûk zîng chuan tûn hma lam zawng zawnga arsi thlâwk ṭhinte aia ropui zâwk leh nasa zâwkin arsite an tla chuai chuai a..... he ram luah tirh aṭangin hetiang rênga nasa hian vân lamah thilmak a la awm ngai âwm lo ve. Mi tam takin ennawm mak tak anga ngaiin an thlir ṭhuap a, thlabâr chunga thlîr tâwk an awm bawk a,” tiin, a ziak. – “American Journal of Science,” vol. 25, pp. 363, 364. “DAR KAR THUM CHHÛNG, reh lai awm miah lova arsite an tlâk chum chum lai chuan Rorelna ni chuan ni chhuah chauh lo chu nghâk tawhin a lang lo hial a.” Our First Century, p. 329. “Arago chhût dânin arsi tla chum chumte chu Boston aṭanga han hmuh theih maiah pawh 240,00 aia tlêm lo an ni.”

Hêng chhinchhiahnate hi Isua Krista lo kal ruala lo thleng tûr an ni lova, A lo kal hun chu a hnai ta tih entîrtute an ni zâwk. Chuvângin, hun rei lote chhûng atân chauha ruat an ni. Tin, hêng thil lo thleng mak takte hi Pathian rawn awmtîr emaw, Pathian remtih avâng chauha lo thleng emaw an nih avângin, hrilhlâwkna thleng famkim an ni kan ti lo thei lo vang, Pathian chuan A mite chu chhinchhiahna lem hmangin a fakder dâwn si lova. Hêng chhinchhiahnate lo thlen hmaa lo awm tûr, ni hrehawm chanchin, zirlai danga kan la zir tûrten vâna chhinchhiahnate chu an thlen famkim tawhzia chiang takin min la hriattîr ang.

HMUH CHHUAH THARTE LEH RANG TAK TAKA VEIVAHNA

6. Ni hnuhnunga thil thar hmuh chuah tûr chanchin eng angin nge hril lâwk a nih?
“Mahse nang, aw Daniel, thu chu thup la, lehkhabu chu châr tlat rawh, tâwpna hun thlengin: mi tam tak an tlân vêl ang a, hriatnate a pung dâwn a ni.” Daniela 12:4.

He hrilhlâwkna ropui tak, kum sânghnih lai kal taa lo ziak tawhah hian kan hun laia thil hmuh chhuah thar tam takte zînga a ṭhen a zâr – Boruaka awihawm loh khawpa chaka kal thei, thlawhtheihnate, khawvêl ram kil khâwr ber hmun pawha mihring aw thawn darh theihna, Radio te, ram tina mi zawng zawngten thil lo tlengte an hmuh theihna tûr khâwlte-hi kan zuk hmu thei a ni.

Ṭhang khat lian chhûng mai pawh hian hriatna a va pung nasa hman êm! Ni hnuhnung atân hmuh chhuah thar tûr a va lo inthup nasa kher êm! “Hriatnate a pung dâwn a ni.” Kum 2,500 lai kal taa hrilhlâwkna thlen famkimna, tûn lai thil thar tam tak hmuh chhuahte hi ngaihtuah teh. BIAK HLATNA KHÂWL TE, THÎRHRUILO KHÂWL TE, RADIO TE, X-RAY TE, HNIMHLUM TE, ROCKET LEH THLALEM te, etc., hêngte hi tûn lai huna thil hmuh chhuah thar tamtakte zînga ṭhenkhat chauh an ni.

Tin, hêng bâkah hian zirna lamah hmasâwnna nasa tak a awm bawk. Zirlai bute leh zirna hmunte a kum a têlin a pung zêl a, finna pêng hrang hrang, damdawi lamah te, chemistry-ahte, physics-ah te nasa taka ṭhanna a awm zêl bawk a, atom chakna – “thil siam thurûk leh bul” – hial pawh hmuh chhuah a lo ni ta.

Khawvêl tâwp a hnai tawh a ni tih chhinchhiahnate kan zawng a nih chuan hawi vêl ila, kan vêla hi an awm thliah thluah mai. Thil thar hmuh chhuah tinrêng leh mihring tlân tawn ruai leh thlâwk tawn sekte hian “tâwpna hun” a hnai tawh a ni tih an puang ṭheuh a ni.

“A inbuatsaihna niah chuan tawlailîrte chu kahphathîr tlein a tle zawr zawr a, feite chu rapthlak takin a phe zawk zawk a. Tawlailirte chu kawtthlêrah an tlân bur bur a, lamlian zauvah te chuan an insu sawk sawk a; meichher angin an lang a, kâwlphe angin an tlân thuak thuak a ni.” Nahuma 2:3,4.

Thîrhlûm râltu thei tlân ri rum rum leh meialh a kah chhuah zuai zuai te han thlîr hian Pathian zâwlnei, Nahuma thu sawite hi ngaihtuah tel loh theih an ni lo. Tûn lai chhuak, thîr tawlailir (Motor, Tramcar leh Rêl) te phei hi chu kâwlphe angin an tlân thuak thuak tak meuh a ni. Motor tlân ri bur bur leh an insu sawk sawk thâwmte hi ngaithla teh. “A inbuatsaihna ni chu” kawngka bulah mai a awm tawh a ni tih hi tunge ringhlel ang?

KHAWVÊL MANGANNA

7. Vân lama chhinchhiahnate lo lan hnuin Isuan leia hnamte zîngah engnge lo thleng dâwn a tih?
“Tuifinriat rûm leh a fâwn avângin- leiah pawh hnam tin hrehawmna leh MANGANNA a thleng ang: MITE THLABAR AVÂNG leh khawvêla thil lo thleng tûr chu ngaihtuah avângin, an thidang ang; vân thiltihtheihna chu a nghîng dâwn si a. Chutih hunah chuan Mihring Fapa hi thiltihtheihna leh ropuina nasa tak nên chhûm zinga lo kal an hmu ang.” Luka 21:25-27.

He lai châng thu tawi tê, fûn khâwm kim hle si ang hian kan hun awm dân chanchin hi a sawi theih chuang âwm lo ve. Tin, hmun dangah, kan chenna hun chanchin lo sawi lâwkna thu hetiangin kan chhiar bawk:

“HNAMTE CHU AN THINUR A, TIN, I THINUR CHU A LO THLENG VE THUNG A, MITTHI RORÊL SAK HUN leh, i bawih zâwlneite hnênah te, mithianghlimte hnênah te, i hming zah mi lian leh mi tête hnênah te, an lâwmman pêk hun leh, lei tichhetute tihchhiat ve thung hun chu a lo thleng bawk a.” Thupuan 11:18.

Kum za kal ta hun chhûng khân “hnamte an thinurin” an innghirngho châmchi tih kan hria a. Tin, indona a chhuak zût a, chûng chuan indopui vawihnih a paw chhuak tawh tih kan hre bawk. He ṭhangthar ral hma hian Indopui vawi thumna a chhuak lo vang, tia sawi lâwk thei tunge awm? Hnamte chu mangangin an la awm reng hlawm a, atom bomb siam an thiam thar hnu lek phei hi chuan khawvêl pum pui mai hi a mangang tak meuh a ni. “Mite, thlabâr avâng leh khawvêla thil lo thleng tûr chu ngaihtuah avângin, an thidang ang,” tiin, Isua pawhin min hrilh. Kum 1900 zet kal taa Isua thu sawite hian kan chênna huna mite buaina leh manganna chanchin hi a lo va sawi dik tak êm!

Isua ṭâwngkate hi tûn lai khawvêl awm dân nêna han hmehbelin Isua lo kal leh dâwn huna chêng kan ni tih kan hre thei. Amah Isua ngei hi he hrilhfiahna siamtu a ni a; hun lo la awm tûr chanchin lama A hriat vekzia leh, a hrilhlâwknate tithleng famkim tûra thiltihtheihna A neihzia a lang chiang êm êm a ni.

MI HAUSA LEH INHLAWHFATE

8. Hnamte manganna belhchhahtu engnge ni?
“MI HAUSATE u, ngai teh u khai! In chunga hrehawm lo thleng dâwn mêk avâng chuan ṭap ula, chiau vak vak rawh u. In rote a chhe ta a, in puante sûlrûlin a ei zo ta. In rangkachak leh in tangkate a tuiek zo ta a; a tuiêk chu in thiam lohzia hriattîrna a ni ang a, in tisa chu mei kâng ang in a ei ang. NI HNUHNUNGAH CHUAN RO IN KHAWL KHAWM A NIH HI. Ngai teh u, in LOVA BUH ATA INHLAWHFATE HLAWH, TIHDERA IN PÊK LOH KHA... a âttute mangang au thâwm pawh MIPUITE LALPA bengah a lo lût ta a. Khawvêlah hian nuamsa takin in awm a, in inkhawhral mai mai a; inthah niin in thinlung in titlai a. Mifel chu thiam loh in chantir a, in that ta a....... a dodâl lova che u. Chutichuan, unaute u, LALPA LO KAL HMA LOH ZAWNG CHHEL TAKIN AWM RAWH U.....LALPA LO KALNA CHU A HNAI TAWH SI A.” Jakoba 5:1-8.

Hêng hrilhlâwkna thu ziaktu hian a ziah laiin, tûn lai huna mi hausate leh inhlawhfate inkâra innghirnghona awm hi a rawn thlîr tih a lang chiang hle. Tin, kan tûn lai khawvêl buai nuak nuak han thlîr hian ni hnuhnungah chuan kan chêng ta tak zet a ni tih a lang bawk. Hêng buainate hi tûn hnai lawka lo chhuak ṭan an ni a, ni hnuhnunga thil awm dan tûr pakhat a ni ve rêng a ni. Lalpa ni lo thlen a hnaih tawhzia min kawhhmuhtu an ni bawk.

ISUA LO KAL LEHNA CHHINCHHIAHNA TlÂNG PUI

9. Eng thilin nge Krista lo kal leh a hnai tawh tih lantir?
“Hnam hrang leh hnam hrang an indo vang a, ram hrang leh ram hrang an indo vang a; hmun hrang hrangah ṬAM te a tla ang a, HRITE a lêng bawk ang a, vân aṭângin THIL HLAUHAWMTE leh chhinchhiahna nasa takte a lo awm ang.” Luka 21:12.

Indopui hmasa khan manganna leh chhiatna nasa takin a rawn zui nghâl a. India ramah te, China ramah te leh Russia ramah te phei chuan mi maktaduai tam tak rilṭâmin an thi a ni. Influenza-in a rawn zui bawk a, “thlei bîk nei hauh lovin,” mi lian leh mi tête, mi maktaduai riat laiin an thih phah a ni. Tin, tûn hma lam zawng zawng aiin tûn hnaia lîrnghîngte hi an nasa zâwkin, chhiatna an rawn thlen nasa zâwk. Hêng zawng zawngte hian Krista lo kal lehna a hnaih tawhzia an entîr vek a ni.

Sakhaw lama tlâk hniamna te, khawlohna leh suahsualnate pawh hi NI HNUHNUNG awm dân tilangtu an ni.

“Tin, hei hi hre rawh, ni hnuhnungahte chuan hun khirh takte a lo awm ang. Mi mahni inhmangaihtu te, tangka ngainatu te, intivei te, chapo te, Pathian sawichhetu te, nu leh pa thu awi lote, lâwm nachâng hre lo te, thianghlim lote, hmangaihna pianpui nei lo te, huaikhawm bâwlte, hêktu te, insûm thei lo te, mi kawlhsen te, inngaihlu te, PATHIAN HMANGAIH AIA mahni lâwmna mai hmangaih zâwktute an ni dâwn si; Pathian ngaihsak anna an nei ang a, nimahsela, a thiltihtheihna chu an awih lo vang.” 2 Timothea 3:1-5.

A TÂWPA CHHINCHHIAHNA ROPUI

10. Krista lo kal lehna chu eng chungah nge a innghah?
“HE RAM CHANCHIN ṬHA hi hnam zawng zawng hriattirna tûrin khawêl zawng zawngah hrilhin a awm ang; CHU MI ZAWHAH chuan TAWPNA chu a lo thleng ang.” Matthaia 24:14.

Pathian ram Chanchin Ṭha, Krista lo kal lehna chanchin chu, ṭawng chi hrang sangkhat dâwn laiin puan darh mêk a ni a, khawvêl mihring zawng zawng za zêla 95 hnênah hril mêk a ni.

Khawvêla Voice of Prophecy hnathawh hi a ropuiin a mak hle mai. He Râlchhân sikul hian mi maktaduai tam tak hnênah chanchin ṭha a pung reng a, chu mi thlâwptu atân khawvêl pum pui huamin Radio-in puan darh chum chum a ni reng bawk. Hetiang hian khawvêl ram tina hriattirna tûrin hmanrua ṭha leh kawng ṭha tak tak an awm a ni.

Tâwpna chu a hnai tak meuh ta. Nang, Isua lo kal lehna hnai tawh tak chanchin puan darh mêk hian i thinlung a chawk harh ve em? Amah chu tâwk tûrin i inpeih em? Kawngka bul ngeiah a awm tawh a ni.
