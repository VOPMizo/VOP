ZIRLAI PANGANA

SUAL HI TU TIHCHHUAH NGE?

Mi tam tak chuan sual awm ṭanna leh a lo awmna chhan thuah hian buaina an nei ṭhin. Sual hnathawh-manganna te, chhiatna rapthlak tak te leh, damdawi in, dam loten an luah khah del dul te hî an hmu a.

Heng thilte hi thiltihtheihna te, finna tawp neilo te leh hmangaihnaa khat rorelna hnuah engtingne an lo awm theih, tiin an zawt ṭhin a ni.

KHAWVEL HMELMA LIAN BER

Lei mihringte leh finnate zawng zawng hi nuai chimih hlen der tum rana nasa taka phiar veltu, fing em em, thil hre em em leh thiltihtheina ropui tak nei mi pakhat a awm tih hi kan hre tur a ni. He mi hi Khawvel Hmelma Lianber, sual awmtirtu chu a ni a; manganna leh khawlohna te, natna leh lungngaihna te, chhiatna leh thihna te leh he leia thil ṭhalo tinrengte bulpui a ni.

Chu mi, thiam loh changa chu tunge ni? Khawi aṭanga lo chhuak nge a nih? Heng buaina zawng zawngah hian a mawhphurtu tunge ni? Mifingho te, ram inrelbawlnaa hruaitute leh sakhaw lama hotute ngaih danin khawvel hian a hun hnuhnung ber a tawng ṭep tawh a, huatna te, duhmna te, phuba lak duhna tein a khat zo ta a ni. HENG THILAH HIAN A MAWHPHURTU CHU PATHIAN A NI THEI LO VANG.

“Pathian chu HMANGAIHNA a ni si a” (1 John 4:8).

Sual hi awm tak meuh a nih phawt chuan a awmtirtu pawh a awm ngei tur a ni. Thianghlimna leh hmangaihnaa khat Pathian chu mawhphurtu nia kan rin theih si loh chuan, sul awm ṭantitrtu chu hre tur leh hmu chhuak turin kan dam a mamawh reng reng a ni. Sual chu engtianga lo awm ṭan nge a nih a, englaiin nge a lo awm ṭan, khawii hmuna rawn inṭan chhuak nge? Tin, sual avanga thil rapthlak tak tak mihringte chunga lo thlengte chu engtenge ni hlawm? Heng zawhna zawng zawng chhannate hi Bible-ah kan hmu a ni.

HE LEIA SUAL AWM ṬAN DAN

1. Bible-in khawvela sual awm ṭan dan chanchin engtinnge min hrilh?
“Tin, Lalpa Pathianin ram-sa a siam zawng zawngah chuan rûl a fing vervek ber a. Tichuan ani chuan hmeichhe hnenah chuan, Pathianin ‘HUANA THING RAH RENG RENG HI IN EI TUR A NI LO,’ a ti elo maw a ti a. Tin, hmeichhia chuan rûl hnenah chuan ‘Tilove, huana thing rahte hi kan ei thei a sin: huan lai taka thing awm rah erawh chu Pahtianin, ‘IN EI TUR A NI LO, TAWH PAWH IN TAWK TUR A NI LO, CHUTILO CHUAN IN THI ANG,’ a ti a ni. Tin, rûl chuan hmeichhe hnenah chuan, ‘THI TEH SUH E: in ei ni apang chuan in mit a lo var ang a, a chhia leh a ṭha hriain Pathian ang in lo ni dawn tih Pathianin a hre reng a nih chu,’ a ti a. Tichuan Hmeichhia chuan thingrah chu ei mi a ni tih leh, mit la tak a ni tih a hriat chuan, a rah ṭhenkhat chu a lo va, a ei ta a; tin, a pasal a pe ve a, ani pawhin a ei ve ta a.” Genesis 3:1-6.

He khawvela sual rawn thlen dan chu chhinchhiah teh. Evi chu Pathian rinawma RINHLELH tira awmin Pathianin in thi ang a tih chu a ring lo va. Pathianin finna leh ropuina chu an laka thup lui tlata ngaih turin Pathian felna leh rinawmna meuh pawh a lo RINGHLEL ta zel a. Pathian rorelna pawh chu ringhlelin a tawpah phei chuan rûl, a hnena thu sawitu hnenah chuan a intulut ta a ni.

A SUALNA BUL CHU PATHIAN HNENA RINAWMNA PAIHTHLAA MI DANG RORELNA HNUAIA INTUKLUHNA CHU A NI.

Manganna leh chhiatna tam tak lo thleng ta zawk chu a rapthlak teh e!

Eden Huanah Setanan a tira mawi taka siam rûl chu hmangin mihring chu a biain a bum thlu ta a. Chuvangin Setana chu sual bul a ni.

“Chu rûlpui tar, Diabola leh Setana an tih, khawvel zawng zawng bumtu chu”(Thupuan 12:9).

2. Diabola emaw Setana emaw an tih hi khawi aṭanga lo kal nge?
“Tin, VANAH CHUAN indona a lo awm a; Mikaela leh A vantirhkohte pawh chuan an do ve a; nimahsela, an hneh ta lova, an hmun chu vanah a awm ta bawk hek lo. Tin, DRAGON LIAN PUI, rûlpui tar, DIABOLA leh SETANA an tih khawvel zawng zawng bumtu chu an paih thla a, LEIAH AN PAIH A; a tirhkohte pawh an paihthla tel ve a” (Thupuan 12:9).

Siam ṭhat leh tuma nasa taka Pathianin a beih hnu leh dawh thei taka a tuar hnuin van hmun aṭang chuan Setana chu a hnawt chhuak ta a ni. Van aṭanga LO KAL a nihzia chu kan hre ta a, nimahsela, vanah ENGTIA lo awm nge a nih tih zawhna chu kan chhan tur a ni ta.

Sual Hi Awmsa Ve Reng A Ni Lo

3. Mi hi sual sa reng em ni a nih?
“ṬHA FAMKIM tak FINNAA KHAT, mawina tinreng Pathian huan, Eden-ah i cheng, . . . . Nang chu vengtu cherub hriak thih i ni; Pathian tlang thianghlimah ka awmtir chia, mei vam iang lung zingah i leng. I ṬHA FAMKIM, SIAMA I AWM NI ATA, NANGMAHA KHAWLOHNA A AWM HMA RENG ZAWNG” Ezekiela 28:12-15.

Setana chanchin sawi nan Tura lal suaksual tak hi Pathianin a rawn hmang a. Hetah hian vantirhkoh fing leh chungnung a nih ṭhin zia min hrilh a. A tirah chuan ṭha famkim leh sualna nei lo a ni.

Duh Thlan Theihna

4. Chu vantirhkoh fing leh chungnung chu engtinnge hel hotu huat thlala takaa lo ṭan tak?
“Ka CHHO vanga . . . KA CHAWISANG ang; KA THU ang . . . CHUNGNUNGBERA ANG MAIIN KA AWM ANG” Isaia 14:13;14.

INNGAIHHLUTNA, CHAPONA leh a tawpa HELNA meuh thlen tak avang chuan vantirhkoh ropui tak, Lucifera-Setana-chu van aṭangin hnawhchhuah a lo ni ta a.

Lucifera, vantirhkoh dangte ang bawka Pathian kutchhuak chuan Pathian chu a lo îtsîk ta a. PATHIAN THUNEIHNA CHUNG LAMA A THUNEIHNA CHAWISAN ATUM TA HIAL A NI. Hmun sang ber luah a tumna tak chuan hmun hnuai hnung berah a pawt thla ta zawk a, van rorelna kalha hel leh Pathian hma aṭanga hnawh chhuah a lo ni ta a ni.

Mihringte hnenah hian Pathianin duh thlan theihna a pe a, chu duh thlan theihna chu nei lo sela chuan khawl ang mai an ni ang. Pathian hlauh vang leh tihluihna avanga A thu awih turin mihringte a duh lova, an duh thlanna hmanga Amah hmangaih leh faka, athu awih turin a duh zawk a ni. Khawl ang mai mihring hnen aṭanga hmangaihnaah lawm tak takna a awm thei lo. Hmangaihna famkim chu DUHTHLANNA aṭanga lo chhuakah chauh a awm thei a ni. Chuvangin chu DUHTHLANNA hman dan dik tak chu kan hriat thiam a ṭul; duhthlan theihna chu thilsiam te kaihhruaitu ber pakhat a ni si a. ENGKIM MAI HI DUHTHLANNA DIK TAKAH AN INNGHAT VEK A, tin, chu duh thlan theihna chu mihringte hnena pek a nih avangin mihringte chuan an hmang tur a ni.

Pathianin Lucifera A siamin sualna nei lovin, ropuina vantirhkohah a siam a. Nimahsela, ama duh thlannain Lucifera chuan diabola-ah a insiamta a ni.

Setana Tihchhiat A Nih Loh Na Chhan

5. A hel tirhin engatinge Setana chu tihboral nghal a nih loh?
“Mi ṭhenkhatin muanga an ruat angin Lalpa chu a thutiam kawngah chuan a muang love: tuma boral a duh lova, mi zawng zawng LO SIM DUHIN NANGMAHNI LAMAH A DAWHTHEI a ni zawk” 2 Petera 3:9.

A hel tirhin Lucifera chuan a bumna hnathawh kawngah a fimkhur em em a. Sualna chu thil thar, la awm ngai reng reng lo a ni a, vantirhkohten hriatna an la nei ngai hek lo. Chutiang hmunah chuan Lucifera chu hruaitu zahawm tak a lo ni tawh ṭhin a ni. PATHIAN RAM ZAWNG ZAWNGA MI CHENGTEN DIK TAKA PAHTIAN RORELNA (FELNA) CHU RINHLELHNA RENG AN NEIH LOH NAN SETANA CHU TIHBORAL NGHAL LOHIN A THIL RUAHMAN LEH TUMTE CHHUNZAWM TURIN HUN PEK RIH A NI A. A TAWPAH MI ZAWNG ZAWNG REM TIHIN TIHBORAL A LA NI ANG.

6. Enga insiamin nge Setanan hna a thawh ṭhin?
“Chu chu a mak love; Setana meuh pawh ENG TIRHKOHVAH a insiam chawp si ṭhin a. Chuvangin a rawngbawltute pawh chu FELNA RAWNGBAWLTUAH INSIAM CHAWP MAHSELA thilmak a ni love; chungho tawpna chu an thiltih ang zelin a ni” II Korinth 11:14,15.

Inven ṭhat a va ṭul tak em! Setana chuan sual hi thil duhawm leh itawm tak angin a lantir a, a ṭhuihhruaite pawh mawi takin a lantir ṭhin a ni. Thil diklo dik anga lantir te, sual hi sual lo anga tarlan te leh thu awih loh hi khawsak nuam ber anga lantirte hi Setana hnathawh vek an ni. Tin, a tirhkohte chu mi hip thei tak, bumnaa khat ni siin an inlar ṭhin a, chuvangin, Pathian thutak leh dawt thu te, Pathian tirhkoh leh dawt thu puang darhtute kan thliar hran theih nan Pathian thu kan zir ngun em em tur a ni.

Mihring Dinhmun Chauhzia

Isua tirhkoh Paula thusawite hian mihringin nun kawng a indona awm hi amah chauhvin bei selang chuan a beidawn thlakzia leh mihring dinhmun chauhzia a tarlang chiang hle.

“Thil ka tih hi a ngaihna ka hre si lo; ka thil tih ṭhin hi ka thiltih duhzawng a ni si lo, ka thil huat zawng tak chu ka ti zawk ṭhin a ni. Ka tih duh loh zawng chu ka tih si chuan, Dan chu a ṭha tia awih ka ni ang a sin. Chutichuan, ka thiltih hi keima tih a ni tawh lova, SUAL KEIMAHA AWM TIH CHU A NI ZAWK. Keimaah (chu chu ka tisaah tihna a ni) thil ṭha reng a awm lo tih ka hria; thil ṭha tih duhna chu keimahah a awm na a, THIL ṬHA TIHNA ERAWH CHU A AWM SI LO. Ṭhatna tih ka duh hi ka ti lova; sualna tih ka duh loh hi ka ti zawk si ṭhin a. Ka duh lohzawng chu ka tih si chuan, a titu chu kei ka ni tawh lova, SUAL KEIMAHA AWM CHU A NI ZAWK E. Chuticuan he dan hi ka hre ta: thil ṭha tih ka duh laiin sualna ka henah a lo awm ṭhin a ni. Ka rilru tak takin Pathian dan chungah ka lawm si ṭhin a: nimahsela, ka taksa pengah te hian dan danglam a awm tih ka hria, chu chuan ka rilru dan a do va, KA TAKSA PENGTEA SUALNA DAN AWM HNENAH CHUAN SALAH MI HRUAI ṬHIN A. Mi rethei ka va ni tehlul em! He thihna taksa lakah hian tuinnge mi chhanchhuak ang?” (Rom 7:15-24).

Kum sang tam ral zelah hian mihringin sual a do theihna chu a lo chau a, tunah chuan sualin a chungah thu a nei ta a. Sual bawihah a lo awm ta a ni. Ama theihna chuan mifel nih leh theih a ni tawh lo. Bible-ah mihring thiltih ṭhat ber pawh “silhfen bawlhhlawh” ang a ni tih kan hmu a.

Mihringin Chhandamtu A Mamawhna

MIHRINGIN PATHIAN AṬANGA CHHUAK CHHANDAMTU A NEI NGEI TUR A NI. AMA BEIHNA MAI CHUAN Setana phuarna SUAL HRUITE CHU A TICHAT THEI NGAI LOVANG. CHHANDAMTU ZARAH CHAUH CHUAN MISUALTEN NUNGCHANG SIAM THARNA AN NEI THEI A NI.

Chutiang chhandamtu chu Bible hian kan hnenah a tarlang a ni.

Sual leh Tuarna

Tunah he kan zirlai aṭang hian sual leh tuarna inlaichinna hmuh chhuah kan duh dawn a. Hmeichhia te mipa te hian engtiziange an tuar ṭhin. Tuarna chu engnge a awmzia? Heng zawhnate hi kan chin fel tur chu a ni.

Pathian nungchang zepui ber chu hmangaihna a ni; A mithmuhah chuan thil engkim a inhawng vek a. Amah chauh chuan mihring tana tlanna hna ropui tak hlen famkimna tura thil ṭulte chu kan chungah a thlentir thei.

Hmangaihna leh tuarna hi thil inhal tak ni awma ngaihtheih inhal hauh silo an ni. Naupangten hling an rapin, hmawlhte te an chuih bur fo a. Hling te chu phawi an nih loh chuan hnai te lain, a pûn zel a, thihna hial pawh a thlentir thei ṭhin. Chuvangin nu leh pate chuan an fate an hmangaih avangin na an ti chungin hling te chu an khei chhuahsak ṭhin a ni.

Natna te, vanduaina te, tlakchhamna te leh harsatna chi tin rengte hi hmangaihna hmanruaah changin Pathianin thlarau lam hnathawh nan a hmang a; mi sualin a thinlung tiruaka, a nungchanga sual hnûm zawng zawng thai bo vek dihna a thlen ṭhin.

7. Mite hian an sualna avangin an tuar ṭhin em ni?
“Engahnge i hliam vanga i ṭah? I na chu tihdam rual a ni love, i khawlohna a nasa I THILTIH SUALTE A PUN AVANGIN, HENG THIL HI I CHUNGAH KA TI A NIH HI” Jeremiah 30:15.
8. Pathianin kan khawlohna phu tâwkin em ni min hrem?
“Tin kan thiltih sual avang leh kan KHAWLOHNA nasa tak avanga kan chunga hetiang thil a lo awm a, NANG KAN PATHIANIN KAN BAWHCHHIATNAIN A PHU AIA NEPA MIN HREM HNUA hetiang chhanchhuahna min la pek cheu si hi” Ezra 9:13.
9. Mite chunga tuarna Pathianin thlentir a phal naah hian engnge a tum chiang tak chu?
“Lalpa chuan a hmangaih chu a thunun ṭhin a, Fa a lawm apiang chu a vaw ṭhin a sin ni. . . . Anni (nu leh pate) chuan ni reilo te, ṭha an tih ang zelin min thunun ngei tak a, ani erawh chuan A THIANGHLIMNA CHU KAN CHAN VE THEIH NAN kan ṭhatna turin min thunun zawk a ni.” (Hebrai 12:6,10).
“MI FEL HREAWMNATE chu a tam a” (Sam 34:19).

Sualin Pathian hnen ata min tihrangin thianghlim lovah min siam ta a, mihringa Pathian anpui awm chu a rawn nuai reh ta vek a. Nimahsela, Pathianin mihringte hi thianghlimna siam thar sak leh, A anpui din thar sak leh a duh em em reng a. A nungchang ṭawmpuitu A THIANGHLIMNA CHANGTU ni turin a duh reng a ni. A hnena kal turin tumah a TILUI ngai lo. A nungchang leh a rorelna dan nena inhmeh thlapin mi sualte leh mifelte hi, anmahni DUHTHLANNA ngeia thianghlimna THLANG turin A kaihruai zawk ṭhin a ni. Chutianga mite khaihhruai nana a hmanraw pakhat chu tuarna hi a ni. Tuarnate chu mimal sualna avanga lo awm an ni thei a, a ni kher lo thei bawk. Tin, Pathian thupekte vawng tura kaihhruai mam hmangaihna avangin tuarna chu a lo thleng a ni bawk ṭhin.

Chaponain Thianghlimna A Dal

10. Tuarna chapona a tibo thei em?
“TIN, HRIATTIRNATE CHU A NASAT EM EM AVANGA KA INNGAIHHLUT LUTUK LOHNAN tisaah HLING mi pe a ni, Setana tirhkoh kut thak hnawih nana mi hmangtu tur chu” II Korinth 12:7.

Paula chu inlarna ropui tak hmuhtir a nih avangin chaponain a tichhe mai ang tih hlauhawm khawp a ni a. Chuvangin a ṭhatna tur atan Pathianin tisa natna engemaw a chungah a thlentir a. TAKSAA HLING AWM nena han tehkhin chu a na hle a ni tih a kawk a. Pathianin hlawhtlinnaah hian bahlahna min siam sak ṭhin a ni. Tirhkoh Paula hian hei hi zirtur a neih chu a ni.

Tuarnain Hriatthiamna A Tipung

11. Amah anga tuarna nei ve theite tan Paula'n eng thil pawimawh tak nge a sawi?
“Kan hreawmna nep tak, tun mit khap kar chhung chauhva awm tur hian, ropuina nasa tak chatuan atan min siam sak nasa tulh tulh a ni” II Korinth 4:17.
"Tunlaia tuarnate hi nakina kan chunga ropuina tihlan tur nen chuan tehkhin thamah ka ruat si lo” Rom 8:18.
"PATHIAN HMANGAIHTU amaha ruat anga a kohvate tan chuan AN ṬHATNA TURIN ENGKIMIN A THAWHSAK HLAWM ṬHIN TIH KAN HRIA” Rom 8:28.

Tuarna hian VANRAMA HLIMNA LEH MUANNA KUMHLUN lo awm turin mi chu tuihal takin a awm tur a ni. Dangro tak chuan tui ṭhatna leh a hlutna chu a hre chiang a. Chaw ngaisang turin mi chu rilṭam takin a awm tur a ni. Ṭhian hlutzia hre tak tak tur chuan khawhar taka awm phawt hi a ni. Mi hrisel lo chuan hriselna hlutzia a hre ber a. Rilrua retheihna hrehawm tak tuartute chuan Van muanna famkim chu an ngai hlu thei ber ang.

Lawng ṭhat leh ṭhat loh fiahna chu thli tleh hun hi a ni. Khawvel thli (fiahna leh buaina) hi Pathian leh A Lalram atana kan rinna fiahna hun chu a ni.

Lawng chhe thema chuana vaukam thleng thei hramin Pathianin min siam fo va. Tuikhur hlua tui chu kan in theih nan kan tuizem min tihruah sak ṭhin. Kan rinchhan min lak bo saka, leia tlu turin min ti lova. Amah ngei chu kan tiang leh kan rinchhan atana kan hman theih nan. “Pathian hmangaihtute tan an ṭhatna turin engkimin a thawh sak” tih leh Pathianin a ṭha ber min siam sak ṭhin tih chu kan ring ngam lo em ni?

Krista Tuarna leh Karma

12. Krista tuarna leh Karma dan hian inlaichinna a nei lo tih hi engtinnge kan hriat theih?

Matthaia 1:21-ah “A mite chu an sualna lakah a CHHANDAM dawn si a” tih kan hmu a. Hebrai-ah ti hian kan hmu “KEIMAHNI ANGA KAWNG TINRENGA THLEMNA TAWK TAWH, SUAL ERAWH CHU SUAL LO” Hebrai 4:15.

Bible-in min hrilh angin Isua nundan zirna aṭangin thil pawimawh tak hnial rual loh kawng thum a awm a: (1) Pathian, mihring tisa puin a awm. (2) Sualna nei lo ṭha famkimin a nung. (3) A tuar ang hi mi tumahin an tuar ngai lo.

Engvangin nge a tuar? Karma dan pawh hmang ni ila Isua chu tuar tur a ni lo; A tuar phah tur engmah dik lohna a nei lo. Isua tawrhna hian tuarna zawng zawng hi Karma dan a mimal thiltih rah vek a ni tih hi a dik lo tih a tilang chiang a ni.

Karma Dan leh Inthlah Chhawnna Dan

Karma dan hi ngaih ruatna aṭanga lo piang a ni a, mihring hriatnaa awm ve pawh a ni lo. Finna (science) lama chhut chhuah chi pawh a ni bawk lo. Pathianin dik takin ro a rel avangin khawvelah hian lungngaihna leh inchen lohna hi a lo awm a ni tih rinna aṭanga lo chhuak a ni.

Inthlah chhawnna dan erawh chu hnial rual a ni lo. Science lam aṭanga finfiah theih a ni. Rilru leh taksa lama chak lohna nu leh paten an neih chu fa teah a lang thei a ni. Bible-in chiang taka min hrilh erawh chu nu leh pa ten KHAWLOHNA chu faten an chhawm thei lo a ni.

"THIL TISUALTU CHU A THI TUR A NI; FAPA CHUAN A PA KHAWLOHNA CHU A TUAR LO VANG A, Pa pawhin fapa khawlohna chu a tuar hlek lovang” Ezekiela 18:20.

Fapa chuan a nu leh a pa sual hlawh chatuana thihna man chu a chawi lovang. Chu mi rual chuan Bible chuan sualna RAH chu chhuan khat aṭanga chhuan dangah a la kal chawng zel a ni tiin min zirtir a. SUAL lam AWNNA pawh hi pian pui a ni.

Inthlah chhawn zelna dan thil awm tur atan a pawimawh a ni. Kawng dangin sual CHHEKKHAWL hnathawh hmuh dan a awm lo. Miten sual chu tena, kalsan duhna an neih theih nan pian pui ngei a nitih hriat hi a pawimawh a ni. An sual rah chu an fate chungah a lang zel tih hriain nu leh paten sual tih hreh nan an hmang ang.

Inthlah chhawn dan hi chiang taka kan hriat chuan tunlai khawvela hrehawmna leh inchen lohnate hi, chu mi vang chu a ni tih kan hria ang. Kan hriat chhuah phak lohva dik lohna lo awm khawm daih tawh chu khawvela mihringte hian kan lo chhawm mai a ni.

Tuarna zawng zawng hi Karma dan anga mi mal thiltih tawh avanga lo thleng chu nise Karna dan chu a dik a ni ang. A ṭhen Karma dan a ṭhen inthlah chhawnna dan a ni thei si lo.

Mihringin a pian chhuahpui taksa awmdan te chu KARMA dan a ni tia ngaih a nih chuan sual aiin karma chu a chungnung zawk a ni ang a. Hrisel lohna leh tuarna tihreh nana damdawi te, inzai te leh mihring beihna zawng zawng te hi hmusit awm chauh a ni dawn a ni.

Khawvela sualna te, tuarna te leh lungngaihna lo awm te hi Pathianin a thilsiamte a awp hneh loh vangah kan ngai tur a ni lo. A Pathian thil ruat chu a thleng famkim zel ang.

Chuvangin mihringte zinga hrehawmna te intluk lohna te a loa wm chhan Bible-in min hrilh chu a dik a, pawm ngei tura phut kan ni.

Pathianin hriatna fim leh ṭha pe chein malsawmna i chungah thlen rawh se.
