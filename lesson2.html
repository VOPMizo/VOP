Châng Pawimawh: “Tin, hril lâwkna thu nghet lehzual chu kan nei a ni, khua a lo vâr a, vârparh arsi chu in thinlunga a lo chhuah hma loh zawng chu chu khâwnvâr hmun thima êng a ngaihsak anga in ngaihsak chuan in ti ṭha ang.” 2 Petera 1:19.
“HRILHLAWKNA hi Pathian thawk khum a ni tih huai taka ṭan tlat ngamtu chu Kristian sakhua chauh hi a ni. Khawvêl thiltihtheihnain a phâk bak chhinchhiahna chiang tak a neihna ngawt pawh hi a dikzia lantirtu pakhat chu a ni ngei mai. Thuthlung Hlui hian hun lo la thleng tûra thilte chu an thlen hma daihin chiang tak leh fiah takin a tarlang diam tawh a, tin, chung hril lâwknate chu Pathian aṭanga chhuak vek an ni tih a târlang bawk. Kristian sakhua chuan khawvel chanchin te, hmasang thil chanchin zirna te, finna te leh mihring thiamna leh finna pêng hrang hrang te hian hril lawkna thleng famkim thei lo pakhat chauh pawh sawi tur an hriat chuan sawi chhuak tûrin huai takin a ngên a ni.”..A.T.Pierson.

ZIRLAI INṬANNA

Kum engemawti chhûng chu Europe-a mipuite khuan an zân mut dâwn apiangin zinga tho leh tur an awm dâwn nge dâwn lo tih leh, an mut hlanin chatuan atân bomb den hlum zawh vek an ni dawn em ni tih an ngaihtuah atalakin mutmu pawh an tuah thei ngai lo va. Europe pal tlanga ram hnehtu hrang hrang an kal vêl zut lai chuan khawvel ram tina mite, ngun taka thil kal dân lo thlîr leh lo enthlithlaitute rilruah chuan hêng zawhna pahnihte hi an lo lang ṭhin:

  1. Rorêltu pakhat chauhin Europe hi a hnehin a ṭhuikhâwm thei ang em?
  2. Europe ram mi chêngte, a hmei a pa nunaute nên lama râwt chiamna awm ṭhîn hian reh hun a nein gang dâwn lo em ni?

Kum zabi sâwmpali lai kal ta chhûng khân mi malin emaw, mi ṭhenkhat ṭangrualin emaw Europe hi hneh tumin an lo bei tawh ṭhin a, nimahsela tumah hlawhtling rêng an la awm ngai lo. Engnge a chhan ni ang le? Eng vângin nge mi thiltithei tak takte pawhin hriamhrei leh hmanraw ṭha tak takte nên meuha an beih pawha an tum chu an hlen theih ngai loh?

Herodotus-a, khawvêl chanchin pa an tih ṭhin chuan a chanchin ziak tûr inhmu rem lo tak tak leh inralkah tak takte a lâwr khâwm hmain Daniala Pathian zâwlneite zinga pakhat chuan khawvêl tawp hun thlenga khawvel pum pui chunga rorêltu lalram hrang hrang lo ding chhuak turte chu, an hun indawt dân chenin a lo sawi lâwk diam tawh a ni.

Kum sanghnih leh zanga lai a ni ta. Babulonah lalram pakhat rorêltu pakhat chauh kuta thuneihna innghat chu din a ni a; chu lalram chu Nebukanezara sipai ram lâkte aṭanga lo piang chhuak a ni. Mimal thuneihna hmanga rorelnaah hian chakna lehchak lohna a awm ṭhinzia he lalram hian a tilang chiang hle. Chutiang rorelna din chhuaktute rêng rêng chuan an lalram din thar chu helh din reng theih a nih dâwn leh dâwn loh te, ram enkawlna leh rêlbawlnaa harsatna tam tak lo chhuak turte an ngaihtuah châwk ṭhin. Nebukanezara pawh hian chutiang bawkin a lalram la awm zêl dan tûrte chu a ngaihtuah nep nep a, chutia a ngaihtuah lai chuan Pathianin mumang mak tak mai a rawn mantir ta a.

Pathianin Daniela hmanga Nebukadnezara mumang a hrilhfiahna hian khawvêl pum pui chunga rorêltu lalram chanchin te a fûn khawm kim ber âwm e. Chang tawi tê tê pariat lekah chu mi chanchin chu kan hmu a, nimahsela khawvêl thiltihtheihna leh ropuina thute chu an khaikhâwm kim tâwk hle si a ni. Tin, chutianga thil tam tak chanchin, thu tlêm têa fûn khawm theih si chu mihring finna phak bak a ni. Tin khawvêl chanchin dik tak, huap zau êm êm si, thu tlêm tea tarlan vek bawk si chu mihring ṭawngin tun hma lamin ziak a lang chiang hle. Mi malin Europe hi hneh a la ni ngai lo. He thilah hian Pathian kut a tel a tih vek theih ang em? tih zawhna chu thu pasarih leka chhân a ni a,

He zirlai hi ngunthlûk taka zir lâk a ni. Hmasâng lehkhabutea hril lâwkna leh Baibl-a hril lâwknate hi ang khata en mai tur an ni love. Pathiana inchhâlte zawng zawng chunga lêng, Pathian nung pakhat chauh a awm a, Ani chuan engkim a hre vek a. Baibl hril lâwknate Bulpui hi a ni.

HRIL LAWKNA TUM

Hril lâwkna thu zirna aṭangin eng malsâwmna pahnih nge lo thleng?
“Dik takin Lalpa Pathian chuan a rawngbâwltu, a zawlneite hnênah a THURUK HRILH lovin engmah a ti lo vang.” Amosa 3:7.
“Tun achinah a lo thlen hmain ka hrilh che u hi, A LO THLEN HUNAH amah kha ka ni tih IN RIN THEIHNA TURIN.” Johana 13:19.
“Hrilh lâwkna THU NGHET LEHZUAL kan nei, khua a lo vâr a, vârparh arsi chu in thinlunga a lo chhuah hma loh zawng chu chu khawnvâr hmun thima ENG an ngaihsak anga in ngaihsak chuan in ti ṭha ang.” 2Petera 1:19.

A hmasain hrilh lâwkna chuan Pathian thu dik zia te, rinawmzia te leh thâwk khum a nihzia te min rin nghehtir a. Baibl hrilh lâwkna thleng famkimte kan hmuhin, a bul aṭanga a tawp thlenga hre tlang vektu kan Pathian leh, A thâwk khum Lehkha Thute min rin ngheh zualtir sauh bawk a ni.

A dawt lehah chuan hrilh lâwkna chu thim zinga êng, hun lo la thleng tûr pawh va chhun êng phût thei khawp hiala thiltihtheihna nei a ni. Thil lo thleng tûrte chu A fate hnênah Pathianin a hriattir a, kum 2,500 lai kal taa hrilh lâwkna thu ziakte pawh naktuk lawka thil lo thleng tûr chanchin mihringten an hrilh lâwknate ai chuan an la dik zâwkin an la rinawm zawk a ni.

LALBER MUMANG

Babulon lal, Nebukanezaran eng a ngaihtuah laiin nge mumang tak tak a lo neih?
“Aw Lalber, nang chuan i khum laizâwlah chuan tun hnua thil lo awm turte i ngaihtuah a, thil inthupte puang ṭhintu chuan tun hnua thil lo la awm tûrte chu a hriattir che a ni e.”
“Nebukadnezzara rorêl kum hnihnaah chuan Nehukadnezzara chuan muamang a nei a; a rilru a lo buai zo ta a, a muhil thei ta lo va.”Daniela 2:29, 1

Nebukanezzara hi lal naupang tak la ni mah sela, thiltithei tak a nih avângin râl a beihna zawng zawngah te chuan ropui taka hnehtu a ni zêl a. A Babulon lalram ropui tak chu chatuana ding reng tûrin leh, khawvêl zawng zawng chunga rorêltu ni kumkhua tûrin siam dân kawng a ngaihtuah ta a. Zan khat chu, chûng thilte, “tûn hnua thil lo thleng tur thu” ngaihtuah avanga a rilru a buai hle laiin a lo muhil a, mang a lo nei ta a.

Lal Nebukanezzara chuan a mangah engnge a hmuh?
“Aw Lalber, i han dâk chhuak a, ngai teh, milim ropui tak a lo awm a. Chu milim hrawlpui, tle êm êm mai chu, i hmaah a ding a; a landan pawh ṭihbaiawm tak a ni. Chu milim chu a lu rangkachak thianghlim tak a ni a, a âwm leh a bânte chu tangkarua a pum leh a malpuite dâr, a kete thîr, a kephah chu a ṭhen thir, a ṭhen bellei a ni a. Nang chuan lung, kut lova laih chhuah thlengin i lo en a, chu chuan milim chu a kephah thir leh belleiah chuan a han delh a, a tikeh sawm ta vek a. Tichuan, thir te, bellei te, dâr te, tangkarua te, rangkachak te chu arualin an kehsawm ta vek a, nipui lai hrui zâwla buhsi ang mai an lo nita a. tin, thliin anmahni a lên bova, an awmna hmun rêng hriat a nita lo va: tin, milim delhtu lung chu tlang lian takah a lo chang a, lei pum hi a khat ta a.”Daniela 2:31-35.

He mumang chungchânga thil danglam tak pakhat awm chu lalber, a mang neitu ber pawhin a mang rêng rêng a hre chhuak zo ta lo hi a ni. Mumang a neih apianga a tih dan fo ṭhin angin he mi ṭum pawh hian a mi fingte –aienthiam-te dawivaihthiam te, dawithiam te a ko khâwm a. Nimahsela, chûng mi fingho, mumang awmze hrilhfiah thiam inti ṭhinte pawh chu lal hmaah chuan mangang tak leh hrilhhai takin an ding ta reng a. Lalber lah chuan a mumang hrilhfiah tûr leh a awmzia pawh hrilh nghal turin a phût tlat mai si a. A mi fingten mumang chu an hnêna a hrilh theih phawt chuan a awmzia chu an hrilh mai ang an tih pawhin, “Mumang chu min hrilh ula, a hrilhfiahna min entir thei tih ka hria ang,” tiin, a chhâng then mai a. Thil ngiat mak tak leh danglam tak a ni i ti ngei ang le. Amaherawhchu nakin hnua thil lo thleng tûr chanchin sawi lâwk theia inchhâl si, engatinge hun rei lo te kal taa thil thleng chanchin an hriat chhuah theih si loh? Hre ngei tûra beisei theih an ni dâwn lawm ni? Lal berin a mi fingte tlâktlai lohzia leh dâwt an lo sawi tawh ṭhinzia a hmuh chuan an zaa tihhlum nghâl turin thu a pe ta a. Chûng mi finghoten thihna an pumpelh leh theih dân chu chanchin ngaihnawm tak a ni. Chutih laia Babulona Pathian dik betu mi pakhat avangin chûng mi fingte chuan thihna an pumpelh ta a ni.

Babulon khawpuiah chuan Juda mi pakhat, Daniela, Jerusalem khua aṭanga sal mante zinga mi a awm a. Pathian dik betu a ni a, lalber thupêk a hriat veleh a ṭhian pathumte nên ṭawngṭaina bik an nei nghâl a (Daniela 2:17-19). An ṭawngṭaina chu Pathianin a chhâng a, mumang leh a awmze hrilhfiahna pawh chu Daniela hnênah chuan hriattir a nita a. Hetah hian Pathian Thu aṭanga thutak hmuh chhuah tuma thinlunga tih tak zetna neia zawngtute tan zir tur ropui tak a awm. Pathian Lehkha Thu ṭhenkhata hriat thiam loh leh buaina deuh i neih chuan hriat thiamna dîlin Pathian hnênah ṭawngṭai la, kaihruai tur chein Amah chu sâwm ṭhin ang che.

Mangang taka lalber awm hnênah chuan eng thu nge Danielan a va hrilh?
“Nimahsela, thil inthupte puangtu Pathian chu vanah a awm a, lal Nebukadnezzara hi tun hnua thil lo la awm tûrte chu a hriattir a nih hi.” Daniela 2:28.

“Ni hnuhnung,” tûn lai hun thu hril lâwkna hi kan ngai pawimawh êm êm tur a ni. Kum 2,500 chhûng zawng hian he mumang hi Pathian hrilh lawkna dikzia lantirtu a ni. Pathian hrilh lâwkna chuan hun lo la awm tura thilte chu chiang taka kan hmuh theih nan hliahtu puanzâr chu a hlîp kiang a, bumna leh sualnaina min phuar theihna lak ata min chhanchhuak a, kan rinna a tinghet bawk a. Arsi ngaihtuahtuten entlang ṭha tak hmanga vânte an thlîr ṭhin ang hian hrilh lâwkna thu zirtute chuan ni hnuhnunga thil lo thleng tûrte chu an thlîr ṭhin a ni.

KHAWVEL LALRAM PALITE

A lu rangkachakin Daniela hun laia khawvêl pum pui chunga rorêltu, Babulon lalram a entir. (606-538 B.C.)

“Aw lalber, nang hi lalte lal, vân Pathianin ram te, thiltihtheihna te, chakna te, ropuina te a pêka chu i ni a…rangkachak lu chu nang hi i ni.” Daniela 2:37,38.

A lu rangkachakin a lalram leh thuneihna a entir tih thu han hriat chu Nebukanezzara tân chuan thil lâwmawm a va ni dâwn tehlul êm!

Babulon ropui, rangkachak lalram, tihchhiat theih rual loh khawpui chu khawpui nghet ber leh chak berte zinga mi a ni a. Chu khawpui chunga rorêltu leh a chhûnga mi chêngte rêng rêngreng chuan chu khawpui chu tlâwm theiin an ring ngai lo a ni. Chu khaw kulh bang chhah lam chu hlam sawm dâwn lai a ni a, a sân lam pawh hlam 50 lai a ni. A khaw pum chu lei laih khuarin a hual chhuah vek avangin hmelma lak aṭangin a him tâwk hle a. Tin, kulh bang chhûng lamah chuan hmêlma kawng dâltu atan bang sâng pui a kual chhuak leh bawk a. Chu khawpui chu hmelmaten ṭâm chhuah tuma hun rei tak chhûng hual a nih thulh pawha invên nân a chhungah chuan kum sâwm chhung tla tawk ei tur chhêk khawl sa diam a ni bawk.

Kil li nei a ni a, a kil tin chu mel 56 zela sei a ni. Chhak lam leh thlang lam hawi zâwngin kawtthlêr 25 an kal tlang a; chhim leh hmar zâwngin chuti zat bawk chu an kal tlang sung bawk a. Nimahsela chu khaw ropui tak mai chu lalram dang thuneihna hnuaiah a la awm ang a, a la tlâwm dâwn a ni. (Jeremia 51:37, 26,58; Isaia 13:29, 20 te hi en la).

A âwm leh bân tangkaruain khawvêl lal ram pahnihna Medo-Persia chu an entir a. (539 – 331 B.C) Daniela 8:20 en la).

“Tin, a hnuah hian ram dang nang aia tenau zâwk a lo ding ang.” Daniela 32:39.

Babulon lalram ropui chu hnam dang kuta awm tûrin tihtlâwm a la ni ang a, chu lalram chu khawvêl zawng zawng chunga rorêltu pahnihna a ni ang tih thu han hriat chu lalber tân chuan a va beidawnthlâk dâwn êm!

Babulon lalram ropui tak hneh tûr chuan Pathianin Persia sipai hotu Kura chu a thlang chhuak a, ani chuan a sipaite hruaiin Babulon chu a va hneh ta a ni. Kura leh a hote chuan Babulon khawpui tan tlanga luang, Euphrates Lui chu khawpui pawn lamah khuapin, an luan sawntir a, tichuan Lui luanna hlui chu zawhin khawpui chhungah chuan an lût ta ruih ruih a, lui kama kawngkharahte chuan sipaite chu an lût ta chiam a.

A dull eh malpui dârin lalram pathumna, Grik lalram a entir a. (331-168 B.C)

“Tin, dâr, ram thumna, lei chung zawng zawnga rorêl tûr chu a awm le hang.” Daniela 2:39.

Kum nga chhûng lekin Alexander Ropuia chu tuma er ngam rual lohin hnehtu ropui takah a in siam a. A Grik sipai huaisen tak takte chuan Medo Persia sipaite chu an rawn do va (Grik sipai pakhat leh Medo Persia sipai sawmhnih inbei ang an ni a), intam hleih hle mah sela, Alexander Ropuia hote chuan an hneh ta zâwk a, tichuan khawvcêl pum puia thuneitu, lalram pathumna chu a lo ding chhuak ta a ni.

Grik lalramin Medo-Persia lalram chu a rawn luahlân ang tih chu hril lâwkna dangah pawh chiang takin kan hmu. (Daniela 8:20,21).

A ke thirin a entir chu lalram palina, lei chung zawng zawnga thuneitu lalram hnuhnung ber, Rom lalram a ni. (168 B.C 476 A.D)

“Tin, ram lina chu thir anga khauh a ni ang: thir chuan a tikehsawmin thil zawng zawng a tikeh ang bawkin ani chuan a tikehsawmin a râwt dip ang.” Daniela 2:40.

Rom lalram Baibl hril lâwkna a tihthlen tak kimzia chu khawvêl chanchin aṭangin chiang takin kan hmu thei: “Rangkachak te, tangkarua te, dâr te, lalram leh lalte entirtute chu Rom thîr lalram chuan a rawn titlâwm ta vek a.” – Gibbon’s History of the Decline and Fall of the Roman Empire, chap 38; para 1

Rom lalram chu lalram panganaina rawn hneh tur a ni ve lova, a kête chu thir, a ṭhen bellei an ni angin ram hrang hrangah ṭhen darh seuhin a awm ang. Tin, chung ram hrangte chu khawvêl tâwp thlenga awm turte an ni. Tin, a kephah leh a zungṭangte chu, a ṭhen belvawtu hlum, a ṭhen thir nia i hmuh ang ngei khan ram inṭhen a ni ang a, mahse amahah chuan thir khauhna chu a awm tho vang. thir leh hlum nêm inpawlh i hmuh ang ngei khân. Tin, a ke zungṭang a ṭhen thir, a ṭhen hlum a ni ang bawk khân ram chu a ṭhenah chuan a chak ang a, a ṭhenah chuan a chhe awl ang. Tin, thir leh hlum nêm inpawlh i hmuh ang ngei khan anni chu mihring chiin an inpawlh ang a; mahse thir leh hlum a inpawlh tak tak thei lo ang bawkin an inzawm reng lovang.” Daniela 2:41-43.

He Baibl hril lâwkna thlen famkim dikzia hi hmasâng leh tun lai Europe chanchin zirtute chuan chiang takin an hre thei ang. Heng lalram hrang hrang –Alemani (Germans) te Granchs (French) te, Burgundians (Switzerland leh Franch ṭhenkhat te, Visigoths (Spanish) te, Suevi (Portuguese) te, Anglo-Saxons (English) te, Lombards (Italians) te, leh a dang ṭhenkhatah te Rom lalram chu a inṭhendarh ta vek a ni. Tin, thir leh bellei a inbelh bawm nghet tak tak ngai lo angin hêng lalramte pawh hi an inzawm thei ngai lo vang a. Rom lalram chu pumkhata ṭhuikhâwm a nil eh tawh ngai lo vang.

Tun lai hun thleng hian hêng lalram hrangte hi an la inzawm khâwm thei ngai lova, inzawm khawm ahnehin innghirnghona leh indona hlir an kârah a la tla ta reng zawk a ni. Hun hrang hrangah heng mite hian thuneihna pumbil tumin, lalram pakhatah siam an tum tawh a, nimahsela hlawhtling ta rêng an awm lo.

  • Kum zabi 8-na - Charlamagne
  • Kum zabi 16-na - Charles V
  • Kum zabi 18-na - Louis XIV
  • Kum zabi 19-na - Napoleon
  • Kum zabi 20-na - Kaiser – Indopui 1, Hitler – Indopui 11

Hêngte hian Europe ram zawng zawng khu pum khata suihfin tumna an nei vek a, chutiang tumna chu a la hlawhchham zelzia tilangtu chauh an ni. Hruaitu ropui eltiang leh indonaa mihrâtkhawkhêng berte pawhin Europe ram hi hnehin inzawm khâwmna siamin emaw Europe hi lalram pakhata ṭhuikhâwm tumna a awm a ni thei e, amaherawhchu hril lâwkna THU PANGA lek, “AN INZAWM RENG LO VANG” tih hian a tâwpna tur chu a lo hril lâwk diam tawh a ni. Daniela 2:43.

“Europe Lalramte Insuihfinna” tura pâwl hrang tam tak ṭangrualin an beih chiamna te kha ngaihtuah the. An la hlawhchham zêl a ni!

Nupui pasal inneih pawlha inzawm khâwmna neih an han tum pawhin, hril lâwkna vêkin, “Nupui pasal an inneih pawlh ang a: nimahsela, an inzawm khâwm lo rêng rêng ang,” a lo tih tawh angin inzawm khâwmna rêng a hring chhuak thei chuang lo. Nupui pasal inneih pawlha Europe lalramte suih khawm tumna leh hlawhchhamna chu hetiang hian lo entir ila:

Denmark lal Christian IX fate:

  • Fapa Pakhat – Denmark rorêltu; Fanu pakhat – England lalnu (Edward VII nupui) Fapa dang – Grik lal; Fanu dang – Russia lal nupui.
  • Tin, England te, Russia te, Grik te, Norway te, Germany te leh Denmark ram roreltute hi unau an ni hlawm a; unau chiah ni lote pawh pate inunau tal an ni.
Lalram lo ding chhuak leh tûrah tunge rorêl ang?
“Tin, chûng lalte niah chuan van Pathianin ram a rawn din ang a, chu chu a chhe ngai tawh lo vang a, a lalna pawh mi dangte tân hnutchhiah a ni hek lo vang; hêng ram zawng zawngte hi a tikehsawmin a tiral zawk ang a, kumkhuain a ding reng ang.” Daniela 2:44

Pathian ram entirtu lung chuan milim chu a keah a zuk dêng a. He laic hâng thu tlêm tê hian Pathian chatuan lalram chu rawn din thuai a ni taw hang a, chu lalram chu din a nih hunah chuan thisen chhuahna tre leh indonate hi a awm tawh lo vang tih chiang takin an tarlang.

Chu lalrama Rorêltu tûr chanchin chu Baibl-ah mawi takin hetiangin ziak a ni:

“Kan tan naupang a lo piang a, fapa pêkin kana wm ta a; rorêlna chu a kokiah a chuan gang a; a hmingah chuan Maka, Remruattua, Pathian chaka Chatuan Pa, REMNA LAL an ti dâwn si a. Davida ṭhutphah chungah leh a ram chungah a rorêlna punzia leh remain tawpintai a nei lo vang, tinghet tûr leh tûn aṭanga chatuan thleng pawha rorêlna leh felnaa tungding turin.” Isaia 9:6,7.

Kum sangkhat leh zakua lai tawhah khan he khawvel tlan tur hian A lo kal a. Ama thisen ngeia A lei lalram chu rawn din turin “heng lalramte lal lai hian” A lo kal leh dawn a ni. Chutih hunah chuan “rorelna chu a kokiah a chuan gang a…chatuan thleng pawha rorelna leh felna tungding turin ro a rel tawh ang.

Tunah hian he hril lâwkna milim kezungṭang hunah hian kan chêng mêk a ni. A hma lam thil zawng zawngte chu dik tak leh mak takin an hunbi teah ṭheuh an thleng famkim zo ta. Tuna kan thlîr leh nghah tur chu khawvêl zawng zawng chunga Rorêltu REMNA LAL lo lan a ni.
