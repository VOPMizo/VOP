ZIRLAI SAWM LEH PARIATNA

Hremhmun Hi Engnge A Nih A, Khawiahnge A Awm?

Châng Thlan: “Pathianin khawvel a hmangaih êm êm a, chutichuan a Fapa mal neih chhun a pe a, amah chu tupawh a ring apiang an boral lohva chatuana nunna an neih zâwk nan.” Johana 3:16.

HE CHANG ropui tak hian thil pawimawh tak tak pahnih a târlang a. A hmasain, Isua Krista anmahni Chhandamtu anga pawmtute (ringtute) chu chatuan nunna pêk an ni ang. A dawt lehah chuan amah hnartute chu an boral thung ang. Tin, hmangaihnain Pathian chu chatuan nunna kawng a rawn siamtîr tih thu a sawi bawk. Pathian ruat ang chhandamna pawm duh lotute tihboralnaah pawh hmangaihna hi tihlan zêl a la nih tûrzia kan la hmu ang.

Hremhmun hi engnge a nih? Tunah hian a awm em? Tuna awm lo a nih chuan engtik aṭanga lo awm tûr nge a nih? Khawiahnge a awm ang? Engang nge ni ang? Engtia rei nge a awm ang?

HREMHMUN thu kan sawi rêng rêng hian mi tam zâwk chuan hmun râpthlâk leh ṭihbaiawm tak, tâwp rêng awm lova mei kâng hluah hluah zinga hrehawm tuarna – thih veleha chhandam tling loten chatuan hremna tuar tûra an kalna niin an ring ṭhin.

Mi sualten, he leia an thil sual tih ang zêla hrehawm tâwpkhâwk an tuarna tûr hmun nia ring an awm bawk.

He mi chanchin hi mi tinin kan hre châk ṭheuh va, Setana erawh chuan hrethiam zân tûrin min duh ngai lo. Pathian nungchang min entleutîr thei tawk chauhin hremhmun chanchinah hian ngaihdân nei tûrin min duh a, chutiang rilru nei tûr chuan mihringte hian thihtheih lohna an nei ve reng e, tiin, awlsam takin mite a thlem thlu ṭhin a ni.

Tun hma lama hrem hmun thua kan ngaihdan lo neih ṭhinte chu theihnghilhin chu mi thu Baibulin a zirtîrnate chu i lo en ang u. Kan zirna aṭang chuan zahngaihnaa khat Pathianin sual nuai bo nana rem a ruat chanchin te kan hmu thei nghe nghe ang.

A THU HMAN DAN KALHMANG HRIATNA

1. King James Version Baibul-a HREMHMUN (HELL) tih hi Hebrai ṭawngin SHEOL a ni a, chu mi awmzia chu THLÂN tihna a ni; nimahsela, vawi sawmthum leh pakhat lai HREMHMUN tia lehlin a nih avangin chhiartute chu an buai theiin a rinawm. Entîrnan:
“Ka thlarau Sheol (sap ṭawngah HELL) hnenah i hnutchhiah dawn si lova: I mi thianghlim hi ṭawihna hmuh i phal dawn hek lo.” Sam 16:10.

Hei hi chiang takin Isua phumna leh thawhlehna thu a ni a, SHEOL hi THLAN a ni.

Grik ṭawnga HADES, THLÂN tihna bawk hi HREM HMUN (HELL) tiin vawi sawm lehlin a ni bawk.

“Kumkhua tan ka nung leh ta a ni. Thihna leh mitthi Khaw chahbite chu ka nei bawk.” Thu puan 1:18. (Matthaia 11:23; 16:18; Luka 10:15 en bawk la.)

Jerusalem khaw daiah khuan kawr ruam bawlhhlawh tak, Hinnom ruam an tih, Gehena an tih bawk chu a awm a, chu kawr ruam chu khawpui bawlhhlawh leh ṭawkhnawk tinreng paih khawm nana hman a ni a. Bawlhhlawhte chu meiin an hl ṭhin a, a kâng bâng te chu a lung leh rannung dang dangten an tlan mur mur ṭhin a ni.[cite: 19] Chu kawr ruam chu HREMHMUN ENTIRTU atan hman a lo ni ta a. Grik ṭawnga Gehena chu Sap ṭawngah HELL tiin, vawi sawmpahnih lai lehin a ni.

“Mit khing hnih neia Gehenna meia paih ai chuan, mit khing khat neia nunnaa luh i tan a ṭha zawk e.”[cite: 19] Matthaia 18:1 (Matthaia 5:22, 29; 10:28; Luka 12:5 te hi en bawk la.)

Heng chang thu pawh hian mi ṭhenkhat chu a tibuai ṭhin. Sapṭawng Baibul, King James Version-a hremhmun HELL tihna, vawi sawmli leh pahnih lai lehlin hi, THLÂN (GRAVE) tia lehlin vek tur an ni zawk. Heng chang ṭhenkhat kan han tarlan takte hi hremhmun thu dik taka kan man fuh theih nan kan hriat phawt a ṭul a ni.

TUNAH HIAN MI ENGZÂT NGE HREMHMUNAH AWM MEK?

2. Isua Krista sawi angin mi tam zawkten eng kawng nge an zawh?
“Kawngka zîmah chuan lût rawh u; BORALNAA KALNA KAWNGKA CHU A ZAU VA, a kawng pawh a zau bawk a, CHU MI LAMA LUT CHU TAM PAWH AN TAM HLE A NI... nunnaa kalna kawngka chu a zim a, a kawng pawh a chêp bawk a, a hmutute pawh tlemte an ni.” Matthaia 7:13, 14.

Mi tam zawk te hian chhandamna atan hun an seng ngai lova, chatuan thilte an ngaihsak ngai hek lo. Kawng zawh nuam lam apiang an zawh ṭhin a ni. Isua, mihringte Chhamdamtu duhawm tak hi an ngaihthah a, an mantir a, krawsah hian an kheng bet a. MIPUITE hian chhiatna lam kawng an zawh fo ve reng a ni.

Zirtirna lâr tak anga thih veleha mi sualte hi hremhmuna paih nghal zêl ni selang chuan tuna KHAWVELA MI CHENGTE LÊT ENGEMAW ZÂT hian chatuan nghaisakna chu an tuar mek a ni ang.

3. Mi sualten hremna an tuar mêk lohzia Baibulin a zirtir thu eng angin nge Peteran a sawi?
“Nimahsela, tunah vante leh lei awmte hi chu mi thu avang vek chuan Pathian ngaihsak lo mite rorel leh boral ni atan khêkin, mei atan khâwl khâwm a ni tawh.” 2 Petera 3:7
“Fel lote chu rorel ni atan chuan hrem tura khek dan a hria.” (Sap ṭawnga a inziak anga lehlin) 2 Petera 2:9

Hei hian mi felloten tuna an tuar mêk lohzia leh la hrem tûra khêk an nih zâwkzia a tilang chiang hle a ni.

Zirlai sawmpanga naah khân rorelna thu kan zir tawh a, tin, he zirlai aṭang hian mi fel lote hremna hun a lo la thlen lohzia kan zir thung dawn a ni.

MI SUALTE HREMNA CHU NAKINAH

4. Mi sualte hremna chu lo la awm tur thil a nihzia engtingnge Isuan a sawi?
“Mihring fapa hi vântirhkoh nên, a Pa ropuinaa inthuamin a lo kal dawn si a; chu mi hunah chuan MITIN AN THILTIH ANG ZÊLIN A RÛL ṬHEUH VANG.” Matthaia 16:27.[cite: 19]
“Ka thu sawi kha, kha mi thu ngei khan, NI KIN NIAH CHUAN AMAH CHU THIAM LOH A LA CHANTIR ANG.” Johana 12:48.

Mi fel lote meia nghaisak thu Baibul-in a sawi reng rengte hi rorel zawhah, tawpna hun lo thlen hunah a ni tih a lang chiang êm êm a ni. Rorelna ni chu lo la thleng tûr thil a ni a, mi sualte pawh hrem an nih dan tûr rêl fel diam a nih hma chuan hrem an la ni rih lo a ni.

5. Chiang taka Isuan a sawi angin mi sualte chu engtikah nge tihboral an nih dawn?[cite: 19]
“Lo chu khawvel hi a ni a; chi ṭhate chu ram fate an ni a; buhlemte chu mi sual fate an ni a; hmêlma, a thehtu chu, diabola a ni a; buh seng chu khawvel tawpna tûr a ni; buh âttute chu vantirhkohte an ni.[cite: 19] Chutichuan, buhlemte chu an la khawm a meiin an hal ang hian, khawvel tawpnaah chuan a la awm ang.” Matthaia 13:38-40.

Hei aia chiang zawk hian a sawi theih chuang awm loh ve. Khawvel tawp hunah a ni mi sualte chu meia kan raltir an nih dawn ni.

6. Mi felte leh mi sualte chungchanga thil lo la thleng tûrte chu engte nge ni?
“Hêng hi mak ti suh u, a hun a thleng dâwn ta, chuti hunah chuan thlana awm zawng zawngin a aw an hria ang a, an chhuak vek ang; thil ṭha titute chu nunna changin an tho leh ang a, thil sual titute chu thiam loh changin an tho leh ang.” Johana 5:28, 29.

Thawhlehna ropui tak pawh hi nakin hun lama lo la awm tûr a ni. Mi felte chu thawhlehna hmasaah kaihthawhin an awm ang (Thupuan 20:5,6), MI SUALTE ERAWH CHU chuta ṭanga kum sangkhat hnuah, THAWHLEHNA HNUHNUNG ZAWKAH CHUAN KAIHTHAWH AN NI ANG (Thupuan 20:5). Mi sualte chuan tunah hian hrem hmunah an thiltih ang zelin tuar mek niin ring ila chuan Baibul thurin pawimawh tak tak pathum, rorelna lo la awm tûr te, Isua lo kal lehna te, thawhlehna te hi ṭûl lo leh awmze nei lovah kan ngaih a ṭûl thung ang. Entirnan: Kaina thihna kha kum sangnga lai a ni ta a. Mi tam takin an rin angin chutih chhung zawng chuan hremhmunah nghaisakin awm ni sela, tin, khawvel tawp hunah meia nghaisak tûr a nih leh nih loh fiah turin rorêlna hmaah hruai ni leh hnuhnawh si sela, thil mak tak ni lovang em ni? Kaina emaw, mi sual dangte emaw rêng rêng, hrem mêk an awm lo ve; van rorelna a famkim hma chuan an thlanah “khekin” an la awm rih vek zawk a ni.

HREM HMUN AWMNA

7. Mi sualten khawiahnge hrem an tuar ang?
“Lei vâng lam dâpin an kal chhova, mi thianghlimte riah hmun leh khaw duhawm tak chu an hual ta a; tin, van aṭangin mei a lo tla a, anni chu a kangral ta a.” Thupuan 20:9.
“Mi sualsualte chungah chuan chhiatna a surtîr ang a, mei leh kât leh thli lum tak hi an no chan tûr a ni ang.” Sâm 11:6

MI SUALTE CHU HE LEI NGEIAH HIAN KHAWVEL TAWP HUNAH TIHBORAL AN NI ANG tih Baibul-in a zirtirnate chu heng chang thu te pawh hian an rem thlap a ni.

“Mi fel mah leiah hian thungrulh an nih chuan, mi suaksual leh thil tisualtu chu eng ang taka nasa lehzual ni ang maw!” Thufingte 11:31.

MISUALTE MEIA TIHBORALNA

8. Mi sualte chu engti kauva sawisak nge an nih ang?
“Ngai teh u, ni chu a lo thleng e, rawhtuina mei angin a kang a ni; mi chapo zawng zawngte leh sual titu zawng zawngte chu buhpâwl an ni ang... zung leh zâr chenin a zuah lo vang.... mi suaksualte chu in rapbet ang a; ni ka ruatah chuan in kephah hnuaia meivâp an ni dawn si a, sipaihote Lalpa chuan a ti.” Malakia 4:1-3.

Meiin buhpawl ro a kan ral raih tawh ang maiin mi suaksualte chu mei vâpah an chang vek ang. Tawp ni rêng nei lovin meiin a kang dawn a ni lo ve, leia vaivutah hian an kîr leh zâwk dâwn a ni. (Thuhriltu 3:20)

9. Mi sualte meiin a kan ral hnuin mei chu a la kang reng ang em?
“Ngai teh, buhpawl ang mai an ni ang; meiin a kâng ang; meialh thiltihtheihna lakah chuan an inchhanhim lo vang; intihlumna tûr meihawl ang a ni lovang a; ai tur mei ang pawh a ni hek lo vang.” Isaia 47:14.
10. Mi sualte hnuhmâ zawng zawng chu eng tinnge a la awm ang?
“Mi suaksualte erawh chu an boral ang a, Lalpa dotute pawh phul hnim duah ang an ni ang: tihboralin an awm ang a, meikhuah an zâmral ang.” “Rei lo tê achinah mi suaksual chu a awm tawh dawn si lo; a ni, a awmna hmun chu en en mah la, a awm tawh lo vang.” Sam 37:20, 10.

Mi sualte hnuhmâ zawng zawng chu eng tinnge a la awm ang?

“Mi suaksualte erawh chu an boral ang a, Lalpa dotute pawh phul hnim duah ang an ni ang: tihboralin an awm ang a, meikhuah an zâmral ang.” “Reilotê achinah mi sualsual chu a awm tawh dawn si lo; a ni, a awmna hmun chu en en mah la, a awm tawh lovang.” Sam 37:20, 10

Mi sualte kangraltu meikhu chu boruak zau takah hian kîr leh tawh lo tûrin a zâmral ang. Mi sualte hnuhma zawng zawng chu a bo vek ang a, an awm rêng rêng tawh lo vang.

MEI MIT THEI LO TIH AWMZIA

11. He mei hi eng mei nge vuah a nih?
“Chu mi zawhah chuan, a vei lama mite hnenah pawh chuan, ‘Ânchhe dawngte u, KUMKHAW mei diabola leh a tirhkohte tan an buatsaihah chuan mi kal bo san rawh u....’ a ti a.” Matthaia 25:41. “Sodom leh Gomorrah, an kiang vela khuate nên, chutiang bawkin... chatuan meia hremna tuar tûrin, hriattîr nâna târ chhuahin an awm ang khân.” Juda 7.
“Mei mit thei lova kal ai chuan piangsuala nunna a luh i tan a ṭha zawk e.” Marka 9:43.

Kumkhaw mei chuan Sodom leh Gomorra chu a kangral raih a ni. Tin, Juda 7-a kan chhiar angin Sodom leh Gomorra tichhiatna chu “chatuan meia hremna tuar turin, hriattîr nâna târ chhuah” a ni tih chiang takin a lang. Mei pui kan chhûng chu “hun rei lo te” chhûng (Jeremia Ṭah Hla 4:6) nimahsela, a thawh chhuah emaw, a hnutchhiah emaw chu chatuan atân a ni ang. Chutiang chu a nih avângin mi suaksualte chu tihboral seuh a ni ang a, chu mi zawhah a kangraltu mei pawh a rêm tawh ang tih kan hre thei.

“Mit thei lo” tih a nihna chhan chu a kan ral leng hma chuan engtikawng maha tihmih rual a nih loh vâng a ni. Jerusalem chu tunah hian a kâng tawh lova, nimahsela, Jerusalem kangtu mei chu Pathianin, “tihmih a ni ngai lovang” tiin A sawi si a ni. (Jeremia 17:27). Tihmih a ni lo rêng a, engkim a kan ral hnu khan a mit ta mai a ni. Chutiang bawkin mi suaksualte chu tih boral dêr an ni ang tih chu a lang fiah em em a ni. Thihna leh thlante chu an tâwp hlen vang vang tawh ang.[cite: 19]

TÂWP HLEN DERNA – THIH HNIHNA

12. Mi suaksualteah chuan, an thih hnu pawh a la awm zui zel tur, thlarau a awm reng em?
“Taksa tihluma, thlarau tilum thei lotute chu hlau suh ula, Gehenna-a THLARAU LEH TAKSA TIBORAL THEITU erawh chu hlau zawk ang che u.” Matthaia 10:28.

Misualte tihboral an nih hunah chuan “thlarau leh taksa” nen tihboral an ni ang a, a bâng rêng rêng hnutchhiah a awm lo vang.

13. Mi sualte tawp hlenna tûr chu engnge ni?
“Thiltisualtu apiang an thi ang.” Ezekiela 18:4, 20.
“Sual man chu thihna a ni si a.” Rom 6:23
“A hnehtu apiang thih hnihna chuan engmah a tih lo vang.” Thupuan 2:11.

Thih hmasakna chu sual avânga lo awm, mi ṭhate leh misualte chunga ang khat veka lo thleng a ni. Amaherawhchu, mi sualte thungrulhna tak tak tûr chu meia tihboralna – THIH HNIHNA – chu mi aṭanga thawlehna rêng rêng awm tawh lohna chu a ni.

14. A chhandam duh lotute hnenah engtinnge Pathianin a hmangaihna a lantir ang?
“Piang lo ang maiin an awm ang.” Obadia 16.

Pathian Lehkhabuin min hrilh angin misualte chu buhpâwl ro ang mai te (Nahuma 1:9, 10), buh si ang te (Matthaia 3:12), a pêng ro ang te (Johana 15:6), hnimhlingnei leh buar ang te (Hebrai 6:8), thing leh hnim ang tein (1 Kor. 3:12) tihboral an ni ang. Kumkhua atan tihboralin an awm ang a (Sâm 92:7), an mang vek ang. (Sâm 9:6)

Aw, Pathian zahngaihna chu a va nasa êm! Kawng bote tuarna zawng zawng chu A tihtawpsak ang a, na tuar rêng rêng a awm tawh lo vang. Chu chu, mihring ngaihtuah chhuah ve, chhandam ni loten thih veleha chatuan hremna tuar nghal anga an rin dan nên han khaikhin teh u. Mihring siam chhuah rin dân hi zawng pawm hleih theih a ni lo ve. Chutiang rin dan ang chuan hmâsânga chhandam tling lova thi tate chuan khawvel tâwp huna thite aiin kum sâng tam takin an lo tuar chuang bik tihna a ni ang.[cite: 19]

Mi ṭhenkhatte chuan mi dang aiin an tuar nasa zawk dawn chuan a lang a, amaherawhchu, hremna chu kumkhuaa awm reng tûr a ni lo va, tawp hun nei tûr a ni, “piang lo ang maiin an awm ang” tih a ni si.

15. Pathianin mi suaksualte chunga A rorêl dân hnuhnung ber chanchin engtingnge A sawi?
“A hna, a hna danglam chu, a thawh theih nân leh, a thiltih, a thiltih danglam chu, a hlen theih nân Lalpa chu, Perazim Tlânga a tho ang khan a tho dâwn si a, Gibeon ruama a thinur ang khân a thinur dâwn a ni.” Isaia 28:21.

Krista chuan A chhandamna pawm duh lotute a tihboral hun tûr – “A hna, a hna danglam chu, a thiltih, a thiltih danglam” a hlen hun tûr chu rilru ngui chungin a ni a thlir reng ni. Tidamtu ngilnei chuan he khawvêl aṭang hian sualna panchhia hi a la la kiang ngei tûr a ni.

Chanchin buin mi sual pakhat hremna tuar chanchin ngaihnawm tak a ziak i lo en teh ang u. Misual tihlumtu tûr chu misual awmna rip chhûngah chuan a va lût a, misual lung in tâng hmel a va hmuh chuan a thin a ṭhâwng zawk mai a, a hnênah chuan, “Jim, nang lah ni reng mai che,” a va ti a. Lung in tâng chuan, “Frank, nang i ni maw le?” tiin, a lo chhang ve a. Naupan têt aṭanga inṭhian fo an ni a, nimahsela, an kawng zawh chu a inpersan nasa hle mai. Frank chuan a ṭhian thi mai tûr nunna chu zuah tûrin a han ngên chiam a, nimahsela, a ṭhian Jim chuan, “Frank, min tihlum ngei tûr a lâwm. Ka phu rengin ka thiam lo a ni. Min tihhlum avangin keiin emaw, mi dangin emaw kan dem thei lo vang che. Ka nunna lak hi i tih tûr a ni e.” tiin, a hrilh a ni.

“Lalpa chuan, ‘mi tinreng ka hmaah an kûn ang a, lei tinreng pawh Pathian hnenah a inpuang ang,’ a ti a,” Rom 14:11

Pathian chuan A thutak chanchin hriatna hun ṭha nei reng sia hawisantute tih loh chu tumah a tiboral lovang. Chung mi sual, chhandamna dawng lote tihboral chu Pathian tân thiltih danglam tak, lawmna rêng a neih lohna chu a ni.

“Mi suaksual thihnaah hian lâwmna rêng ka nei lo va, mi suaksualin a awm dân a kalsana a nun hi ka lawmzâwng a ni zawk. I awm dân ṭha lo aṭang chuan kir r'u, kir r'u ... engahnge in thih ang le?” Ezekiela 33:11.

Isua Krista chaun mi zawng zawng – a chhandamna pawm duhtute zawng zawng chhandam a tum tlat a, amaherawhchu, mihringte duhthlan theihna hi engmah lovah a ngaihsak thei lova, chhandam ni turin a tilui thei hek lo.

THIL SIAM THAR

16. Sual leh a hnuhma zawng zawngte hi engtia nuai bo vek nge an nih ang?
“THIL BULTE CHU MEIA EMRALIN A AWM TAWH ANGA, lei leh a chhunga hnathawh awmte hi A KANG RAL ANG.” 2 Petera 3:10.
“Ngai teh u, van tharte leh lei thar ka siam a nih hi; tun hma thilte chu hriat rengin a awm tawh lo vang a, rilruah pawh a lut tawh hek lo vang.” Isaia 65:17.

Sual bawlhhlawh tling khawm zozai hi meiin a kângrâl thuai tawh ang a, tin, sual a awm leh theih tawh loh nân Pathianin a thil siamte hi a hum tawh ang.

“Tawp dêrna a thlentîr dâwn a ni; hrehawm vawi hnihna a thleng lo vang.” Nahuma 1:9.

Tunah chuan “Hrem Hmun Hi Engnge A Nih a, Khawiahnge A awm?” tih thua Baibul zirtirna chu i hmaah kan rawn pharh ta a. He lei ngei hi ni kin niah chuan em tui dûl dûlin a awm ang a, misualte leh an hnuhma zawng zawngte chu nuai bo vekin a la awm ang. He lei, sual tak aṭanga tlenfai hi thuhnuairawlhte chuan an rochung tawh ang. Chutichuan, chhandam fate leh chhandam lohte chu he lei ngeiah hian an lawmman pek an ni dawn a ni. (Thufingte 11:31)

Ṭhian duh tak, lei chung leh a chhûnga mihringte hnêna hêng thil lo thleng tûr atan hian inbuatsaiha, inpeih a mamawhzia kan rawn hriat nawntîr leh duh a che. Heng chhiatna, ni hnuhnunga lo thleng tûr lak aṭang hian pumpelhna kawng a awm e. Isua hi, chu Kawng chu a ni a, Amahah chuan himna a awm a, tin, Amahah chuan chatuan nunna a awm a, Remna leh Muanna ram panna kawng a ni.

Tuna hun ṭha i neih lai hian Pathian chu hmêlhriatin pâwl la, zahngaihna kawng chu hawnin a la awm reng e. Amaherawhchu Khar thuai a ni tawh ang. Sual simte âuthâwm chu Pathianin a la ngaithla reng a, Isua thisen chuan sualte chu vûr âi pawha vâr zawkin a la tlêngfai thei reng bawk a ni. I muang sual hlauh dah ang e, tunah, tunah la la hian i sualte nên Pathian chu rawn pan la, A inhlanna chu pawm la, A thu ring la, tichuan Ani'n i sual zawng zawng ngaidamin lâwm takin A lo pawm dawn che nia.[cite: 19]
