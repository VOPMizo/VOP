ZIRLAI SAWM PAHNIHNA

INPÊKNA KIMAH HNEHNA A AWM

Châng Thlan: “Miin khawvêl hi a pumin nei sela, a nun chân si sela, a tân enge sâwt ang? A nih loh leh, miin a nun aiah enge a pêk ang?” Matthaia 16:26.

Tuifinriat zau taka lawngpûte, thim chah mupin a hual vêlte leh, an kalna lam tûr kawhhmuhtu neih chhun, chhim leh hmar kâwktu chhe vek tawhte rilru chu nasa takin a hahin a mangang ṭhin. Pan lam tûr an hriat theih nân thimna chuan kian zai rêl thuai thuaiin, vâna arsite chu lo de leh chuai tawh selang an ti hliah hliah ṭhin a ni.

Chutianga tuifinriata kawng bo ai pawha la râpthlâk zâwk chu nunna tuipuia bo hi a ni. A râpthlâkzia chu kan hre thiam kher lo pawh a ni ang e, nimahsela, vân hmun min hruai thleng theitu, nunna Lalpa aṭanga pên bo hi thil râpthlâk nasa tak a ni.

He zirlai hian mi zawng zawngin KAWNG AN BO tawhzia leh, mihring zawhna ropui ber–‘Kawng bo tawhte chu engtiangin nge chhandam an nih leh theih ang?’ tih chhânna a rawn târlang dâwn a. Chuvângin, i kawngte chhun êngtu, thutak êngin buaina chhumpui a rawn chhêm kian sak che avângin, i thinlung chu lâwmnain lo khat sela tih kan duhsak che a ni.

Pathian Lehkhabu zirtîrna angin KAWNG BO hian enge awmzia a neih?
“Abrahama te, Isaaka te, Jakoba te leh zâwlnei zawng zawngte chu Pathian ram chhûnga awm in hmuh a. NANGNI PAWNA HNAWH CHHUAH in nih in inhriat hunah, chutah chuan ṬAH LEH HA ṬHIAL chu a awm ang” Luka 13:28.

He châng thu hian an Chhandamtu leh siamtuin, an tâna thil a lo zam diam tawh chang tlâka khawsa lo zawng zawngte hnêna, lungngaihna sawi hleihtheih loh lo thleng tûr chanchin min hriattîr a ni.

Mihringin A Dinhmun Derthâwnzia A Hre Tûr A Ni

Kawng boin enge a hriat tûr?
“Mi rethei ka va ni tehlul êm! He thihna taksa lakah hian tuin nge min chhan chhuak ang?” Rom 7:24.

Mahniin a inchhan chhuak thei lo tih a hre tûr a ni.

“ETHIOPIA MI HÂNGIN A VUN A TIDANGLAMIN KEITÊIN A ZIA A THUP THEI EM NI? Chuti a nih chuan nangni thil sual tih chîng ṭhînte pawhin thil ṭha in ti thei ang chu!” Jeremia 13:23.

Kan nihna hi a ṭhat lohzia hriain, sualin min hruai bona aṭanga kan beiseina neih chhun chu kan tâna Pathianin a lo ruat, Chhandamtu zawn chhuah a ni tih hi kan hre tûr a ni. Damlote pawhin doctor an pan hmain an dam lo a ni tih an inhre hmasa ṭhîn.

“Isuan chu chu a lo hria a, an hnênah chuan, ‘Mi chakte chuan doctor an ngai lo va, mi bawrhsâwmte zâwkin an ngai a ni; mi fel takte lam turin lo kal ka ni lova, mi sualte zâwk lam tûra lo kal ka ni,’ a ti a.” Marka 2:17.

Miin tuiah a tlã a ni tih a inhriat chuan amah chhan tumtute chu hrilh ngai lovin lo man a tum ṭhîn.

Nunna Hlutzia

Isua Krista'n nunna hlutzia eng angin nge a sawi?
“Miin khawvêl hi a pumin nei sela, a nun chân si sela, a tân enge sâwt ang? Miin a nun aiah enge a pêk theih ang?” Marka 8:36, 37.

Isuan chhandam kan nih theih nân A nunna pêk a huamzia aṭangin kan nunna hi engti kauva ngai hlu tur nge kan nih kan hre thei. Sualna avânga kan hloh tawh zawng zawngte min leikîrsak leh nân man min pêk ve tûr chungchânga rawn inrawlh ve theihna a nei a ni.

Mihringin zawhna a zawh theih azawnga ropui ber enge ni?
“CHHANDAMA AWM TÛRIN ENGE KA TIH TÛR?” Tirhkohte 16:30.

A awmzia hi a chiang tâwk hle a. Tisa chhiatna laka chhandamna ni lovin, sual ânchhe lak aṭanga chhandamna hmu tûrin miin enge a tih tûr tih a kâwk zâwk a ni.

Chhânna Duhawm Berte

He zawhna ropui tak hi Pathian Lehkhabuin eng angin nge a chhân?
“LALPA ISUA RING LA, TICHUAN CHHANDAMIN I AWM ANG,” Tirhkohte 16:31.
Isuan a hnathawh ber nia a sawi chu enge ni?
“BOTE ZAWNGA CHHANDAM tûrin Mihring Fapa hi a lo kal a ni rêng a ni,” Luka 19:10.

He mi thu bawk hi tirhkoh Paulan a lehkha thawnah a sawi chhuak leh a:

“KRISTA ISUA MISUALTE CHHANDAM TÛRIN KHAWVÊLAH HIAN A LO KAL tih hi a rinawmin a pawm tlâk êm êm a ni; an zînga mi sual ber ka nih hi.” 1 Timothea 1:15.

Lalpa Isua Kristaa rinna awmzia hi chiang taka kan hriat thiam a mamawh êm êm a, chu mi thu chu he zirlaiah hian rawn hrilhfiah kan tum a ni.

Chhandamnaa Thil Pathumte

Chhandamnaa thil pathum awmte chu engte nge?

a) Sual avânga thiam loh channa la kiang tûra Pathian thil ruahman, chu mi pawmna azâra THIAM CHANTÎRNA; (b) Sual thiltihtheihna la kiang tûra Pathian thil ruahman azâra TIHTHIANGHLIMNA (c) Isua lo kal zawha sual ânchhia hlih kian a zâra ropui taka tihdanglamna. Ropuina thu hi chu zirlai dangah zir a la ni ang.

Thiamchantîrna

Engtianga mihringte hi thiam chantîr nge an nih?
“Chuvângin, RINNA AVÂNGA THIAM CHANGA awmin, kan Lalpa ISUA KRISTA AVÂNGIN Pathian nên inremin i awm ang u,” Rome 5:1.

Pathian inrema mihring a awm theihna emaw, thiam chantîrna a nih theihna kawng emaw sialtu chu Isua Krista thihna a ni.

“Hmêlma kan nih lai maha a Fapa THIHNA avânga Pathian nena lo inrem tawh kan nih si chuan, inrema kan awm tawh hnu hian A NUNNA AVÂNGIN CHHANDAMIN kan awm ngei ang.” Rom 5:10.

Mihring mahni insiam ṭhat theihna neiin Pathian dân pawh zâwm teh rêng mahsela, sualna LO AWM TAWH chuan thiam loh a chantîr ta rêng rêng a. Chutichuan, kan bawhchhiatna zawng zawng chu Isuan a chungah a nghat ta zâwk a, Kalvari kraws chungah thihna tuarin kan bat chu min tlâk sak ta a ni.

Rinnaa Krista chu CHHANDAMTUA kan pawm hian kan sualte chu, a thihna avângin, felnain a rawn hliah khuh a. Eng anga sual nasa pawh lo ni tawh mah ila, Krista nungchangin kan nungchang hi a rawn hliah khuh a, tichuan sualna nei ngai rêng rêng lo ang maiin Pathian hmaah kan ding thei ta a ni. Hei hi RINNA AVÂNGA THIAM CHANNA, Krista felna keimahni felna anga ruatna chu a ni a, vân ram thlenna tûra phalna a ni.

He rahka hmasa ber hian kan nihna khawih danglam chuang lovin, Pathian hmaa kan dinhmun a rawn tidanglam a. Chuvâng chuan vân rama lût thei turin kan awm dante hi SIAM ṬHAT a ni phawt tûr a ni.

“Inngaihhmang leh mi bawlhhlawh . . . rêng rêng chuan ... Krista leh Pathian ramah chuan chan rêng an nei lo a ni tih in hre hle si a.” Efesi 5:5. “Thianghlimna lovin tuman Lalpa an hmu lovang.” Hebrai 12:14.
Tihthianghlima kan awm theih nân Pathianin eng thil nge min ruat sak?
“Unau, Lalpâ duh takte u, thlarau tihthianghlim leh thutak awiha chhandama awm turin a tir ata Pathianin a thlan che u avângin, in chung thuah hian Pathian hnênah lâwmthu hril fo tûr rêng kan ni.” 2 Thesalonika 2:13.

Kan mizia rawn siam danglam a, rawn tithartu chu Thlarau Thianghlim, Pathian Mi Pathumte zînga Pakhat hi a ni. Chu siam tharna min rawn thlen sak chu piantharna kan tih hi a ni.

“Isuan a chhâng a, a hnênah, “Tih tak meuhvin, tih tak meuhvin ka hrilh a che, Mi tupawh (chung lam aṭanga) an pianthar loh chuan, Pathian râm an hmu thei lovang,” a ti a.' Johana 3:3.

Siamtharlehna

He thil bawk hi Paulan eng angin nge a sawi ve?
“Chu mi avâng chuan tupawh KRISTAA a awm chuan, THIL SIAM THAR a lo ni a (emaw, siam tharna a lo awm a, A.R.V); thil hluite chu an ral ta a; ngai teh u, a lo thar ta.” 2 Korin 5:17.

He thil pawimawh tak hi Paulan mihring nunphung rêng nêna a danglamzia min hrilh a, ‘Mihring hlui leh mihring thar’ tiin a vuah a ni.

“Chutichuan, in taksa pêng leia awmte hi tihlum rawh u, inngaihna te, bawlhhlawhna te, hurna te, châkna sual te, duhâmna te hi (duhâm chu mi lem biakna a ni si a;)... tauhna te, thinurna te, huatna te, sawichhiatna te, in kaa chhuak, ṭawng zahmawhte hi tibo rawh u, dâwt inhrilh tawn suh u; MIHRING HLUI chu a thiltihte chawpin in HLÎPTHLA TAWH a, MIHRING THAR chuan in inthuam tawh si a, chu mihring thar chu, hriat famkimna nei tûrin, a siamtu anpui anga siam tharin a awm mêk a.” Kolosa 3:8-10.

Thlarau Thianghlim thiltihtheihna nasa tak chuan ringtuteah “Pathian anpui nihna” a siamsak ṭhîn rêng a ni. Kan nunphung min siam danglam sak a. Chu chu keimahniah hian Krista a lo nung ta tihna a ni.

“Krista hnênah khenbehin kan awm ta; nimahsela, ka nung a ni; keimah erawh chu ka ni tawh lo, KRISTA CHU KEIMAHAH A NUNG ZÂWK A NI; tûnah tisaa ka nung hi rinnaa nung ka ni–Pathian Fapa, mi hmangaiha ka aia inpetu, rinnaah chuan.” Galatia 2:20.
“A nunna avângin chhandamin kan awm ngei ang” Rom 5:10.

Rinnain Isua chu kan thinlung chhûngah hian a lo chêng a, A nunna chuan min rawn fan chhuak a, tichuan A felna chu kan hnênah pêk a ni ṭhîn. Hei hi min tithianghlimtu chu a ni a, vân ram thleng tlâka min siamtu chu A felna, kan hnêna pêka awm chu a ni.

Bible Piantharna Leh Thil Hrang Hranga Thlarau Inlet Kuala Rrinna

11. Bible thurina piantharna leh thil hran hrana thlarau inlet kual chhâwnga rinnain eng danglamna nge an neih?

Thurin pakhat chuan, mi a lo thihin mihring vêkah a nih loh pawhin, a dam chhûnga a sual leh ṭhat a thuin, thil dangah a piang leh a. Chutiang chuan, thil hrang hrangah a inlet kual chhâwng zêl niin a zirtîr a. Nun hmasak bera, lâwmman beiseina rêng rêng nei lo khawpa nun ṭhaa nung thei tân erawh chuan, pian nawn ngai lova chhandamna (moksha) hmuh nghâl theih angin, a zirtîr bawk.

Pathian lehkhabu zirtîrna ang chuan mihring hi SUAL leh SETANA BAWIH A NI.

“Tih tak meuhvin, tih tak meuhvin ka hrilh a che u, tupawh thil tisual apiang, sual bawih a ni.” Johana 8:34.
“Mi ngamtu apiangin, chu mi ngei chuan bawihah a siam ṭhîn a ni.” 2 Petera 2:19.

Pathian chungchâng ngaihdân a dik loh avângin sual a lo awm a, chuvângin, Pathian chungchâng ngaihdân dik takin sual bawih aṭanga zalênna min pe thei.

“Hei hi chatuanna nunna a ni, nang Pathian tak chauh leh i tirh Isua Krista hriat hi.” Johana 17:3.

Sual hrênga tângten zalênna an neih theihna kawng pakhat chauh a awm:

“Fapain bawih a bân che u chuan, in chhuak tak meuh ang.” Johana 8:36.

Thil hran hrana thlarau inlet ṭhîna rinna hian sual awm dân tak hi engah mah a ngai lova, mihringin chhandam beiseina a neih chu a lâk bosak bawk tih kan hre thei.

Pathian Lehkhabuin min hrilh ang chuan sual bawih aṭanga mihringin zalênna a hmuh theihna kawng chu piantharna – Thlarau thianghlim zara – tunah leh damchunga lo awm hi a ni. Chu chuan sual thiltihtheihna a rawn tichhe si a.

“Krista Isuaa Nunna Thlarau dân chuan sualna leh thihna dân ata chu mi tichhuak tawh si a.” Rom 8:2.

Iuan Min Chhandamna chu a Famkim a Ni.

“Chutichuan, Pathian hnêna lo kalte chu a tâwp khâwk thlengin a chhandam thei bawk a ni, anmahni ṭawngṭaisak tûrin chatuana a nun reng avângin.” Hebrai 7:25.
“Tichuan, IN TLÛK LOH NÂNA VÊNG THEI che u...” Juda 24.

Inpumpêkna Famkim

12. He piantharna hi engtia neih theih nge?
“Nangni pawh in SIM LOH chuan chutiang bawkin in boral ang.” Luka 13:3.
“SIM ULA, in sual ngaihdam nân Isua Krista hmingin BAPTISMA chang ṭheuh rawh u; tichuan, Thlarau Thianghlim pêk in hmu ang.” Tirhkohte 2:38.

Simna neih chhan hi misualin a hre sual tûr a ni lo. PIANTHARNA lovin tuman Pathian ram an thleng lo vang a, SIMNA LOVIN tuman piantharna an nei thei hek lo. Chuvângin, simna awmzia hi chiang taka hre thiam tûrin tih tak zetna neiin i zir ang u.

13. Sim tûra mite hruai ṭhîntu chu enge ni?
“Pathian ṭhatnain simna tûrin a hruai ṭhîn che tih hre lovin, a ṭhatna ngahzia leh, a entheihzia leh a dawhtheihzia te chu i ngainep em ni?” Rom 2:4.

Kraws hmaa misual a din a, chutah chuan hmangaihna inlâr chhuak ropui tak a han hmuh meuh chuan Pathianin ṭhatna a ngahzia a hmu chhuak a, a nunah chuan ‘Pathian duhzâwng anga lungngaihna’ a lo awm ṭhîn a ni. Sualin thil a rawn thlen avâng a lungngai a ni lova, thil sual a TIH avâng zâwka lungngai a ni, a thil sual tih chuan SIAMTU leh Tlantu thinlung a chhun sak si a. Pathian rorêlna kalha hel a ni tih a inhre chhuak a, tichuan, hel tawh lova Pathian thu zâwm tawh zâwk tûrin Pathian hnênah a intulût a, inpumpêkna famkim a siam ta a, Davida angin:

“Ka sualnate lakah hian i hmai thup la, ka khawlohna zawng zawng hi thai bo vang che. AW Pathian KEIMAHAH HIAN THINLUNG THIANGHLIM TAK SIAM LA: Ka kaw chhûngah hian thlarau dik tak siam thar leh ang che. I hnên ata hi mi paih bo suh la; I thlarau thianghlim hi mi lâk sak suh ang che: tiin a au chhuak thei ta a ni. Sâm 51:9-11.

Tehkhin thua fapa tlân bo angin a nun hun tam tak chu engmah lo atân lo hmang ral tawh mah sela:

“KA THO VANG A, ka pa hnênah ka kal ang a, a hnênah, ‘Ka pa, vana mi chungah leh nangma mithmuhin thil ka tisual e; i fapaa vuah tlâk ka ni ta love, i hnêna inhlawhfa pakhat angin mi siam ve rawh’ ka ti ang,” tiin a au chhuak ta zâwk a ni. Luka 15:18, 19.

Lungngaihna dik tak nêna misualin Pathian a pan leh chuan, lo lâwm tûrin Pathian a ngen chiam pawh a ṭûl love, he tehkhin thu hian sual simte Pathianin a lawm dân a târlang chiang hle a ni.

“Chutichuan, hla taka a la awm laiin a pain a lo hmu a, a KHAWNGAIH ÊM ÊM A, A TLÂN A, a ir a chuktuah a, A FÂWP NGAWIH NGAWIH A... A pa chuan a bawihte hnenah, ‘PUAN ṬHA BER kha han la thuai ula, han sintîr rawh u; a kutah zungbun te, a kephahah pheikhawk te buntîr rawh u,’ a ti a” Luka 15:20, 22.

Chutiang bawkin Pathianin sual sim lehte chu an sual khuh tûrin Krista felna puan a sintîr a. Miin Krista hnêna inpumpêkna a han neih hian a thinlung thar chu thiltihtheihna tharin a rawn thunun a, ama theihna a tih theih rual loh chu a chungah a lo thleng ṭhîn. A theih bâk chham, thiltihtheihna ropui tak chu a nunah a lo lang ta ṭhin a ni. An thinlung Krista hnena hlânte rêng rêng chu Krista kulhbîng, he khawvêl buai nuk nuk kâra a chelh rengte an ni. Chu kulhbîngah chuan Krista chauh lo chu tumah dang thuneitu an awm lo. Chuvangin, Setana pawhin nasa takin rûnin suam ṭhin mah sela, vân thiltihtheihna kuta awm a nih avângin a chim ngai lo vang. Krista felna rinna azâra thinlunga Krista chêntîr rengna hi sual laka invên him dân kawng awm chhun a ni.

Sual Puanna, Dintharna leh Ngaihdamna

Pathian duhzâwng anga lungngaihna-simna a awm tak zet leh tak zet loh hriatna enge ni?
“Bawhchhiatna zêptute chu an hmuingil lo vang a, zêp lova inpuanga hawisantute chuan zahngaihna an hmu ang.” Thufingte 28:13.

Pathian chauh kalha sualnate chu Pathian hnênah puan tur a ni a, midangte chung a thil tihsualte chu chûng mite hnênah ngei chuan puan tûr a ni.

“In sualte thupha chawi tawn ula, in ṭawngṭai sak tawn ṭhîn rawh u.” Jakoba 5:16.

Sual puanna rêng rêngte chu an chiangin an fiah tûr a ni; kan tihsual ngei chu kan puang tûr a ni. Pêk kîr leh ṭûl a awm chuan, (tih theih thlâwt loh a nih loh chuan) pêk kîr leh ngei ni rawh se.

Sual simna chu sual puanna leh pêk kîr lehnain a zui chuan sual ngaihdamna pawh tiam a ni.

“Kan sualte thupha kan chawi chuan, kan sualte NGAIDAM tûr leh, FEL LOHNA ZAWNG ZAWNG thlêngfai tûrin amah chu a rinawmin a fel a ni.” 1 Johana 1:9.

Tih ṭûlte kan tih phawt chuan ngaihdamna thutiam chu Pathianin a rawn hlen zêl ṭhin a, a thutiam kawngah tlin lohna rêng a nei ve ngai si lova. Kan sualte thupha kan chawi ang zêlin ngaihdamna min pe ṭhîn a ni.

Mi Zawng Zawng Hnêna Kohna

15. Misualin tih ṭûlte a tih hnuin a bawlhhlawhnaten ngaihdamna dawng tlâk lovah an la siam che u em?
“In sualte chu sen lâr angin awm tawh mah sela, vûr angin a vâr ang; sen duk angin awm tawh mah sela, berâm hmul angin a awm ang.” Isaia 1:18.
Isua kohnain huam phâk lohte a nei rêng em?
“NANGNI THAWKRIM LEH PHURRIT PHUR ZAWNG ZAWNGTE u, ka hnênah lo kal ula, keiman ka chawlhtîr ang che u. Ka nghâwngkawl bât ula, ka hnênah zir rawh u; thuhnuairawlh leh rilrua inngaitlawm tak ka nih hi; tichuan, in thlarau tân chawlhna in hmu ang. Ka nghâwngkawl chu a nuam a, ka phur pawh a zâng a ni.” Matthaia 11:28-30. “TUPAWH KA HNÊNA LO KAL CHU KA HNAWT CHHUAK TAWP LO ANG.” Johana 6:37.

Zirtu duh tak, Isua Krista chu nangmah Chhandamtu angin i pawm tawh em? Misualte châkkhai petu a ni a, midang tumahin an pe thei lo. Sual leh sual avânga thil lo awmte hi khawvêl ram tina an darh avângin Chhandamtu an awm thliah thluah tûr a tihna ni lo ve. “Pathianin khawvel a hmangaih êm êm a. Chutichuan a Fapa mal neih chhun a pe a, amah chu tupawh a ring apiang an boral loh va, chatuana nunna an neih zâwk nân.” Johana 3:16. Amah chu tûnah hian i thlang dâwn lo'm ni?
