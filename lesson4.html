ZIRLAI PALINA

PATHIAN RÊLDIKNA DÂN DANGLAM NGAI LO

Châng Thlan: “Lalpâ dân chu a ṭha famkim a, nunna a siam ṭha ṭhîn,” Sâm 19:7.

Pathian Lehkhabu pum puiah hian thu mal (Sâp ṭawngah) 773,746 a awm a. Chûng zawng zawng chu Pathian thâwkkhuma pêk vek ni mah sela, Amah ngeiin A ziak chu thu 297 emaw chauh an ni. Chûngte chu Exodus 20:3-17-ah kan hmu a, ṭhatna dân – thu sâwm pêkte hi an ni.

Exodus 31:18 thu kan chhiar chuan, “Tichuan Sinai Tlânga amah a biak zawh chuan thu hriattirna lung phêk pahnih, lung phêk, Pathian kutzunga ziak chu Mosia a pe ta a,” tih kan hmu ang. A dân thianghlim, ṭhatna dân chu mihringte ziaktîr lova Amah ngeiin lung phêk pahnihaa A kutzunga a ziak chhuah kherna hian chu dân chu a pawimawhzia a tilang chiang êm êm a ni.

Dân thianghlim leh ṭha famkim a ni rêng a. He ṭhatna dân, thu sâwm pêkte hi Pathian ngaihtuahna sawi chhuaha awm ta chu an niin, Kristâ nungchang tihlanna emaw, ziak chhuahna emaw a ni.

Mihring hi Pathian anpuia siam a ni a, nimahsela Pathian nungchang puan chhuahna dân thute hi a awih avâng chauhin Pathian anpui a nihna chu a chhawm zêl thei ang. Chu ṭhatna dân chu awih tûrin mihringte hi tihluih an ni lova, Pathian nungchang nên thliar hran theih loh khawpa inzawm tlat an ni.

Mihringin remna leh muanna te, hlimna te nei reng tûr leh, nung dama awm reng thei tûrin Pathian ṭhatna dân nên inhmeh thlapa a khawsak a ṭul a. Pathian nungchang chu danglam ngai lo rêng rêng a nih avângin dân, a nungchanga innghat pawh chu a danglam ngai lovang. “A thununna thu (thupêkte) zawng zawngte chu a rinawm a ni. Chûng chu kumkhaw tlaitluan atân tihnghehin a awm a,” Sâm 111:7, 8.

“He dâna a enga pawh hi mihring zawm tûrin a awm reng ang a, KUMKHAW TLAITLUANIN AN ZAIN AN DING RENG ANG. Hun inher zêl leh THIL DANGLAM ZUNG ZUNGTEAH HIAN HE DAN HI A INNGHAT LOVA, PATHIAN NUNGCHANG LEH MIHRING INKARA INLAICHÎNNA DANGLAM THEI LOVAH CHUAN A INNGHAT ZÂWK A NI.” – John Wesley

Hmangaihna, Pathian Nungchang Zepui

“Pathian chu HMANGAIHNA a niI.” Chuvângin, Pathian nungchanga dân inzam chu hmangaihnaa innghat a ni lo thei lo vang. Chu dân AWIHNA LEH ZAWMNA CHU HMANGAIHNA THILTIHA INLÂR CHHUAK CHU A NI.

“Hmangaihnain vêngte a tikhawlo ngai lova chuvângin, HMANGAIHNA HI DAN ZAWH FAMKIMNA A NI.” Rom 13:10

He kaihruaitu, hmangaihna dân hi THUPEK TAWI TÊ TÊ SÂWM infinkhawm a ni.

“A thuthlung zawm tûra thu a pêk che u, THU SAWM PÊK CHU in hnênah a puang a, LUNGPHÊK pahnihah a ziak a,” Deuteronomi 4:13.

He zirlaia târlan thu sâwm pêkte hi ngunthlûk tak leh ṭawngṭaina nên zîr tûrin kan duh che a, an zavai hian thlarau lam thil an nihzia chhinchhiah la, thinlung thununtu an ni tih hre kiau bawk ang che.

PATHIANIN RAM A NEI

Pathian ram chu engtianga zau nge a nih?

“Lalpa chuan a lalṭhutphah chu vânahte khian a tinghet a; A RAM CHUAN ENGKIM CHUNGAH RO A REL A.” Sâm 103:19.

A ram chhûnga mi chêngte chu dân zâwm tûr an ni em?

“Dân hretu maite chu Pathian mithmuhin mi fel an ni lova...DAN ANGA TITUTE chu mi fela chhiarin an awm zâwk ang.” Rom 2:13.

“He miah hian Pathian fate kan hmangaih tih kan hria, Pathian kan hmangaih a, A THUPÊKTE KAN ZAWM HIAN, Pathian kan hmangaihna chu hei hi a ni, A THUPÊKTE KAN ZAWM HI; A thupêkte chu a khirh si lova.” 1 Johana 5:2, 3.

Pathian dân chu eng ang nge a nih?

“Lalpâ dân chu a ṬHA FAMKIMA.” Sâm 19:7.

“Dân chu a THIANGHLIM A, thupêk pawh chu a THIANGHLIM A, A FEL A, A ṬHA A NI... Dân chu THLARAU LAM THIL A NI.” Rom 7:12, 14.

Danglam thei lo leh ṭha famkim Pathian chauhin dân thianghlim leh ṭha famkim, siam ṭhat ngai leh tawh lo tûr, tihdanglam ṭul tawh hek lo chu a siam thei a ni.

Thil siam zawh aṭanga kum 2,500 lai hnuah engtiangin nge mihring hnênah Pathian dân pêk a nih?

“Sinai Tlângah chuan Lalpa chu meia A LO CHHUK AVANGIN a khu phui ta mup a ... tichuan tlâng pumpui a nghîng dur dur a.” Exodus 10:18.

“Hêng thute hi, tlângah mei te, chhûm te, thim chhah mup aṭangtein Lalpâ âw ring takin in zavaia pung khâwmho hnênah a sawi a. A BELHCHHAH LEH CHUANG LOVA. Chûng chu LUNGPHÊK PAHNIHAH (Pathianin) A ZIAK A, mi (Mosia) pe a.” Deuteronomi 5:22.

Mipui pung khâwmho hnênah THU SÂWM PÊKTE chauh hi Pathianin A puang a. He dân thianghlim hi ziak chhuak tûrin emaw, puang tûrin emaw mihring a ruat lo rêng rêng a ni.

Eng vângin nge hetiang kawng mak taka Pathianin A dân thu a puan?

“Hlau suh u: PATHIANIN a rawn FIAH CHE U nih ni, IN MITHMUHA ṬIHBAIA a awm hi, IN SUAL LOHNA TÛRIN a ni e.” Exodus 20:20.

Sual lantîrtu engnge ni?

“Tupawh thil tisual apiangin dân pawh a bawhchhe ṭhin; SUAL HI DÂN BAWHCHHIATNA A NI SI A.” 1 Johana 3:4.

Ṭhatna dân BAWHCHHIATNA chu SUAL a ni tih chu a lang chiang tawk ta hle a. He nunna tehfung hi thupêk, tawi fel taka ziak chhuah a ni a, a zain sawm a ni. Hêng thupêkte hi chiang taka awm an ni ang bawk hian sual pawh thil awm ngei a ni. Mi ṭhenkhatten an rin anga sual hi thil awm lo, ngaihtuah chhuah chawp mai a ni lova, thil awm ngei a ni zâwk e.

Mihringin ama thiltihah mawh a phur a ngem?

“Mi, thil tisual chu, a thi ang.” Ezekiela 18:4

“Tupawh Dân nei lova thil tisual chu, Dân lo bawkin a boral ang; tin, tupawh Dân neia thil tisual chu, Dâna rorêlin a awm ang.” Rom 2:12.

Finna leh ngaihtuah theihna neia Pathianin A siamte chuan duh thlan theihna hlu tak an dawng vek a. chu thilpêk hlu tak chu dawng ni lo selang chuan mihring hi khâwl ang chauh a ni ang a, ama duh thlannain Siamtu chibai bûk duhna a nei lo vang a, hmangaihna a nei hek lo vang. A nungchang siam ṭhatna rêng rêng pawh a nei bawk hek lo vang. Duh thlan theihna nei lovin mihring hi awm ni sela chuan a khawsak dân tûr a mak dâwn êm avângin han suangtuah chhin pawh thil harsa tak a ni. Eng pawh ni se, duhthlan theihna nei a nih avangin a thiltihah mawh a phur ngei tûr a lo ni ta a ni.

“Miin a theh apiang, chu mi vêk chu a seng bawk dâwn a si a.” Galatia 6:7.

PATHIAN THU SÂWM PÊK ZIRRNA

Kumkhaw tlaitluan atâna Pathianin dân a siam thupêk hmasa ber engnge ni?

“KEIMAH lo chu PATHIAN DANG rêng rêng i nei tûr a ni lo,” Exodus 20:3.

“Jehova, tuma siam lohva Mahnia lo awm kumkhua, thil engkim bul leh enklawtu chu zahna sâng ber pêk tûr a ni a, chibai bûkna ropui ber pêk bawk tûr a ni. Mihring hi a rawngbawlnaah leh ngainat zâwng rêng rêngah Pathian aia ngaih pawimawh zâwk a neih khap tlat a ni a. Thil engpawh, Pathian kan hmangaihna pawt hniam thei khawpa kan ngaihhlut chu, CHU MI NGEI CHU PATHIANAH KAN SIAM ṬHIN A NI.”

Thupêk pahnihna enge ni?

“MILEM SIAM CHAWP leh thil a ANPUI RÊNG RÊNG–CHUNG LAM VÂNA mi emaw, HNUAI LAM LEIA mi emaw, LEI HNUAI LAMA TUIA mi emaw I TÂNA I SIAM TÛR A NI LO: chûng chu CHIBAI I BÛK TÛR A NI LOVA, an RAWNG I BAWL TÛR A NI HEK LO: Kei Lalpa i Pathian hi, Pathian thîkthuchhia, pate khawlohna avânga fate hrem ṭhin, mi hawtute chu chhuan thumna leh chhuan lîna thlenga hrem ṭhîn leh, mi hmangaiha ka thu pêkte pawmtu sâng tam takte chunga khawngaihna lantîr ṭhîn ka nih hi.” Exodus 20:4-6.

Thupêk pahnihna chuan milem siam chawp leh thil a an pui rêng rêng hmanga Pathian chibai bûk a khap a. Thil siamtea chatuan Pa anpui siam tumna hian mihringin Pathian a ngaih hniamzia a tilang chiang hle a ni. Chutianga Pathian anpui siam tumna chuan mihring rilru chu Siamtu lam aṭanga kaipêngin thil siam lam a hawitîr a, tichuan Pathian a lo ngainêp tial tial a, ama dinhmun ngei pawh a lo phet hniam nghâl ṭhîn a ni.

“Kei Lalpa i Pathian hi, Pathian thîkthuchhia ka ni.” Pathian leh A mite inlaichînna nghet tak leh thianghlim tak chu nupui pasal inneihna nên tehkhin a ni. Chuvângin, milem biakna hi thlarau lam nunnaa uirêna a ni a. Pathianin a huatzia leh a lawm lohzia chu thîkthuchhia a nih thu A puan chhuahna hian a tilang fiah hle.

“Pate khawlohna avânga fate hrem ṭhîn, mi hawtute chu chhuan thumna leh chhuan lina thlenga hrem ṭhîn...ka nih hi.” Exodus 20:5.
Nu leh pa khawlohnain fâte chungah tuarna a thlen fo ṭhin rêng a, mahse nu leh pa khawlohna avâng chuan faten hremna an tuar ziah lo ve, an khawlohna te chu ṭawmpuitu an nih loh chuan. Sual lam ûmna te, ei leh bâr kawnga serh leh sâng te hi PA AṬANGIN FATE HNÊNAH CHHUAN THUMNA LEH CHHUAN LINA THLENGIN HLAN CHHÂWN ZÊL AN NI. He thil râpthlâk tak, dik êm êm si hian sual kawng zawh lo hrâm tûra chakna mite hnênah thlen mawlh rawh se. Mihring inthlah chhâwng zêlah hian hetiang hi a awm ṭhin a ni tih tun laiin chiang taka hriat a ni a; miten a dikzia an fiah tawh a ni.

“Mi hmangaiha ka thupêkte pawmtu sâng tam takte chunga khawngaihna lantîr ṭhin ka ni,” Exodus 20:6. Thupêk pahnihna hian pathian dang chibai bûk khapna chauh min khawhhmuh lova, Pathian dik chibai bûkna pawh min hriattîr bawk. A rawngbâwlnaa rinawmte hnênah chuan, Amah hawtute chu chhuan thumna leh chhuan lina thleng chauha a hrem ṭhin ang ni lovin, SÂNG TAM TAKTE CHUNGAH khawngaihna a lantîr ṭhin a ni.

Thupêk pathumna enge ni?

“Lalpa i PATHIAN HMING chu i lam mai mai tûr a ni lo; Lalpa chuan a HMING LAM MAI MAI chu THIAM LOH CHANTÎR LOHVIN a chhuah dâwn si lova,” Exodus 20:7.

He thupêk hian chhechham a khap bâkah eng khaw lawilova Pathian hming, a narâna lam mai mai a khap tlat a. Kan nîtin tîti khawchâng sawihonaa ṭul lo tak a Pathian hming kan sawi chhuah phawng phawng te, thil tênau mai mai atâna A hnena kan dîlna leh ṭawngṭaina te, ngaihtuah lo baksaka A hming kan lam fonate hian Amah kan zah lohzia leh chawimawi lohzia an târlang a ni. “A hming chu a thianghlimin a zahawm a ni.” Mi tinin Pathian ropuina te, thianghlimna te leh felna te hi ngaihtuah ṭhîn selang chuan A nungchang duhawm tak chuan an thinlung a hneh sak ngei ang. A hming thianghlim hi zah êm êm chunga lam ni chauh rawh e.

11. Thupêk palinain enge min hrilh?

“CHAWLNI hi THIANGHLIM taka SERH tûr a ni tih HRE RENG RAWH. Ni ruk chhûngin i thawk tûr a ni a, i hna zawng zawng chu i thawk tûr ani: a ni sarih ni erawh chu Lalpa I PATHIAN tân chawlhni a ni e” chu mi nî chuan hna rêng rêng i thawk tûr a ni lo...ni ruk chhûngin Lalpan vân leh lei te, tuifinriat leh a chhûnga awm zawng zawngte chu a siam a, a ni sarih nî chuan a châwl ta si a: chuvangin Lalpan chawlhnî chu mal a sâwm a, a tithianghlim ta a ni.” Exodus 20:8-11.

Thil siam hriat reng nâna Chawlhni hi keini, Pathian kutchhuakten Siamtu chauh chibai kan bûk tûr a ni tih kan rilrua min hriattîr rengtu a nih avângin, thupêk pawimawh êm êm a ni. Chutichuan, chawlhni thupêk chu Pathian biakna leh chibai bûkna lungphûma innghat tlat a ni a, chu mi thu chu chiang tâwk taka târlan a ni bawk. Pathian chibai bûkna chu englai mahin chhilh bovin a awm lo vang a, thil hlui hmantlâk tawh lo, ṭangkaina nei tawh lo angin kan en tûr a ni lo. Mihringte hi Pathian hnên chauha chibai bûkna pe tûr an nihzia hi englai pawha hriattîrtu atân Chawlhni hi Eden Huanah khân din a ni. Tichuan Pathian chu Siamtu a nih chhûng chuan, chu mi hriat rengna leh chhinchhiahna, Chawlhni leh Siamtu chibai bûkna hi a awm reng fo ang.

12. Thupêk panganain engti tûrin nge min hrilh?

“I NU leh i PA chawimawi rawh: tichuan Lalpa i Pathianin ram a pêkah che hian i dam rei dâwn nia.” Exodus 20:12.

Nu leh pa hi mi dang zawng zawng ai pawha hmangaih leh zah hlawh tûr an ni a, an chunga Pathianin mawhphurhna a nghah pawh hi a rip hle rêng a ni. Naupangte enkawlna leh kaihhruainaah dinhmun pawimawh êm êm chang an ni a, Pathian aiawha ding an ni. Chuvângin, nu leh pa thuneihna dik tak kalhtu chuan Pathian thuneihna pawh a kalh a ni ṭhin. Thupêk pangana hian naupangte, an nu leh pa chawimawi tûr leh an thu awih tûr ringawtin a phût a ni lova, hmangaih tûr leh duat tûr te, chhâwk zângkhai tûr te, an hming ṭhatna humsak tûr te leh an lo tar huna thlamuan leh chhawmdâwl tûr tein a phût bawk a ni. Thuneihna chang tûra Pathianin a ruatte rêng rêng chu zah leh chawimawi tûr an ni tih min kawhhmuh bawk.

13. Thupêk paruknain enge a khap?

“TUAL i THAT tûr a NI LO.” Exodus 20:13.

Nun titawi tûra dik lo taka thiltih tinrêng–huatna rilru leh phuba lâk duhna rilru te, mi dang chunga thil pawi thleng thei khawpa tisa châkna lama sûmkâr neih lohna te, mi chunga thil pawi thlentîr duhna hrim hrim te (“tupawh a unapa hua chu tualthattu a ni”), mahni hmasial vânga pachhiate leh chhawmdâwl ngaite thlahthlam te, taksa hrìsêlna tichhe khawpa hnathawhte leh mahni inenkawlnaa insûm lohna te – hêng zawng zawngte hi thupêk parukna bawhchhiatna a ni thei vek a ni.

14. Thupêk pasarihnain enge min hrilh?

“I UIRE tûr A NI LO.” Exodus 20:14.

He thupêk hian thil bawlhhlawh, a tiha tih chauh a khap a ni lova, ngaihtuahna bawlhhlawh, tisa châkna chawktho tûr hurna lam ngaihtuahna te pawh a khap a ni. Pawn lam lan dâna thianghlimna mai hi a tâwk lova, thinlung chhûngril bera ngaihtuahna leh duhna te pawh an thianghlim tûr a ni. Ngaihtuahna sual leh hmeichhe ngaihmelh te hi a tiha tih tluka dân bawhchhiatna a ni tih Kristan min hrilh.

15. Thupêk pariatnain ti lo tûra min hrilh chu enge ni?

“I RU tûr a ni LO.” Exodus 20:15.

He thupêk hian a rûka thil tihsual leh vântlâng hriata thil tihsual a huam tel ve ve a. Mihring inrûkna te, sala inmanna te leh mi dang ram lâksak tûra indona te, mi suam te leh a rûka mi thil lâk sak te a dem vek a ni. Mihring nunnaah hian thil tê berah pawh rinawma awm tûrin he thupek hian a ngiat tlat a; sumdâwnnaa hlêp lutuk te a khap a, leiba te rêng rêng pawh pe zat zat tûrin a phût bawk a. Mi dangte mãwlna te, chak lohna te leh vânduaina te denchhena mahni hlâwkna tûr lo dap ruaite hi vâna lehkhabuahte chuan thil sual ti anga ziak luh zêl an ni.

16. Thupêk pakuanain a tum enge ni?

“I VÊNGTE chungah HRETU DERAH i insiam tûr a ni lo,” Exodus 20:16.

Dâwt thu sawina rêng rêng te, mahni vêngte bum tuma beihna tinrêng te pawh he thupêk hian a huam tel vek a. Bum tumna hi thil sual a tling reng tawh a ni. Mit men dân te, kut lek vêl dân te, hmêl lan dân te hian thu sawi ang bawkin dâwt thu an lantîr thei. Uar lutuka thu sawi te, a dik lo zâwnga thil lantir ṭâlh tumnate leh, mi dang hruai sual nâna thudik ṭhenkhat sawi te pawh thupêk pakuana hian a khap tlat a ni. Mahni vêngte tihchhiat tuma sêsêa thu lâk te, an hming hliauna zâwn te leh an chanchin a dik lo zawnga tlângaupui te a khap bawk. Mi dangten an tuar theihna tûra thudik zêp pawh thupêk pakuana bawhchhiatna a tling a ni.

17. Thupêk sawmnain enge min hrilh?

“I VÊNGTE CHHUNGTE chu i AWT TÛR A NI LOVA, I VÊNGTE NUPUI chu i AWT TÛR A NI LO, a BAWIHPA emaw a BAWIHNU emaw a BAWNGPA emaw, a SABENGTUNG emaw, i vêngte ta RÊNG RÊNG chu engmah i awt tûr a ni lo.” Exodus 20:17.

Thupêk sâwmna hian sualna zawng zawng bul a zuk khawih a; sual thiltih lo chhuahna ṭhin, mahni ṭanghma chauh sial duhna te hi a khap a. Miin Pathian thu awih leh zawm avângin thil sual tih châkna–midang ta awhna–laka a invên him ṭhin chuan a mihringpuite zîngah thiam loh chantîrin a awm ngai lovang. Bible-a kan chhiar angin duhâmna (awhna) hi milem biakna a tling a ni.

Dân Petu ropuiin ropuina râpthlâk tak leh thiltihtheihna nasa tak nêna, mei leh khawpui ri dur dur zînga A dân thianghlim puan chhuahte chu târlan an ni ta. A dân thu puan chhuah rual hian Pathianin Dân Bulpui a nihzia leh lei leh vân Siamtu a nihzia te A miten an hriat reng a, an theihnghilh leh tawh lohna tûrin leh, Amah chu an zahin an ṭih dêk tûr a ni tih an hriat theih nân A thiltihtheihna leh ropuina A lantîr nghâl a. A dân nghehzia te, pawimawhzia te leh thianghlimzia te A lantîr bawk. A âwka thu râpthlâk tak leh ṭihbaiawm tak chu mipuiten an hriatin an khûr zawih zawih a, tuar har an ti êm êm a; tehna ropui tak chanchin Pathian kâ ngei aṭanga an han hriat meuh chuan an sualziate an hmu fiah uar uar a, Pathian thianghlim mithmuhah chuan thiam loh chang an ni tih an lo inhre ta a ni.

Tûnah hian keinin Pathian âw chu bengin kan hre ve kher lo a ni thei a, A thiltihtheihna pawh hringhrânin kan zîngah hian lantîrin a awm kher lo a ni thei e, nimahsela he dân famkim hi a famkimna ang biaia kan hmuh thlengin i zir ang u khai, a rawn din chhuaktu, Engkimtitheia hnênah chuan kan thinlung zawng zawng hi thuhnuairawlh chungin i hlân vek ang u.

18. Hruaitu ropui tak tak ṭhenkhatten Thu Sâwm Pêk chanchin hi eng angin nge an lo sawi hlawm?

Martin Luthera chuan, “Dân hnûk thlatu chuan MIHRING KHAWSAK TLÂN DIAL DIALNA TLANGPUI pawh a pawtchim nghâl a ni,” a ti. Alexander Campbell chuan hetiang hian a sawi a: “Pathian Thupêk Sâwmte hi Thuthlun Hluiah chauh ni lovin, A inpuan chhuahna rêng rêngah NUNGCHANG ṬHATNA DÂN emaw, SAKHAW KHAIKHÂWMNA emaw a ni tih a lang chiang hle.” Dwigh L. Moody, thu hriltu ropui berte zînga pakhatin, “Bûka awma Tling lovin” tih a ziakah chuan, “Miten Bible thu ṭhenkhatte hi an duh duhin an sâwtin an sawisêl a ni thei e, nimahsela, mi rinawmte zîngah thu sâwm pêk ṭhat lohna hmu ta rêng ka la hre ngai lo. Ring lo mite chuan Dân Petu chu an nuihzatin, dân anchhia aṭanga min chhanchhuaktu chu an hnar a ni thei e, nimahsela thupêkte hi an dikzia an pha thei ngai lo...THU SÂWM PÊKTE HI HMAN VEK AN LA NI A, bawhchhiatna avânga hremna pawh a awm reng a ni tih hi hre thiam tûrin mite hnênah hrilh a ṭul a ni,” tiin, a ziak. Thuhriltu ropui leh hmingthang tak Spurgeon-a pawhin, “Pathian dân hi THIANGHLIM LEH FAMKIM a ni...Thupêk sei lutuk emaw, tawi lua emaw a awm lova, thil dang nêna tehkhin phâk rual loha famkim anihna ngawt pawh hian Pathian aṭanga chhuak anihzaia a tilang a ni,” tiin, a ziak bawk.

CHATUAN ATÂNA HMAN TÛR DÂN

19. Dân chu chatuan thlenga hman zêl tûr a ni tih Bibl-in a zirtîr em?

“A kut thiltihte chu thutak leh rorêlna fel ani. A THUNUNNA THU ZAWNG ZAWNGTE CHU A RINAWM A NI, CHUNG CHU KUMKHAW TLAITLUANG ATAN TIHNGHEHIN A AWM A, thutak leh dik taka tih a ni hlawm a.” Sâm 111:7, 8.

Mihring dân siam chawpte hi tihdanglam an ṭul fo ṭhîn, a chhan chu MIHRING SIAM an nih avângin an FAMKIM TÂWK VE LO a ni THU SÂWM PEK DÂN ERAWH HI CHU KUM SÂNG TAM TAK RAL HNU PAWHIN A LA DANGLAM CHUANG LOVA; hriat thiam theihna ṭha tak neite chuan Lal Davida angin, “Lalpâ DÂN CHU A ṬHA FAMKIM A,” an ti ṭhîn a ni. Sâm 19:7.

Pathian finna chuan tun hma hun te, hun awm mêk te leh hun lo la awm tûrte lam pawh a hmuh tlang vek avângin mihringtena an nungchang leh thu awihna teh nâna dân famkim an mamawh chu A hria a ni. Hnam zawng zawng hi bul HMUN KHAT veka A siam avângin an za atân chuan ṭhatna dân thuhmun hman a ni ngei tûr a ni. Chu dân chu mi zawng zawngin englai pawha an hman tûr a ni. Chu ṭhatna dânah chuan Pathianin hun rei lo te chhûng chauh hman tûr thupek a zep tel lovang tih chu a lang chiang hle a ni. Puan chhuah a nih dân te, a chhûnga thu leh a tum te enin a Petu Pathian ang bawka danglam ngai lo tûr, chatuan chatuana awm reng tûr a ni tih a lang bawk.
