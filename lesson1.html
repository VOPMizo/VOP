ZIRLAI PAKHATNA

PATHIAN THUTAK ZAWNNA

Châng Pawimawh: “I thu hi ka kê atânte hian khâwnvâr a ni a, ka kawng atân êng a ni.” Sâm 119:105

Mi tam takin Pathian thutak hi an nunnaa thil pawimawh bera ngaiin ṭhahnemngai takin an zawng ṭhin a, thutak chu mihringte zawn ber tûr pawh a ni rêng a ni. Tunah thutak zawngtu sâng tam takte zinga mi i lo nih ve tâk avang hian kan lâwm êm êm a ni. Thutak zawnna chuan a zawngtu tih tak zetna leh thutak chu hmuh chhuah a nih hun apianga thinlung zawng zawnga pawmna a ngiat tlat a ni.

Pathian thutak kan han tih hi Pathian nihna nêna thliar hran theih loh khawpa inzawm tlat a nih avângin thutak hmuh chhuahna chu Pathian hmuhna pawh a ni nghâl ṭhin a. Tin, Pathian hnên aṭang chauh lo chuan thutak puan chhuahna famkim neih theih a ni lo. A aw chu thuneihnaa khat a ni a. A awka thu ziakna lehkhabu pawh thutak bu a ni a, chu lehkhabu chu thutak zawngtute tâna himna leh kaihhruaitu dik awm chhun a ni.

“HAWH TEH U, I NGAIHTUAH TLANG TEH ANG U”

Baibl Râlchhan Skul enkawltuten Pathian Lehkhabu Thianghlim hi Pathian thuneihna hriattirtu leh thutak khurpui a ni tih leh, thutaka khat Pathian hnên min thlen theitu a ni tih kan ring a. Amaherawhchu, Pathian Lehkhabu Thianghlim hi kan rinna innghahna a nih vang maia i Kaihruaitu atana lo pawm ve tûrin sâwm che kan ni lo va; Isaia 1:18 a kan hmuh angin, “Hawh the u, i ngaihtuah tlang the ang u,” tiin, Pathianin mi tin hi min sâwm ṭheuh zâwk a ni. Tin, Pathian Lehkhabu chu Pathian thuneihna aw a nih tak zet chuan Pathian thâwk khum ngai a nihzia fiahna pawh a neih nghâl kan beisei thei ang.

Pathian Lehkhabu Thianghlim chu mihringte hnêna Pathian aw a nihzia achu hêng zirlaite hian fiah tâwk taka an hriattir che kan beisei a, tin, ngun tak leh ṭhahnemngai taka lo zir tûrin kan ngên a che.

MAMAWH BULPUI CHU

Eng vângin nge mihringin Pathian thâwk khum lehkhabu a mamawh?
“In khawlohnate chu nangni leh in Pathian kârat lain a tiṭhen zâwk che u a ni a, benga a hriat duh lohna turin in sualnate chuan a hmai chu a hliah sak bawk che u a ni.” Isaia 59:2.

He châng thu dikzia hi rinhlelh rual a ni lo. Mihringte hi Pathian laka tih hranin an awm tawh a. Khawvêl zawng zawng hi sualnain a lo khat zo ta a ni. Nimahsela, Pathianin misualte a siam a ni lo va. Ama anpui ngeia mihringte hi A siam an ni. (Genesis 1:26,27). A tîrah chuan mihringte chu thianghlim an nih avângin Pathian nêna inhmatawna an inpâwlna leh inbiakna dâltu engmah a awm lova, amaherawhchu, mi sualten Pathian an hnaih theihna chu, an sual tâk aṭang khân, Pathian thianghlimna chuan a phal ta lo a ni. Chutichuan, khawvela sual lo lût chuan, daidangtu awm lova mihringin Pathian a pâwl ṭhinna chu a lo tichat ta a ni. Chutia Pathian hnên aṭanga mihringte chu tihhran an lo nih tâk avâng chuan an inkâra inzawmna a awm leh theih nân Pathianin kawng engemaw a ruat a lo ṭûl ta a ni.

Suala khat mihringte leh Pathian inkarah inzawmna kawng Pathianin a zawng le hang tih eng vângin nge kan rin?
“Hmangaihna nei lo chuan Pathian a hre lo; PATHIAN CHU HMANGAIHNA A NI SI A” 1Johana 4:8.

Pathian nungchang zepui ber chu hmangaihna hi a ni a, hmangaihna chu malsâwmna leh ṭanpuina thlentu a ni. Mihring chuan Pathian lo hnar tawhin, Amah dodâlin leh A lakah helin malsâwmna dawng tlak lovah lo insiam tawh mah sela, Pathian chuan A tiboral nghal mai lo va; an sualnate hre chhuak tur leh anmahni duhthlanna ngeia Ama lam lo hawi a, A tâna rinawmna lo nei leh turin hruai kir leh A tum zawk a ni. Mihringte A hmangaihzia leh chhandam A duhzia thu, Pathian thâwk khum lehkhabua puan chhuah aia mihringte hnêna hriattir dan kawng ṭha zâwk engnge awm thei ang le? Chu lehkhabu hmang chuan suala khat mihringte leh Ama inkârah inzawmna awm reng tûr chu Pathianin A lo thlung kai leh ta a ni.

Pathianin mihringte chu inhmatawna a pâwl theih tak si loh chuan A thuneihnaa khat lehkhabu chhûnga thute chu engtinnge mihringte hnênah A puan theih ang?
“In zingah hian zâwlnei in awm chuan amah chu kei Lalpa hi inlârnain ka inhriattir ang a, mang lamah ka bia ang.” Numbers 12:6
“Miten PATHIAN THLARAU THIANGHLIM TIRHIN Pathian hnên ata an sawi zâwk ṭhin a ni.” 2 Petera 1:21.
“Lalpa thlarau chuan keimah mi hmangin a sawi a, a thu chu ka leiah a awm a.” 2 Samuela 23:2.

Mi fing, thu rill eh bul chanchin ziaktuten Pathian chungchâng sawi tur an ngah hle ṭhin. Nimahsela, Pathian thâwk khum lehkhabuah hian chuan Pathian chu mihringte hnêna thu sawi zâwkin kan hmu thung a ni. Mihringten anmahni thua an ziakte hi chu suangtuahna mai a ni ṭhin a. Pathian kaihhruainaa an ziak erawh chu thuneihnaa khat a ni ṭhin. Tin, mihring thil siam chhuahte rêng rêng hian nunna an nei ve lo va, Pathian aṭanga chhuakte erawh chu nunna pe thei an ni. Mihringte hnêna A duhzâwng puangtu atâna A mi thlante rilru chu inlârna leh mumang hmangin Pathianin A thunun a, mihring duhna chu lâk kianin a awm zâwk ṭhin a ni. Petera pawhin, “Thu hril lâwk rêng rêng hi mihring thua chhuak a ni ngai si lo va,” tiin, min hrilh. 2 Petera 1:21.

Chutianga mihringte chu zuk thunun an nih avang chuan Pathianin lehkhabu ṭha famkim – “nun tak khurpui chu A siam thei a ni.

KHAWVEL PAKHAT – LEHKHABU PAKHAT

Pathian thâwk khum lehkhabu pakhat chauh a awm ang tih rin theihna engnge awm?
“Tin, lei chung zawng zawnga awm turin bul hmun khat vekin hnam tin mi a siam a.” Tirhkohte 17:26.

Khawvêla hnam tin hi kawng tam takin danglam ṭheuh mah sela, an zaa bul chu Pathian a ni tih chiang takin kah hre thei; bul hmun khat vekin hnam tin hi A siam a ni. Taksa inrel dân leh awm dân te, taksain a mamawh –ei leh bâr tur te, ni êng leh boruak thiang te, etc. leh chhûng lam awm dân te hi a inthuhmun vek a ni. “Danglamna rêng a awm lo va, mi zawng zawngin thil an tisual tawh vek a, Pathian ropuina an chang zo ta lo.” Rom 3:23. “Tumah mi fel tak rêng an awm lo, pakhat mah an awm hauh lo.” Rom 3:10. Khawvel mi zawng zawng hi, a hmei apain sual thiltih leh tih duhnain an khat zo vek tawh a ni.

Chuvângin, hnam tina mi zawng zawng hian sual thiltihtheihna te, thiam loh chantîrna te leh ânchhia aṭanga chhanchhuahna an mamawh êm êm vek a, a tîra mihringin thianghlimna leh sual tell ova a nunna kha din thar sak leh an mamawh ṭheuh a ni. Tin, mihringte thlarau mamawh pe tûrin Pathian thil ruat leh ruahman bâkah thil dang rêng a awm thei chuang lo. Tichuan Pathian thâwk khum lehkhabu, mihringte mamawh zawng zawng puan chhuahna chauh lo hi chu mihringte mamawh petu lehkhabu dang a awm lo a ni.

LEKHABU ROPUI

Pathian Lehkhabu hi engzâta ṭhen sawm nge a nih?
“Zawlnei thianghlimte thu sawi lâwk leh, in tirhkohte kaah Lalpa leh Chhandamtu thupêk chu in hriat rnegna turin.” 2 Petera 3:2.

He châng thu hian zâwlnei thianghlimte thu sawi. Thuthlung Hlui leh, Isua Krista Tirhkohte chanchin ziak, Thuthlung Thar thu a târlang a ni tih a lang chiang tâwk hle. Thuthlung Hluiah hian bu hrang sawmthum leh pakua a awm a. Thuthlung Tharah bu sawmhnih leh pasarih: Pathian Lehkhabu pumpuiah bu sawmruk leh paruk a awm a nih chu. Heng bu hrang hrangte hi mi sawmli laiin kum sângkhat leh saruk lai chhûnga an ziak a ni.

A chanchin chu hetiang hian sawi a ni: “A chhûnga thute hi lal te, lalber te, lal fa te, hla phuahtu te, mi thianghlim te, mi fing te, sangha mantu te, ram enkawl dân chungchânga mi thiam te, Aigupta finna lama thiamna sâng tak nei te, Babulon skul chhuak te leh Jerusalem khuaa zirtirtu ke bula zirte ziak a ni a. Thlalêra mi te, mahni ram aṭanga hnawh chhuah tâwk te, berâmpu puan ina khawsa te, hlobet hring dup hmuna mi leh chawlhna tui kama awm te ziak a ni. An zinga ṭhenkhatte chu chhiahkhawntu te, mi hausa te, pachhia te, thu hriltu te, sipai hotu te leh rorêltu te; mi chi hrang hrang, khawsak dân hrang leh hnathawh hrang hrang neite an ni.. He lehkhabu ropui tak mai hi.. lehkhabu tam tak infinkhâwm a ni a, khawvêl chanchin te, chi thlah tut e, dân te, nun dâ leh khawsak dân te, hril lâwkna thu te, hl ate, damdawi lam leh taksa inenkawl thianghlimna dân te, ram enkawl dân leh rêlbâwl dân te, mimal leh mipui khawsak dân te a fûn khawm vek a ni. Thil hrang hrang chanchin ziakna ni mahsela, inkalhna awm miah lovin fel tak leh inrem takin a chhûnga thute chu an inzawm thliai vek a. An za chuan lehkhabu pakhat an ni a; hiring ziak rêng rêngah he lehkhabu tluk emaw khûmtu emaw hi a awm lo va, chu chuan mihring thiltihtheihna mai aṭanga lo chhuak a nih lohzia a tilang chiang hle a ni.

Pathian Lehkhabu kan han tih hi thâwk khuma pek lehkhabu, Pathian thu puan ziak chhuahna hi a ni a. Chu lehkhabua thu puan chhuah, mihringin amaha a hmuh chhuah theih loh leh a hriat theih loh chu, Pathianin mihring hnênah chuan A rawn hriattir a, tin, A thâwk khumna te, A kaihhruaina leh thununna te avângin Pathian ngaihtuah te, thupêk te leh tum te chu mihring tân dik tak leh fiah taka ziak chhuahin a awm thei a ni.

Pathian Lehkhabu ziaktu zawng zawng ten an thu ziakte hi Pathian hnên aṭangin an dawn vek em ni?
“Pathian Lehkha Thu tinrêng, Pathian thâwk khuma pêk…a ni.” 2 Tim. 3:16.

Hei hi Paulan Thuthlung Hlui thu a sawina a ni. “Pathianin a ti,” “Lalpan hetiangin a sawi,” “Lalpan thu a pêk chu,” “Lalpa thu chu,” tihte hi Pathianin Lehkhabua a bu hmasa pangaahte ngawt pawh hian vawi zasarih dâwn lai sawi lan a ni a, chu chuan hêng bu hmasa pangate hi Pathian Thu an ni tih chiang takin a tilang. Tin, khawvel chanchin lam ziakna, Josua bu aṭanga Estheri bu thlenga kan en chuan a chunga târlan ang khi vawi zali zet ziak a ni tih kan hmu bawk ang. Thuthlung Hluia hril lâwkna bu lamah pawh vawi sângkhat leh zathum lai târlan a ni. Chutichuan Thuthlung Hlui pum puiah:

  • “Lalpa thu ngai rawh u.”
  • “Lalpan a sawi tawh a ni.”
  • “Lalpa awin a sawi ka hria a.”
  • “Lalpa thu ka hnênah a lo thleng a.”
  • “Ka thu sawite hi an hnenah i hrilh tur a ni.”

tihte hi vawi sânghnih leh zathum zet a chuang a ni.

Lalpa Isua Krista, A thuneihna leh nihna tumahin an el phâk rual loh chuan, a hun laia Pathian Lehkha Thu awmte chu Mosia Dân te, Zâwlnei Lehkhabute leh Fakna Hla Bu te tein A vuah hlawm a. (Luka 21:11. 45)[cite: 7] Hetiang hian Thuthlung Hlui bu pum pui hi thâwk khum a nihzia Amah ngeiina hriattirin A nemnghet a ni.

Zawhna pangana hnuaiah khan Pathian Thu châng pakhat, tirhkohte thu ziak sawina thu kan chhiar tawh a, chu chuan Thuthlung Thar pawh hi thâwk khuma pêk a nihzia a tilang a. Tin, Pathianin Ama duh anga mihringte ziaktirin A kaihruai a; ziaktute thu ziak dân kalhmangte pawh a hnâwl sak kher lo va, an thu ziak dân te, an rilru put dân te leh an mihrinna zia te pawh A thununna hnuaiah chuan zalên takin a hmantir a, nimahsela, A thununna chu a ṭhat tâwk êm avangin an thu ziak chu “Pathian thu nung reng leh awm reng” an lo nit a vek a ni. 1Petera 1:23.

PATHIAN LEHKHABU TUM

Pathian Lehkha Thu mihringte hnêna pêk thua Pathian thil tum engnge ni?
“Pathian Lehkha Thu tinrêng, Pathian thâwk khuma pêk tawh phawt chu, zirtir kawngah te, thiam loh chantir kawngah te, felna zirtir kawngah te a sâwt bawk a ni; Pathian mi chu, hna ṭha tinrêng thawk tura kim taka inthuamin a famkim theih nân.” 2 Timothea 3:16,17.

Thurin bulpui leh inzirtirna ṭhenkhatte chu sakhaw dik leh finna dik tungdingtu leh kengkawhtu an ni hlawm a, chutiang thurin leh inzirtirna chu Pathian Lehkhabuah hian hmuh theih a ni.

Mihringte hian zilhhauna hi an lâwm ngai lo va, nimahsela, an mamawh a ni tih Pathianin A hriat avangin an chungah A thlentîr ṭhin. Tin, Pathian Lehkhabu hian tumah duhsak bik leh thlei bik nei lovin, kan zain kan sualnate min kawhhmuh ṭheuh a ni. Zilhhauna chauh hi kan chungah Lalpan A thlentir lova, zirtirna pawh kan dawng tel ṭhin. Kan tih turte min hriattir a, felna kawng min kawhhmuh a.[cite: 7] A dân ropui takte nena inhmeh, thil ṭha tih kawngah te min zirtir ṭhin a ni. Pathian Thu zirtirna dikzia hriaa zâwmtute chu mi famkim, “hna ṭha tinrêng thawk tura kim taka in thuam” an ni. Rom 15:4 ah Pathian Lehkha Thua chhelna leh thlamuanna hmua beiseina kan neih theihna turin, hman laia ziak apiangte chu keimahni min zirtirna turin ziak a ni si a,” tih thu kan chhiar bawk.

Chhelna, thlamuanna, beiseina – hêngte hi tun laiin khawvel hian a va mamawh nasa em! Khawvel hian beidawngte hnenah beiseina thar neih leh dan kawng tam tak a kawhhmuhin a tiam tak nâ a, beiseina khurpui awm chhun chu Pathian Lehkhabu chauh hi a ni a, thiltihtheihna pawh chuta ṭanga lo luang chhuak chu a ni. Chu thiltihtheihna chuan mihring nunna a tidanglam thei a, nungchang a siam ṭha thei a, chak lote hnênah chakna thar a pe thei bawk; lunghnualte hnênah huaisenna te, hnuk chat lek lek te hnenah beiseina te a thlen ṭhin. Tirhkoh Paula chuan, “Pathian thu hi a nung a, thil a ti thei a, khandaih hriam tawn eng ang ai pawhin a hriam a,” tiin, min hrilh. Hebrai 4:12. Tin, Peteran Pathian thu chhe thei lo, “nung reng leh awm rengah chuan” piang thar kan ni tih min hrilh bawk. 1 Petera 1:23. Isua ngei pawhin hetiangin a sawi: “In hnêna thu ka sawite hi thlarau a ni, nunna pawh a ni,” tiin. Johana 6:23.

KHAWVEL THIMA ENNA

Pathian Lehkha Thu hlutzia engtinnge Lal Davida zawlnei ni bawk chuan a sawi?
“I thu hi ka ke atânte hian khâwnvâr a ni a, ka kawng atân eng a ni.” Sâm 119:105.

Ephesi 5:13 ah hian, “tihlana awm apiang chu êng a ni si a,” tih thu kan chhiar a, chu chu Pathian Lehkhabuin êng awmzia a hrilhfiahna a ni. Eng awmnaah chuan thimna rêng a awm thei ngai lo a ni. Khawvêl hi thlarau lam leh tisa lama thimnain a khat zo ta a, mihringten kalna lam tur pawh an hr eta lo. Anmahni thil siam chhuah ngeite chu an chunga thimna rawn thlentu an ni a, chu thimna hual vêl chuan an awm a, êng an mamawh tak meuh a ni. Chu êng an mamawh êm êm chu Pathian Lehkhabu hi a ni a, mihringten an zui a ṭûl a ni. Nunna mamawh leh châkkhai zawng zawngte hi chutah chuan a awm vek. Dr.J.Wilbur Chapman chuan mawi takin Pathian Lehkhabu thu hetiangin a ziak a:

“I lu a natin a tluk a awm lova; i thinlung a nguiin a tluk a awm lo! Chhûna ni a lan loh lai leh zâna arsite an lan loh laiin Pathian Lehkhabu tluk rêng a awm lo! Faten an nu an sûn lai te, i fa duh ngawih ngawih i sûn lai te, hnuk chat ṭêpa I awm lai te leh, nunna kawng zawhnaa i zin kawng tâwp i thlen a, i thih dâwn lai tein Pathian Lehkhabu ang rêng thil dang a awm mawlh lo!” tiin.

TIHBORAL THEIH A NI LO

Pathian Lehkhabu tihchhiar tuma beituten eng ang chauha hlawhtlinna nge an neih tawh ṭhin?
“Nangmah beihna tura hriamhrei chher rêng rêng a sâwt lo vang; lei tinrêng thupawia nangmah rawn khingtute chu thiamloh i chantir ang.” Isaia 54:17.

Hêng thute hi Pathian ṭawngka ngei an ni a, an dik êm êm a ni. Pathian leh A Lehkhabu hi engti kawng engti kawng maha tihboral leh hneh theih an ni lo. Kum sawmnga aia tam mah a nit a. Robert Ingersoll-a chuan kum sâwm chhung lekin Pathian Lehkhabu hi miten an chhiar duh tawh lo vang a, hlui hlâwn hlen a ni ang tiin, a sawi a. Amaherawhchu, 1945 chhûng ngawt pawh khan bu vaibelchhethum leh maktaduaithum zet hralh chhuah a la nit a fo. Tin, kum 150 lai kal taah khân Voltaire-a Pathian thu awi lo dang pakhat pawhin Pathian Lehkhabu hi puak darh (exploded) chhe zo vek tawh angin a sawi bawk.[cite: 7] Pathian Lehkhabu chu a puak darh the meuh mai, nimahsela, a puah darh dan erawh chu Voltaire-a ngaih dân nen chuan a inang lo hle âwm e. Ṭawng hrang hrang sangkhat leh za aia tam mahah a lo puak darh ta chum chum zâwk a ni. Lehkhabu dang rêng rêng, Pathian Lehkhabu anga ṭawng chi hrang tam taka chhut hi a awm lo va, a tluka hralh chhuah tam a awm bawk hek lo. A kum atêlin a dinhmun hi a la nghet zual sauh sauh zêl a ni.

Pathian Lehkha Thu hi a buin emaw, a lehlin tawh sain emawa kan neih hmain hetiang kawng li hian a lo awm hmasa tawh a.

  1. Chatuan ata Pathian rilruah a lo awm hmasa phawt a.
  2. Pathian rilru aṭang chuan mihringte rilruah hlan chhâwnin a awm a.
  3. Mihring rilrua Pathianin A dah chu mihring ṭawng leh thuin siam chhuah a lo nil eh a. Chu chu Pathian kaihhruaina leh thununna ang zela kaltir a nih avangin Pathian Thu a lo ni thei a ni.
  4. Chu mi hnuah chuan miten an ziak chhâwng a, an letling a, vawiin ni thleng pawh hian chu hna chu a la kal zel a ni.

Dik lo leh kim lo awm miah lova Pathian Lehkha Thu vawn him zel a nihna hi a makin a ropui êm êm a ni.[cite: 7] Kut ziak, Alexandrias Manuscript an tih (Protestant Kohhran kuta awm) te, Sinaitic Manuscript (tun hma chuan Grik Kohhran kawl a ni a, tunah erawh chuan British thil hlui kawl ṭhatnaah a awm a) an tih te, Katholik Kohhran kuta awm, Vatican Manuscript an tihte hi kawng mak takin vawn ṭhat vek an ni. Hêng kutziak hrang pathumte hi khaikhinin entawn tlâng vek mah ila, thurin thuah an inremin an inhmurem thlap vek a ni.

Hun inher zel te, dodâlna te leh thil dang rêng rêngin Pathian Thu hi an tichhe thei lo va, an tiboral thei hek lo vang. Chatuan Pathian Thu a ni si a.

THAWK KHUM CHHINCHHIAHNA

Pathianin pathiana inchâl vete hnên aṭangin engnge a ngiat?
“In thu chu rawn sawi rawh u, Lalpan a ti; pathian in intihna chhan berte chu lantir rawh u; Jakob-ho Lalin a ti… Pathian in ni tih kan hriat theihna turin tun hnua thil lo la thleng turte chu sawi rawh u le” Isaia 41:21-23.

Pathian hriat lawktîrna dawngtu tan lo chuan nakin hun hi lehkhabu khup ang a ni. Mihringin dik takin thil lo thleng tur chanchin a sawi lâwk thei lova, aienthiam leh kutzeenthiam bumin mi tam tak chu an awm ṭhin. Heng mite sawi lâwkna hi chu dâwtah a tâwp fo ṭhin a ni.

Pathian chauhin daw hlek lovin nakin hnua thil lo thleng turte A hril lâwk thei. Chutichuan, dik taka Pathian Lehkhabu hrilh lâwkna thleng famkimte hian chu Lehkhabu chu Pathian thâwk khum a nihzia an tilang chiang hle a ni.

Pathian chuan engnge nia A inchhâl?
“Tun hmasâng thilte chu hre reng rawh u: kei hi Pathian ka ni a, a dang rêng rêng an awm si lo; kei hi Pathian ka ni, keimah ang rêng rêng an awm lo; keiin a tawp tur chu a tir aṭang rêngin ka sawi ṭhin a, thil la tih lohte chu hmasâng aṭang rengin ka sawi ṭhîn; ‘Ka remruat chu a ding chhuak ang a, ka duhzawng zawng zawng chu ka ti vek ang,’ ka ti a.” Isaia 46:8,10.

Leilung buaina chungah Pathian chu lalṭhutthlengah A ṭhu a, engkim mai hi A rawn hmu thei vek a, A ngilneihnain ṭha bera A hriat angin ro A rêl ṭhin. Tun hma, tun lai leh nakin hun zawng zawngte chiang takin A hre vek a. Khawvêl chanchin leh awm dan ur A hrilh lâwknaa khat daw hlek lova lo thleng famkim ṭhinte hian Baibl hi Pathian thâwk khum leh hrilh lâwknaa khat a ni tih a lan chiantir hle. Pathian Lehkhabu i zirnaah hian hrilh lâwkna thute i la zir zel dawn a, chûng chuan Pathian thâwk khum a nihzia an hriattir chein, I thinlung an tivârin an tiharh zêl ang a, A thu thua thil ṭha ti ṭhintu Pathian nung chuan A âng chhûngah a dah ang che.

Pathian Lehkhabu hi khawvel hnam leh chite chunga lêng, thiltihtheihna nei a ni a, a thiltihtheihnain mite a hneh dân te pawh englai pawhin a pangngai reng a ni. Mihring nun dân leh chêt dante hian an thunun lo va, mi tam takte tuarna a lak kian sakin, Pathianah hahchawlhna leh lungawina a hmuhtir zawk ṭhin.[cite: 7] Khawvel hmun tinah mi a siamṭha ṭhin a ni.

Khawvêl hnam khat tân emaw, ṭawng chi khat tan emaw chauha siam a nil ova, a thiltihtheihna chu hnam zawng zawng chungah kumkhaw tlaitluanin a pangngai reng ang. Mi zawng zawng tân a ni.

Mihring ngaihtuahna a siam ṭhat sak a, ṭawngkam duhawm leh ziak dân mawi te rilru ngaihtuahna sawi chhuah thiamna te, hla thu mawi tak tak ziak thiamna te a pe; khawvel lehkhabu ziak zawng zawngah Pathian Lehkhabu tluka hnathawk nasal eh sulhnu ngah hi a awm lo.

Zirtu duh tak, A thu thianghlim aṭanga thil ropui tak takte i chhar chhuah theih nân Pathianin i mitte leh thinlung te tihvâr sak che rawh se, tih hi i tana kan ṭawngṭaina a ni.
