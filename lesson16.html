ZIRLAI SAWMPARUKNA

SETANA PHUARNA

ChângThlan: “Hêng hi mak ti suh u, a hun a thleng dâwn ta, chutih hunah chuan thlâna awm zawng zawngin a âw an hria ang a, an chhuak vek ang; thil ṭha titute chu nunna changin an tho leh ang a, thil sual titûte chu thiam loh changin an tho leh ang,” Johana 5:28, 29.

A va ropui êm! Hei ngei hi hriat i lo duh fo chu a nih hi. Setana hi a hnathawh huatthlala tak aṭanga chawlhtîr han ni ta sela, mihringte tâna a vânneih thlâk tûrzia hi i ngaihtuah tawh ngai em? Engtikah emaw chuan Setana hi kum sângkhat chhûng phuar a la ni dâwn a ni.

Pathian Lehkhabuah chuan hun lo la awm tûr leh tun lai hun daidangtu puanzâr chu kaihhlîmin a awm a, thil lo la thleng tûr tam takte chu an hun indawt dân tein kan hmu thei a. Tin, chûng thilte chu, Setana thanga kan awh hlauh loh nân, kan hriat a ṭûl a ni. Hriat thiam awlsam taka Pathianin min rawn puan chhuahsak avâng hian a va tihzia em! Chûng thil avâng chuan hun lo la awm tûrte chiang taka hmuh theihna leh huai taka tâwn theihna kan nei ta a ni.

He zirlai chhûnga zir tûr ropui tak awmte i hnêna rawn thlen hi phûrawm kan ti ngawt mai. KUM SÂNG ROREL INṬAN LAIA THIL LO THLENGTE, KUM SÂNG CHHÛNGA THIL THLENGTE LEH KUM SÂNG TÂWPA THIL THLENG CHANCHIN a ni ber a. He kum sângkhat hun chhûng hi Sapṭawngin Millenium a ni a, amaherawhchu, Baibul-ah millenium tih i a chuang lova, kum sângkhat tihna a ni. Latin ṭawnga thu pahnih, mille (sângkhat) leh, annus (kum) tih infin khâwm a ni.

THUPUAN PAWIMAWH

Isua Krista lo kal leh hma lawkin eng thupuan pawimawh tak nge puan ni ang?
“MI FEL LO CHU, TIHFEL LOHIN AWM RENG RAWH SE; mi bawlhhlawh chu, tihbawlhhlawhin awm reng rawh se; MI FEL CHU, TIHFELIN AWM RENG RAWH SE; mi thianghlim chu, tihthianghlimin awm reng rawh se. Ngai teh, ka lo kal thuai dâwn e; mi tin an thiltih ang zêla pêk tûrin ka lâwmman ka hnênah a awma.” Thu puan 22:11, 12.

He thil pawimawh tak puan a nih hian mihringte chûng thu zawng zawng chu rêl fel diam a ni tawh ang a, Pathian hmaa tu dinhmun mah tihdanglam a ni tawh lo vang.

CHHANDAM LOHTE MANGANNA

Isua Krista lo kal lehna chuan chhandam lohte chu eng angin nge a rawn khawih ang?
“Vân chu lehkhabu zial mi ziala a bo angin, a bo ta a. Tlâng leh thliarkâr tinrêng chu an awmna ata sawnin an awm ta hlawm a. Leia lal te, mi lian te, sipai hotu ber te, mi hausa te, mi chak te, bawi leh bawi lo tinrêngte chu pûkah leh tlânga lungpuiah chuan an biru a: tin, tlângte leh lungpuite hnênah chuan, ‘Min delh ula, lalṭhutphaha ṭhua hmêl lakah leh Berâm No thinurna lakah min hliah rawh u, an thinurna ni ropui tak chu a lo thlen tak hi; tichuan, tunge ding thei ang i le?’ an ti a,” Thupuan 6:14-17.
“Chu mi niah chuan miten an tangka milemte leh an rangkachak milem te, biak tûra an siam sakte chu, bui-kelek hnênah te leh bâk hnênah te chuan an paih ang a; lei hi nasa tak taka tinghîng tûra a thawh hunah, Lalpa hlauhawmna lak aṭang leh a mawina ropuina lak aṭang chuan, lungpui pûkahte leh khâm lungpui khî-ahte chuan an lût ang,” Isaia 20:20, 21.
“Chutih hunah chuan Mihring Fapa chhinchhiahna chu vânah a lang ang. Chu mi hunah chuan leia chitinin an âwmte an chûm ang a, mihring Fapa hi thiltihtheihna leh ropuina nasa tak nen vân chhûm chunga lo kal an hmu ang. Vântirhkohte chu tawtawrâwt ri nasa tak nên a tîr chhuak ang.” Matthaia 24:30, 31.

Tunlai mite hi thiltihtheihna te, hlimhlawpna leh hlêpna te ûmin an phî ruai hlawm a. Chu mi niah chuan mi suaksualte leh nawmsip bâwlnaa inchiahpiahte chanvo chu nasa taka lungngaia rûmna a ni ang. Chu mi niah chuan duhâmte chuan an rangkachak milem ngaihhlut êm êmte chu ṭih leh hlau êm êm chungin an hawisan tawh ang.

Inbuatsaihna nei lote leh, inbuatsaihna nei tûra mi dangte ṭanpui ngai lote tân chuan Isua lo kal lehna chu inchhîr ngawih ngawihna nî a ni ang.

“Lalpa ni duhte u, in chung a pik e! Engatinge Lalpa nî chu in duh? ÊNG NI LOVIN, THIM A NI ZÂWK E,” Amosa 5:18.

Zahngaihna kawng a la inhawn avâng hian kan lâwm a va ṭûl êm! Nunna kawng kan zawhnaah hian tih tak zetna kan va ngai tak êm ve le!

KHAWVEL CHHIATNA

Isua Krista lo kal lehna chuan lei hi eng angin nge a rawn khawih danglam ang?
“A pasarihna chuan berhbu chu boruakah a leih bua a; tichuan, pindan chhûngnung aṭangin Lalṭhutphah ata, ‘A kin ta,’ tiin, aw ring tak a lo chhuak a. Kâwlphê te, âw te, khawpuirî te a lo awm ta a; LÎR pawh NASA TAKIN A LO NGHÎNG a, leia mihring awm achin chutianga nasa rêng rêng a la awm ngai lo, lîrnghîng chuti kauva nasa leh râpthlâk chu... Thliarkâr tinrêng a bo ta vek a, tlângte pawh awm ta lova,” Thupuan 16:17, 18, 20.

Isua Krista lo kal leh hunah chuan lir nasa takin a lo nghîng ang a, chu chuan khua te, mihring kutchhuak tinrêng te leh leilung hmêl lan dân zawng zawngte hi a rawn tidanglam vek dâwn a ni.

Kum 1908 khân Italy ram chhim lampangah khuan second tlêm te chhûng lîr a nghîng chiam a, mi 76,483 laiin an thìhphah a. Kum 1923 khân Japan ramah second rei lo te chhûng lek bawk lîr a nghîng leh a, mihring 100,000 laiin an thìhphah leh ta a ni. Isua a lo inlâr huna lîrnghîngin lei pum a rawn sawi nghîn dur dur a, chhiatna a rawn thlen tûr chu a va râpthlâk dâwn tehlul êm!

KUM SÂNG ṬANTIRHA THIL THLENGTE

Isua lo kal hunah chuan chhandam ni ve lote chu an inchhanhim thei ang em?
“Chu mi.... hunah chuan Dân Bawhchhepa chu a lo lang tawh ang, Lalpa Isua'n ani chu a kâa thâwin a tihlum ang.” 2 Thesalonika 2:8.
“Chu mi nia Lalpa thahte chuan lei tâwp aṭangin lei tâwp thlengin an dâp ang a; sûn loh leh khâwm loh leh phûm lovin an awm ang a; lei chunga êkdûr mai an ni ang.” Jeremia 25:33.

Vân ṭhat famkimna hmaah chuan mi sualte chu an nung thei lo va; Isua ropuina êngin lei a han tuam vêl chu chhandam ni ve lote chuan an tuar zo lo vang a, lei chungah hian an tlu hlum phiar ang.

Krista rawn inlârna chuan engangin nge mi thianghlimte a khawih ve ang?
“Chu mi nîah chuan, ‘NGAI TEH U, HEI HI KAN PATHIAN A NIH HI; ani hi kan nghâk reng a, tichuan, min chhandam ang: Hei hi Lalpa chu a nih hi; ani hi kan nghâk reng a, a chhandamnaah chuan kan lâwmin kan hlim ang, an ti ang,” Isaia 25:9.
“Chu mi hnuah chuan keini nunga lo la awm renga te chi, boruakah Lalpa hmuak tûrin anmahni rualin chhûm zîngahte khian lâk chhohvin kan awm ang; chutichuan kumkhuain Lalpa hnênah kan awm tawh ang,” 1 Thesalonika 4:17.
“Kan zain kan muhîl kher lo vang a, nimahsela, kan zain tâwtawrâwt hnuhnung ber rîk hunah chuan, rei lo teah, mit khap kâr lovah, tihdanglamin kan awm vek tawh ang; tâwtawrâwt chu a ri dâwn si a ... keini pawh tihdanglamin kan awm ang. He ṭawihthei hian ṭawihtheih lohna a sin tûr a ni a, he thithei hian thihtheih lohna sin bawk tûr a ni,” 1 Korin 15:51-53.

Isua Krista a rawn inlâr leh hunah chuan tlante, nunga lo la awm rengate chuan lâwm takin an lo hmuak ang a, mit khap kâr lovah an taksate chu ropuinaa thuam a ni ang a, thihtheih lohna, hun rei tak chhûng an lo thlîr tawh ṭhin chu, an chang tawh ang.

Isua lo kal lehnaah chuan mi fel thi tawh chungah eng danglamna nge lo thleng ang?
“Lalpa ngei chu âuthâwm nên, vântirhkoh chungnung ber âw nên, Pathian tâwtawrâwt ri nên, vân aṭangin a lo chhuk dâwn si a; KRISTAA THI TAWHTE CHU AN THO HMASA ANG,” 1 Thesalonika 4:16
“Mithi tawhtte chu ṭawihtheih lova kaihthawhin an awm ang.” 1 Korin 15:52.

Mi thianghlim nunga awmte ang bawkin mi fel thi tawhte chu ṭawihthei lo leh thithei lova siam an ni ang a, Krista'n a lo tiam lâwk tawh angin vân hmunah chuan a hruai chho ang.

“In tân hmun ka han siam chuan KA LO KIR LEH ANG A, ka hnênah ka la ang che u, ka awmnaah nangni pawh in awm theih nân,” Johana 14:3.

Mi fel thi tawhte thawhlehna hi kum sâng rorêl inṭan tîrha lo thleng a ni tih chiang tak a hriat theihna kan nei:

“. . . An lo nung a, kum sângkhat atân Krista nên rorêlin an awm a. Mithi dangte erawh chu kum sangkhat chu a vei hmâ loh chuan an la nung rih lo va. Chu chu thawhlehna hmasa ber chu a ni. Thawhlehna hmasa bera telte chu an engthâwlin an thianghlim a ni,” Thupuan 20:4-6.

Kan zir tawh zawng kha hetiang hian khaikhâwmna i lo nei teh ang. 1) Isua lo kal lehna. 2) Lei chung zawng zawngah lîr a nghîng. 3) Chhandam lohte chu Isua ênnain a tihlum 4) Chhandam fate, nunga lo la awm rengate erawhchu ṭawihtheih lohna leh thihtheihlohna sintîr an ni. 5) Mi fel thi tawhte chu thihtheih lohna leh ṭawihtheih lohnaa thuam an ni bawk. 6) Chhandama awm zawng zawngte chuan vân hmun thlengin Isua an zui. 7) Chhandam lohte erawhchu kum sângkhat atân mitthi an ni.

KUM SÂNGKHAT CHHÛNGIN

He kum sângkhat chhûnga khawvêl awm dân zâwlnei Jeremian engtinnge a lo sawi?
“Leilung chu ka han en a, ngai teh, ṬIAU LEH RUAKIN A AWM A; vânte pawhin êng an nei lo. Tlângte pawh an phe hlawk hlawk a. Ka han en a, ngai teh, MIHRING AN AWM LOVA, chunglêng sava zawng zawng pawh an thlâwk bo zo ta. Ka han en a, ngai teh, thlawhhma ṭhate chu thlalêr a chang ta, khawpui zawng zawng pawh a chim zo ta, Lalpa leh a thinurna nasa tak hmaah chuan,” Jeremia 4:23-26.
“Ngai teh, Lalpa chuan lei hi a tiruakin, a tiram dâwn a, a khaikhup ang a, a awmate chu a tidarh dawn.... lei hi tihruah fai kêlhin leh tihchhiat fai kêlhin a awm ang; chu thu chu Lalpan a sawi tawh si a.... Lei hi a keh chhe vek a, lei hi tihralin a awm zo va, lei hi nasa takin a nghîng a; Lei hi zu rui mi angin a pai thlawk thlawk ang a, bâwktê sawi angin a sawi luai luai ang a; a bawhchhiatna chu a chungah rit takin a awm ang a, tichuan a tlu ang a, a tho leh tawh lo vang,” Isaia 24:1, 3, 19, 20.

Hetiang hian mitthlaa chiang taka hmuh fiah uar theihin kum sâng rorêl chhûnga lei awm dân tûr chu ziak a ni a. A ruak fai kêlh a, a chhûnga chêng rêng rêng an awm hauh lo a ni. Mi suaksual zawng zawngte an thih vek tawh avâng leh, mi thianghlimte chu vâna hruai chhohva an awm vek bawk si avângin lei chu a ruak ngei a ni tih kan hre thei.

Mi ṭhenkhat chuan kum sâng rorêl chhûng hian lei chungah muanna leh hlâwknate awmin an ring a, chûng ngaih dân dik lo takte leh Baibul zirtîr dân hi a inpersan nasa hle a ni.

SETANA PHUARNA

Lei chunga nungcha zawng zawng an awm tawh loh hnu chuan Setana din hmun eng ang nge ni ta?
“Dragon, rûlpui tar chu (chu chu Diabola leh Setana a ni) a man a, kum sângkhat atân a phuar a, leilâwtah chuan a paih lût a, a khâr khum a, kum sângkhat chu a vei hma loh chuan hnam tin a bum leh tawh lohna tûrin a châr hnan ta a,” Thupuan 20: 2, 3.

Leilâwta Setana paih luh leh phuar tih awmzia hi hnamte bum theihna rêng rêng a neih lohna hi a ni. Chawlh hun nei thei lo lêka buaite chu thil awm dâna phuar hlâwm reng an ni angin Setana pawh thil awm dan -a phuar a ni ang a, bumna hna thawhna hun remchâng rêng a nei dâwn si lo va. Isua lo kal lehnaah chuan mi thianghlim zawng zawngte vânah hruai chhohin an awm ang a, mi sualte zawng zawng lah chu tihhlum an ni vek tawh dâwn si a, Setan chauh he khawvêl thim- leilâwtah hian – hnutchhiah a ni ang.

Kum sângruk lai chu Setana hian a kawng bawlhhlawh tak mai chu mihringte a lo zawhtir tawh a, chhiarsên lohten, A bumna thangah chuan lo âwk tawhin, chatuan nunna an hloh phah tawh a. Sual rah leh mi sualte hnuhma râpthlâk tak mai kum sangkhat chhûng lai han hmuh leh ngaihtuah chu Setana tân chuan a thaw ipik thlâk hlê ang le. Zu bâwiha a lo siam tawh te, bawlhhlawh taka a lo nuntîr tawh te, Chawlhni a bawhchhittîr te, chapona a neihtîr zawng zawng te, nimahsela, Isua Krista thiltihtheihna azara tihzalêna awm leh tâte chanchin te chu a ngaihtuah chhuak leh vek dâwn a ni.

Chu mi nîah chuan harsatna leh dodâlna kâra Isua zui thlangte chu chhandam an lo nih tâk avânga inchhîr rêng rêng an awm lo vang.

Kum sângkhat chhûngin mi felten eng hamṭhatna nge an dawn ang?
“Lalṭhutphahte ka hmu a, a chungah mi an ṭhu a, an hnênah chuan rorêlna an pe a,” Thupuan 20:4.
“Thawhlehna hmasabera telte chu an engthâwlin an thianghlim a ni; chûngho chungah chuan thih hnihna chuan thu rêng a nei lo va, Pathian leh Krista puithiamte an ni ang a, amah nên kum sângkhat rorêlin an awm zâwk ang,” Thupuan 20:6.
“Mi thianghlimte chuan khawvêl an ngaihtuah dâwn tih in hre lo em ni le? Khawvêl mah hi in ngaihtuaha a awm dâwn chuan, thil tê berte pawh chu ngaihtuah tlâk lo em ni in nih le? Tun dam chhûng khawsak thute chu sawi loh, vântirhkohte lam pawh kan ngaihtuah dâwn tih in hre lo vem ni?” 1 Korin 6:2, 3.

Tlante hnênah chuan boralte nunna chanchin chhinchhiahna enfiah theihna pêk a ni ang a. Vâna chhinchhiahna famkim takah chuan mi sualte chanchin chu kimtaka târlan vek a ni ang a, Pathian felna leh boralte thiam lohzia hriatpuitu an ni ang. Pathian chu a fel tih leh A lâwmman pêk dân kawngah a fel a ni tih chu Pathian mi thianghlimte chuan an hre chiang ang.

KUM SANG RORÊL TÂWPNA

Kum Sâng rorêl tâwpah thil engtenge lo thleng ang?
“Khaw thianghlim JERUSALEM THAR CHU mo a pasal ela a inchei anga peih diamin, PATHIAN HNÊNA MI VÂN ATA LO CHHUK ka hmu bawk a. Lalṭhutphah aṭangin aw ring takin, “Ngai teh, Pathian bâwktê chu mihring zîngah a lo awm ta, ani chu an hnênah a awm ang a, anni chu A mite an ni ang a, Pathian ngei chu an hnênah a awm ang a, an Pathian a ni bawk ang... Ani chuan, Thlarauvin tlâng lian pui, sâng takah mi kalpui a, Jerusalem khaw thianghlim chu Pathian ropuina neiin, Pathian hnêna mi vân ata lo chhuk mi entir a,” Thupuan 21:2, 3, 10

Khawpui thianghlim Jerusalem Thar chu he leiah hian a lo chhuk ang a. Tlante chuan lei, ram zo vek tawh hnu leh chhe zo vek tawh chu an hmu ang a, chu mi hnua thil lo thleng zêlte pawh chu an hmu bawk ang.

11. Setana chu phuar a nihna ata engtia phelhin nge a awm ang?
“MITTHI DANGTE ERAWH CHU KUM SANGKHAT CHU A VEI HMA LOH CHUAN AN LA NUNG RIH LO VA. Kum sângkhat chu a vei hunah chuan SETANA CHU A TÂNNA ATA CHU CHHUAHIN A AWM ANG, lei kil lia hnamte, Goga leh Magoga chu buma, indonaa hruai khâwm tûrin a kal chhuak ang; chûngho tamzia chu tuifinriata ṭiauvut ang mai an ni,” Thupuan 20:7, 8.

Kum sângkhat a lo vei hnu chuan mitthi dangte–misual thite chu an lo nung leh ang a. Isua'n thu a han sawi chuan hmasâng misual thi tawh zawng zawng chu an thlân, sual chhinchhiahna nei aṭang chuan an lo chhuak ang. Chûng mi chitin reng infin khâwmte chuan an lâwmman dik tak, kum sâng rorêl chhûnga rêl thlûk diam tawh chu an hmu tawh ang.

Misual thi tawh zawng zawngte kaihthawha an awm a, lei hi an rawn awh khahna chu Setana chhuah lehna chu a ni. Setana, mihringte hmêlma lian ber chuan a hnathawh huatthlala tak mai, kum sângkhat lai a lo chawlhpui tawh chu a rawn chhunzawm leh ang a, “lei kil lia hnamte chu bum” turin a kal chhuak leh ang.

Misualte chuan hlim taka chatuan lâwmnatea vûr tlante lêng vêl chu an thlîr ang a. Khaw duhawm–Isua kraws putu leh Isua hniak hnung zuitu ni duh sela chuan an chênna ni ve thei mai tûr- pawh chu an thlîr vawng vawng ang.

SUALNA A TÂWP TA

12. Sual leh helna chu chatuan atân engtingne tihtâwp hlen a nih ang?
“Vân aṭangin mei alo tla a, anni chu a kângral ta a,” Thupuan 20:9
“Ngai teh u, ni chu a lo thleng e, rawhtuina mei angin a kâng a ni; zung leh zâr chenin a zuah lo vang.... mi suaksualte chu in rapbet ang.... in kephah hnuaia meivâp an ni dâwn si a,” Malakia 4:1-3.
“Akâng ral che, (Setana) leiah vaivutah ka chantîr che. I awm ngai rêng rêng tawh lo vang le,” Ezekiela 28:18, 19.

Sual laka he khawvêl tithianghlimtu mei vêk chuan Setana leh mi sualte chu a kângral vek ang a, sual hnuhma zawng zawng pawh chatuan atân a boral tawh ang.

“Lalpa kalhin engnge in suangtuah? Tâwp derna a thlentîr dâwn a ni: hrehawm vawi hnihna a thleng lovang,” Nahuma 1:9.

Baibul-a Setana chanchin târlan hmasak ber chu Eden huana kan zaa nu, Evi a bumnaa a hlawhtlin thu a ni (Genesis 3:1-6). He zirlaiah hian lei mihringte bum tuma a beihna hnuhnung ber te, a hlawhchham thute leh tihboral hlen a la nih tûr thute kan zir a ni (Thupuan 20:1-3).

Kum sâng rorêl zawha thil lo thlengte khai khâwmna lo nei ila: 1. Krista leh mi thianghlimte vân aṭangin an lo chhuk, 2. Khaw thianghlim a lo chhuk a, leiah hian a innghat, 3. Mi sual thi tawhte kaih thawh an ni, 4. Setana phelh a ni a, a bumna hna chhunzawm theihna a nei leh, 5. Mi sualte mei dîlah tihboral an ni.

SUAL HNUHMA ZAWNG ZAWNG A BO TA

“Pathianin A thil siam zawng zawng aṭang hian sual leh a hnuhma zawng zawng leh, Setana chu chatuan atân a nuai bo ang a. He lei hi a tîra Eden huan mawina famkim chang leh tûrin a dinthar ang a, sualin a tihmêlhem leh tawh lovang. Chutih hunah chuan Pathian kutchhuak zawng zawngte chu hlim tâwp rêng awm lovin, inremna famkim biaia suih khâwmin an lêng za dial dial tawh ang. Ṭhian duh tak, nang, chu chatuan hmunah chuan chên ve i duh lâwm ni?

“Ngai teh u, vân tharte leh lei thar ka siam a nih hi; tunhma thilte chu hriat rengin a awm tawh lo vang a, rilruah pawh a lût tawh hek lo vang,” Isaia 65:17
“Vân thar leh lei thar ka hmu a; vân hmasa leh lei hmasa chu a boral tawh a, tuifinriat pawh a awm tawh lo a ni.... An mit ata mittui zawng zawng (Pathianin) a hrufai ang a; thihna a awm leh tawh lovang a, lungngaih te, ṭah te, nat te pawh a awm leh tawh hek lovang, thil hmasate chu a ral ta,” Thupuan 21:1, 4.

He zirlaia kan zir tâkte hi hmasâng aṭanga Pathian zâwlneiten nghâkhlel taka an lo thlîr ṭhin a ni. Harsatna–manganna leh lungngaihna hual vêlin awm mah sela, thutak an hriat chu Pathian hnên ata a ni tih an hriat tlat avângin Setana leh, sual leh lungngaihna zawng zawng lâk kian a nih a, Pathian faten felna rah hlim taka an la seng hun tur chu an thlîr fan fan thei a ni. Zirtu duh tak, nang pawhin chutiang beiseina chu lo nei ve mawlh teh.
