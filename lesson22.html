ZIRLAI SAWMHNIH LEH PAHNIHNA

BAPTISMA NUNTHAR

Châng thlàn: “Petera chuan an hnênah, ‘Sim ula, in sual ngaihdam nan Isua Krista hmingin baptisma chang ṭheuh rawh u,’ a ti a.” Tirhkohte 2:38.

Awm hmun nghet tak tak nei lova insuan insuan ṭhin kan nih avang hian kan khua leh tui nihna pawh a danglam fo a ṭul ṭhîn a. Chutianga mi, ram khat aṭanga ram danga a va pêm luh chuan chu ram thar mi leh sa nih tâkna chhinchiah nân vantlang hriata chhechham a ṭul ṭhîn.

Thlarau lam khawvelah hian thiltihtheihna pahnih—Setana lalram leh Pathian lalram an ni. Heng thiltihtheihna pahnihte hi “thim thuneihna” leh “A fapa hmangaih taka ram” tia sawi an ni bawk (Kolosa 1:13).

Hmun dang lehah chuan “thim ata chhuak a, a êng mak taka lût tûra” koh kan nihzia hrilh kan ni (1 Petera 2:9). Mithianghlim Paula chu koh a nih khân a hnathawh tûr chungchâng hetiangin hrilh a ni:

“An mit tivâr turin an hnenah ka tirh che hi, chutichuan thim chu êng lamah an hawisan ang, sual ngaihhnathiamna an hmuh nân leh mi rin avânga thianghlim tawhte zingâ ro chan an neih nân,” tiin (Tirhkohte 26:18).

Pathian chuan thim thuneihna leh Setana thuneihna aṭanga intihrang tawhte chu thinlung thar pu an nihna vantlang hmuha puang chhuak tûrin a duh a ni. Isua ngeiin hetiangin a sawi:

“Chuti chuan mite hmâa mi ṭan apiang, ka Pa vana mi hmaah ka ṭan ve ang. Nimahsela, tupawh mite hmaa mi phat apiang ka Pa vâna mi hmâah ka phat vê ang,” Matthaia 10:32, 33.

Pathian rama mi chêngte zinga mi nih theihna kawng chu baptisma hi a ni. Baptisma channaah hian miin ram danga mi cheng a lo nih tâkna chu vântlang mithmuhin a tilang a, Pathian khawngaihnain a thinlung chhûnga danglamna a rawn thlensak pawn lama hmuh theiha a lantirna a ni.

Baptisma chan avânga khua leh tui thar kan lo nihna hi he khawvel lalte khua leh tui thu sawina a ni lova, Pathian ram, thlarau lam lalram, lalram thianghlima mi chengte kan lo nihna thu a ni zâwk. Chu chuan he khawvêla ram khata mi chêng kan nihna emaw, ram hmangaihtu kan nihna hi rawn khak buaina a nei lo, Kristian chu khawi rama chêng pawh ni mah sela, ram tâna mi rinawm, ram hmangaihtu a ni zel si a.

KOHHRAN—KRISTA TAKSA

1. Kohhran hi enge vuah a nih?
“Amah chu engkim zawng zawng chungah lu atân kohhran ho chûngah chuân a pe a, kohhran ho chu a taksa a ni.” (Efesi 1:22, 23). “Chutichuan nangniho hi Krista taksa chu in ni a, a pengte in ni ṭheuh bawk a.” (1 Korin 12:27). “Tuna ka tuarsakna cheah hian ka lawm a, Krista hrehawm tuar bâk chu a taksa avângin ka tisaah hian ka chhunzawm zêl a ni; a taksa chu kohhranho hi an ni,” (Kolosa 1:24).

Kohhranho chu mihring taksa nen hian tehkhin a ni a. Taksa pêng hrang hrangte zîngah thawhho dial dialna a awm ang bawk hian thla-rau lama Krista taksa, kohhranah pawh hian inzawm khâwmna ṭha tak a awm tûr a ni.

2. He thlarau lam taksa, kohhranah hian eng-tiangin nge mi an lo luh theih?
“Krista chu amah ngei taksa chhandamtu a ni a, kohhranho lu a ni ang bawk hian,”(Efesi 5:23). Taksa pumkhata awm turin Thlarau pakhat chauhvah baptis-in kan awm ṭheuh si a,” (1 Korin 12:13).

Isuâ Krista chu kohhranho lu a nih avangin taksa pumkhata baptis tih awmzia chu Krista a baptis tihna a ni.

BAPTISMA HMUN KHAT

3. Baptisma engzat nge awm?
“Taksa pakhat…Lalpa pakhat, rinna hmunkhat, baptisma hmunkhat…chauh a awm,” (Efesi 4:4, 6).

Matheilovin Paula hian tuia baptisma channa thu a sawi niin a rinawm a, Baptisma chandân chu KAWNG KHAT CHAUH a awm a, vawi khat aia tam chan theih loh tih thu a sawi tumna erawh chu a ni lo ve, amah ngeiin mi ṭhenkhat baptisma a chan nawntîr si a. Chu chu Efesi khaw ropui takah a nih nghe nghe kha. Chu khuaah chuan tun hmaa Baptistu Johanan tuia a lo baptis (hnim phum) tawhte a va hmu a, an hnenah chuan Johanan a chanchin a lo sawi ṭhin, Isua chanchian te a zirtirna an han dawn leh chuan êng an hmuh ang zela kal tûr an nihzia an hre chhuak ta a, tichuan Lalpa Isua Krista hmingin an chang nawn leh ta a ni, (Tirhkohte 19:1-5).

Pathian chuan lei mihringte hi êng an hmuh ang zela kal turin a duh a. Efesi khua a mite tan thil thar lo thleng a, Isua chu a lo kal ta. Krista Isuaa baptisma chan chu an tân a lo ṭul ta a ni.

Tunah hian keiniho hi ni hnuhnunga cheng kan ni a, he hunah hian Pathianin kohhran a nei a, chu kohhran chuan Pathian thupekte vawngin, Isua rinna a nei bawk a ni, (Thupuan 14:12). He kohhran emaw, taksaah emaw hian lâwi lût tûrin Pathian chuan A fate a ko mek a, chu kohhrana lûtte chuan baptisma chan pawh an duh ngei ang. Chu kohhran huang chhûnga lo lut, tun hmaa Bible zirtirna anga baptisma la chang ngai lote chuan Bible zirtirna ang ngeia baptisma chu an kal tlang ngei turah an ngai tûr a ni. Pathian lehkhathu anga baptisma chang tawhte tan pawh he ni hnuhnunga Pathian kohhrana lo lut tûra baptisma chan leh chu a thiang a ni tih chu kan Bible chang taran tâkte khian an sawi chiang tawk hle a ni.

BAPTISMA CHAN HRIATPUINA

4. Baptisma chan hi Isuan a rem tiin a hriatpui reng em?
“Tichuan Isua chu an hnênah a lo kal a, anniho chu a bia a, ‘Lei leh vâna thuneihna zawng zawng ka hnênah pek a ni tawh. Chutichuan kal ula, hnam tina mi zirtirahte siam ula, thu ka pêk zawng zawng apiang che u pawm turin zirtir rawh u. Ngai teh u, kei kumkhuain, khawvêl tawp thlengin in hnênah ka awm zel ang,’ a ti a,” (Matthaia 28:18-20).

He thupêk, baptisma chang tûr leh chantîr tûra thupêk hi thiltihtheihnain a zui zel dawn a ni. Isua hmangaihtu, Amah chawimawi leh zui duhtu thinlungah chuan baptisma thua zirtirna hi a chiang êm êm a, thil pawimawh tak a ni bawk. Thil pawimawh tak ni lo selang chuan vâna a lawn hma lawk thleng khan uar takin Isuan a sawi hauh lovang. Thil pawimawh a nih leh nih loh hi kan zawh pawh a ṭul love, a chiang tâwk êm êm a ni. Thlarau Thianghlim baptisma hian tuia baptisma channa a lân a ni lova, Thlarau Thianghlim baptisma chang tawhte chu tuia baptisma changtu an la ni fo zawk a ni (Tirhkohte 10:45-48).

ISUAN HMA A HRUAI

5. Isuan baptisma a chang em?
“Chumi lai chuan a hnêna baptisma chang turin Isua Galili ram aṭangin Jordan luiah Johana hnênah a lo kal a, ‘I mi baptis ka ṭul zawk si a, ka hnênah i lo kal zawk a ni maw? tiin khap a tum a. Nimahsela, Isuan a hnenah, ‘Tunah rem ti hram rawh, hetianga felna hlen chhuah hi ka tan a mawi a ni,’ tiin a chang a. Tichuan rem a ti ta a. Isuan baptisma a chan veleh tui ata a chhuak ta nghal a, ngaiteh, a tân vân a lo inhawng a, Pathian thlarau chu ṭhuro anga lo chhukin, a chunga lo fu a hmu a, ngai teh, vân aṭangin âwin, ‘Hei hi ka fapa duh tak, ka lawm êm êma chu a ni,’ a ti a,” (Matthaia 3:13-17).

Krista baptisma channaah hian zir tûr pawimawh tak kan hmu. Baptistu Johana hian mipuite hnenah an sualte sima baptisma chang tûrin a lo zirtir ṭhin a. Nimahsela Isua hi chuan sual sim tur a nei ve lo tih chiang takin a hria a. Pathian Beram No, “khawvêl sual kalpuitu tûr” a ni tih a hre bawk. Chutiang sual neilo tân chuan baptisma chan a ṭul em ni? Johana pawhin Isua meuh han baptistu nih chu tling lo a inti hliah hliah a ni. Isua erawh chuan hnial buai lovin, “hetianga felna zawng zawng hlen chhuah hi ka tan a mawi a ni,” tiin tawi tê awmzenei tak siin a chhang ta hlauh zawk a. Isuaah chuan engmah sual a awm ve loh avangin sual ngaihdam nâna baptisma chan chu a tan thil ṭul a ni lo.

Amaherawh chu mi zawng zawng sual chu a phur tur a ni. Adama hnuhnung niin emaw, lei mihringte aiawha dingin sual phurtu anga Amah pawmtute kaltlang atâna ruat chu A kal tlang ta a ni. Kan hma min hruaisak ta. Baptisma channaah pawh kan tan Entawn tûr a lo ni ta. Isua khan baptisma lo chang lo selang chuan mi ṭhenkhat chuan thil tih ṭul leh pawimawh lovah an ngai mai thei ang, nimahsela, Isuan kan entawn tûrin min lo hnutchhiah tak avangin kan tih tur a nih zia hai rual a ni ta lo. Baptisma chantir turin mite hnenah thu a pe chauh a ni lova, Amah ngei pawhin baptisma a chang a ni. A hniak hnungah chuan kal ṭheuh ang u.

BAPTISMA CHANG TURTE

6. Baptisma chang tlâkte chu tutenge?
“Baptisma channa turin enginnge mi dal kher? Philipan, I THINLUNG ZAWNG ZAWNGA I RIN CHUAN i chang thei e,’ a ti a. Anin a chhâng a, ‘Krista Isua chu Pathian Fapa a ni tih ka ring e,’ a ti a.” (Tirhkohte 8:36,37. Sâp ṭawng aṭanga lehlin).
“Petera chuan an hnenah, ‘Sim ula in sual ngaihdam nân Isua Krista hmingin baptisma chang ṭheuh rawh u; tichuan, Thlarau Thiang-hlim pêk hi in hmu ang,’ a ti a,” (Tirhkohte 2:38).
“Chutichuan, kal ula, hnam tina mi zirtirahte siam ula,… baptis ula,” (Matthaia 28:19).

Pathian lehkhain a zirtir ang a baptisma chang tur chuan miin thil ṭhenkhat kal tlang hmasak tûr a nei tih kan hria. A hmasa berin Isua chanchin ṭha, Amah azara misualte hnena chhandamna thlen thei chanchin hi zirtir a ni phawt tûr a ni. Chumi thu zirtirna aṭang chuan misual chuan Isua hi Pathian Fapa a ni tih leh boralte zawnga lo kal a ni tih a RING tur a ni. Chumi hnuah chuan sual avanga thiamloh chang a nihzia hriain SUAL SIMNA a nei tur a ni. Suala thi a ni tih hriatna, Krista thihna azara NUN THIANGHLIMA NUNNA leh chutiang taka nunreng tumna nghet tak a nei reng tur a ni.

Hêng aṭangte hian baptisma chang tûrte chu an channa chhan awmziate chiang taka hrethiam rual an ni tûr a ni tih kan hmu. Chutiang te chauh chu zirtir theih an ni a, rinna nei tham leh sual simna nei thei rual an ni bawk. Nausênte chu baptisma chang thei rual an la ni lova, Bible pawh hian, apostolte hun lai pawh khan, nausên baptisma chan thu reng min hrilh lo. An tân baptisma chan pawh a ṭûl lo reng a ni. Thil sual pawh an la ti si lova. An nu leh pa sualna lah chu an chunga tla tur a ni si lo. Zâwlnei Ezekiela chuan Pathian hnen aṭanga hriattirna dawngin hêng thute hi min hrilh:

“Thil tisualtu chu a thi tûr a ni:fapa chuan a pa khawlohna chu a tuar lovang.” (Ezekiela 18:20).

Mi ṭhenkhat chuan nausên an nih laia tui thlawr chu an duhtawk mai a, baptisma diktak kal tlangin Isua Krista hniak hnung zui ṭûl an ti tawh ṭhin lo.

Thuthlung thar hunah nausên baptisma chan thu reng reng Bible-in a tarlang lo va. Isuan naupang tê tê ko khawmin mal a sawm ṭhin tih thu erawh chu kan hmu. Hetianga naupangte tâna ṭawngṭaina bîk neihsakna hi kohhrante tana thil mawi tak leh duhawm tak a ni. Galili dung leh vang fang ṭhintu, Lalpa chuan naupangte hi a la hmangaih cheu a ni.

BIBLE ZIRTIRNA ANGA BAPTISMA

7. Bible zirtir dan anga baptisma chu engtiang nge?
“Tawlailir chu a dintir a, an pahnihin, Philipa leh mitilreh chu, tuiah chuan an zuk kal dûn a; Philipa chuan ani chu a baptis ta a. Tui ata an chhuah chhuah veleh Lalpa Thlarau chuan Philipa chu a la bo a; mitilreh chuan ani chu a hmu leh ta lo,” (Tirhkohte 8:38, 39).

He laia Baptisma channa hi Pathian Thlarau kaihhruaina hnuaia awm a ni tih chiang takin a lang a. Philipa hi Thlarau kaihhruaina mak tak dawng hmasa zeta kal ta chu a ni.

“Tho la, chhim lama Jerusalem khua aṭanga Gaza khaw zuk chhun kawngah khân kal rawh.” (Tirhkohte 8:26).

Chumi hmunah chuan Philipa chuan Ethiopia lalnu ro zawng zawng enkawltu, Jerusalem aṭanga a lo kir lam chu a va hmu a., a kal pah chuan Isaia bung sawmnga leh pathumna hi a chhiar a. Philipa hnênah chuan thupek dang a lo thleng leh ta nghal a:

“Thlarau chuan Philipa hnênah, ‘Saw tawlailir saw va hnaih la, va pawl rawh,’a ti a” (Tirhkohte 8:29).

Tichuan, Philipa chuan Ethiopia mi hnenah chuan a Bible chhiarlai thu chu Isua, Pathian Fapaah a thlen famkim tâkzia thute leh, Krista chu a rin a, baptisma chang tûr a nihzia te a hrilhfiah ta a. Mitilrehin Isua Krista a rin thu a puan hnu chuan baptisma a chang ta a ni.

He laia baptisma chan chungchâng thu hi Pathian Thlarau Thianghlimin a sawi hnem hlein a lang a ni thei e. Baptisma chungchâng thu hi miten an hriat theih nan a ni phawt ang. Baptisma changa leh chantirtu chungthu sawi nan, “An pahnihin” tih kan hmu a, an pahnih hian tuiah chuan an zuk kal dun a, an pahnih vekin tui ata an lo chhuak chho dun leh a ni.

Hemi thu aṭang hian baptisma chu tui chhunga taksa pum hnim phumna bâk ngaidan dang kan nei thei dawn em ni? Tuia inthlawr satliah mai hi Bible zirtirna anga baptisma chu ni selang chuan an pahnih hniha tuia zuk kal dun kher kher a ṭul lovang le.

“Isua baptisma a chan veleh tui ata a chhuak ta nghal a,” (Matthaia 3:16). Heta tui a tih hi Jordan lui a ni. Mite baptisma chantîr nân Johana hian Jordan lui pawn lai a thlang lo tih kan hria.

“Johana pawhin Ainon- ah, Salim khaw kiangah a baptis bawk a, chutah chuan tui a tam avângin,” (Johana 3:23).

He châng hian baptisma chan nân tui tamtak a mamawhzia min hriattîr a ni.

Baptis (baptize) tih hi Grik ṭawng, BAPTIZO aṭanga lâk a ni a, chu chu a bul lehzual BAPTO aṭanga lak chhuah a ni. A awmzia chu “hnim phum,” “hnim pil,” “luang thei thila khuh,”etc.,tihna a ni. Rawng dumna tuia thil zuk hnim ang te leh, thirdengin a thir rawhlin, bura tuia a zuk thlak thawih ang hi a ni. Engpawhnise, a awmzia chu “thlawr” emaw, “theh” emaw zawng a ni hauh lo mai. Hnuai lama hnim phum, chiah pil tihna a ni zâwk.

BIBLE ZIRNA SANG NEITEN BAPTISMA CHAN DAN AN HRILHFIAH

John Calvina chuan hetiang hian a zirtir:

“Baptis (baptize) tih hi hnim phum tihna a ni a; chutiang hnim phûm chu HMASANG Kohhran zînga an tih dan ṭhin a ni tih a lang chiang.”–Institudes of Christian Religion.

Martin Luthera chuan hetiang hian a ziak:

“Baptisma tih hi Grik ṭawng a ni a; Latin ṭawng chuan “hnim phum” tia lehlin theih a ni. Chu chu thil eng emaw pil daih khawpa tuia kan hnim ang hi a ni.”—Baptist Encyclopedia, article, “Baptism.”

Dean A P Stanley-a chuan hetiang hian a ziak:

“Kum zabi sâwmpathumna tirlam chhung zawng kha chuan baptisma hi Thuthlung Thara mi ang, hnim phumin chan a ni reng a, chu chu a thu, baptis tih awmzia a ni rêng a ni.”– Christian Institution, chap. 1, pp. 19, 20.

BAPTISMA AWMZIA

8. Engtiangin nge Paulan baptisma hi a hrilh-fiah?
“Baptismaah chuan a hnena phum in lo ni ve tawh si a, chumi vekah chuan mithi zing ata amah kaithotu Pathian hnathawh rinna avanga a hnena kaihthawh in nih ve tawh kha.” (Kolosa 2:12).
“Keini zingah Krista Isuaa baptima chang apiangte chu a thihnaah baptis-in kan awm tih in hre lo emaw ni? Chuvangin, a thihnaah baptisma kan chanin, a hnenah phum vein kan awm. Pa ropuinain mithi zing ata Krista a kaihthawh ang khân, keini pawh nunna tharin kan awm theih nan,” (Rom 6:3, 4).

Baptisma hi dik taka, a nih dân tûr ang taka chan a nih chuan kan chhandamtu Krista Isua THIHNA, PHUMNA leh THAWHLEHNA entîrtu a ni. Chhinchiahna rilru khawih tak leh mawi tak a va ni em!

9. Bible zirtirna anga baptisma channa hian engtiangin nge sual sim lehte thihna, phumna leh thawhlehna a lo entîr ve?
“Krista hnenah khenbehin ka awm ta, nimahsela, ka nung a ni; keimah erawh chu ka ni tawh lo, Krista chu keimahah a nûng tawh zawk a ni; tunah tisaah ka nung hi rinnaa nung ka ni, Pathian Fapa, mi hmangaih a, ka aia inpetu rinnaah chuan,” (Galatia 2:20).
“Chutichuan, Krista hnênah kaihthawhin in awm ve tawh chuan, chung lam, Pathian dinglama ṭhu Krista awmnaa thil awmte chu zawng rawh u,” (Kolosa 3:1).

Mi sual, a sualte a sim tawh chu a nun hlui sual thihsan anga entîr a ni ṭhîn. Chutianga simna neite chuan Pathian dân a bawhchhiat avângin inchhir ngawih ngawihna a nei a, milem biakna te, tual thahna te, ruk rûk te, uirena te, depdena te, dâwt sawina te, insum theihlohna te, nawmsipbawlna leh hlimhlawpna khawlo te, etc., an hawisanin an hnuchhawn ṭhin a ni.

Mi reng reng an thih hnuah chauh phûm an ni ṭhîn a. Chutiang bawkin baptisma channaa mi phûm a nih dawn chuan suala thihna a awm hmasa tê tê tûr a ni.

Thihna aṭanga Isua kaihthawha a awm ang bawkin sual simte chu nun thara an nun theih nan baptismaa phûma an awmna aṭangin an tho chhuak a, chutianga piantharna chu Thlarau Thianghlim avanga lo awm a ni. Isua Kristaah chuan thil siam thar a lo ni a, khawvel rem zawnga che tawh lovin, nitina rilru thara nun reng tawh tur a ni.

Inhnim phuma baptisma channaah chauh hian THIHNAA PHUMNA leh THAWH-LEHNAIN ENTIRMAWI TAK chu an vawng ṭha reng thei a, chu entirna nei thil chu misual sim lehten rinna avanga nun thara nung tur leh, thitihtheihna leh hnehna an neih theih nana an kal tlang ṭhin chu a ni. Lalpa chu thuhnuai-rawlh takin i zui zel ang u.

KRISTA THAWHLEHNA

10. Isua Krista thawhlehna hriatrengna tur, Bible-in a sawi chu enge ni?
“A thihna anpuiin amah nen kan inzawm tawh si chuan, a thawhlehna anpui pawhin kan inzawm bawk ang.” (Rom 6:5).

Hêng châng thu hi baptisma chanchin sawi chhunzawm zelna a ni a, baptisma hi thawhlehna hriatrengna a ni tih a sawi chiang hle. Thawhlehna hriatreng nâna Pathiannî (Sunday) hmanna erawh hi chu Bible aṭanga fiah theihna reng a awm lo.

Mihringte chhandam nana Pathian thil ruahmanah hian Krista thihna leh thawhlehna hi rahbi pawimawh tak an ni a, chu Pathian thil ruahman rah chu thianghlimna hi a ni. Chatuan Pa chuan A Fapa Isua Krista chu siamtu leh siamthar lehtu atan a ruat tawh a, tichuan thil siam leh siamthar lehna hriat-rengna atân Pathianin Chawlhni (Sabbath) hi a ruat ta a ni, (Ezekiela 20:20; Exodus 20:20, 12; Exodus 20:8–11; 31:17, 13; Johana 1:1-3 te hi en la).

Zirtu duhtak, kawng him awm chhun chu Bible zirtîrna chiang takte zawm hi a ni. Mihring inzirtîrna leh pi-pu thurochhiahte hi zawng zui an hlauhawm. Chuvangin, Isua Krista thihna, thawhlehnaa i rinna entîr turin chhandamtu nen chuan baptisma changin, phûmin i awm ve dawn lawm ni? He kailawn hi tihtak zeta rinawma i zawh phawt chuan, “Hei hi ka fapa duh tak, ka lawm êm êma chu a ni,” tih thu hi Pa hnen aṭangin i hre ve thei ang.
