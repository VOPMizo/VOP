SAWMA PAKHAT LEH MALSAWMNA

Châng Thlan: “Ram chhûnga mi, sâwma pakhat zawng zawng, lova thil hâwngte pawh, theirahte pawh, Lalpa pual a ni a, Lalpa tâna thianghlim a ni.” Leviticus 27:30.

Khawvêla hnathawh hrang hrang zîngah hian chhandamna hnã hi a ropui ber a, chu hna ropui tak Neitu chu Pathian a ni. Amah ang bawka Neitu nihna chang ve midang chu Isua Krista a ni. Vana vantirhkoh zawng zawngte pawhin Pa leh Fapa chu an puibawm a, chhandam fate zawng zawng pawh he hna thianghlim tak thawktu leh a hlawkna dawngtute an ni.

Pa Pathian leh Fapa chuan he hna hi a lo hlawhtlin theih nân an neih zawng zawng hmangin a lungphûm an lo phûm ta a; inpumpekna aṭanga lo piang chhuak a ni. Chu inpumpekna changtute nunah chuan inpumpêkna a lang vê tûr a ni a, Pathian nên ṭangruala thawktu an ni bawk tur a ni.

A TIRIN

Pathian leh khawvêl inlaichinna enge ni?
“A tîrin Pathianin lei leh van a siam a.” Genesis 1:1. “ Leilung leh a chhûnga thil awm zawng zawng hi Lalpa ta a ni a, khawvêl leh a chhûnga chêngte nen hian.” Sam 24:1.
“‘Tangkarua ka ta a ni a, rangkachak pawh keima ta a ni,’ Sipai hote Lalpa chuan a ti,” Haggaia 2:8.
“Ramhnuaia sa tinrêngte hi ka ta a ni si a, tlâng sangkhat lai chunga ransa awmte pawh chu. Tlânga sava awm zawng zawng pawh ka hre vek a, ransate pawh hi ka ta a ni,”Sam 50:10, 11.

Pathian chu thil engkim Siamtu a nih avangin khawvêl pumpui leh a hausakna zawng zawngte hi Ama tân a hauh thei a. A kut chhuakte kan nih avangin Ama ta kan ni.

Pathian chuan neitu a nihna hi mihringten chiang takin hre thiam sela a duh a, Eden Huanah pawh khan thei kung pakhat, a rahte mihringin an ei a khap tlat a dah kha. Chu thei rah chu ei lovin mihringin Pathian chu Neitu a ni tih a lantîr thei a ni. Pathianin mihringin ti se a tih te hi a va mawlin a va khirh lo em! Zahngaihnaa khat Pathian zawng a ni ngei mai! Chutiang tih tûr mawl tak na nâ nâ chu mihring pawhin an zawm lo hauh lovang chu!

NEITU A NIH RINHLELHNA

Mihringin Pathian thupêk a ngaih pawimawh lohna engtin nge a lantîr?
“Tichuan hmeichhia chuan thei rah chu ei mi a ni tih leh, mit la tak a ni tih leh, mi tifing tûrin thei rah îtawm tak a ni tih a hriat chuan, a rah ṭhenkhat chu a lo a, a ei ta a; a pasal a pe ve a, ani pawhin a ei ve ta a,” Genesis 3:6.

He thlemna hi diabola hnen aṭanga lo kal a ni a, Pathian rinna tlâkchhamna a tilang a ni. An thiltih kawnga Pathianin khap theihna a neih chu an ngaipawimawh tâwk lova, tichuan, leilung leh a chhûnga thil awm zawng zawng neitu a nihna pawh rinhlelh a lo ni ta a ni. Khawvela sual luh ṬANNA a ni ta a ni.

Thlarau chhandamna ruahman

Pathianin eng thil nge a ruahman tâk thuai?
“In thlahtute pêk chhâwn, in awmdân engkhaw ni lawilo lak ata chu, thil chhe thei, tangka leh rangkachaka tlan in ni lo va, beram no hmêlhem lo leh bawlhhlawh kai lo anga, Krista thisen hlu chuan tlan in ni zawk tih in hre si; ani chu leilung pian hmain ruat lâwkin a awm ngei mai, nimahsela, hun hnuhnungahte hian nangmahni avangin inlârin a awm a.” 1 Petera 1:18-20.
“Mahni ta pawh in ni lova, mana lei in ni tawh a sin; chuvangin in taksaah chuan Pathian chawimawi rawh u,” 1 Korin 6:19, 20.

Sual aṭanga mihring TLAN emaw, lei kîr emaw chu mihring siam hmaa lo ruahman lâwk diam tawh a ni. Amaherawhchu, mihring a lo sual hnuah chauh chu thil ruahman chu hman a ni. Mihringte hian kan hrethiam emaw, hrethiam lo emaw, THIL SIAMNA leh TLANNA avangin Pathian ta vek kan ni.

Mipa naupang pakhat hian lawng lem a siam a. Vawikhat chu dîla a lawng lem a infiampui laiin thli a lo tleh thut mai a, a lawng lem a thlunna lazai chua chhêm chah sak ta hmawk mai a, a hmuh phâk lovah a len bosak a. Chawlhkar engemawti hnuah chuan chu mipi naupang leh a pa chu khawlai an leng dûn a, thil hlui zawrhnaah hian an va kal a, lawng lem tê kha an va hmu hlawl maia, mipa naupang chuan a lawng lem a han hmuh chuan a lawm ta êm êm a, a LEI leh ta a. Mihring ṭawngte hrethiam vê ang hrima cheiin a be vêl a, a chângin a pawm a, a han fâwp thûl ṭhin. “Vawihnih ka ta i ni ta a nih hi. Ka kut chhuak, ka siam i ni a, tûnah hian ka lei leh ta che a nih hi,” a ti a, a hâwnpui ta a. Chutiang bawk chuan keini pawh hi vawihnih Pathian ta kan ni ta. A siam kan nih avangin A ta kan ni phawta, min lei avangin A ta kan ni nawn leh ta. Kan laka Pathianin a ngiatte hi kan hriatthiam chuan A lâwm êm êm ṭhin a ni.

KAN RINAWMNA FIAHIN

Thil engkim Neitu a nihna kan hriat thiamzia tilang tûrin kan hnên aṭangin Pathianin enge a beisei?
“Ram chhûnga mi, sawma pakhat zawng zawng, lova thil hawngte pawh, thei rahte pawh Lalpa pual a ni a, Lalpa tâna thianghlim a ni…. Ran rual leh beram rual zînga sawma pakhat …. A sawmna zêl chu Lalpa pual a ni ang,” Leviticus 27:30, 32.

Thil engkim Neitu a nihzia hrethiam tûra mi thianghlim pahnih (Evi leh Adama) a beisei ang bawk khan keini pawh hi Pathian chuan min la beisei fo va. Thei rah ei thiang lo erawh chu hmang tawh lovin hlâwkna kan hmuh te, hlawh chhuah te, thilpêk kan dawnte leh kan peipun te aṭanga sawma pakhat A hnena pe turin min duh a ni.

SAWMA PAKHAT chu a hmasa bera A hnena pêk tûrin A ruat a, chu chu Ama tân liau liau a hauh tlat a ni. Chuvangin, mahni ta anga en tûrin dikna rêng rêng kan nei lo.

Evi leh Adaman Pathian thi ngiat mawl tê an pawisa lo thei mai kha mak kan ti ṭhin a, amaherawhchu, Pathianin sawma pakhat pe tûra min duhna hi a khirh ta zâwk em ni? He “thei rah ei thiang lo” sawma pakhat pêkah hian rinawm ta lo ila, thiamloh chang kan nihna a nêp chuang a ngem?

PATHIANIN TISUALTUTE A DEMNA

Pathianin Israel fate a demna ṭawngkamte chu engte nge ni?
“Mihringin Pathian ta chu a rûksak thei dawn em ni? Mahse nangni chuan min ruksak ṭhin. Nangni erawh chuan ‘engah nge kan rûksk che?’ in ti a, ‘sâwma pakhat leh thil hlanah te hian.’ Anchhia lawh in ni ta; nangni leh hnam pumpui hian, kei hi min rûksak si ṭhin a,” Malakia 3:8, 9.

Mi huan kan hman hawhsak chuan hman hawh man kan pe ṭhin a, mî in kan luah hawh pawhin luah man kan chawi ṭhîn a ni. Leilung Lalpa, Pathian hnênah hian chûng aia mawhphurhna lian zawk chu kan nei a ni lawm ni? Kan chungthu Rêltu tûr hi RAWKIN, a hnênah rinawmlohna kan lantîr dâwn em ni? Jehova thuneihnate hi a chungnungin a sang ber a, boral phah lovin kan ngaihthah mai mai thei lo a ni.

SAWMA PAKHAT AṬANGA MALSAWMNA

Sawma pakhat pêka kan rinawm chuan Pathianin malsawmna pêk min tiam em?
“‘Ka inah ei tûr a awm theih nân sâwma pakhat zawng zawng chu thawhlâwm inah rawn la rawh u, vân tukverhte chu hawn sak che uin malsawmna dawn sen loh khawp hial ka vûr dâwn che u nge dawn lo, mi han fiah tehreng u,’ sipai hote Lalpa chuan a ti. ‘Eiraltu chu ka khap sak ang che u a, tichuan, in lei rahte chu ka tihchhiatsak tawh lo ang che u a, huana in grep rah chu a hlam tla ral tawh hek lovang,’” Malakia 3:10, 11.
“I sumin Lalpa chawimawi la, i thil lo pung hmasa ber zawng zawngte nên; tichuan i buh in chu a lo khat liam ang,” Thufingte 3:9,10

Heng Bible châng târlante hi Pathian thuhawih kawnga fiahna min pêk ṭhenkhatte lanna an ni a, amaherawhchu, sum leh paia hlawkna kan hmuh theih nâna sâwma pakhat hi pe tûrin min duh lova, kan thiltih kawng tinrêngah:

“KRISTA HMANGAIHNA chuan min TIRLUI a ni” (2 Korin 5:14) ti thei zel tûrin min duh zâwk a ni.

Krista hmangaihna chuan min tirhluih avângin kan thiltih zawng zawngah sum leh paia nasa taka malsawmna min pêk Pathianin A tiam hi chanchin lâwmawm a va ni em!

Zirtu duh tak, Pathian malsawmna dawn emaw, A ãnchhe dawn emaw chu i tan engmah lo a ni dawn em ni? I chunga Pathian ṭhatna hi i suangtuah phâk aia hlu zâwk leh ropui zâwk a ni a, leilung thilte nêna tehkhin phâk rual a ni lo. Â thute hi ngaithla teh:

“Lalpa in Pathian thu in pawm phawt chuan hêng malsâwmna zawng zawng te hi in chungah a lo awm ang a, a zui zêl ang che u. In lam leh ram lamahte chuan malsâwmin in awm ang a; in rîla rah te, in lei rah te, in ran rual pung, in bawngpui leh in berâm rual pungte chu malsâwmin a awm ang a. In vâk chhuak te chu malsâwmin in awm ang a, in lo hawngte pawh malsâwmin in awm ang,” Deuteronomi 28:2-6.

SAWMA PAKHATIN RINAWMNA MIN ZIRTIR

“Tupawh tlemte chunga rinawm chu, tam tak chungah pawh a rinawm ṭhin; tupawh tlêmte chunga rinawm lo chu tam tak chungah pawh a rinawm lo ṭhîn a ni.”Luka 16:10.

Sawma pakhat pêkna hian Pathian thilte leh mahni thilte enkawlnaah rinawmna nei turin min ṭanpui a ni.

SAWMA PAKHATIN AWHNA LAK AṬANGIN MIN HUM

“Fimkhur ula, AWHNA rêng rêng lakah chuan INVENG rawh u; sum ngah hi mihring damchhan a ni lo ve,” Luka 12:15.
“Duhâmna chu milem biakna a ni,” Kolossa 3:5.
“Tangka sum ngainat hi sual tinreng bul a ni si a,”1 Timothea 6:10.

Sawma pakhat pêkna hian Tlantuin kan tâna chhandamna a rawn thlen avânga kan lâwmna leh mi dangte pawhin chu chhandamna chu chang ve se kan tihna a entîr a ni.

Rinawm taka sawma pakhat petute hnênah Lalpan malsâwmna tam tak pêk a tiam a. A rinawm zia chu sang tam takin an mi mal thil hriat leh nunna aṭangin an sawi thei.

Vawi khat chu William-a leh lawng hotu hi kawng laiah ṭhingṭhiin an ṭawngṭai a, William-a chu kum 16 mi, naupang rethei tak a ni a, lawng hotu pa ṭawngṭai chu a ngaithla kiau a. An ṭawngṭai zawh chuan lawng hotupa chuan, “Engtikah emaw chuan New York-ah hian tuemaw chu sahbawn siamtu ropui tak a la ni dawn a ni. Chumi chu nang pawh hi ila ni maithei, sahbawn siam leh sathau khawnvâr siam te i thiam si a, ṭha takin awm la, Krista hnênah i thinlung pe la; LALPA HNENAH A PUALTE CHU PE ZAT ZAT ZEL ANG CHE, i sahbawn siamte chu ṭha tak leh dik taka bûk ni zêl rawh se. ”tiin Williama chu a hrilh a. Chu naupang chu sahbawm lalpa an tih huai huai, William Golgate-a hi a ni; a sahbawn siamte chuh India ram bazârahte pawh hian hmuh theih a ni; Sawma pakhat te, sawma pahnih te, sawma pathum te, sawma pali te, sawma panga te a pe ṭhîn a, a tawpah phei chuan a zavaiin a pe ta hial a ni.

Kawpûr (camphor) an tih siam chhuaktu hmingthang tak pawh hi Rs 3,000,000 lai leibat neia a awm lai pawha rinawm taka sâwma pakhat petu a ni. He thil duhawm tak, sâwma pakhat pêkna hi malsâwmna hlu takin a umzui zêl a ni.

Thlarau malsawmna hi malsawmna ropui ber a ni a, Mi ṭhenkhatin daktawr ropui tak Grenfell-a nu an kawm ṭumin, a hnênah, “I fapa in Labrador tâna a thiltih hi chhuang em em tûr i ni e,” an ti a. Ani chuan, “Labrador-in ka fapa tâna a thiltih hi a ropui zaw mah a sin,” tiin a chhang thung a ni. A ni, sâwma pakhat pêknaah pawh hian rinawm taka petuten malsâwmna an dawn hian an sawma pakhat pêkin mihringte emaw, Pathian emaw hnêna ṭhatna a thlen aiin a ropuiin a nasa zawk zêl a ni.

SAWMA PAKHAT HMANLAI AṬANGIN

Bible-ah hian khawilaiah nge sawma pakhat chanchin kan hmuhna hmasak ber?
“Salem khaw lal Melkisedeka chuan chhang leh uaiin a rawn la a; ani chu Pathian Chungnungbera puithiam a ni a. Ani chuan Abrama chu mal a sawm a. ‘Lei leh van neitu Pathian Chungnungbera Abrama chu malsawmin awm rawh se; i hmêlmate i kuta petu, Pathian Chungnungbera chu, fakin awm bawk rawh se’ a ti a. Abrama chuan ani chu engkim chu sawma pakhat a pe a,” Genesis 14:18-20.

Hemi hnu rei vak lovah Abrahama tupa Jakoban Pathian hnênah hetiang hian thutiam a siam bawk:

“Jakoba chuan, ‘Ka pa ina thlamuang taka ka hawn lehna turin Pathian ka hnênah a awm a, he ka kalna kawngah hian min vêng a, ei tûr chhang leh silh tûr silhfên min pêk dâwn chuan Lalpa chu ka Pathian a ni ang a; I mi pêk tûr zawng zawngah chuan SAWMA PAKHAT chu ka PE NGEI ANG CHE,’ tiin, thu a tiam ta a,”Genesis 28:20-22.

Hêng Bibl châng thu pahnih aṭangte hian sawma pakhat pêkna chu mihringte sual tirh aṭang leh mihring tlanna tûr ruahman a nih aṭang khan a lo awm tawh a ni tih a lang.

THUTHLUNG THAR PUITHIAM HNA CHHAWMDAWLNA

Kristian rawngbawltute ei hmuh nân Pathianin sawma pakhat hi a ruat em?
“THIL SERH BUATSAIH VELTUTE CHUAN PATHIAN BIAKIN AMI THIL (sawma pakhat leh thawhlawm) AN EI LEH, maicham rawngbawltute chuan maicham nên chanvo an nei tih in hrelo em ni? Chutiang takin Lalpa chuan Chanchin Ṭha hriltûte chu Chanchin Ṭha chu an EI HMUH NAN a ruat a ni,” 1 Korin 9:13,14.
“Chutah chuan Isua, Melkisedeka awmdan anga chatuana Puithiam Lalber lo ni tawhin.” “Abrahaman engkimah sawma pakhat a pêk ve bawka kha,” Hebrai 6:20; 7:2.

Levia thlahte zînga puithiam hna chang te, Melkisedeka awmdân ang zuitute chu mipuite sawma pakhat pêka ei hmu an ni a, (Number 18:21-24) chu mi thu chu a ni kan châng tarlan hmasak zawka Paulan a sawi kha.

Tirhkoh Paulan uar taka Krista Puithiam hnathawh thu leh chu mi azara malsawmna ropui tak lo awm thu a sawi rual hian Abrahaman Melkisedeka hnêna sawma pakhat a pêk thua sawi nghal a, Isua chu, Melkisedeka awm dân anga chatuana Puithiam Lalber a nih thu uar takin a sawi bawk. Chutichuan, sawma pakhat dân hi englai pawha khawvêl ram tina awm reng tûr a nihzia târlan a ni.

“Nangni lehkha ziaktu leh Pharisai vervê te u, in chung a pik e! pudina te, mohuuri te, zira te chu sawma pakhat in pe ṭhîn a, dân thu pawimawh zawk felna te, khawngaihna te, rinawmna te chu inngaihthah leh si ṭhîn; nimahsela chung chu a sin in tih tûr ni, A DANG CHU TIH ṬHULH CHUANG LOVIN,” Matthaia 23:23.

Thil te tak te te a sawma pakhat ang pêknaah hian dem an hlawh a ni lo va, fak an hlawh zâwk a ni. Felna te, khawngaihna te, rinawmna te an ngaihthah avâng erawh chuan demawm an ni. Mi ṭhenkhat chuan sâwma pakhat dân hi Levia puithiamte rawngbawlnaa bei ve, Isua thih ruala ral ta zînga mi kha a ni a, kan zawm a ṭul tawh lo ve, an ti a ni thei e. Amaherawhchu, sâwma pakhat dân hi Levia hun hma daih, Melkisedeka hun lai pawha lo awm tawh a ni. Krista chu, ‘Levia awm dân anga’, tih ni lova, “MELKISEDEKA AWM DAN ANGA PUITHIAM LALBER” tih a ni zâwk. Chuvangin, kan Puithiam Lalber atana Krista a din chhûng hi chuan sawma pakhat hi kan pe ngêi tûr a ni. Puithiam rawngbãwlnaa thil inthlâk (Levia aṭanga Krista ) a awm mai avângin rinawm taka sawma pakhat pêkna, Pathian chu engkim Neitu a nih hriattîrna hi a danglam chuang lova, pêk kawnga mihringte mawhphurhna hi a la ngai reng a ni.

He khawvêla hnathawh azawnga hna ropui ber leh huap zau ber, Chanchin Ṭha rawngbãwlna Enkawltu, kan Lalpa hian khawvêl ram zawng zawng huapa A hnathawh hi tluang tak leh fel taka tikal zel tûrin kawng ṭha tak a nei tûr a ni reng e. “Lei leh vâna thuneihna zawng zawng ka hnêna pêk a ni tawh. Chutichuan kal ula, hnam tina mîte zirtîrahte siam ula,” tia a thupêkah hian khawvêl pumpui huapa a kohhran lungphûm chu a nghat a. Chu kohhran huang chhunga sum dahna inah chuan A mite chuan sâwma pakhat leh thawhlâwmte an rawn la ṭhîn tûr a ni. Rinawm taka A miten sâwma pakhat an rawn pêk phawt chuan malsâwmnain a lo vur zel ang.

PATHIAN SUM HNAR DANG

Sawma pakhat aia tam pe tûrin Pathianin min beisei em?
“Mihringin Pathian ta chu a rûksak tur em ni? Mahse nangni chuan min ruksak ṭhîn. Nangni erawh chuan ‘engahte hian nge kan rûksak che?’ in ti a, sâwma pakhat leh thil hlanahte hian. Ânchhia lawh in ni ta; nangni he hnam pumpui hian kei hi min rûksak si ṭhîn a.” Malakia 3:8, 9.
“Lalpa chu a hming ropuina bel ula, thil hlan tûr keng ula, a hmaah lo kal rawh u; thianghlim mawina inbelin Pathian chu chibai bûk rawh u,” 1 Chronicles 16:29.

Thawhlâwm hi sâwma pakhat kan pêk hnua kan thil pêk a ni. Sâwma pakhat hi chuhu Lalpa ta liau liau a nih avângin mahni tâna kan vawn chuan Pathian rawk kan ni lo thei lovang. Pathianin pêk tûra a ruat ngheh hi kan pe hmasã ang a, chumi hnuah thawhlâwmte chu kan pe chauh tûr a ni.

Lalpa hnêna kan thawhlâwm pêkte hi duhthlanna hmanga kan pêk a ni a, nimahsela kan duhthlanna hmanga pe kan ni tih tilang tûrin phal taka pe tûrin min beisei a ni.

“Lalpa in Pathianin malsâwmna a dawntîr che u ang zêlin mi tinin an theih ang tâwk an pe tûr a ni,” Deuteronomi 16:17.

Pathian chuan kan tân A neih zawng zawng A pe a, Amah ang bawka thil phalna chu kan nei ve dawn lawm ni?

PETU RILRU PUT DAN

Engang rilru pu chugnin nge sâwma pakhat leh thawhlâwmte hi kan pek ang?
“Theh tlem chuan a ât tlem bawk ang a, theh tam chuan a ât tam bawk ang. Mi tinin ui chunga pe lovin, pêk loh theihloh anga ngâi hek lovin, an thinlunga an tum ang zêlin pe ṭheuh rawh se; Pathian chuan HLIM TAKA PETU a ni a HMANGAIH ni,” 2 Korin 9:6, 7.

Sâwma pakhat pêkna hian mimal rinawmna a entîr a, thawhlâwm hian a thil phalna a lantîr thung a. Hmeithai thawhlâwm Isuan a hriatpui kha. Pathian ngaiha thil pêkin hlutna a neihdân tilangtu ṭha tak a ni. Engzat nge? Tih a ni lova, rilru putdân te, hmangaihna te leh inpumpêkna tea innghat a ni zawk.

PATHIAN SUM IN

Khawi hmunah nge sâwma pakhat leh thawhlâwmte chu dah luh tûr?
“Sâwma pakhat zawng zawng chu thawhlâwm inah rawn la rawh u,” Malakia 3:10.

Sâwma pakhatte leh thawhlâwmte hi miin a duh ang anga a hman ral tûr an ni lova, Pathian sum in, A kohhranah rawn thlen hmasak phawt tûr a ni. Sâwma pakhat pêk tûr nei rêng rêngte chu englai pawhin a tihdân hmang hrilh kan inpeih reng e.

He Pâwl, Pathian leh Mihring Ṭangrual Pâwl hi Pâwl thianghlim tak a ni a, a thil tumte pawh a sâng thei ang ber—thlarau chhandamna a ni. Chu Pâwl atân chuan Pathian leh Krista chuan an neih zawng zawng an rawn thawh ta a, keini pawhin, a hlâwkna dawngtute kan nih avâng hian, hlim takin kan neihzâwngte chu kan thawh ve dâwn lâwm ni?
