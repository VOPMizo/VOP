ZIRLAI PAKUANA

KROS-IN HNEHNA A PHUR CHHUAK

Châng Thlan: “Nimahsela, Pathian chuan a mi hmangaihna chu fak tlâkin a lantîr a, misualte kan la nih laia Krista kan aia a thih avâng khan,” Rome 5:8.

PATHIAN chu HMANGAIHNA a ni; A nungchang te, a dân te, a ram te pawh HMANGAIHNA vek an ni. Nimahsela, chutiang taka Pathian awmdân chu khawvêl hian a va hlat nasa tak êm! Mi tam takte chuan Pathian hi khawngaihna rêng rêng nei lo, nawm leh mâka mihringte hrem ṭhin niin an ring.

EMMANUELA

Isua Krista pian hma kum zasarih laiah khan, ‘Isua chu nula thianghlim hrin a ni ang a, a hmingah Emmanuela an sa ang,’ (Isaia 7:14) tiin lo hrilhlâwk diam tawh a ni. Emmanuela chu “PATHIAN KAN HNÊNAH A AWM” (Matthaia 1:23) tihna a ni a, chu chu mihring tisaa Pathian a inthuama lei mihring sualte zînga a khawsakna chu a ni. He leia a lo kal chhan pakhat chu mihringre hnêna Pa nungchang târlan a ni.

A nunna chu a ṭha a, mihring tlu tawhte tâna felna tuikhur kang chat ngai lo a ni. Amaherawhchu an TÂN THI LO selang chuan, chu felna chu misualten dawn theihna rêng an nei lo vang. Isua Krista thihnaah hian a ni hmangaihna chu chiang lehzuala a lan ni. “Pathianin khawvêl a hmangaih êm êm a, chutichuan A Fapa mal neih chhun a pe a, amah chu tupawh a ring apiang an boral loh va, chatuana nunna an neih zâwk nân,” (Johana 3:16).

PATHIAN BERÂM NO

Isua, Messia chu Johana'n tunge a ni a tih?
“En teh u, Pathian Berâmno, khawvêl sual kalpuitu tûr saw,” (Johana 1:29).

Zirlai hmasaa kan zir tawh ang khan, rana inthawinate kha Israelten Pathian an biakna thil pawimawh tak a ni. Tirhkoh Paula pawhin “bâwngpate leh kêlte thisen chuan sualte tibo thei rual a ni si lo,” (Hebrai 10:4) tiin min hrilh. Chuti a nih si chuan inthawinate chu eng atân nge ni? Pathian inhlanna lo awm tûra rinna mite thinlunga chhêm alh rengtu atân a ni.

Krista kha engtik lai khan nge talh tawh anga sawi a nih ṭhin?
“Leilung pian laia talh tawh Beramno,” (Thupuan 13:8).

PATHIAN FAPA chu MIHRING TISAA INTHUAMIN A LO LANG ANG A, misualten an thisen hlu avânga tlanna an hmuh theih nân BERAM NO anga HLAN a la ni ang, tih chu leilung pian hma pawhin lo ruat lâwk diam tawh a ni.

Chuvângin, berâm no anga Isua Krista hlanna chu ruat lâwkna pawh awm lêm lova eng emaw tia lo thleng palh ve mai a ni lova, lo ruahman diam tawh anga lo thleng a ni zâwk e. Nun dân ropui tak nin tawh avâng emaw, chak lohna avâng emawin thihna A tuar a ni lova, mârtar chang pawh a ni hek lo. TLANNA THLEN TÛRIN MISUAL AIA THIH AMAH NGEIIN A THLANG TA ZÂWK A NI!

LEIA A LO KAL HMASAK LEH A LO KAL CHHAN

Isua Krista'n he leia a lo kal chhan enge a ni a tih?
“Mihring Fapa hi… mi tam tak TLAN NÂNA A NUN PE TÛR ZÂWKA A LO KAL ANG KHÂN,” (Matthaia 20:28).

Misualte tlan tûra lo kal a ni tih Krista'n a hai ngai lo rêng rêng a. MIHRING TLAN NÂN a nun a pe ngei tûr a ni. Chu mi chanchin chu a zirtîrte hnênah pawh vawi tam tak a hrilh nghe nghe a ni (Matthaia 16:21 en la).

Hnam hrang hrang tân Pathianin Tlantu hrang a ruat sak zêl em ni?
PATHIAN PAKHAT CHAUH a awm a. Pathian leh mihring inkâra PALAI PAKHAT chauh a awm bawk a, amah chu mihring a ni, Krista Isua, mi zawng zawng tlan nana inpea chu; chu chu a hun tak zêla hriattîrin a awm tûr a ni,” (1 Timothea 2:5, 6).
“Keimah hi KAWNG LEH THUTAK LEH NUNNA ka ni; Keimaha kal lo chu tumah Pa hnên an thleng ngai lo,” (Johana 14:6).

Isua Krista thihna chu khawvêl zawng zawng tân hlâwkna a ni tih rinhlelhna rêng kan neih loh nân mi thianghlim Johana, Lalpa kiang hnai chêna khawsa reng ṭhin chuan, “Ani chu kan sualte thupha chawina pawh a ni,” (1 Johana 2:2) tiin min hrilh.

Mithianghlim Petera pawhin vân ram luh theih nana thil pawimawhte a sawinaah, “Midang tuma hnênah chhandamna a awm lo; vân hnuaia mihring sak zîngah MIN CHHANDAM TÛR HMING DANG RÊNG AN AWM LO,” (Tirhkohte 4:12) tiin min hrilh bawk.

Sual hian khawvêl zawng zawng a fan chhuah vek avângin sual laka tlanna pawhin khawvêl zawng zawng a huap tûr a ni.

PATHIAN LEHKHABU THUCHAH BULPUI BER

Tirhkoh Paula sawi angin Isua Krista chanchin ṭha lairil ber enge ni?
“Isua Krista, Amah ngei khenbeha awm thu chauh lo chu in zîngah engmah rêng hriat ka tum loh kha,” (1 Korin 2:2).

“Pathian lehkhabu thuchah bulpui ber innghahna chu Isua Krista thihna chu a ni. Mihring tisa pua a lo lan chhan ber chu mihringte sual thawina atan A thih theihna tûrin a ni. Tirhkoh Paula chuan tlanna “thuruk” chu “Krista hausakna chhui chhuah rual loh, a tîr ata Pathian Isua Krista–A engkim siamtu hnêna thurûk chu a ni,” (Efesi 3:8, 9) tiin a puang a.

Zâwlnei Isaian Isua Krista thihna tuar tûr thu eng angin nge a lo sawi lâwk?
“Nimahsela, ani chu kan bawhchhiatnate avângin hliamin a awm a, kan khawlohnate avângin vuak thitlinin a awm a; kan thlamuanna tûra thununna chu a chungah a tla a, a vuakna vualtea tihdamin kan awm ta. Kan za hian beram angin kan vâk bova; mahni kawng kawngah kan pêng ṭheuh va; Lalpa chuan Ani chungah chu kan zaa khawlohna chu a nghat ta a,” (Isaia 53:5, 6).

Isaia bung 53-na hi Isua pian hma kum zasarih laiah khan ziak a lo ni tawh nain, zâwlnei hian thil thleng diam tawh chanchin sawi ang maiin a ziak a. Isua Krista thihnaa thil thlengte chu an thlen famkim hma daihin a lo sawi lâwk a ni.

Zâwlnei Isaia hian Judate biakbûk rawngbâwlna chanchin a hriat chian pui êm êm a. Pathianin sual simte chu ran lu chunga an sualte puang tûr leh thiamlohna rêng nei lo ran chungah chuan nghat vek tûrin a duh a. Tichuan misual ran rawn kaih chu, sual tlan nân tihhlum a ni ṭhîn.

Zâwlnei hian chûng rana inthwina zawng zawngte chu Pathian inhlanna Ropui, chu mi thisen chauha sual tlanna awm thei hlimthla, chauh an ni tih chiang takin a hria. Isua Krista chuan kan zaa bawhchhiatna chu a chungah a nghat a, kan tâna tlanna hna chu a hlen theih nân hliam leh vuak thitlin leh tihhlumin a awm a, kan thlamuanna tûra THUNUNNA chu a chungah a tla ta a ni.

ISUAN THIHNA A TUAR DÂN

Isua Krista thihna leh mârtarte thihna danglamna enge ni?
“Ka Pathian, ka Pathian, engah nge mi kalsan?” (Matthaia 27:46).

Mârtarte chuan taksa thihna chauh an tuar ṭhîn. Khawvêl chanchin kan en chuan mârtar chang mêkten thi tûra nghaisak an nih laia Pathian fakna hla an sak thute kan hmu a. Felna avânga tuar an ni tih an chhia leh ṭha hriatna thiangin a hriattîr avângin, thihna darkâr hnuhnung berah pwh, hnehtu anga inhriatna lian tak an nei ṭhîn a ni.

Isua Krista thihna erawh chu a va danglam nasa êm! Rinna leh hmangaihna hi hnehtu a nihzia lanna a ni a, amaherawhchu taksa thihna chauh a ni love. Isua Krista'n a chunga thute khi a sawi chhuah lai khan MI ZAWNG ZAWNG TÂNA THIH HNIHNA, SUAL MAN THIHNA chu a tem nghâl a ni. Chatuan aṭanga a Pa nêna lo khawsa reng ṭhîn, mihringa a lo chan avânga channa lian tak vânin a neihzia chiang taka hretu chu A PAIN A KALSAN TA RIH A NI. Chu aia thil râpthlâk chu engmah a awm thei chuang lo.

SUAL MAN A PE

ZANGAIHNA lâk kian a nih huna misualte chunga LUNGNGAIHNA la thleng tûr chu Krista chuan a tuar a. Sual avânga Pa thinurna a chunga tla chuan a no inlai mêk chu a tikha nasa êm êm a, chu no chuan Pathian Fapa thinlung a tikeh ta hial a ni.

Miten thihna chu an pumpelh theih nân Isua Krista chuan a lo tuar ta a ni. Nimahsela, Isua Krista chu mi mal Chhandamtu anga pawmna takzet a awm chauhvin thihna chu pumpelh theih a ni.

Isua thihna chu HMANGAIHNA INPUAN CHHUAHNA ROPUI a ni. Misualte tlan nân Pathianin inhlanna ROPUI ZÂWK a siam thei chuang lo va, misualte tân pawha CHHANDAMNA kawng awm CHHUN a ni bawk. Khawvêl chanchina thil pawimawh ber a ni a, misual zawng zawng huap phâkin a zau va, nimahsela, mi malin a duh thlanna a pawm a nih chauh loh chuan chhandamna chu hmuh theih a ni chuang lo vang.

Kalvari Tlânga Krista thihna chuan Setana tâwp hlenna tûr a ruat fel ta a, Pathian nungchang leh Setana nungchang a târlang nghâl bawk.

THAWHLEHNA, CHHIAR SÊN LOH TÂNA BEISEINA

Isua thihna kha nun ṭhaa nungte tâwp dân ṭhin anga ngaih ni selang chuan mipui chhiarsên loh, Amaha rinna lo nei ṭhînte hnênah ngei pawh beiseina rêng a thlensak lo vang. A thawhlehna chauhvin a thihna thurûk hriat thiamna min pe thei a ni. Misual thih ai a thi a, mihring tlanna ruahmanna kawng chu zawh chhuakin, he chhandamna kawng mak tak mai hi hlen chhuak tûrin a tho leh ta a ni.

Isua thihna aṭanga tûk thum tûkah, ngun taka thlân an vên khupna hmunah, thil enge lo thleng?
“Ngai teh, LÎR NASA TAKIN A LO NGHÎNG a, Lalpa vântirhkoh vân ata a lo chhuk a, lung chu a rawn lum sawn a… Vântirhkoh chuan, ‘Hlau suh u! Isua an khenbeha kha, in zawng tih ka hria e. Hetah a awm tawh lo ve; a sawi ang ngeiin A THAWHLEH TAWH KHA,’ a ti a,” (Matthaia 28:2-6).
Isua thihna leh thawhlehna chungchânga Kristiante rindân engang nge ni?

Kristianten an rin chu hêngte hi an ni:

  1. ISUA KHÂN THIHNA A TUAR TAK TAK A NI.
  2. Mithi dangte ang bawkin A RUANG (taksa) kha thlânah phûm a ni.
  3. Tûk thum tûkah THIHNA ATA A THO LEH.
  4. A THAWHLEH HNU-ah vânah a lawn hma ni sawmli chhûng lai zirtirte hnênah leh mi dangte hnênah VAWI TAM TAK A INLÂR.
  5. Chu mi zawhah Pathian kut ding lamah A CHHO TA A NI.

Thawhlehna han tih awmzia hi thihna aṭanga nunna neia Isua a lo thawhlehna hi a ni ber a. Thlân chhûnga an zalh tawh hnuin nunna neiin a lo tho chhuak leh a ni tih hi Kristiante chuan nghet takin an ring tlat a ni.

THUTHLUNG HLUI HRIATTÎRNA

Krista'n a thawhlehna hriatpuitu atân enge a hman?
“Hetiangin ziak a ni: ‘Krista chuan hrehawm a tuar ang a, ni thum niin mithi zîng a ta a tho leh ang, ama hmingin simna thu leh sual ngaihnathiamna thu Jerusalem khuaa ṭanin hnam tin hnênah an hril ang,’ tiin. CHÛNG THIL ZAWNG ZAWNG CHU A HRETU IN NI E,” (Luka 24:46-48).

A thawhlehna aṭanga ni sawmnga a tlin hmain tirhkoh Peteran mipui sang tam tak tling khâwm hmaah thawhlehna thu a tlangaupui ṭan a. Isua thawhlehna nemnghettu leh fiahtu atân Thuthlung Hlui a hmang a. Chu mi ni la la chuan THLÂN a lo RUAH TÂKNA CHHAN a sawi chu, mi sângthum laiin an rinphah ta a ni (Tirhkohte 2:14-41).

Hêng zirtîrte hi hriatpuitu rintlâk an ni hlawm em? Rin tlâk an nih chuan Isua Krista thawhlehna paih thlâk tuma hnialna zawng zawngte hi rintlâk loh vek an ni thung ang. Tichuan, zirtîrte hi hriatpuitu rinawm leh dik an nihzia leh, Isua thawhlehna chanchin dikzia nemngheh nân, hêng thil pathumte hi kan rawn târlang e:

  1. Thuthlung Thara chanchin ziak chhuahte ngun taka zuk chhûtna leh enfiahna.
  2. Zirtîr hmasaten nun ṭha an neih theihna.
  3. An hun lai aṭanga tûn hun thlenga Kristiante chanchin kal zêl hi.

Hêng thil pathumah hian zirtîrte chu hriatpuitu dik tak an nihzia leh, Isua thawhlehna dikzia hmuh theih a ni.

THU HRETU HMASATE

Hriatpuitu hmasaten hamṭhatna an neih bik chu enge ni?
“Chutichuan, Lal Isua kan zînga lût ṭhin chhuak ṭhina a awm chhûng zawng– Johana baptisma chan a chin, kan hnên ata vâna hruai chhohva a awm ni thleng khan–hêng min pawl ṭhinte zîngah hian mi pakhat thawhlehna min hriatpuitu atân a awm tûr a ni,” (Tirhkohte 1:21, 22).

Hêng zirtîrhote hi Isua nên, he leia rawng an bawl lai khan an khawsa tlâng dial dial ṭhin a. Chuvângin an thil hriat phâk ngei chanchin tlângaupuitu an ni a, rinhlelhna a awm tûr a ni lo ve.

Zirtîrten Isua Krista thawhlehna chungchânga an thil hriatte engtin nge an ziak?
“Chu tirhkohte hnênah chuan hrehawm chu a tuar hnuin hriatna tak takin nungin a inentîr a, ni sawmli zet an hnênah a inlâr a, Pathian ram thu a sawi ṭhin a,” (Tirhkohte 1:3).

Chanchin Ṭha pali: Matthaia, Marka, Luka leh Johana-te hi Isua thawhlehna thlâwptu kan neih ṭhat berte an ni a, an za hian kros lêra Isua thihna thu te, Josefa, Arimathai khuaa miin Isua ruang a dîl thu leh thlâna a zalh tâk thute an ziak vek a. Matthaia leh Marka hian thlân kawngkâa LUNG LIAN PUI a awm thu an ziak. Matthaia te, Marka te, Luka-ten hmeichhe ṭhenkhat thlân en tûra an kal thu a ziak a. Puithiam lalte leh Farisaiten Pilata hnên aṭanga vêngtu sipai dah tûr leh, lung chu chhinchhiah tûra an dîl hlawhtlin thu chu Matthaia chauhin min hrilh.

Nimahsela, chuta ṭanga atûk thum tûkah chuan thlân chu a ruak ta huai mai le. Hmun hrang hranga kan hmuh angin, Krista chu Mari Magdalini hnênah te, hmeichhe ṭhenkhat hnênah te, Petera hnênah te, Emmau khaw pana zirtîr pahnih kalte hnênah te, Galili dîl kam zirtîr pasarih hnênah te, Galili tlânga unau zanga hnênah te; Jakoba hnênah te, vân a lawn dâwna zirtîr sâwmpakhat hnênahte a inlâr a. Chutichuan, Krista chu, a thawhleh hnuin, Paula hnêna a inlar tiam lovin a tlêm berah VAWI SÂWM LAI a inlâr a ni. Hmun hrang hranga inlâr niin a lang nghe nghe. A hmasa ṭhenkhatte khi a thawhleh nîa lo thleng vek an ni.

Kan hmuh tâk ang hian thawhlehna hriatpui an nihna finfiah leh zual tûrin hriatna an neih belhchhah zêl a. Tûn hmaa an hmuh fo tawh ṭhin Krista hmêl an HMUH LEH TÂK avângin AN MITTE CHU HRIATPUITU A NI A. A âwka thute an ngaihthlâk leh tâk avângin AN BENGTE CHU HRIATPUITU A NI. A khawngaihna duhawm tak leh thûk takin an chhûnglam nunnate a rawn CHAWH PHÛR-sak avângin AN THINLUNGTE CHU HRIATPUITU A NI BAWK. Rilru leh thlarau lam thlenga hriatpuitu nihna an nei hi thil pawimawh tak a ni.

THAWHLEHNAIN HUAISENNA LEH THILTIHTHEIHNA A THLEN

Engvângin nge Juda rorêltuten Petera leh Johana mak an tih?
“Petera leh Johana huaizia an hmuhin, lehkha thiam lo mi leh hnamchawm mai an ni tih an hriatin, mak an ti hle a, Isua hnênah an awm tawh tih an hre ta a,” (Tirhkohte 4:13).

A Lalpa phatsantu Petera nunna chu a va danglam nasa ta êm! Petera chu hriatpuitu hmasate zînga pakhat ve mai a ni. Chung hriatpuitute chuan HLIM HMÊL PU RENG CHUNGIN THIHNA AN TÂWK A, Isua mithi zîng ata a thawhleh thu an phat ngai hek lo. Dâwt hmangte leh bumhmangte angin an khawsa ngai lova; tihduhdahna hnuaia khawsak dân te, leh nungchang kaihhruai nana an inzirtîrna sang takte chuan chiang taka an thil hriat chanchin tlângaupuitu an nihzia a tilang chiang êm êm a ni.

Isua thawhleh aṭanga kum sawmhnih panga hnuah khân Kristian kohhran hnênah eng thucah nge Paulan a ziak?
“A hmasa berin, ka hmuh ve bawk chu ka hrilh chhâwn che u kha; Krista chu Pathian Lehkha Thu ang zelakan sualte avânga a thih a, an phûm a Pathian Lehkha Thu ang zêla ni thum nia KAIHTHAWH a awm a, Kifaa (Petera) hnênah a inlâr te kha; chu mi hnuah chuan unau zanga aia tam hnênah a inlâr leh a, CHU MI ZÎNGA TAM ZÂWK CHU TÛN THLENGIN AN LA DAM CHEU VA, ṭhenkhat erawh chu an muhîl ta a,” (1Korin 15:3-6).

Hriatpuitu hmasate thu hril hi phat rual lohvin enfiah theih an ni a. Mite pawhin thawhlehna thu chu an pawm hmain a dik leh dik loh enfiahna an nei hmasa te te ang tih kan hre thei a ni. Isua thih lai leh thawhleh lai khân nasa takin lîr a nghîng a. Lîr a nghîn leh nghîn loh chu awlsam taka enfiah theih a ni. THLÂN A LO RUAH TAK HUAI THU kan hmu. Hetiang hi a nih avângin hriatpuitu hmasaten “A tho leh ta ngei a ni” tih chanchin danglam tak mai an tlângaupui avânga mi sang tam takin an lo ring ta kha a mak lo ve.

Kum sawmhnih leh panga lai hnuah khân tirhkoh Paulan, Isua a tho leh ngei a ni tih HRETU MI ZANGA deuh thaw an zînga la damte an awm thu a sawi chhuak a. “A tâwp berah chuan… keima hnênah pawh a inlâr,” (1Korin 15:8) tiin hriatpuitu dik tak a nihzia a puang bawk.

KHAWVÊL CHANCHIN THU TIHTLÛK

Mithi zîng aṭanga thawhlehna ni aṭang khân, Kristian dik tak mi maktaduai tam takte hnênah, Krista thawhlehnain a THILTIHTHEIHNA a rawn thlen chu hmuh theih a ni. “Krista nangmahnia awm, ropuina beisei chhan” (Kolosa 1:27) avângin hrehawmna leh manganna tâwp khâwk pawh tawrh an huam zêl a ni. He khawvêla khualzin hahte kalna kawng ên zêltu chu vân aṭanga Chhandamtu Isua Krista, mithi zîng ata kaihthawha awm, rawn INLÂRNA rinna a ni.

Khawi hmunah pawh mîten Chhandamtu, khenbehna awma mithi zîng ata kaihthawhleha chu, an rin phawt chuan, nungchang leh chetze kawng tinrêngah siamtharna mak tak an nei ṭhîn a ni.

ENFIAHNA KHAIDIAT A INZAWM KIM

Isua Krista chu mithi zîng ata a tho leh ta ngei a ni tih chu phat rual a ni lova, a fiahnate pawh hi hnâwl mai theih leh ṭhiat mai theih an ni lo. Hmâsâng aṭang tawhin, mi fing pui puiten ‘thawnthu phuahchawp mai mai a ni e,’ tiin finfiah an lo tum tawh ṭhin a, nimahsela, chanchin dik, sawi nghîn theih rual loh a nihzia chiang takin an chhar chhuah phah fo zâwk ṭhîn.

Chutichuan, Isua Krista thawhlehna chuan fiahna khaidiat zawm kimin, ISUA CHU CHATUAN AṬANGA PATHIAN FAPA A NIHZIA LEH, A THIHNA CHU MIHRINGTE TLAN NÂN A NIHZIA A LANTÎR A. Zirtu duh tak, i CHHANDAMTU LEH ka CHHANDAMTU a nihzia pawh a lantîr bawk. Kut pharin he thu hi A puang chhuak e: “Kei hi kawng leh thutak leh nunna ka ni, keimaha kal lo chu tumah Pa hnên an thleng ngai lo,” (Johana 14:6). Tûnah hian, “Ka hnênah lo kal ula, keiman ka chawlhtîr ang che u,” (Matthaia 11:28) tiin a sâwm reng che a ni.
