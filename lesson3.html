ZIRLAI PATHUMNA

THUA THILSIAMNA

Châng Thlan: “A tîrin Pathianin lei leh vãn a siam a,” Genesis 1:1.

Pathian hi awm tak meuh a nih leh nih loh i ngaihtuah tawh ngai em? Chutianga ngaihtuahna chu thil thar a ni lova, tûn lai erawh hi chuan tûn hma lam deuhte aiin miten an ngaihtuah nasa zâwk a ni. Pathian chu awm ngei a nihzia leh, mihring thiltihahte hian A kaihhruaina kut chu englai pawhin a lang a ni tih hi chiang taka hmuh theih leh hriat theih reng a ni. Tûn lai ang hian ringhleltu hnênah Pathian hmangaihna leh thiltihtheihna chanchin hrilhfiah theihna hun remchâng a la awm ngai âwm love.

Pathian awm i rinhlelh deuh chuan khaw thian zâna arsite an dêt chuai laiin dâk chhuak la, chung lam boruak zau taka arsi zozaite khi pakhat khatin chhiar chhin teh ang che. Entlang i neih phei chuan hmang la, mit lâwnga i hmuh phâk loh arsi vân boruak zau takah khian tuin nge han dah a, tuin nge an hunbi dik taka her vêltîr? tiin inzâwt bawk teh ang che.

Sana chhûnga thil kual inher vêl te, lungmantam ṭhenkhat awm te leh a lîte hi anmahnia inchuktuah khâwmin sanaah an lo insiam ta mai em ni? A siamtu kut themthiam tak awm lovin, anmahni thuin mihring dam chhûng daih hiala hunbi dik vawng tûrin an invuah khâwm mai em ni? Chutiang thilte mah siamtu awm lova kan rin si loh chuan engtin nge vân boruak zau taka hunbi dik tak vawnga, arsi inher vêl zawng zawngte khi siamtu awm lova kan rin theih ang? Thil siamte hian ngawi rengin fakna hla an sa chhuak ṭhin a sin:

Ngawi rengin an au dial dial e,
Aw râwl duhawm taka puang vêlin,
Chatuana êng chunga zai vâwrin,
“Min siamtu kut chu Pathian a ni si,” an ti.

Pathian leh a thil siam zawng zawng chanchin hi dik taka hriat leh, chu miah chuan kan rinna kan nghah tlat a mamawh êm êm a ni. He mi thua buaina a awm chuan thil dang dangah pawh buaina a awm zêl ang.

THIL SIAMTE HIAN AMA DUHZAWNGA THIL ENGKIM KALTÎRTU A AWM TIH AN LANTÎR A

Bible-a kan hmuh angin Pathian chu thawh tur nei lova awm hle hle mai lova, A duhzâwng ti tûra vântirhkoh thianghlim, a sînga sîng hmun sîng tam tak leh, a sâng a sâng hmun sâng tam tak hual vêlin hmun sâng leh thianghlimah a awm zâwk a ni. Lei buaina chung lamah khian Pathian chu rorêlin a ṭhu a, thil engkim hi a hmu tlang vek a; a chatuan hmun ropui tak aṭangin A duhzâwng ang zêlin engkim hi a kaltîr a ni.

LEI AWM ṬANNA

Pathian Thu Thianghlim ziak chhuah inṭanna thu pawimawh takte chu engte nge?

“A tîrin Pathianin lei leh vãn a SIAM a.” Genesis 1:1.

“Pathianin lei leh vân a siam (bara) a.” He thu pawimawh tak hi Bible bu pum puiah hian a inzam chhuak vek a. Hebrai ṭawnga bara (siam) tihin a kawh leh awmzia tak chu ENGMAH LO AṬANGA THIL SIAM CHHUAH tih hi a ni. He khawvêl siamnaah pawh hian thil engemaw awm sa aṭangin Pathianin a siam chhuak a ni lova, thil engkim, hmuh theih leh kuta khawih theih thilte, thlarau lam thilte zawng zawng pawh Lalpa Jehova thu aṭanga lo piang chhuak vek an ni a, A remruat angin, thil engmah lo aṭangin siam chhuah vek an ni.

Chu chu mihringte tân hriatthiam tak tak theih loh, thil inthup leh thurûk a ni a, mihring hnênah puan chhuah a ni lo. Chungnungbera thurûkte chu mihring finna hian a zawng chhuak zo lova, Ama awm dân an hrefiah zo lo ang bawkin thil siam thurûkte pawh chu an hre thiam sêng lo a ni.

Genesis 1:1 thu awmzia hi thil siam zawng zawng hi Pathian an ni vek a lãwm, tihna angin mi ṭhenkhat chuan an ngai a; nimahsela he châng thu hian Pathian leh thil siamte chu chiang takin a thliar hrang zâwk a. Thil siamte rêng rêng hi a tak aṭanga riruang nei sa diama rawn indin chhuak an ni lova. A âwka thuin, engmah lo aṭangin, kan awmna lei ngei pawh hi A siam chhuak a ni.

Thiamna lama mi ropui tak pakhat chuan hetiang hian a ziak a, “He thil siam chanchin ziak chhuaha awm hian Pathian mizia te, thiltihtheihna te, pumkhatna te a tilang a; Pathian pakhat aia tam awma rinna leh chatuan aṭanga thilte thi lo awm ve rêng anga rinna a hnâwlzia a tilang bawk. Pathian pakhat chauhin engmah lo aṭangin thil A siam chhuak a, Amah chu thil engkim awm hma pawha lo awm tawh a ni. Thil rêng rêngte hi awm lo thei lo an nih avanga lo awm ta mai nia ringtu mi ṭhenkhat an awm. Nimahsela, chutiang rinna chu Genesis 1:1 thu kalhmang nên a inpersan nasa hle mai. Chatuan aṭanga lo awm tawh Pathian chuan A duhzâwng ang zêlin thil engkim hi a rawn din chhuak a ni si a." – Outline Studies, Moorehead, p. 20.

Eng hmangin nge Pathianin thil siamte hi a rawn din chhuahtîr?

“Lalpâ THUIN vânte chu siam a ni a; chûnga awmho zawng zawngte pawh chu A KAA THAW chuan siam an ni bawk a. . . . Ani chuan a SAWI a, a lo awm ta a; THU A PE A, a lo awm nghet ta si a.” Sâm 33:6, 9.

Engmah lo aṭanga Pathianin thil siam chhuak thei lova rinna hi Pathian a nihzia fiahna pawimawh ber thlauhthlâkna a ni.

THIL SIAM ROPUI TAK

Vãn lam chanchin zirnain enge min hriattîr?

“Chung lamah khian in mitte lên chho ula, KHINGTE KHI TU SIAM NGE ni en rawh u; ani chuan sipaihote khi indawt zelin a hruai chhuak ṭhîn a, an (arsite) hmingin a ko ṭheuh ṭhin a; A CHAKNA NASAT avang leh, THILTIHTHEIHNA LAMA A CHAK avangin pakhat mah kim lo an awm ngai lo.” Isaia 40:26.

Arsi ngaihtuah miten min hrilh angin arsi ṭhenkhatte chu an awmna a hla êm êm a, kan awmna lam hawi zâwnga an êngte chu sekhawn khata mel nuaihnih deuhthawa chaka an lo kal chung pawhin kan awmna lei hi an lo la thleng rih lo a ni. Chuti khawpa arsi ṭhenkhatte nêna kan inkar hlatzia chu suangtuah phâk tunge awm ang le?

Mihring ngaihtuahna leh rilruin a chhui chhuah theih rual loh a ni reng a ni. Amaherawhchu, Siamtu kut chhuak chanchinte kan ngaihtuahnaah hian lungawi tâwkna leh rilruin hlâwkna a hmuh theih dân kawng pakhat a awm a; chu kawng awm chhun chu he zirlai hmabâk zêla thutak tarlan awihna leh pawmna a ni.

RINNA PAWIMAWHZIA

Thil siam thurûkte chu engtin nge kan hriat thiam theih ang?

“RINNAIN KHAWVELTE hi PATHIAN THUA SIAM FEL DIAM a ni tawh tih kan hria, chutichuan thil hmuh theihte hi thil langtheitea siam a ni lo. . . .Nimahsela, RINNA LOVIN AMA LAWM ZAWNG MI NIH RUAL A NI LO; Pathian hnêna lo kal chuanin, ani chu a awm tih leh, a zawngtute hnêna lawmman pêk hmang a ni tih rin tûr a ni.” Hebrai 11:3, 6.

Tirhkoh Paulan a sawi ang hian miin Pathian thil siam thurûkte a hriatthiama a pawm theih dâwn chuan RINNA a nei tûr a ni. Naupang têin a nu leh pa chanchin rinna a a pawm ang hian keini pawhin kan Pa vâna mi aṭanga chhuak kan nihzia hi rinnain kan pawm mai tûr a ni.

Mi ṭhenkhat chuan chiang taka chhût chhuah dim dêm theih leh fiah theih thilte chauh lo chu awih leh rin an tum ngai lo. Nimahsela, an ṭhenawm khawvêngtea rinna an neihzia erawh chu chhût chhuah seuh an tum ngai kher si lo. Ṭhenawm khawvêngte nêna khawsak tlân dial dialna min neihtîrtu chu inrintawnna hi a ni. Thlarau lam thil hriat thiam nâna RINNA neih a mamawhzia chu tirhkoh Paulan min hrilh bawk a.

Miten chhang siamtuah rinna an nei; a chhang siamah chuan thihna tûr a phul lo ang tih an ring a ni. Rêl kal hun leh thlen hunah te rinna an nei bawk; rêl chu a lo thleng ṭhin tih leh a kal chhuak leh ṭhin tih ring lo ni selang chuan rêl chhuah dâwn leh thlen dâwn te hian rêl chawlh hmun panin an kal ngai lovang. Kâwla ni chhuak chhiara hnathawk tûra chhuak ṭhinte pawh hian thla tâwpah chuan hlawh hmuh tûr a awm ang tih an ring ṭheuh a ni. Ni tin khawsakna thil zawng zawngte hi rinna a innghat vek an ni. Kâwlphe thiltihtheihna te hi mihringten an la hrethiam tak tak lova, nimahsela chu thilah chuan rinna an nei tlat si ang hian Pathian leh A hnathawh chanchinte chu hrethiam vek mah suh ila RINNA kan nei mai tûr a ni. Zãn thim taka kâwlphe khawnvãr, a hmeh ênna a i han hmeh hian êng a lo chhuak ang tih i ring angin Amah chu ring la, A kut chhuak thilahte chuan Amah chu i hmu fiah thei ang.

KUT CHHUAK THILIN SIAMTU AN PUANG CHHUAK

Thil siamte hian enge an hriattîr?

“Vãnte khian PATHIAN ROPUIZIA AN HRIATTIR A; boruak zau tak khian a KUT CHHUAK CHU A LANTIR ṭhin a. . . Thu sawi rêng rêng leh ṭawng rêng rêng a awm lova; an ãw chu benga hriat theih a ni lo.” Sâm 19:1, 3.

A THIL HMUH THEIH LOH (a chatuan thiltihtheihna leh a PATHIANZIA CHU) khawvel siam tirh ata FIAH TAKA HMUHIN a awm a, THIL SIAMAH chuan a LANG a ni; chuvang chuan chhuanlam tur an nei lo.” Rom 1:20.

He kan chênna leia nungcha tinrêngte a siam bakah hian Pathianin ni leh thla leh, khawvêl dangte leh arsite A siam bawk a. Zãnah pâwnah chhuakin arsite khi thlîr vêl teh. An siamtu chawimawiin an de sèp sèp hlawm a ni. Pathian chu a awm ngei a ni tih arsite aṭanga hriat theih loh khawpa ram thim khawvêlah hian a awm lo. Leilung thil zawng zawng, hnimhnah te, rannung te leh nunna nei lo thilte hian lo chhuahna bulpui ropui tak pakhat an nei ṭheuh tih an tilang vek a ni.

Sana te, motor te leh khâwlchhutna te hian kum maktaduai tam tak chhûnga engemawti mai maia anmahnia lo inchuktuah khâwm khep khepa lo piang chhuak ta hlawl angin tuinnge sawi ngai le? Kut chhuak thilte rêng rêng hian ruahman hmasatu leh siamtu an nei vek tih kan rin si chuan, he khawvêl leh a chhûnga zawm zawng zawngte pawh hian Ruahmantu leh Siamtu an nei tih chu kan ring thei dâwn lâwm ni?

Arsite ngaihtuahtu hmingthang tak pakhat, Sir James Jeans chuan kum tam tak chhûng ngun tak leh fimkhur taka thil siam chanchin a zir hnuin hetiang hian a sawi chhuak ta a: “Thil engkim mai hian eng hun lai khân emaw, hun chhui phâk loh ni si lovah, engmah lo aṭanga thil siam chhuahna a awm a ni tih chiang takin an lantîr vek a. Tûna an awm dân aṭanga han chhût hian anmahni ringawta lo piang chhuak thei zawng an ni lo tawp mai” tiin. Wider Aspects of Cosmology, p. 55.

Mi fing hmingthang dang pakhat, Sir Oliver Lodge pawhin, “Kan lo awm chhuah dân leh khawvel lo pian chhuah dân hi engmah lo aṭanga thil siam chhuahna ngaihtuahna tel lo chuan kan hre thiam thei ngai lo vang. Engmah lo aṭanga thil siamnaah chuan rilru hmanna te, tumna te, leh ruahmanna te a awm zêl a, chutianga thil siam chhuahna pawh chu a awm ta rêng bawk a ni,” tiin a sawi.- The Great Design, p. 231.

Leilung leh a chhûnga thil awmte, ni leh thla leh arsite zawng zawng hian thil siam thei tûra fiahna leh thiltihtheihna nei MI PAKHAT a awm a ni tih an zain an puang ṭheuh a ni.

Thil siam zawng zawng famkim biaina—mihringte inkungkaih tlân dân mak tak te, nungcha tinrêngin chêt theihna an neih dân te, thisen zâm vêl an neih dân te, rilru leh hriatna chi hrang hrang an neih dân te, ruhrel leh khawsak dân bîk an neih ṭheuh dân te, an ei zawn dân te leh thlai chi zawng zawng famkimna mak tak hian siamtu remruatna leh ruahmanna famkimzia an tilang chiang êm êm a ni.

Ruahmantu ropui, leilung leh a chhûnga thil awm zawng zawngte, ni leh thla leh arsite Siamtu engkim Bul chu PATHIAN kan tih hi a ni.

THIL SIAMNAA PATHIAN THIL TUM

Eng vângin nge Pathianin lei hi a siam?

“Lalpa vãn siamtu chuan (ani chu Pathian, leilung hi dina siamtu chu a ni; ani chuan leilung hi a tinghet a, ENGMAHLOVAH A SIAM LO VA; MIHRING AWM NAN A DIN ZAWK A NI) heti hian a ti si a: “Kei hi Lalpa chu ka ni; a dang rêng rêng an awm lo.” Isaiah 45:18.

Lei hi mi thianghlim leh felte chênna tûra siam a ni a, mi chêngte awm ta hlek lo ni selang chuan a thlâwn maia siam a ni ang a, siam a nihna pawh chu thil âtthlâk tak a ni bawk ang. Eng pawh nise, he lei hi dinin a awm a, ngaihtuahna leh finna neite khawsak nâna siam a ni tih chu sawi tam ngai lovin a hriat a ni.

MIHRING AWM ṬANNA

Mihring hi engtianga siam nge a nih?

“Pathianin, “Kan anpuiin, keimahni ang takin mihring siam ila . . . a ti a.” Gen. 1:26.

“Lalpa Pathianin LEIA VAIVUTIN mihring a siam a, a hnârah chuan NUNNA THAW chu a thaw lût a; tichuan, mihring chu MI NUNG lo ni ta a.” Gen. 2:7.

Hetah hian mihring awm ṭanna chu chiang taka târlan a ni a; fiah tâwk tak leh chiang tâwk taka ziak chhuah a nih avangin hriatthiam loh tûr leh, ngaih sual tûr pawh engmah a awm lo. Pathian chuan Ama anpui ngeiin mihring a siam a, chu chu thurûk a ni love. Thlai aṭangin emaw hniam zâwka nung, rannungte aṭangin emaw zawi zawiin, pianphung danglam chho hret hretin mihring hi tûna a awm dân ang hian a rawn inlet chhuak a ni tia ngaihruatna a awm tûr a ni lo. Chutianga ngaihruatna neite chuan Siamtu kut chhuak ropui takte hi lei mihringte kut chhuak tluk lekah an ngai tihna a ni. Mihringte hian leilung leh a chhûnga thil awm te, ni leh thla leh arsite chunga thuneihna chungchângah Pathian an hmaih ṭhin avângin a tîra mihring dinhmun duhawm tak chu an pawt hniam tial tial zêl a ni. Chung lama arsi khawvêlte siam a, lei chunga pangpâr mawi tak takte rawng chi hrang hrang neia siam a, lei leh vânte khi A thiltihtheihnaa luah khahtîrtu chuan, lei mawi tak chunga rorêl tûrin thil siam zawng zawnga ropui ber leh sâng ber, nunna nei mihring A siam bawk a ni. Chutichuan, Pathian thâwk khum lehkhabua kan hmuh angin kan thlahtu bul chu rannung tê tak tê emaw, chhmêk emaw, ke pali nei te emaw an ni lova, Siamtu ropui tak chu a ni zâwk e. Ani chuan Ama anpui ngeiin mihring a siam a, chu chu hmun danga hriatna famkim nei fâwm chu a ni. (Efesi 4:24; Kolosa 3:10 enla.)

Mihring taksaah hian ngun taka mi thiam leh mi fingten an en fiah hnuin, leia thil awmte hi a kim biai a ni tih an hmu a, chu chuan mihring hi mi nung a lo nih hma chuan leia vaivut mai a ni tih Bible-in a sawi hi a nemnghet a ni.

MIHRINGTE AWM CHHAN

Eng vangin nge Pathianin mihring a siam?

“Nang, kan Lalpa leh kan Pathian, ropuina te, chawimawina te, thiltihtheihna te chu hmu tlâk i ni e; NANGIN ENGKIM I SIAM A, I DUH AVANGA awm leh siam a ni bawk si a.” Thupuan 4:11.

“Ka fapate chu hla tak aṭangin rawn hruai ula, ka fanute chu kâwlkil aṭangin rawn hruai rawh u; ka hming pu apiangte leh KA ROPUINA TURA KA SIAM apiangte chu.” Isaiah 43:6, 7.

“Tichuan, PATHIANIN AMA ANPUIIN MIHRING A SIAM A, Pathian anpui ngeiin a ni a siam ni; mipaah leh hmeichhiaah A SIAM A NI. ‘Chi tam tak thlaha, lo PUNGIN, leilung hi LUAH KHAT ula, in thu thuin AWMTIR rawh u; tuifinriat sangha chungah te, chunglêng sava chungah te THU NEI bawk rawhu u,’ a ti a.” Genesis 1:27, 28.

Mihring hnêna nunna pêk a nih hma daihin he lei hi siam a ni tawh tih chu chiang takin a lang a. Kan nu leh pa hmasa ber (Evi leh Adama) siam an nih khân thil siam hna chu zawhin a lo awm ta a ni.

Mihring hi siam a lo nih a, he lei chunga thuneitu atâna ruat a lo nih khân Pathianin chawimawina leh ropuina a khumtir a, vântirhkohte aia hnuai deuh chauhvin a siam a. (Sâm 8:5)

A nunphung chu Pathian duhzâwng nêna inremin a awm a, a rilru leh ngaihtuahna chuan Pathian thilte a hrethiam thei a, a duhnate chu a thianghlim bawk a. A taksa duhna leh mamawh thilte chu a thunun a, Pathian anpui a nihna chu hlim tak leh thianghlim takin a kengkawh zêl a, Pathian duhzâwng tih kawngah thu awihna famkim a nei bawk a.

Sualnain a tlâkbuak hmaa mihring awm dân tûra Pathian remruat, “Lo pungin leilung hi luah khat ula,” tih a ni. Pathian anpui, anmahni ang bawka thianghlim leh fel, inthlah chhawng zêl tûra ruat an ni.

PATHIAN VANTIRHKOHTE

Pathian lalṭhutphah vêlah tutenge awm?

“Ka han en a; lalṭhutphah leh Thilnungte leh upate vêlah chuan VANTIRHKOH TAM TAK aw ka hria a; an tam lam chu A SANGA SANG HMUN SANG TAM TAK an ni.” Thupuan 5:11.

Vântirhkohte tam lam chu chhiarsêng loh a ni. (Hebrai 12:22) Pathian chauh hi amaha lo awm sa tawh reng a nih avângin vântirhkohte pawh hi mihring ang bawka engmah lo aṭanga siam chhuah an ni. Amaherawhchu, mihringte aia chungnung deuh zawka siam an ni a. (Sâm 8:5) Pathian tâna midangte rawngbawltute an ni bawk. (Hebrai 1:14)

VAN LAMA AWMHOTE

Khawvêl danga mi chêngte chanchin Bible-in engtinnge min hrilh?

“Nangmah chauh hi Lalpa i ni a, vânte leh a chhunga awm zawng zawngte nên, leilung leh a chhunga awm zawng zawngte nên, tuifinriat leh a chhunga awm zawng zawngte nen i siam a, a zain i vawng ṭha ṭhin a.” Nehemia 9:6.

He châng thu hian kan khawvêla mi chêng zawng zawngte hi Pathian chhûngkaw ropui taka tlêm tê chauh kan ni tih min hriattir a. Isaia 40:15 tehkhinthu ang chuan leia chêngte hi “Bâlṭina tui far khat ang lek” emaw, “Bûknaa thil dip bâng kai ang lek” emaw nên tehkhin an ni. Vân boruak zau taka Pathian thil siam dang tam takte chu Nehemia 9:6–ah hian VANA AWMHOTE tiin ziak a ni.

Ram zau êm êm, a zauzia sawi hleih theih lohahte chuan Pathianin a vântirhkohte a tîr chhuak reng a, A fantir fo ṭhin. Arsi ropui tak takte khian ni angin an vêla herte an nei a. Chûngah chuan finna sâng tak nei, mi chêng an awm a ni tih hi a rinhlelhawm loh a ni. Vân boruak zau taka cheimawite dah thliah thluah ringawt chu Pathian tâna tihâwm pawh niin a lang lo, mi chêngte an awm ngei a rinawm a ni.

“Pathian FINNA leh HRIATNA ngahzia chu a va thûk êm! A rorêlnate chu HRIAT THEIH RUAL LOH leh, a KAWNGTE CHU CHHUI THEIH RUAL A VA NI LO EM!” Rom 11:33.

I SIAMTU HRERENG RAWH

Solomonan engti tûrin nge râwl tharte a ngen?

“I VANGLAI HIAN I SIAMTU HRE RENG RAWH.” Thuhriltu 12:1.

Nunna lawi lohzia leh khawloh ṭhinzia te chiang taka a hriat tawh hnuin khawvêla mi fing ber, Solomona chuan hêng thute hi ṭhalaite tân a sawi chhuak a. Isaiah 51:13 a kan chhiar, “Lalpa anmahni Siamtu, vân kaipharha leilung nghahchhante phûmtu, THEIHNGHILHA” tih hi kan zînga mi tam zâwkte awmdân sawina a nih a rinhlelhawm love. Mahni Siamtu theihnghilh hi thil râpthlâk a va ni tehlul êm!

Tun laia rawltharte hi mihring livir chuan a man ṭan mêk a. Pathian chu thil engkim Siamtu leh leilung chunga Rorêltu a ni tih rinna hi mihringte finna pêng hrang hrang, Pathian chu Siamtu a nihna kalh zâwnga zirtîrna leh, mihring hi siamtu awm chuang lova eng emawti mai maia chhuak ve mai a ni tia zirtîrnain a rawn luahlân ṭan mêk bawk a. Siamtu, Engkimtitheia zahna leh ṭihna thianghlim paih thlâkna rah chu mihring nunnaa khawlohna leh bawlhhlawhnate an ni.

I TANA KAN DUH

Zirtu duh tak i rilrua Pathian i lo ngaihdân ṭhin chu eng ang pawh lo ni sela, tûnah hian Ani chu Siamtu Ropui, leilung Dintu leh Bible Thianghlim Ziaktu a ni tih hi i lo ring ve dâwn lâwm ni? MIHRINGIN CHATUAN THUTAKA THLUN NGHEH TLAT A NIH THEIH NANA zirtirna a mamawh êm êm chu Bible Thianghlim hian a târlang a; tin, Bible thutak zawng zawng innghahna chu Siamtu Pathian a ni. Chu thurin pawimawh tak chu, a kila lung ṭangkai ber a ni angin, Bible thurin pawimawh zawng zawngte suih khâwmtu leh kam nghettu a ni. Thil engkim hi Pathian thiltihtheihnaa siam a ni tih kan rin loh chuan Bible thurin pawimawh zawng zawngte chu engmah lovah kan chantîr tluk a ni ang. Thil siamte leh Pathian inzawm dân chungchângah hian ngaihdân leh rindan dik tak neih a mamawhzia i lo hriat thiam kan duh a ni.
