ZIRLAI SAWMHNIH PANGÂNA

SEVENTH-DAY ADVENTIST THURIN BULPUITE

Châng Thlàn: “He ram Chanchin Ṭha hi hnam zawng zawng hriattîrna tûrin khawvêl zawng zawngah hrilhin a awm ang; chu mi zawhah chuan tâwpna chu a lo thleng ang.” Matthaia 24:14.

He zirlai hian Seventh-day Adventist-te thurin bulpui, a tlângpuiin kum1950 leh a hnu lama an lehkhabu (official directory) a an chhut ang chu a rawn târlan sak dâwn che a ni.

BIBLE-A RINNA

1. Seventh-day Adventist-tena rinna leh, thiltih tehfung, dik lohna nei ngai lo an pawm tlat chu enge ni?

1) Thuthlung Hlui leh Thuthlung Thar thu zawng zawngte hi Pathian thâwk khuma pêk an ni a, mihringte tâna A duhzâwng chu hêng bûahte hian a chuang kim a, rinna leh thiltih tehna, dik lohna rêng nei lo chu a ni (Johana 5:31; Sâm 119:105).

“Pathian lehkha thu tinrêng, Pathian thâwk khuma pêk tawh phawt chu, zirtir kawngah te, thiam loh chantîr kawngah te, zilh kawngah te, felna zirtîr kawngah te a sâwk bawk a ni.” 2 Timothea 3:16.

PATHIANA MI PATHUM

2. Pathian chungchângah Adventist-ten enge an rin?

(2) Pathiana Mi Pathumte chu: a) CHATUAN PA, mi awm ngei, thlarau Mi, Engkimtithei, Engkimhria, Hmuntina awm, finna leh hmangaihna tâwp nei lo nei te; b) Lalpa ISUA KRISTA, Chatuan Pa Fapa, amah azâra engkim hi siam leh amah azâra tlante chhandamna chu tihfamkim tûr te; c) THLARAU THIANGHLIM, Pathian Mi Pathumna, tlanna hna ropui taka piantharna thlentu te hi an ni. (Matthaia 28:19; 1 Korin 8:6; 1 Johana 5:11,12; 15:26).

“Chutichuan, kal ula, hnam tina mi zirtîrahte siam ula, Pa leh Fapa leh Thlarau Thianghlim hmingin baptis ula.” Matthaia 28:19.

ISUA KRISTA, MESSIA CHU

3) Isua Krista, Pathian Fapa chu Pathian a ni a, Chatuan Pa awmdân leh nihna ang bawk nei a ni. Bethlehema A lo pian hma pawh khân Pathian nên an lo awm tawh rêng a, khawvêl leh a chhûnga awm zawng zawngte Siamtu a ni. “Ani chu khawvêlah a awm, khawvêl ama siam a ni.” Pathian a nihna chu vawng rengin mihring awmdân hi a rawn inbel a, leiah hian mihring angin a lo chêng a, kan entawn atân a nunnaah chuan felna dânte a lantîr a; Pathian nêna an inzawmzia thilmak tam tak a tihin a lantîr bawk a; krawsah kan tân a thî a, thihna aṭang chuan a tho leh a, Pa hnênah kan tâna min dîlsakin a awm ta reng a ni (Johana 1:10; Hebrai 1:1,2, 10; Kolosa 1:16-19; Johana 1:1-3, 14; Hebrai 2:9-18; 8:1, 2; 4:14-16; 7:25).

“Chutichuan, amaha Pathian hnêna lo kalte chu a tâwp khâwk thlengin a chhandam thei bawk a ni, anmahni ṭawngṭai sak tûrin chatuana a nun reng avângin.” Hebrai 7:25.
“Ani (Isua Krista) chu khawvêlah a awm, khawvêl hi ama siam a ni,” Johana 1:10.
3. Pianthar hi engtianga pawimawh nge?

4) Miin chhandamna a chan theih nân piantharna a nei tûr a ni; chu piantharna chu Lalpa Isua Krista zâra Pathian thiltihtheihna avânga nunna leh nungchang zawng zawng siam tharna a ni. (Johana 3:16; Mathaia 18:3 Tirhkohte 2:37-39; Johana 3:3; Galatia 6:15; 2 Korin 5:17)

“Isuan a chhâng a, ‘Tih tak meuhvin, tih tak meuhvin ka hrilh a che, mi tupawh tuiah leh thlarauva an pian loh chuan Pathian ramah an lût thei lo vang,’ a ti a” Johana 3:5.

BAPTISMA DÂN

4. Baptisma thua Seventh-day Adventist-te rin dân enge ni?

5) Baptisma hi Kristian Kohhran dân pakhat a ni a, sual simna leh ngaihdamna a zui tûr a ni. He dân zawmna hian Krista thihna, phûmna leh thawhlehna rinna a lantîr a, baptisma chan dân dik tak chu inhnim phûm a ni. (Rom 6:1-6; Tirhkohte 16:30-33; Marka 16:15, 16; Kolosa 2:12).

“Petera chuan an hnênah, ‘Sim ula, in sual ngaihdam nân Isua Krista hmingin baptisma chang ṭheuh rawh u; tichuan, Thlarau Thianghlim pêk hi in hmu ang,’ A ti a.” Tirhkohte 2:38.
“Chuvangin, a thihnaah baptisma kan chanin, a hnêna phûm vein kan awm; Pa ropuinain mithi zîng ata Krista a kaihthawh ang khân, keini pawh nunna tharin kan awm theih nân.” Rom 6:4.

DÂN, MI ZAWNG ZAWNG HMAN TÛR

5. Thu sâwm pêk dân chungchângah hian Seventh-day Adventist-te dinhmun enge ni?

6) Nungchang siam ṭhatna kawnga Pathian duhzâwng chu A thusâwm pêk dânah hian a infûn khâwm a, chûng ṭhatna dânte chu danglam thei lo, eng lai pawha mi zawng zawngten an hman reng tûr a ni (Exodus 20:1-17; Sâm 111:7, 8; 1 Johana 5:2).

“Lalpa dân chu a ṭha famkim a, nunna a siam ṭha ṭhîn;
Lalpa thu hriattîr chu a rinawm a, mi mâwl a tifing ṭhîn.”
“Chûng chu kumkhaw tlaitluan atân tihnghehin a awm a.” Sâm 19:7; 111:8.

7) He dân danglam thei lova thupêk 4-na hian ni sarih ni, Chawlhnî hi serh tûrin a ngiat a. Thil siam hriatrengna leh ringtute tih thianghlimna leh, Isua hnêna kalte tâna thlarau chawlh hahdamna entîrtu a ni bawk (Genesis 2:1-3; Exodus 20:8-11; 31:12-17; Hebrai 4:1-10; Mârka 2:27).

“Chawlhni hi thianghlim taka serh tûr a ni tih hre reng rawh. Ni ruk chhûngin i thawk tûr a ni; a ni sarih ni erawh chu Lalpa i Pathian tân chawlhnî a ni e: chu mi ni chuan hna rêng rêng i thawk tûr a ni lo…ni ruk chhûngin Lalpan vânte leh lei te, tuifinriat leh a chhûnga awm zawng zawngte chu a siam a, a ni sarih nî chuan a lo châwl ta si a; chuvângin, Lalpan chawlhni chu mal a sâwm a, a thithianghlim ta a ni.” Exodus 20:8-11.

DÂN LEH KHAWNGAIHNA INLAICHÎNNA

6. Dân bawhchhiatna sual aṭangin engtin nge mihring hi chhandam a nih?

8) Sual man chu thihna a ni a, thu sâwm pêk dân hian sual chu a târlang ṭhîn a ni. Nimahsela, dân hian dân bawhchhetu chu sual lak aṭangin a chhandam thei lo va, sual lo tûrin thiltihtheihna a pe thei hek lo. Sual laka himna chu Pathianin A hmangaihna leh zahngaihnain kawng a buatsaih zâwk a. Krista, Mi Fel ngei chu mi sual aia thi tûrin A ruat a, “Amahah chuan Pathian felna kan lo nih theihna tûrin, sual rêng rêng hre lo chu Pathianin keimahni avângin sualah a siam ta a ni.” 2 Korin 5:21. Chutichuan, MI CHU DÂN ZAWMNA AVANGA THIAM CHANTÎR NI LO VIN, KRISTA ISUAA KHAWNGAIHNA AVANG ZÂWKIN THIAMCHANTÎR A LO NI ṬHÎN. Miin Krista a pawm hian Pathian nên inremin a lo awm a, tûn hmaa a thil sual tihteah pawh chuan Krista thisen avângin thiam chantîr a lo ni a, Krista chu a chhûnga a lo chên tâk avângin sual thiltihtheihna aṭanga chhandam a lo ni ta a. Tichuan chanchin ṭha chu a ring apiang tân chhandamna tura thiltihtheihna a lo ni ta a ni (Rom 1:16). He thil hi Pathian Thlarau Thianghlim rawn awmtîr a ni a, Sual-Phurtu hnênah chuan a kaihruai a, inlaichînna thuthlung tharah chuan a hruai lût a. Krista awmpuina azâra thiltihtheihna avângin a thinlungah chuan dân chu ziakin a lo awm ta a, tichuan a nunna chu Pathian dân remin a lo awm thei ta a ni. Hetianga piantharna ropui tak mai hi Krista aṭang veka chhuak a ni. (1 Johana 2:1,2,3; Rom 3:20; 5:8-10; 7:7; Efesi 2:8-10; 3:17; Galatia 2:20; Hebrai 8:8-12)

“A nih leh, rin avângin Dân chu engmah lovah kan siam em ni? Hnai lo ve, Dân chu kan tinghet zâwk a ni,” Rom 3:31.

THIH THEIH LOHNA PATHIAN THILTHLÂWN PÊK

7. Thihtheih lohna thua Bible zirtîrna chu enge ni?

9) Pathian chauhin thih theih lohna a nei (1 Timothea 6:16). Mihring thitheite erawh hi chuan an awmdân rêng rêngah, sual lam leh thih theihna an nei a, chatuan nunna chu Kristaa rinna azâra Pathian thilthlâwn pêk liau liau a ni.

“Sual man chu thihna a ni si a; Pathian thilthlâwn pêk erawh chu kan Lalpa Krista Isua zârah chuan chatuana nunna a ni.” Rom 6:23.
“Fapa chu neitu chuan nunna chu a nei.” 1 Johana 5:12.

Krista lo kal leh huna mi fel thi tawhte, thlân aṭanga kaihthawh an nih a, mi fel la damte tihdanglam an nih hunah thih theih lohna hi mi felte hnênah pêk a ni ang a, chutih hunah chuan fela ruatte chuan thih theih lohna chu an sin tawh ang. (2 Timothea 4:8; 1Thess. 4:13-17)

“Ngai teh u, thurûk ka hrilh dâwn che u hi: Kan zain kan muhîl kher lo vang a, nimahsela, kan zain tawtawrâwt hnuhnung ber rîk hunah chuan, rei lo têah, mit khap kâr lovah, tihdanglamin kan awm vek tawh ang; tawtawrâwt chu a ri dâwn si a, chutichuan, mithite chu ṭawih thei lova kaihthawhin an awm ang a, keini pawh tihdanglamin kan awm tawh ang. He ṭawih thei hian ṭawih theih lohna a sin tûr a ni a, he thi thei pawh hian thih theih lohna a sin bawk tûr a ni.” 1 Korin 15:51-53.

10) MÎ A LO THIH HIAN HRIATNA RÊNG RÊNG NEI LO VIN A AWM A, mi ṭha leh mi sualte pawh ang khat vekin an thih aṭanga thawhlehna nî thlengin thlânah an awm ṭheuh a ni (Sâm 146:3, 4; Johana 5:28,29; Sâm 146:4; Joba 14:12-14).

“Thih hnu chuan nangmah hriat rengna a awm si lo va; thlânah tuin nge lâwm thu hrilh dawn che?” Sâm 6:5.
“Mi nungte chuan an thi ang tih an hria a; nimahsela, mithite chuan engmah rêng an hre lo” Thuhriltu 9:5. “Anmahni zînga tam tak, lei vaivuta muhîl tawhte chu an lo ṭhang harh ang a, ṭhenkhat chu chatuan nunnaah, ṭhenkhat chu zahna leh chatuan hmusitnaah.” Daniela 12:2.

THAWHLEHNA PAHNIHTE

8 Mithîte chuan engmah an hriat loh avangin an lâwmman an hmùh theih nân thíl enge mamawh ni ta?

11) Mi felte leh mi fel lote tân thawhlehna a awm a mamawh ta a ni. Mi felte thawh lehna chu Krista lo kal leh hunah a awm ang a, mi fel lote thawh lehna erawh chu chûmi hnu kum sângkhatah, kum sâng rorêl zawhah a awm chauh ang (Johana 5:28, 29; 1 Thes. 4:13-18; Thupuan 20:5-10).

“Hêng hi mak ti suh u, a hun a thleng dâwn ta, chutih hunah chuan thlâna awm zawng zawngin a aw an hria ang a, an chhuak vek ang; thil ṭha titûte chu nunna changin an tho leh ang a, thil sual titûte chu thiam loh changin an tho leh ang.” Johana 5:28, 29.
9. Mi suaksualte tâwp dân tûr thua Bible zirtîrna enge ni?

12) Sual sim lo zawng zawng leh Setana, sual awmtîrtu chu ni hnuhnunga mei chuan awm ngai lo ang maiin a siam anga, tichuan, Pathian thil siam zau tak hi sual leh mi sual zawng zawng lak aṭangin tlenfai vek a ni ang (Rom 6:23; Malakia 4:1-3; Thupuan 20:9,10; Obadia 16:2; Petera 3:7-10).

“Misualte erawh chu an boral ang a, Lalpa dotute pawh phûl hnim duah ang an ni ang. Tihboralin an awm ang a, meikhûah an zâm ral ang.” Sâm 37:20. “Piang lo ang main an awm ang.” Obadia 16.

NÎTIN BIBLE ZIRNA

10. Thlarau lam nunna tiṭhang zêl tûrin enge tih ṭûl?

13) Pathian Thu zirna aṭangin Pathianin kan hnênah thu A rawn sawi ṭhîn a, ênna leh chakna te min rawn pe a, ṭawngṭaina kan neih te hian kan thlarau chu Pathian nên a inzawm ṭhîn a ni. Hêng thilte hi sual donaa hnehtu kan nih theihna tûr leh, Kristian nungchang siam nâna Pathian thil ruatte an ni (Sâm 119:130; 19:7,8; Johana 6:63; 17:17; 1 Petera 2:2; 1Thess. 5:17; Luka 18:1;l Sâm 55:17; Isaia 50:4).

RORÊL HUN THUCHAH

11. Tûn lai hian eng thuchah nge Pathianin khawvêl hnênah a pêk mêk?

14) Lei mihringte chunga rorêlna A thlen dâwn emaw, an awmdân khawih danglam thei thil a thlentîr dâwn emaw rêng rêng chuan Pathianin A puang hmasa zêl ṭhîn a. Chutiang tak chuan Krista lo kal lehna hnai tawh tak pawh hi min rawn hriattîr hmasa a ni. Chu mi thu chu Thupuan 14-ah hian kan hmu a, vantirhkoh pathumte thuchah tih a ni. Hêng vântirhkoh pathumte thuchah hian Krista lo kal leh hun atâna mite an inbuatsaih theih nana siamṭhatna hna awm tûr chu min rawn târlan sak a:

“Leia awmte hnênah leh, hnam tinrêng te, chi tinrêngte, ṭawng tinrêng te, mi tinrêng te hnêna tlângaupui tûra chatuana Chanchin Ṭha nei vântirhkoh dang vânlaizâwla thlâwk ka hmu a; ani chuan, aw ring takin, ‘A rorêl hun chu a lo thlen tâk avangin Pathian chu hlau ula, chawimawi rawh u; vân te, lei te, tuifinriat te, tuihnâ te Siamtu chu chibai bûk rawh u,’ a ti a.” Thupuan 14:6, 7.

15) Thupuan 14 thuchah puan darh rual hian biakbûk tlenfai hna pawh ṭan nghâl a ni a, chu chu rorêl lâwk inṭanna a ni bawk. A hmasain mithîteah ṭan a ni a, chu mi zawhah mi nungteah. He rorêlna hi lei vaivuta muhîl tawh zawng zawngte zîngah tunge thawhlehna hmasaa tel tlâk tûr tih leh, mi nungte zîngah hian tunge tihdanglama awm tlâk tûr ngaihtuahna a ni (1 Petera 4:17, 18; Daniela 7:9, 10; Thupuan 14:6,7; Luka 20:35; 1 Thess. 4:16,17).

“Mifel lo chu, tihfel lohvin awm reng rawh se; mi bawlhhlawh chu, tihbawlhhlawhin awm reng rawh se; mi fel chu, tihfelin awm reng rawh se; mi thianghlim chu, tihthianghlimin awm reng rawh se. Ngai teh, ka lo kal thuai dâwn e; mi tin an thiltih ang zêla pêk tûrin ka lâwmman ka hnênah a awm a.” Thupuan 22:11,12.

HRISêL NÂNA NUNDAN

12. Seventh-day Adventist-te nundân enge ni?

16) Krista zuitute chu Pathian zahtu, he khawvêl nawmsakna leh hlimhlawpna te ṭâwmpui ve lo an ni tûr a ni. Ringtu tinin an taksa hi Thlarau Thianghlim in a ni a, chuvângin, fel takin, mâwi takin leh zahawm takin a chei tûr a ni tih an hre tûr a ni. Chubâkah, ei leh bâr kawngah te, chêtze kawng tinrêngah te mi inngaitlâwm leh thuhnuairawlh, Lalpa zuitu ni tlâka an nunna enkawl fo an ni tûr a ni. Chutiang nunna kawng zawhtute chu ruih theih thil rêng rêng lak aṭangin an invawng fihlîm ang a, taksa leh thlarau lam tichhe thei thil rêng rêng chu an khawih lo vang (1Korin 3:16, 17; 6:19; 9:25; 10:31; 1 Timothea 2:9,10; 1 Johana 2:6; 1 Thess. 5:23).

“Intihsiaknaahte chuan a bei ṭâlh ṭâlh apiang chu ENGKIMAH AN INSÛM ṬHÎN A NI. Anni zawngin lallukhum chhe thei hmu turin an bei a, keini erawh chuan lallukhum chhe thei lo hmu tûrin.” 1 Korin 9:25. “Chutichuan, in ei pawhin, in in pawhin, in tih apiangah pawh, Pathian ropuina tûr hlîrin tirawh u.” 1 Korin 10:31.

RINGTUTE HI SUM ENKAWLTUTE AN NI

13. Pathian chu thil engkim neitu a ni tih kan hre thiam a ni tih engtin nge kan nunnaah kan lantîr ang?

17) Chanchin Ṭha hna enkawl nâna thawhlâwm leh Sâwmapakhat, Pathianin a ruatte hi kan nunna nên lama Pathian ta kan nihzia hriattîrtu an ni a, kan kuta kan neihte hi Pathian min kawltîr chauh a ni a, A sum enkawltute kan nih avâng chuan A hnênah A sum kan enkawl dânte chu kan thlen ṭhîn tûr a ni (Leviticus 27:30; Malakia 3:8-12; Matthaia 23:23; 1 Korin 9:9-14; 2 Korin 9:6-15).

“‘Ka inah ei tûr a awm theih nân sâwmapakhat zawng zawng chu thawhlâwm inah rawn la rawh u, vân tukverhte chu hawnsak che uin malsâwmna dawn sên loh khawp hial ka vûr dâwn che u nge dâwn lo, mi han fiah teh reng u,’ sipaihote Lalpa chuan a ti.” Malakia 3:10.

THLARAU THIANGHLIM THILPÊKTE

14. Engvangin nge Thlarau Thianghlim thilpêkte hi Kohhranah a awm?

18) 1 Korin 12-ah leh Efesi 4-a kan hmuh angin Thlarau Thianghlim thilpêkte chu Pathianin Kohhranah hian a dah a. Chûng thilpêkte chu Bible thu rem thlap zêla kal an ni a, mi thianghlimte tihfamkimna te, rawngbâwlna hna atân te leh Krista taksa siam ṭhat nân tea hman an ni (Thupuan 12:17; 19:10; 1 Korin 1:5-7; Efesi 4:8-13).

CHANCHIN ṬHA HUN TAWNG ROPUI

15. Chanchin Ṭhain a han tawng tûr thil ropui tak chu enge ni?

19) Krista lo kal lehna hi kohhran beisei ropui tak a ni fova, chu hun chu chhandamna ruahman leh chanchin ṭhain a tawng tûr ropui tak chu a ni. Krista lo kal lehna chu hmuh theih ngei, thil awm tak tak tûr a ni a, chu mi rual chuan mithi thawhlehna te, mi sualte tihboralna te, lei tlenfaina te, mi felte hnêna lâwmman pêkna te leh chatuan lalram dinna te a awm nghâl bawk ang. Hrilhlâwkna thleng famkim ṭêp tam takte leh Daniela leh Thupuana hrilhlâwkna thu hrang hrangte hian KRISTA LO KAL LEHNA CHU, “A HNAI TAWH HLÊ” a ni tih an lantîr a, a hun dik tak erawh chu puan lâwk a ni lo. Mihring Fapa hi “in rin loh dârkâra” lo kal tûr a nih avângin ringtu zawng zawngte chu inring renga awm tûra fuih an ni (Matthaia 24:44; Luka 21:25-27; 17:26-30; Johana 14:1-3; Tirhkohte 1::9-11; Thupuan 1:7; Hebrai 9:28; Jakoba 5:1-8; Joela 3:9-16; 2 Timothea 3:1-5; Daniela 7:27; Matthaia 24:36, 44).

“Chutih hunah chuan Mihring Fapa hi thiltihtheihna leh ropuina nasa tak nên chhûm zînga lo kal an hmu ang.” Luka 21:27.
“Kan Pathian ropui tak Chhandamtu Isua Krista ropuina inlârna chu kan nghâk mêk a ni.” Tita 2:13.

KRISTÂ KUM SANG RORÊL HUN

16. Kum sâng chhûngin Kristan khawiah nge ro a rêl?

20) Kum sâng rorêl hun chhûng hi thawhlehna hmasa leh thawhlehna hnuhnung inkâr hun a ni a, chu mi chhûng chuan mi thianghlimte chu vânah an Tlantu duh tak nên an chêng zâ ang a. Kum sâng a vei hnu chuan Khaw Thianghlim chu mi thianghlim zawng zawng te nên leiah hian a lo chhuk ang a. Chu mi rual chuan mi sual thi tawhte chu kaihthawhin an awm ang a, an hotu ber Setana hovin Khaw Duhawm chu lei vâng lam dâpin hual tûrin an inhâwr khâwm ang a, nimahsela, vân aṭangin Pathianin mei leh kât a rawn surtîr ang a, chu chuan a kâng ral vek ang. Setana leh misualte kângtu mei vêk chuan he lei hi bawlhhlawhna tinrêng lak aṭangin a kâng fai ang a, Pathian thil siam ropui tak mai chu sual bawlhhlawh rêng rêng awm tawh lo tûrin tihthianghlim a ni tawh ang. (Thupuan 20:4, 5; Zakaria 14:1-4; 2 Petera 3:7-10).

“Thawh lehna hmasa bera telte chu an engthâwlin an thianghlim a ni; chûngho chûngah chuan thih hnihna chuan thû rêng a nei lo va, Pathian leh Krista puithiamte an ni ang a, amah nên kum sângkhat rorêlin an awm zâwk ang.” Thupuan 20:6.

CHATUANA MI THIANGHLIMTE CHÊNNA

17. Mi thianghlimten khawiah nge chatuan hun an hman ang?

21) Pathianin thil engkim A siam thar ang a. He lei hi a tîra a mâwina ang ngei khân din thar leh a ni ang a, Lalpa mi thianghlimte chuan chatuanin an luah tawh ang. Chutih hunah chuan Abrahama hnêna thutiam, Krista zâra chatuana lei hi rochungtu, nihna chu a thleng famkim tawh ang.

“Ram leh rorêlna leh vân pumpui hnuaia ramte ropuina chu Chungnungbera mi thianghlimte hnênah chuan pêk a ni ang: ram hlun a ni a, awp ram zawng zawngin a rawng an bâwl ang a, a thû an âwih ang.” Daniela 7:27.

Lal Krista chuan ropui takin ro a rêl ang a, lei leh vân leh, tuifinriata chêng zawng zawngte chuan lalṭhutphaha ṭhûa hnênah leh Berâm No hnênah chuan chawimawina te, ropuina te, malsâwmna te, thiltihtheihna te an pe ang (Genesis 13:14-17; Rom 4:13; Hebrai 11:8-16; Matthaia 5:5; Isaia 35; Daniela 7:27).

“Vân thar leh lei thar ka hmu a; vân hmasa leh lei hmasa chu a boral tawh a, tuifinriat pawh a awm tawh lo a ni… Pathian chuan an mit ata mittui zawng zawng a hrufai tawh ang a; thihna a awm leh tawh lo vang a, lungngaih te, ṭah te, nat te pawh a awm leh tawh hek lo vang; thil hmasate chu a ral ta.” Thupuan 21:1, 4.

Hêng Bible châng ṭhenkhatte nêna rawn târlante hi, a tlângpuiin, Seventh-day Adventist-te thurin bulpui deuh deuhte chu an ni. Hêng zirtîrnate hi Bible nêna inrem leh inhmeh thlap vek an ni tih kan rawn târlang bawk e.
