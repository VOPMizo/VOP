ZIRLAI PARIATNA

THISEN LEH CHHANDAMNA

Châng Thlan: “Thísen chhuah lo vin ngaihdamna rêng a awm lo.” Hebrai 9:22.

Lungngaihna zawnga lungngaihna râpthlâk ber chu Evi leh Adaman thlêmtu fing vervêk tak, Setana bâwiha an intukluhnaah khân a lo thleng ta a. An hlimna tûra engkim buatsaih sak an ni chung leh, fîmkhur êm êm tûra hrilh mawlh mawlh an ni chung khân, hmangaihaa khat Pa Pathian chu an hnuchhawn ta a; an pên bona aṭang chuan lei mihringte zînga manganna leh lungngaihna zozaite hi an lo chhuak ta a ni.

Pathianin Evi leh Adama hnênah thei rah ei thiang lo an ei phawt chuan an thi ngei tûr a ni, tiin a hrilh hmasa diam tawh si a, engvângin nge Evi leh Adama chungah khân thihna chu thlentîr nghâl a nih tâk loh? AN AIA THI TÛR, TLANTU HMUHSAK AN NIH TÂK VANG A NI. Leilung nghahchhante phûm a nih hmâ daihin Pa leh Fapa inkârah chuan thuthlung–lei mihringte hi Setanan a rawn hneh hlauh chuan Fapa, Isua Krista chu Chhandamtû-ah a ṭang tûr a ni–tih chu an lo nemnghet diam tawh a (Zekaria 6:13). CHUVANGIN THIHNAIN AN CHUNGAH RO A RÊL TA LO A NI. Ani chu “leilung pian tirha talh tawh Berâm No” a nih avângin mihring lo sual veleh khân thihna an tuar nghâl tûr laka chhandam tûr leh, an thiam lohna leh hremna chu Ama kova nghat a, tlanna siam sak tûrin Pa hnênah a inpe ta nghâl a. Fapain (Isua Krista) a thihnaa sual tân tlanna a siam zet loh chuan sual ngaihdamna rêng a awm thei dâwn lo a ni.

Amaherawhchu, tlanna atâna Pathian Fapa thihna chu kum zabi tam tak ral hmâ loh chuan a thleng lawk lova, chu chuan chhan ṭha tak a nei ngei tûr a ni. He thil pawimawh tak hi thleng lawk lo va hun rei tak a inrial chhûng khân, chu thil lo thleng tûr chu mîten an thlîr theih nân, Pathianin an hnênah entîr nei thil tam tak a pe a ni.

Hêng zirlaite pawimawhzia hi kan hriat thiam a ṭûl tak meuh a, chuvângin, he zirlai leh a dang lo awm zêl tûrte chu ngun taka i lo zir ṭhin kan beisei e.

Pathian Lehkhabu hi tu chanchin nge ni deuh ber?
“Pathian lehkhâ in chhiar ngun ṭhîn, a chhûngah chatuana nunna awmin in rin avângin: CHÛNG LEHKHA CHU KA (ISUA) CHANCHIN HRIATTÎR CHU A NI SI A.” Johana 5:39.
“Mosia leh zâwlnei zawng zawng lehkhaah a ṭan a, Pathian Lehkha zawng zawnga AMÂ CHANCHIN (ISUA) ziak apiang chu anmahni a hrilhfiah zêl a.” Luka 24:27.

Fapain sual laka tlanna siam a huam leh thlan avângin Pathian Lehkhabute hi kan nei a, lei mihring inthlah chhâwng zêlte pawh hi chu mi avâng vêk chuan an awm thei a ni.

Eden Huanah khân Pathianin Evi leh Adama hnênah entîr nei leh zirtîr nei thil enge a pêk?
“Lalpa Pathian chuan Adama tân leh a nupui tân chuan savun kawrfualte a siam sak a, a hâktîr ta a.” Genesis 3:21.

Mihring tlûk hnua Pathian lanna hmasa ber chu Genesis 3:8, 9–ah hian kan hmu; a pawimawhin a mawi hlê mai. Mi sual, Amah bihrûk santu Adama chu rawn zawngin, A rawn ko a ni.

Adama hian chiang taka a thiltih awmzia leh a thu awihlohna avânga thil lo thleng tûrte chu hriatthiamna a la nei lova. Pathianin, “ I ei ni lâ lâin i thi ngei tûr a ni” ti a sual man thihna a nihzia a hrilh pawh khân, thihna chu a la hmuh ngai rêng rêng loh avângin, a awmzia Adama rilrua tihlan fiah nân, inthawi nâna hman, ran vunte chu Adama leh Evi a hâktîr ta a ni. Tichuan, Adaman tûn hmâ zawng zawnga a la hmuh ngai rêng rêng loh, thihna a han hmuh chuan sual râpthlâkzia chu a hmufiah ta uar mai le! A ke bul maiah chuan berâm no, thisen kai hnuang chu nunna nei tawh lovin a let reng a. Nunna neiin a awmleh tawh ngai lo vang a, tûnhmâ angin a lîm zek zek thei dâwn tawh hek lo tih a han hriat chuan, a tân thihna chuan awmze thar a rawn nei ta a. Chutah zet chuan Berâm No, Pathian Fapain a tân thihna a tuar loh chuan a ke bula berâm no thi ta ang chuan, beiseina leh Pathian nêna inzawmna rêng nei lovin a thi ve ngei tûr a ni tih a lo hre thiam ṭan ta a. Chuta ṭang chuan A SAVUN KAWRFUAL HÂK CHUAN A SUALNA A RAWN HRIAT NAWNTÎR CHAMCHÎ TA A NI. Chu chauh pawh ni lovin, SUAL AṬANGA CHHANDAMNA THU PAWH A RAWN HRIATTÎR BAWK A. Pathian Fapa thihna azâra tlanna avângin sual sim lehte chuan felna puan an la sin dâwn tih entîrtu a ni bawk. Mihring thiltihtheihnaa chhandamna chu hmuh theih rual a nih lohzia leh, khawngaihnaa chhandam an nihzia pawh chiang takin târlan sak an ni ta a.

TLANNA ATÃNA THISEN

Rana inthawina hi engtik lai hunah khân nge mihring zîngah a lo awm tawh?
“Rinnain Abela chuan Kaina ai chuan inthawina ṭha zâwk Pathian hnênah a hlân a, chu mi avâng chuan mi fel a ni tih hriattîrna a hlawh a, Pathianin a thilpêkte kawngah chuan thu a hriattîr a.” Hebrai 11:4.

Kaina leh Abela hi mihring hmasa ber, Adama fapate an ni a, an chanchin chu Genesis 4:3-5 a târlan angin hetiang hi a ni:

“Nakinah chuan heti hhi a ni a, Kaina chuan Lalpâ hnêna hlan atân lova mi a rawn la a. Abela pawh chuan a berâm rual zînga piang hmasa berte leh, a thau chu a rawn la bawk a. Lalpa chuan Abela leh a thil hlan chu a lâwm a; Kaina leh a thil hlan erawh chu a lâwm ve lo va.”

“Rinna hi Pathian Thu hriatna avângin a lo awm ṭhîn,” (Rom 10:17). Abela pawh hian hriattîr a nih angin thil hlan atân ran a rawn la a. Kaina erawh chuan Pathian lawm lohzâwng tûr thil hlan a rawn keng thung a. Thil hlan Pathian chibai bûknaa dân khermei tak a nih avângin, chu mi kawnga mihringin Pathian thu awih a mamawh êm êm a ni. Inthawinate leh thil hlante hi mihringin Pathian a en dân leh a rindân hmuh theiha a lantîrna a ni a, chêtzia a sakhua a thlanna a ni. Chûng thil zawng zawng–thil hlan leh inthawinate chu–khawvêl chanchina thil pawimawh ber, Isua Kraws lo entîrtu leh hlimthlâ vek an ni a. Chuvângin, kraws hlimthlâ chu a tak a lo thlen hmâin Thuthlung Hlui hunah chuan hman a ni a, Abela maichâma thil hlan zawng zawng aṭanga Kalhlên Kût hnuhnung ber thlenga thil hlan tinrêngte chuan, an zain Kalvari kraws lam chu an kâwk a. A ni, hun leh kumte zawng zawng pawh hian Krista an khenbehna tlâng, Golgotha hmun chu a hawi vek a ni.

Sual thupha châwina atân engvângin nge thisen hman a nih?
“TISA NUNNA CHU THISENAH CHUAN A AWM SI A: in nunna atâna inremna siamna chu thisen ngei chu a ni si a,” Leviticus 17:11.

Sual hi nuai bo hlen a la nih theih nân sual avânga thihna chu a awm tûrin Pathianin a ruat a lo ṭûl ta a (Rome 6:23). Taksa nunna tungdingtu leh châwmtu ber chu thisenah hian a awm a, chuvângin mihring taksa aṭanga a thisen siak chhuah vek a nih chuan, a nunna lâk sak ang a ni. Nunna atâna thil pawimawh tak a nih avângin hnuk chat lek lek tawhte pawh thisen pêk belh avânga dam khawchhuak ṭha leh an tam ve rêng a ni.

Sual thupha chawi nâna thisen hman a ni hi, hman âwm rêng pawh a lo ni e. Chutichuan Pathian Fapa thihnaa thisen luang chhuak chuan dân bawhchhiatna a thil ngiat chu a rawn hlen famkim a, sual thupha chawina a rawn siam ta a ni.

Amaherawhchu, ran thisen hian entîr a neih avâng chauhin tlanna a thlentîr a. CHHANDAMTUN SUAL TLAN NÃNA A THISEN A FARTÎR TÛR ENTÎRTU CHAUH A NI.

KHAWVÊL CHHANDAMTU

Chhandamtu chu tu thlaha lo piang tûr nge a nih?
“Abrahama hnênah a ni, thu chu tiamin a awm ni, a chi thlah hnênah pawh. Mi tam tak chung thu sawi angin, ‘Chi thlahte hnênah pawh,’ a ti lova; mi pakhat chung thu sawi angin, ‘I chi thlah hnênah pawh,’ a ti a ni zâwk a, chu chu Krista chu a ni,” Galatia 3:16.

Pathianin Abrahama chu a in leh lo awmna Mesopotamia ram–mi lem biakna hual vêla a awmna hmun aṭangin–Pathian nung leh dik chibai bûktu, hnam tam takte Pâ ni tûrin A ko chhuak a. A thlah aṭanga khawvêl Chhandamtu, Pathian-mihring, Isua chu lo piang tûr a nih avângin ‘amahah chuan leia hnam tin an la thawveng’ dâwn a ni.

Aigupta rama hun rei tak chhûng bâwih an tânna aṭanga hruai chhuah an nih hnû khân, Abrahama thlahte hnênah eng thil hlu tak nge lo thleng ta?
“Hêng thute hi, tlângah mei te, chhûm te, thim chhah mup aṭangtein Lalpan aw ring takin in zavaia pung khâwmho hnênah a sawi a, a belhchhah leh chuang lo va. Chu chu lungphêk pahnihah a ziak a, mi pe a.” Deuteronomy 5:22.
“A thuthlung zawm tûra thu a pêk che u, thusâwm pêk chu in hnênah a puang a, lungphêk pahnihah a ziak a.” Deuteronomy 4:13.

Pathianin Sinai Tlânga thu sâwm pêk a puan chhuah khân a ṭihbaiawm thei ang berin a puang chhuak a, mipuite pawhin thih phah thei hial dâwnin an inhre hlawm a ni.

“Mosia chuan mipuite hnênah chuan, ‘Hlau suh u: Pathianin a rawn fiah che u a nih hi, in mithmuha ṭihbaiawma a awmna hi, in sual lohna tûrin a ni,’ a ti a.” Exodus 20:20 (Hebrai 12:18-21 en bawk la).

Dân hi sual min khawhhmuhtu leh Pathian nungchang ziak chhuahna a nih avângin, Pathian ang bawka thianghlim leh ṭha famkim, danglam thei lo a ni. Chu dân bawhchhiatna avâng chuan a ni tlanna siam a lo mamawh tâk ni. Bawhchhiat a lo nih tâk avângin dân chuan thil ngiat a rawn nei ta nghâl a, a thil ngiat pawh chu pêk tlâk a ni tûr a ni.

AN ZÎNGA KA CHÊN THEIH NÂN

Dân thu puan chhuah hnuin Israel fâte hruaitu atâna Pathian ruat, Mosia hnênah eng thu nge lo thleng?
“Hmun thianghlim mi siam sak rawh se, an zînga ka chên ve theih nân.” Exodus 25:8.

A tîra Pathian ruat ang chuan mihring leh Siamtu chu an kâra daidangtu awm miah lova inpâwl ṭhîn tûr a ni a, chu chu Eden Huanah pawh khân awm tûra ruat a ni. Nimahsela, Pathianin thil a lo zam sa chu sualin a rawn khak buai tâk avângin, Eden Huan chhûng ata mihringte chu hnawh chhuah an ni ta a. Thil an lo tihsual tâk avângin lungngaihna leh, Pathian lak aṭanga tihhranna chu an chanpual a lo ni ta a ni.

Amaherawhchu, Pathianin a mite nêna inzawm lehna kawng a buatsaih leh ta. An chênna tûra buatsaih, Eden Huanah chuan khawsa thei tawh lo mah se, anmahni ringawta khawsa tûrin Pathianin a thlahthlam awzâwng lo mai. Paradis-a an khawsak theih tawh si loh chuan tûnhmâ anga hmaichhana Pathian biak leh pâwl theihna chu khawiah nge an hmuh tâk ang le? A, hmangaihna chu a va ropui êm! A mîte ngei han tuithlâr chu Pathianin a duh lova, biak bûkah, an kârah puan zâr tal tlâktîrin an zînga chên a tum a, sual avângin hmaichhanah inbe thei tawh lo mah sela, a theih ang tâwka hnaia zuk pâwl chu a tum tlat a ni. Chu thil chu Isua, Emmanuela (chu chu Pathian kan hnênah a a awm tihna a ni (Matt. 1:23), he khawvela mihring tîsâa inthuam a, kan zînga a lo la chên tûrzia (Johana 1:14) entîrtu mawi tak chu a ni.

THIANGHLIMNA LOVIN TUMÂ’N PATHIAN AN HMU THEI LO

Pathianin Israel fâte hnênah eng thil nge zirtîr a mamawh?
“Mi zawng zawng nêna inremna leh thianghlimna ûm rawh u, chu mi thianghlimna lovin tuma’n Lalpa an hmu lo vang,” Hebrai 12:14.

Pathianin A dân a puan chhuah hmâ leh a puan chhuah hnuin Abrahama thlahte (Israel fâte) chuan ‘Lalpan a sawi zawng zawng chu kan ti ang a, tih âwih takin kan awm ang,’ tiin a rualin an chhâng dual dual a,” (Exodus 19:8; 24:3, 7). Tîsa chak lohzia leh thianghlimna an tlâkchham nasatzia rêng rêng an hre hauh lo; Pathian ṭanpuina lova dân an zawm theih lohzia te an hriatthiam a la ṭûl nasa êm êm a ni.

Ni rei lote hnuah khân mi lem chibai an bûk leh ta dual dual a, dân thianghlima a hmasa ber leh a dawttu chu an bawhchhe ta nghâl a, anhmahni maia engmah an nih lohzia chu chiang takin an hmaah târlan a ni ta. Dânin thianghlimna a ngiat chu an thleng pha lova, nunna petu tûra ruat dân chuan thihna leh thiamlòh chantîrna a rawn thlen sak ta zâwk a, Pathian chauh lo chuan beiseina lâwmawm a pe thei lo a ni.

HLIMTHLÂA CHANCHIN ṬHA

Biak bûk rawngbâwlna chu enge ni?
“Chûngte chuan vân thilte chu a LEM leh HLIM rawng an bâwl ṭhîn a. Mosian biak bûk a sak dâwn laia Pathian hrilha a awm ang khân; ‘Tlâng ENTAWN tûr chu an entîr che ang khân engkim siam tûrin fîmkhur rawh,’ a tih kha.” Hebrai 8:5.

Pathianin Israel fâte chu beiseina rêng rêng nei thei hauh lo tûrin a hnutchhiah lova, Eden Huana berâm no talhin Krista a lo entîr lâwk ang khân, biak bûk rawngbâwlnaah pawh hian thil hlante leh thisen rawngbâwlna hmangin, sual laka tlanna kawng a buatsaih sakzia a zirtîr nawn leh ta a ni.

Biak bûk rawngbâwlnaah hian thil hlan leh inthawina dân khirh tak tak leh, khermei tak tak Pathianin a ruat a. Chu chu Israel fâte tân chanchin ṭha a ni. Pathian an biak leh pâwl theihna kawng kawhhmuhtu a ni a, chûng inthawina leh thil hlante aṭang chuan Pathian hnaih theih dân an zir ṭhîn tûr a ni. Inthawina atâna ran thisen chhuah chuan Krista, Pathian Berâm No thisen zârah Pathian nên inremin an awm thei tih pawh an zir bawk tûr a ni. Chutichuan, an tân hmun thianghlim bera a va kal thei angin anni pawhin Pathian chu an hnaih thei a ni. Israel fâte zînga rinawmte tân chuan he thil hi Isua thisen avânga hmun thianghlim bera Pathian mite an la luh tûr lo entîr lâwktu a ni (Hebrai 10:19). Pathian dân, thu sâwm pêk chuan an sual avângin thiamloh chantîr tawh mah se, Pathian Berâm No chu an tân a la thi dâwn a ni tih an hriat avângin beiseina ropui tak an ni a. Hêng inthawinaah te leh thil hlanahte hian Pathian thianghlimna leh Pathian anna, Kristaa awm chu hmuh theih a ni.

Biak bûk rawngbâwlnaah chuan mihring tlanna innghahna ber, Krista kraws chu a pawimawh ber a ni a, chu krawsah chuan Pathian thutak zawng zawng a inchhûng khâwm a, chu mi aṭang vêk chuan thuthlung hlui awm ṭan tih aṭanga thuthlung thar tâwpna thlengin êng a zâm chhuak a. Chu êng chuan rinna fate hnênah chatuan hmun ropui chu a lantîr a ni. A ni, kraws chuan chu ai pawha ropui zâwk chu a ni rinna fâte hnênah a thlensak a ni. Chutichuan, Pathian hmangaihna chu thil siam zawng zawng hnênah hian a rawn inlâr chhuak a, he khawvêl lalpa, Setana chu paih chhuah a ni a, he khawvêl lalpa, Setana chu paih chhuah a ni ta. Setanan Pathian a mawhchhiatna leh hêkna leh hêkna zawng zawngte chu hnial thlâk an ni a, Vân lam a sawichhiatna zawng zawng pawh tihbo vek an ni. Tichuan, Pathian dân felna leh a danglam theih lohna chu târlan zêlin a awm a, Tlantuah chuan vântirhkohte leh mihringte chu suih khâwm an ni a, Krista kraws chu thil siam zawng zawngte tân fakna hla a lo chang ta a ni.

He lei biak bûk rawngbâwlnaa Pathianin inthawina dân a siamte hian entîr leh zirtîr an va lo nei pawimawh tehlul êm! Kalvari krawsa Isua Krista hnathawh ropui tak leh vân biak bûka Puithiam Lalber anga a rawngbâwlna hlimthla an ni (Hebrai 8:1).

SUAL AWM DÂN

Inthawina dânin a hmasa berin eng nge a zirtîr?
“Sual man chu thihna a ni si a,” Rome 6:23.

He mi thu hi hriat nawntîr fo an ni a; tûk tin leh tlai tinin an hnam pum tân berâm no hlan a ni ziah a; tûk tinin mi malin sual tâna inthawina tûr te, hâlral inthawina tûr te leh lâwm thu sawina turte biak bûkah chuan an rawn la ṭhîn. Hêng thil hlan zawng zawngah hian ran talhna leh a hmun ruata ran thisen thehna a awm zêl bawk. Tichuan, Israel fâte biak bûk rawngbâwlna zawng zawngah chuan, “SUAL MAN THIHNA” a nihzia chiang takin hmuh theih a ni. Sual leh thihna chu ṭhen hran theih loh khawpa inzâwm tlat an nihzia chiang taka zuk hriatna chuan chanchin ṭha ngaihhlutna leh hriat thiamna min neihtîr a ni.

Sual hi taksa ṭhang zêlin a ṭhan bopui mai theih thil a ni lo ve. He khawvêlah hian a dana dan rual lohin a lo lût a, min rawn chîm zo ta a ni. Pathian dân danglam ngai lo bawhchhiatna a ni a, mihring chaknaa hneh theih rual a ni lova, a man thihna leh kan chunga ânchhia a rawn thlente hi Pathian Berâm No, Isua Krista, chauhin min hlih kian sak thei.

SUAL LEH NGAIHDAMNA

11. Pathianin Israel fâte hnêna a zirtîr duh dang enge ni?
“THISEN CHHUAH LOVIN NGAIHDAMNA RÊNG A AWM LO” Hebrai 9:22.

Biak bûk rawngbâwlnaa thil hlante chuan Israel fâte hnênah ngaihdamna atâna man pêk a sânzia a hriattîr a. Sual ngaihdamna hi tlin lohna inhaider sakna satliah mai a ni lova, man nei a ni. Pathian Berâm No nunna ngei chu. Amaherawhchu, mihring thinlungah hian kraws zâra tlanna hi thil pawimawh tehchiam ni lova ngaihna a awm ṭhîn a. Kalvari krawsa tuarna lo pawhin Pathian chuan ngaihdamna min pe thova rinna te a lo chhuak ṭhîn. Nimahsela, thil hlante leh rana inthawinate hian thisen chhuahna lova sual ngaihdamna a awm theih lohzia chiang takin min zirtîr a ni.

“KA INDOTÎR ANG”

12. Eden Huana rûl hnêna Pathianin ânchhia a lawh khân eng thutiam nge a siam tel?
“I (Setana) thlahte leh a thlahte (Pathian mite) pawh ka la indotîr bawk ang; chu mi chuan i lû a la tithitling ang a, nangin a ke artui a la tithitling ang.” Genesis 3:15.

Hetah hian mihring, suala tlu tawhte hnêna thutiam hmasa ber kan hmû a. Hmeichhe thlahah Pathian-mihring, Setana la rawn tiboraltu tûr lo pian tûr thu kan hmu bawk. Pathianin mihringte hi sual haw tûr leh do turin a la kaihruai dâwn tih pawh kan hmû, chu chuan sual simna te, sual avânga lungngaihna nasa tak te leh sual hawisanna te a entîr a ni. Krista chu Chhandamtu leh Lala awm tûra chawimawina pêk a nih avângin sual simna leh ngaihdamna chu Pathianin min rawn pe ta a (Tirhkohte 5:31), amaherawhchu, Pathian ṭhatna leh hmangaihna ropui tak kan hmuh chauhin simna leh sual hawisanna chu kan nei thei ang.

“Pathian ṭhatnain simna tûrin a hruai ṭhîn che tih hre lovin, a ṭhatna ngahzia leh, a entheihzia leh, a dawhtheihzia te chu i ngainêp em ni?” Rome 2:4.

He lei biak bûk rawngbâwlnaa Pathian ṭhatna ngahzia te, dawhtheihzia te leh entheihzia te ngaih pawimawh êm êmna hian mi sualte chu simna a neihtîr a, sual huatna a neihtîr bawk a, Pathian ṭanpuina zawng tûrin a kaihruai a, Tlanna lo awm tûra rinna a neihtîrtu a ni bawk.

PUANZÂR THLÊR

13. Kraws lêra Isua a thih lai khân eng thil pawimawh tak nge lo thleng?
“Isua chu aw ring takin a au leh a, a thlarau a thlah ta a. Ngai teh, Pathian biak in puan zâr chu a chung lam aṭangin a hnuai lam thlengin a lo thlêr chhuak ta a.” Matthaia 27:50, 51.

Tichuan Pathian Berâm Novin kraws lêra thihna a tuar lai chuan leia biak bûk rawngbâwlna, A TAK HLIMTHLÂ CHU A LO TÂWP TA tih chiang takin Pathianin a rawn lantîr ta a ni. Thil hlanten an lo entîr leh kawh chu a tak takin a lo thleng famkim a. Ran thisena inthawinate chuan an hun bi tiam chin an thlen tâk avângin ṭangkaina rêng an nei ta lo a ni.

He zirlai dawta zirlai dang lo awm tûrah kimchang zâwk deuhin Isua thihna thu kan zir ang a, kan beiseina tithar tûra Kalvari kraws va thlîrkîr ṭhin a mamawhzia kan zir bawk ang.
